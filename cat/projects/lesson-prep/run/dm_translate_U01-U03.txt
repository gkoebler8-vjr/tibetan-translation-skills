U01: The subject matter for today: 1. Reflecting on death and impermanence. Regarding impermanence, there are two types: coarse impermanence and subtle impermanence. The second, subtle impermanence, is the moment-by-moment transformation of phenomena. The method for meditating on one’s own death and impermanence consists of three techniques. Meditating on the certainty of one’s own death.
U02: Meditate on the uncertainty of the time of death. Meditate on the fact that at the time of death, nothing is of benefit except the Dharma. Regarding karma and its results: as stated in the *Abhidharmakośa*, "The variety of the world is formed from karma." For example, the external vessel-world also arises from the cause of karma. Contemplate the suffering of cyclic existence.
U03: Having realized that samsara is of the nature of the three sufferings, the arising of the mind that wishes to be liberated from it is taught as renunciation. The three sufferings are: the suffering of suffering, the suffering of change, and the pervasive suffering of conditioning.
