# rays — construal sketch (Pass 1; working material, never shipped)

Page: U01–U12 of nyi ma'i 'od zer (Ayang Thubten Rinpoche), opening. Speaker throughout: the commentator.
U01–U05 = ONE Tibetan period (homage): chain of attributes bzhugs shing / ldan pa dang / snang ba dang / khyab cing / bstan te … brlan te grol bar mdzad pa'i → head NP bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shAkya seng ge la → finite verb 'dud do (I bow).
U06–U09 = frame de yang rgyud bla ma las … ces gsungs so + ONE verse sentence over 15 padas (canonical 16; bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang missing after bltams pa dang).
U10 = one period, finite verb bshad par bya'o (I shall explain). U11–U12 = four short definitional sentences (dgos 'brel).

## U01
SPINE: [subj elided = the Buddha, head of the U05 relative] rang don du (for his own sake) chos sku (apposition: as dharmakaya) … bzhugs (abides, hon.) shing → U02.
RELATIONS: chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa = dharmakaya with no shifting or change | khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa = the element pure (gyis manner) by nature | la = locus: in it glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi = adventitious stains wholly subsided (twofold purity) | zhing = and | rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa = not compounded by (gyis agent) causes and conditions | spros pa dang bral ba = free from elaboration | nam mkha' ltar = like space (simile) | srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du = in (du locus) the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana | bzhugs shing = abides, and.
NEG/QUANT: med ('pho 'gyur med); ma byas; bral; rab tu (wholly; preverb-like, not stacked as an adverb).
TERMS: chos sku dharmakaya · khams the element · rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa pure by nature · glo bur gyi dri ma adventitious stains · 'dus ma byas not compounded · spros bral free from elaboration · srid zhi samsara and nirvana · mnyam pa nyid equality.
Q1: la after rnam par dag pa = locus ("the element pure by nature, in which the adventitious stains have subsided": the two purities) vs. a second dative NP ("the dharmakaya … for the naturally pure element"); locus taken (no second verb for a dative).
Q2: scope of nam mkha' ltar: adverbial to bzhugs ("like space he abides in the equality") vs. with spros pa dang bral ba ("free from elaboration like space"); verb reading taken (no shad between; ltar before the verb phrase).
Q3: zhi with dri ma: "subsided/pacified" is a poor English collocation with stains; surface choice in Pass 3.

## U02
SPINE: ldan pa (endowed) dang → U03; same subject; still inside rang don (shing after bzhugs binds U01–U02; dang after ldan pa opens gzhan don).
RELATIONS: stobs bcu dang mi 'jigs pa bzhi la sogs pa'i yon tan = qualities such as (la sogs pa'i) the ten powers and the four fearlessnesses | ldan pa = possesses | dang = and.
NEG/QUANT: bcu, bzhi, la sogs pa (and others: the enumeration is open).
TERMS: stobs bcu ten powers · mi 'jigs pa bzhi four fearlessnesses · yon tan qualities.
Q4: are the qualities the dharmakaya's (the qualities of freedom) or the Buddha's in general? Grammar: inside the rang don clause chain (shing … dang); dharmakaya section taken; check in grounding.

## U03
SPINE: gzhan don du (for the sake of others) longs sku nges pa lnga ldan (apposition: as the sambhogakaya with the five certainties) … gtso bor (r = as) snang ba (appears) dang → U04.
RELATIONS: rgyal tshab sems dpa'i 'khor rnams kyi = of the retinue(s) consisting of bodhisattvas who are regents (appositional genitive) | 'dren pa'i gtso bo = the chief guide | -r = as | snang ba = appears.
TERMS: longs sku sambhogakaya · nges pa lnga five certainties · rgyal tshab the Victor's regents · sems dpa' bodhisattva · 'khor retinue · 'dren pa guide · gtso bo chief.
Q5: 'dren pa'i gtso bo = "chief guide" (taken) vs. "chief among guides".
Q6: rgyal tshab sems dpa'i 'khor = retinue made up of regent bodhisattvas (taken; = the certain retinue) vs. retinues belonging to the regents.

## U04
SPINE: mchog gi sprul skus (instrumental = agent of khyab) … khyab (pervades) cing → U05.
RELATIONS: brtse ba'i thugs rje chen po'i sprin las = from (las source) the cloud of his loving great compassion | sems can gyi khams la = the realm of beings (la object of khyab) | phyogs ris med par = without partiality (manner) | cing = and.
TERMS: brtse ba love/loving · thugs rje chen po great compassion · mchog gi sprul sku supreme nirmanakaya · sems can gyi khams realm of beings.
Q7: sprul skus agent ("his supreme nirmanakaya pervades", taken: case frame of khyab) vs. means ("through it he pervades"). Image: cloud (U04) → rain (U05) → moistening: one image, kept physical.

## U05
SPINE: mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan (displays as the twelve deeds) te → char gyis … rgyud brlan (moistens) te → grol bar mdzad (frees, hon.) pa'i → [head] bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shAkya seng ge la → lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do (I bow; speaker = the commentator; finite verb of the whole period).
RELATIONS: su = as (the deeds displayed) | te = and then (sequence) | zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis = with (gyis means) the rain of the sacred Dharma of peace and bliss | rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud = the minds(treams) of disciples whose potential has (yang dag par) properly/fully awakened | te = and so | grol bar mdzad pa'i = who liberates (relative to the head) | la = to (goal) | lus ngag yid gsum = with the three: body, speech and mind (instrument) | gus pas = with reverence (manner).
NEG/QUANT: bcu gnyis; rnams; gsum.
TERMS: mdzad pa deeds · bstan display · dam pa'i chos sacred Dharma · zhi bde peace and bliss · rigs potential · gdul bya disciples · rgyud mind(stream) · grol free · bde bar gshegs pa sugata · gtso bo chief · gus pa reverence · 'dud bow.
Q8: yang dag par: content ("fully/truly awakened") vs. Sanskrit-preverb calque to drop; kept as "fully" in the draft.
Q9: subject of bstan/brlan/grol = the nirmanakaya (continuing U04's agent) or the Buddha (head of the relative); same referent; English pronoun choice only.

## U06 (frame + padas 1–4)
SPINE: frame de yang rgyud bla ma las (closing ces gsungs so in U09). Verse sentence: [subj] 'jig rten mkhyen → gzigs nas (having looked) → chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par (without stirring) → sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis (by means of) → [list U07–U09] → ston (displays, U09 last pada).
RELATIONS: de yang = connective tying the citation to the homage (accordingly) | thugs rje chen pos = with great compassion (instrumental, manner of gzigs) | 'jig rten kun la = on the whole world (la object) | gzigs nas ni = having looked (nas sequence; ni filler) | chos kyi sku las = from (las) the dharmakaya | ma g.yos par = without stirring | sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis = through (kyis means) emanations of many forms.
NEG/QUANT: ma g.yos; kun; sna tshogs.
TERMS: 'jig rten mkhyen the Knower of the World · thugs rje chen po great compassion · chos kyi sku dharmakaya · sprul pa emanation.
Q10 (fork on the subject): 'jig rten mkhyen = epithet and subject of the whole sentence ("the Knower of the World", lokavit) vs. finite verb ("with great compassion he knows the world"). Grammar: mkhyen carries no particle; the instrumental fits gzigs better than mkhyen; gzigs nas needs a subject. Epithet taken in the draft; to be grounded.

## U07 (padas 5–8 as given)
SPINE: list items (objects of ston), each closed by dang.
RELATIONS: skye ba mngon par skye ba = ? | dga' ldan gnas nas 'pho ba = passing from (nas source) the Tushita abode (descent) | lhums su 'jug = entering (su goal) the womb (hon.) | bltams pa = birth (hon.) | btsun mo'i 'khor dgyes rol ba = delighting (hon.) in the circle of queens.
TERMS: dga' ldan Tushita · lhums womb · btsun mo queens · 'khor circle/retinue.
Q11: skye ba mngon par skye ba: (a) two items, births (jātaka) and taking birth (in Tushita); (b) one item, "taking birth" with an internal object; (c) "birth after birth". Undecided before grounding; draft has a placeholder reading (b/c).
Var1: canonical text has bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang (skill in the arts) after bltams pa dang; absent here (brief). Translate as given; editor note.

## U08 (padas 9–12)
RELATIONS: nges 'byung = renunciation | dka' ba spyod pa = practicing austerities | byang chub snying por gshegs pa = going (hon.; r goal) to the seat of awakening | bdud sde 'joms = crushing Mara's hosts | rdzogs par ni / byang chub = perfect awakening, one item over the pada break (ni filler) | chos kyi 'khor lo = the wheel of Dharma (verb "turning" elided).
TERMS: nges 'byung renunciation · byang chub snying po seat of awakening · bdud sde hosts of Mara · rdzogs pa'i byang chub perfect awakening · chos kyi 'khor lo wheel of Dharma.
Q12: rdzogs par with byang chub (perfect awakening, taken) vs. completing 'joms ("crushes Mara's hosts completely"); the compound rdzogs par byang chub decides.

## U09 (padas 13–15 + closing frame)
SPINE: mya ngan 'das par gshegs (passing into nirvana) mdzad rnams (deeds, resumptive plural of the whole list) → yongs su ma dag zhing rnams su (in impure realms, su locus) → srid pa ji srid gnas par (for as long as existence lasts) → ston (displays, present) | ces gsungs so (closing frame).
NEG/QUANT: ma dag; ji srid.
Q13: yongs su ma dag = "impure" (taken: yongs su mirrors Skt pari-, failure #16) vs. "not wholly pure".

## U10
SPINE: gnyis pa ni (the second [section]:) … bsdus pa 'di ni (topic: this condensation) … bstan bcos … snying po (apposition) bshad par bya'o (I shall explain; first person, commitment).
RELATIONS: phung po dang 'chi bdag dang lha'i bu dang nyon mongs pa ste bdud bzhi = the four maras, namely (ste) … | las rgyal ba'i = victorious over (las) | thub pa mchog gi gsung rab = the scriptures of the supreme Sage | rtag chad kyi mtha' dang bral ba = free from the extremes of permanence and annihilation | rgya mtsho'i klong ltar dpag tu med pa = immeasurable like the ocean's depths | las = from (source/partitive) | mdo sde dang rgyud sde rnams dang / klu sgrub dang thogs med la sogs pa'i bstan bcos dang / chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po'i man ngag zab mo'i don = the sutras and tantras, the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga and others, and the meaning of the profound pith instructions of the great Dharma Lord Drikungpa | klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i = worthy of honor like a naga king's crown jewel | nang nas = from within | ma rtogs pa dang log par rtog pa dang the tshom gyi dri ma med par = without (manner) the stains of not understanding, misunderstanding and doubt | bsdus pa = condensed | bai DUrya la gsol ba btab na (na condition: when one prays) dgos 'dod thams cad 'byung ba bzhin (just as) | gnas skabs dang mthar thug gi don thams cad 'grub par byed pa'i bstan bcos = the treatise that accomplishes every aim, temporary and ultimate | gzhi dang lam dang 'bras bu gsum du bstan pa'i snying po = the Heart [of the Teaching], set out as the three: ground, path and fruition.
NEG/QUANT: bzhi; dpag tu med; med (dri ma); thams cad ×2; gsum.
TERMS: thub pa the Sage · bdud bzhi the four maras · gsung rab scriptures · rtag chad permanence and annihilation · mdo sde sutras · rgyud sde tantras · bstan bcos treatise · chos rje Dharma Lord · man ngag pith instructions · klu rgyal naga king · bai DUrya vaidurya · dgos 'dod needs and wishes · gnas skabs dang mthar thug temporary and ultimate · gzhi lam 'bras bu ground, path and fruition.
Q14 (head noun): bsdus pa 'di = the root text (Zhedang Dorje's Heart, condensed from these sources, praised, and the object of bshad) vs. the commentary itself ("in this condensed explanation I shall explain the Heart"). Root text taken: the stainless-compilation praise and the wish-gem simile praise a treatise being explained.
Q15: scope of klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i: the nearest item (Drikungpa's instructions, taken) vs. the whole list.
Q16: las … nang nas: one source chain (from the Sage's scriptures, i.e. the sutras and tantras, and from …) taken.
Q17: snying po = the title echo (theg chen bstan pa'i snying po) "the Heart of the Teaching" vs. plain "the essence of what is taught as ground, path and fruition".
Q18: bai DUrya: the gem prayed to for wishes (functions as the wish-fulfilling jewel); render "vaidurya" (house: Sanskrit kept) vs. "beryl/lapis".
Issue: gnyis pa ni is a sa bcad marker; the first heading is not on this page.

## U11
SPINE: de yang (now) dgos sogs kyi chos bzhi las (of [las partitive] the four points, purpose and the rest) brjod bya ni (the subject matter) gzhi lam 'bras bu gsum mo (is the three: ground, path, fruition).
TERMS: dgos sogs chos bzhi the four points (purpose and the rest) · brjod bya subject matter.

## U12
SPINE: dgos pa ni … rtogs pa'o (the purpose: understanding it through study and reflection) | dgos pa'i dgos pa ni … mngon du 'gyur ba'o (the ultimate purpose: the fruition becoming manifest through meditating on its meaning) | 'brel ba ni … 'brel pa'o (the connection).
RELATIONS: thos bsam gyis = through (instr.) study and reflection | de = it (the subject matter) | de'i don = its meaning | bsgoms pas = by meditating (pas cause/means) | 'bras bu mngon du 'gyur = the fruition becomes manifest | snga ma la brten te = relying on the earlier (te manner/sequence) | phyi ma rim gyis = the later ones in order | thabs dang thabs las byung ba'i tshul gyis = in the manner of means and what arises from (las) the means.
TERMS: dgos pa purpose · dgos pa'i dgos pa ultimate purpose (lit. purpose of the purpose) · 'brel ba connection · thos bsam study and reflection · 'bras bu fruition · mngon du 'gyur become manifest · thabs means.
Q19: 'bras bu = fruition (the third of gzhi lam 'bras bu, kept the same word for the echo).
