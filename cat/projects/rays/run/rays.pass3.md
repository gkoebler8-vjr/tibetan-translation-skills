# rays — Pass 3 (style pass, after grounding), before the check

U01: For his own sake, he is the dharmakaya, which never shifts or changes: the element that is pure by nature, in which the adventitious stains have been cleared away. Not compounded by causes and conditions, free from elaboration, like space, he abides in the equality of samsara and nirvana,

U02: and he possesses qualities such as the ten powers and the four fearlessnesses.

U03: For the sake of others, he is the sambhogakaya endowed with the five certainties, and he appears as the chief guide of his retinue of bodhisattvas, the Victor's regents.

U04: From the clouds of his great and loving compassion, his supreme nirmanakaya pervades the realm of beings impartially

U05: and displays the twelve deeds. Then, with the rain of the sacred Dharma of peace and bliss, it moistens the minds of disciples whose potential has fully awakened, and so sets them free. I bow with reverence in body, speech and mind to him, Shakyasimha, chief of the sugatas.

U06: Accordingly, in the *Sublime Continuum* it is said:
Out of great compassion, knowing the world,
he looks upon all the worlds,
and then, never stirring from the dharmakaya,
through emanations of many kinds

U07:
he takes birth,
descends from the Tushita heaven,
enters the womb and is born,
delights in his circle of queens,

U08:
renounces the world, practices austerities,
goes to the seat of awakening,
defeats the hosts of Mara,
attains perfect awakening,
turns the wheel of Dharma,

U09:
and passes into nirvana.
He displays these deeds in impure realms
for as long as existence lasts.

U10: Second. The supreme Sage conquered the four maras: the aggregates, the lord of death, the son of the gods and the afflictions. His scriptures are free from the extremes of permanence and annihilation and as immeasurable as the ocean's depths. This treatise was condensed from their sutras and tantras, from the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga and others, and from the meaning of the great Dharma Lord Drikungpa's profound pith instructions, worthy of honor like a naga king's crown jewel. It is free of the stains of not understanding, misunderstanding and doubt. Just as every need and wish comes forth when one prays to the vaidurya, the finest of gems, it fulfills every aim, temporary and ultimate. It is the *Heart of the Teaching*, which sets out the three—ground, path and fruition—and I shall explain it.

U11: Now, of the four points—purpose and the rest—the subject matter is the three: ground, path and fruition.

U12: The purpose is to understand it through study and reflection. The ultimate purpose is that the fruition becomes manifest through meditation on its meaning. The connection is that each relies on the one before, and in this way, step by step, they are linked as means and what arises from the means.
