# MITRA (dm.py translate, style: literal, keep every clause and connective) — tantra
U01 Vajragarbha asked: Since the nature of the eye is empty, how does consciousness exist within it? Since the nature of the ear is empty, how does consciousness exist within it? 
U02 Since the nature of the nose is empty, how can consciousness exist within it? Since the nature of the tongue is empty, how can consciousness exist within it? 
U03 Since the nature of the body is empty, how can consciousness exist within it? Since the nature of the mind is empty, how can consciousness exist in its midst? 
U04 Therefore, there is no form and no seer; there is no sound and no hearer; there is no smell and no smeller; there is no taste and no taster. 
U05 There is no contact, and there is no one who experiences contact; there is no mind, and there is nothing that has arisen from the mind. 
U06 The Blessed One spoke: Listen just as it is to the path of suchness, which is non-dual and has completely abandoned duality. It is without conceptual thought and without consciousness; likewise, it is not realized by logicians. 
U07 The secret of all the Buddhas is equal like the sky; the Hearers do not know it, for they are obscured by the darkness of not knowing. 
U08 What is the wisdom that awakens from habitual tendencies? It is the emanation of the Pratyekabuddhas. It is a secret, the very domain of the Buddhas’ activity; all of them do not know it. 
U09 With a mind that seeks to cross over the terrors of the ocean of saṃsāra, that which is encircled within the center of the body is said to symbolize the crossing. 
U10 Through the mind of consciousness and primordial wisdom, the defining characteristic and the basis of the definition are symbolized. Through primordial wisdom, one views the objects of knowledge. Through the objects of knowledge, one examines the states of existence. 
U11 With a mind that examines the states of existence, one proceeds just as one desires and so forth; regarding the process of proceeding, one has proceeded through many thousands of previous births. 
U12 Having contemplated the characteristics previously taught by the Yoga as being empty of characteristics, the one endowed with intelligence should sow the seed upon the field of the body’s emptiness and so forth.
