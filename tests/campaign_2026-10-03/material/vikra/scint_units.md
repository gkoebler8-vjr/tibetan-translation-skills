# Test page V3 — Jigten Sumgön, from "Scintillation of the Precious Vajra" (chos rje 'jig rten mgon po'i rnam thar rdo rje'i rin po che 'bar ba)

Work: *chos rje 'jig rten mgon po'i rnam thar rdo rje'i rin po che 'bar ba* ("Scintillation of the Precious Vajra", Tib. Funkensprühen), a biography of Jigten Sumgön (1143–1217) by Chenga Sherab Jungne (1187–1241). The passage: Phagmodrupa's circle calls Jigten Sumgön a tenth-level bodhisattva, he answers that his mind is the realisation of mahamudra, sameness with all buddhas and beings, he proclaims it with a lion's voice in the assembly, and, asked where he will go after his passing, replies that there is no single buddha field; the biographer then states that he is the sixth Bhagavan Vajradhara.
