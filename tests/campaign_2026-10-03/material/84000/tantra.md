# TANTRA: Emergence from Sampuṭa (Sampuṭodbhava Tantra), Toh 381, Chapter 1, passages 1.14-1.25

## Metadata

- **Full title (84000 long title, English):** The Foundation of All Tantras, the Great Sovereign Compendium “Emergence from Sampuṭa” (short title: Emergence from Sampuṭa)
- **Tibetan title:** ཡང་དག་པར་སྦྱོར་བ་ཞེས་བྱ་བའི་རྒྱུད་ཆེན་པོ།
- **Sanskrit title (84000):** Saṃpuṭodbhava­sarva­tantra­nidāna­mahā­kalpa­rājaḥ
- **Toh:** Toh 381 (Degé Kangyur, Unexcelled Yoga Tantras)

- **URL (Tibetan source view, folio pages):** https://read.84000.co/source/toh381/folio/74
- **Passage context:** Chapter 1, shortly after the opening dialogue in which Vajragarbha and the bodhisattvas ask what the "foundation of all tantras" is. All twelve units are verse stanzas (84000 stanza numbers {1.1.13}-{1.1.24}); the doctrinal content is the emptiness of the sense-spheres and consciousness (U01-U05), the secret of all buddhas as nondual and beyond reasoning (U06-U08), and the investigation of rebirth destinies and the planting of the seed of the empty body (U09-U12). Prose is limited to the speaker-introductions (e.g. "Vajragarbha said:") inside U01 and U06. Alignment note: this span deliberately starts at 1.14 because in 1.11-1.13 the 84000 English (following the Sanskrit) has two extra half-stanzas that the Tibetan lacks, so those passages are not pada-aligned; 1.14-1.25 are one-to-one stanza matches.

### U01 (verse) - 84000 passage 1.14 - F.74.a

passage uuid: `7b62686b-4534-4b7a-b137-717fa8a5e678`

**Tibetan**

```
རྡོ་རྗེ་སྙིང་པོས་གསོལ་བ། མིག་གི་རང་བཞིན་སྟོང་པ་ཡི། །

```

**84000 English**

```
Vajragarbha said:
“Since the eye by its nature is empty,
How can consciousness arise in its midst?
Since the ear by its nature is empty,
How can consciousness arise in its midst? {1.1.13}
```
