# Reference English (H) for test page B_tantra — 84000 translation, verbatim (see B_tantra.md for translator and version)

U01 All the goddesses in numbers equaling the dust particles on Mount Sumeru, headed by Nairātmyā Yoginī and including Locanā, Māmakī, Pāṇḍurā, Tārā, Bhṛkuṭī, Cundā, Parṇaśabarī, and Ahomukhā, heard these words and were utterly amazed. [2.4.65]

U02 They swooned, were afraid, collapsed on the ground, and trembled. The vajra holder saw all the goddesses and praised them so they would rise up again. [2.4.66]

U03 “You, goddesses, are associated / With earth, water, wind, and fire. / Listen, as I will explain in detail / The reality that no one knows.” [2.4.67]

U04 Hearing the Blessed One’s words as if in a dream, the goddesses all regained consciousness. [2.4.68]

U05 The Blessed One said: / “Beings are truly buddhas, / But are veiled by adventitious impurities. / Once dispelled, they are buddhas.” [2.4.69]

U06 The goddesses responded, “So it is, Blessed One! This is neither true nor a lie!” [2.4.70]

U07 The Blessed One said: / “A worldly person who lacks intelligence / Will collapse after eating strong poison. / A person without delusion who knows the principles / Will completely destroy the very same. [2.4.71]

U08 “Likewise, those who know the means of extinguishing / And are comfortable in the Hevajra / Will not succumb to ignorance and the rest, / And will not be bound by delusion and the like. [2.4.72]

U09 “There is not a single being who is not a buddha, / Because each and every one is awakened, / Whether they are a hell being, a preta, an animal, / A deva, an asura, or a human being. [2.4.73]

U10 “Worms living in feces and other such beings / Are naturally and continuously blissful / Because they do not know the bliss / Experienced by devas or asuras. [2.4.74]

U11 “A buddha cannot be found anywhere else, / In any of the world systems. / The mind itself is perfectly awakened; / A buddha does not appear anywhere else. [2.4.75]

U12 “Even caṇḍālas, veṇukāras, and other castes / Who focus on killing for their sustenance / Will succeed if they learn the Hevajra Tantra, / There is no doubt about this. [2.4.76]
