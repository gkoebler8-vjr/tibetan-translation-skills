## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U09 "realms that are not wholly pure" for yongs su ma dag zhing: impure realms come out as partly pure. Cause: grammar misread (scope of the negation), which went unflagged.
2. U06 "who looks … on all the world" for gzigs nas: the sequence "having looked, he then shows" flattens to simultaneity. Cause: verse constraint (relative clause chosen to fit the line).
3. U10: the first English sentence carries the whole source chain before its verb and is hard to follow. Cause: register (the Tibetan period kept too long under compression).
4. U01–U05: the homage is held as one relative-clause chain, "To him who …; who …; who … — to the chief … I bow", with a doubled dative and heavy appositions in U01. Cause: register (Tibetan period structure carried over).
5. U08 "awakening's heart" (a calque for the seat of awakening) and the "his full / awakening" line break. Cause: term choice and verse constraint.
