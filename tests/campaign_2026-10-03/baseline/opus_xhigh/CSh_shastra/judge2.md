## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U07 "the outer seal and the moments are correctly indicated": the outer seal becomes an object of the indication rather than the means of the consecration, and this conflicts with C's own reading in U08. Cause: construal fork (comitative dang), decided silently.
2. U05 and later, rab tu bsgrub pa rendered "realization / realize": this merges with rtogs pa ("realized", U12) and blurs "bringing about" the joys with cognitive realization. Cause: term choice / dictionary sense.
3. U05–U08 prose: long calqued nominal chains ("the very own-nature", "the very instruction", "whose defining mark is that it is not delimited by determination…") are faithful but not reader-edition English. Cause: register (literal commentary style with no style pass).
4. U04 root verse "and the fourth is that, thus, again": the line is bent to fit the later gloss and does not work as English, so it only makes sense with the note. Cause: construal fork, resolved toward the commentary at the cost of the line.
5. Verses U02, U10, U11 have no consistent metre, and U11 has a bracket inside a line. Cause: verse constraint not applied (bare run, no verse procedure).
