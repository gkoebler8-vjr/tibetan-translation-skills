## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U07 "The daughters too ... feel desire": daughters made the agent of desire instead of its object, breaking the parallel with the mother clause. Cause: grammar misread (unmarked absolutive taken as subject).
2. U07 "it is thinking 'This is the enemy' that they kill him": agentless clause resolved to the sons killing the father with no Q, where the passage likely means one kills the sons as enemies. Cause: construal fork (unrecorded).
3. U06 "The celestial palace is the final goal": genitive gi read as apposition, so the palace itself, not its culmination, becomes buddhahood. Cause: grammar misread (alternative flagged but not chosen).
4. U06 "the act of the exalted accumulation of merit", "the exalted death": word-for-word phrasing that a reader edition cannot carry. Cause: register.
5. U02 "Acala's meditation": mi g.yo taken as the deity without recording the "unwavering meditation" alternative. Cause: construal fork.
