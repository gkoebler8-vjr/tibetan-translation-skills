# CT_tantra — bare run (no skill), Claude Opus at max effort

## Glossary (begun with this passage)
- Acala — mi g.yo ba, "the Immovable". Black Acala at the center is Chandamaharoshana himself; White Acala and the others are his emanations in the four cardinal directions.
- Akshobhya, Mamaki — mi bskyod pa, mA ma kI: the father and the mother in the account of conception in U07.
- archways — rta babs (torana): the ornamented arches above the four gates.
- Blessed One, Blessed Lady — bcom ldan 'das, bcom ldan 'das ma (bhagavan, bhagavati): Acala and his consort, the two speakers.
- castes, the four — bram ze, rje rigs, rgyal rigs, dmangs rigs: brahmins, vaishyas (merchants), kshatriyas (nobles), shudras (laborers).
- consort — shes rab (prajna) where it names the female partner; in the pair wisdom and method the same word is rendered "wisdom".
- curved knife — gri gug (kartari): the emblem of Dveshavajri.
- Dveshavajri — zhe sdang rdo rje ma, "Vajra Lady of Hatred": the consort at the center.
- equipoise — mnyam gzhag (samahita): meditative absorption.
- exceptional — khyad par 'phags pa: said of the accumulation of merit, of death and of bliss.
- existence, nonexistence — dngos po, dngos po med pa (bhava, abhava).
- gandharva — dri za: the being of the intermediate state that seeks a birth.
- Guide, Lord — 'dren pa, gtso bo: the Blessed Lady's terms of address.
- imprints — bag chags (vasana): habitual tendencies carried from past births.
- level of buddhahood — sangs rgyas kyi sa (buddhabhumi).
- maras, the four — bdud bzhi: of the aggregates, of the afflictions, of the lord of death and of the divine son; here identified with Brahma, Ishvara (Shiva), Vishnu and Indra.
- Mohavajri — gti mug rdo rje ma, "Vajra Lady of Delusion": first of the goddesses of the intermediate directions.
- one taste — ro gcig.
- Prajnaparamita — shes rab pha rol tu phyin pa: the perfection of wisdom, here as a goddess.
- purity — rnam par dag pa (visuddhi): the pure meaning that a feature of the mandala or a stage of the meditation stands for; "cleanses" in U04 renders sbyong byed.
- seven eggs of Brahma, seven underworlds — tshangs pa'i sgo nga bdun, sa 'og bdun: the worlds above and below.
- siddhi — dngos grub.
- standard lists — four immeasurables (tshad med pa'i gnas bzhi), four truths, four concentrations (bsam gtan, dhyana), eight branches of the noble path, nine branches of scripture (gsung rab yan lag dgu), five wisdoms.
- threatening forefinger — sdigs mdzub (tarjani).
- white and red — dkar dmar: the male and female generative elements.
- wisdom and method — shes rab, thabs (prajna, upaya).
- womb — skye gnas, literally "place of birth".

### U01
TEXT:
The yogi who unites with her
gains siddhi swiftly, past all doubt.
Or else, without a consort, he
should meditate in utter equipoise.
NOTES:
Q: "Unites with her" renders de yi sbyor bas, "by her (or its) union", taken as union with the consort of the practice taught just before the span; it could also mean "through that practice".
Q: "Consort" renders shes rab (prajna) in its tantric sense of the female partner; the genitive in shes rab dang bral ba'i could also attach to the equipoise, "an equipoise free of a consort", with the same practical sense.

### U05
TEXT:
Here, the square is the four immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four archways are the four concentrations. The eight pillars are the eight branches of the noble path. The single-layered wall is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the womb. The many colors are the many emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red of the directions is great passion. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are the brahmins, the vaishyas, the kshatriyas and the shudras. The moon and sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of Black Acala. The curved knife is Dveshavajri. The directions beginning with the east are White Acala and the others. The intermediate directions beginning with the southeast, the quarter of fire, belong to Mohavajri and the others. This is the purity of the mandala.
NOTES:
Q: "The red of the directions" (phyogs dmar po) is taken as the red of the four cardinal quarters, set against the four colors of the corners.
Q: The castes follow the usual sense of the Tibetan terms: rje rigs as vaishya, rgyal rigs as kshatriya. If the Sanskrit had the common order brahmin, kshatriya, vaishya, shudra, yellow and green would swap castes. The fourth term, written da mangs rigs, is read as dmangs rigs.
Q: rngul, "sweat", stands where the red female element is expected (compare "the white and the red" in U06); kept as written.
Q: 'phar ma gcig pa, "single-layered wall", may mean a single course or tier of the wall.

### U06
TEXT:
Now I shall state the purity of the meditation. First, the offering is the act of gathering an exceptional accumulation of merit. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom: an exceptional death, the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva. The culmination of the palace is the level of buddhahood. The lotus is the womb. The moon and sun are the white and the red.
NOTES:
Q: It is unclear whether "an exceptional death, the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva" all describe emptiness, as the Tibetan syntax suggests, or whether "the stainless body is the gandharva's body" is a separate correspondence.
Q: gzhal yang khang (for gzhal yas khang) gi mthar thug pa, "the culmination of the palace", may mean the palace as the final stage of the visualization, or the palace in its ultimate sense.

### U07
TEXT:
The syllable HUM is the mind of the gandharva between the mother and the father. Akshobhya is the father; Mamaki is the mother. Seeing the two in mutual passion, it feels hatred for the father and desire for the mother, and through delusion it enters, as any sentient being does. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and takes his place. Taking the mother, through the exceptional bliss of the imprints of endless births, he in turn begets sons. The sons are White Acala and the others, and the daughters are Mohavajri and the others. The sons too kill the father and go to others: they kill him thinking, "This is the enemy." The daughters too, through the exceptional bliss of the imprints of endless births, feel desire.
NOTES:
Q: "He in turn begets sons" renders de yang bur skye bar byed, "makes that one too be born as a son"; it could also mean that he himself is born as a son, or that he has the slain father reborn as his son.
Q: The Tibetan reads "the daughters are White Acala and the others, and also Mohavajri and the others"; since the Acalas are male, this is taken as sons and daughters.
Q: "Go to others" (gzhan la bgrod pa) may carry the sexual sense of going to a woman, or mean passing on to other births.

### U08
TEXT:
The sword is wisdom and the noose is method. Or else, the noose is wisdom and the sword is method. The two become one taste: this is the threatening forefinger. The left eye, looking downward, protects the seven underworlds. The right eye, looking upward, protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the ground protects the earth. The right foot, striking out, terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma, the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara, the mara of the lord of death is Vishnu, and the mara of the divine son is Indra.
NOTES:
Q: "Eye" is supplied: the text says only "the left" and "the right", looking down and up; taken as the deity's squinting gaze rather than his hands.

### U09
TEXT:
The earth is the enjoyment of the young women all over the earth. It is a lotus seat because he dwells long in the young woman. Being born from the womb is the seat of moon and sun. The man, born from semen and sweat, is existence; the woman is nonexistence. Black is consciousness, white is form, yellow is feeling, red is perception, and green is Amoghasiddhi.
NOTES:
Q: "The earth" seems to take up "protects the earth" in U08: the protector of the earth, a title of kings, enjoys the young women of his realm.
Q: Green is given as Amoghasiddhi where the series of aggregates leads one to expect formations; kept as written.

### U12
TEXT:
The Teacher, Victor, one alone,
truly abides in fivefold form;
and Prajnaparamita too
truly abides in fivefold form.
NOTES:
Q: Prajnaparamita (shes rab pha rol phyin nyid) is read as the goddess, the Victor's female counterpart, rather than the perfection of wisdom as a quality; the five forms are presumably the five buddhas and their five consorts.
