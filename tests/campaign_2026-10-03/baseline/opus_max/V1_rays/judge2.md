## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U09 "realms that are impure" drops yongs su (utterly impure). Cause: omission under compression, to fit the metre (verse constraint).
2. U08 the verse barely scans, and the enjambed "perfect / enlightenment" is awkward. Cause: verse constraint (C kept the Tibetan's line split at the cost of rhythm).
3. U10 "this Heart of the Teachings, set forth as …" adds "Teachings" to make a title echo, and bstan pa is rendered twice. Cause: construal fork (a pun on the title, resolved by gloss).
4. U01 the homage opens with a dense chain of appositions between dashes before the main clause arrives. Cause: register (the Tibetan relative chain is kept too literally for the English).
5. U12 the calque "the purpose of the purpose" and the stiff "what arises from the means". Cause: dictionary sense (literal rendering of dgos pa'i dgos pa and of thabs las byung ba).
