## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
No meaning errors were found in C. These five are English-quality problems.
1. U08 "vanquishing the hosts of mara, perfect / enlightenment, the wheel of Dharma": the split keeps the Tibetan line break and reads awkwardly in English; the line is a noun list with uneven rhythm. Cause: verse constraint.
2. U01–U05: one five-unit period, "To him who… who… who… and who… — to the Lion of the Shakyas… I bow", with the main verb about 150 words in. Faithful, but heavy for a reader edition. Cause: register (Tibetan period kept whole).
3. U10: the first sentence stacks "From the scriptures… from the sutras… from among all these" with long appositions before "this text has been condensed". Cause: grammar-faithful syntax, not an editor's restructuring.
4. U04 "his loving great compassion": a word-for-word calque of brtse ba'i thugs rje chen po. Cause: term choice / calque.
5. U12 "The purpose of the purpose", and "understand it" for the three topics. Cause: term choice (a literal technical term instead of "ultimate purpose") and a slip in pronoun number.
