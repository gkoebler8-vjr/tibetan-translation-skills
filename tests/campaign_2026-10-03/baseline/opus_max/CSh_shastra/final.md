### U05
TEXT:
Here in this Yoginī tantra one is a master (ācārya) because one conducts oneself, in every respect, far removed from evil, unwholesome dharmas; and [the consecration is called the master's] because it is the saṃvara of that very [master]. The nature of the master consecration is what is called "bringing about, from the karmamudrā, the intrinsic nature of the four joys by means of the four moments"; but one is not to be [reckoned] consecrated with the master consecration through having obtained the irreversible consecration, since that is common to the Kriyā tantras and the rest. Hence the statement "One also becomes endowed with the good fortune of expounding, hearing, and so on, all the Yoga and Yoginī tantras and the rest" is of no account. Therefore, first of all, in order to become entitled to hear, meditate on, and so on, the Yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, one must be consecrated with the master consecration itself. And it should be understood that this [consecration] is marked, in accordance with the ritual manual (abhisamaya), by the defining characteristics of the master, the secret, the wisdom, and the fourth [consecrations].
NOTES:
Q: The first clause is a nirukti of ācārya: ā- (kun du "in every respect"; also ārāt "far", ring du) + √car (spyod pa "conduct oneself"); the English can only approximate it.
Q: de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas so is compressed; taken as explaining the name "master consecration" (the saṃvara, i.e., consecration, proper to the master); alternatively "that very [conduct] is his saṃvara", playing on saṃvara as "restraint".
Q: mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba: the "irreversible" (avaivartika?) consecration of the Yoga-tantra rite, here denied the status of the Yoginī-tantra master consecration. slob dpon gyis (instr.) at its first occurrence is read as slob dpon gyi, as later in the unit.
Q: mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du: "in accordance with the ritual manual (abhisamaya)"; alternatively "according to [the candidate's] realization", which would anticipate the grading by faculties in U06–U07.

### U06
TEXT:
To those of dull faculties who have obtained a master consecration of this kind, one should teach—after ascertaining [them] by [their] inclination—the meditation on the karmamudrā and on the mudrā with elaborations. Likewise, in the domain of the completion stage as well, the experiencing of the four joys, whose nature is the four moments, [in] the jewel abiding in the secret [place], through the guru's instruction itself—that is called the secret consecration, since it is not to be taught to yogins applied to meditation with elaborations. Here, to those of middling faculties who have been consecrated [with it], the meditation on the samayamudrā is to be taught and explained.
NOTES:
Q: mos pas nges par bzung nas: "after ascertaining [them] by [their] inclination", with the master as subject; alternatively "having fixed [the practice] by imaginative resolve (adhimokṣa)".
Q: spros pa'i phyag rgya, "mudrā with elaborations" (prapañca), is not one of the usual four mudrās; it may stand in apposition to the karmamudrā ("the karmamudrā, the mudrā with elaborations") rather than name a second practice.
Q: gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu has no case marker; read as locative on the model of snying gi nor bur in U07 (the maṇi as bodily locus); alternatively "the jewel abiding in secret, [namely] the guru's instruction".

### U07
TEXT:
Likewise, wisdom (prajñā) is distinctive gnosis: the knowing of all dharmas as mind alone (cittamātra). The consecration for bringing that about is the wisdom-gnosis consecration (prajñājñānābhiṣeka). Moreover, the wisdom-gnosis is to be understood as this: through the guru's instruction itself, the external mudrā and the moments are properly signified in the heart-jewel, whose form gathers into one the nature of the three consciousnesses—the dependent (paratantra), the imagined (parikalpita), and the transformed. To those of the highest faculties who have been consecrated with that consecration, the dharmamudrā is to be taught and explained by way of the illusion-like concentration (māyopamasamādhi).
NOTES:
Q: khyad par can gyi ye shes ni shes rab ste is read as a nirukti of prajñā, pra- taken as "distinctive"; the Tibetan order is "distinctive gnosis is wisdom".
Q: yongs su gyur pa "transformed" (pariṇata? parāvṛtta?) stands where the three-nature scheme has yongs su grub pa (pariniṣpanna, "perfected"); possibly a corruption, possibly a deliberate "transformed [consciousness]". Calling the three "consciousnesses" (rnam par shes pa) is likewise unusual.

### U08
TEXT:
Likewise there is also the fourth: "that, thus, again". "That" means the wisdom-gnosis. "Thus" (tathā) means thusness (tathatā): among [the terms] "thusness", "reality-limit" (bhūtakoṭi) and "dharma-realm" (dharmadhātu) there is no difference in meaning. The consecration by which its nature is seen or brought about is what the word "thus" is meant to express. The word "again" means "it is to be conferred again, directly after the wisdom-gnosis consecration". Moreover, that [fourth] is this: that in which, through the guru's instruction itself, one apprehends, by the yoga of objectless apprehension, the external mudrā and the perfection of the moment, which is characterized as not delimited into separate, determinate objects—that is to be called the fourth consecration. Merely teaching the instruction does not amount to the fourth consecration. Otherwise—since the Blessed One, with "by it one is bathed and consecrated" and so on, holds consecration here to be the washing away of stains—how could it come about merely by teaching an instruction?
NOTES:
Q: "thus"/"thusness" keep the tathā/tathatā gloss audible in English; "suchness" would lose it.
Q: phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang precedes gang; construed as a second object of dmigs par byed pa beside "the perfection of the moment" (cf. U07, where the external mudrā and the moments are signified together); alternatively comitative, "with the external mudrā".
Q: The Tibetan runs two clauses together (brjod par bya ba'i man ngag); literally "it does not become the fourth consecration merely by teaching the instruction that is to be called 'the fourth consecration'". Divided after brjod par bya ba; the sense is not affected.
Q: yul so sor nges par yongs su bcad pa ma yin pa'i mtshan nyid can may describe the fourth moment, vilakṣaṇa ("beyond characteristics").

### U11
