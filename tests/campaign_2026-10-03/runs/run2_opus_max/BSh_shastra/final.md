### U01
HEADER: U01 · prose · instruction (sādhana exposition inside the commentary, third person) · academic · not a citation
TEXT:
Here, early in the morning, the *mantrin* engaged in meditation visualizes the seed syllable of Nairātmyā, together with a garland of light rays, on a moon disk at his own heart. Then he washes his face and so on, and protects himself, the place and the yoga. After that he enters the place of meditation and sits on a comfortable seat; then he consecrates the *argha* with his own mantra. In the sky in front, the mantrin should visualize the maṇḍala of Nairātmyā, which gathers all the *tathāgata*s. Then he should worship it with the eight offering goddesses who arise from the seed syllable at his own heart.
NOTES:
Q: bsgom pa'i dbang du byas pa'i sngags pa = "the mantrin engaged in meditation" (pa'i attaches the phrase to the mantrin); alt. "here, as regards meditation, the mantrin …" (dbang du byas = adhikṛtya), which is MITRA's reading.
Q: de bzhin gshegs pa thams cad sdud par byed pa taken as describing the maṇḍala ("which gathers all the tathāgatas"); alt. the mantrin's own act: "the mantrin gathers all the tathāgatas, visualizing Nairātmyā's maṇḍala in the sky in front".
Q: lha mo brgyad kyis: instrumental (he worships by means of the goddesses) or agentive (the goddesses perform the worship); "with" keeps both.
Q: rang gi sngags, "his own mantra": probably the mantra of his own deity; kept literal.
Alt: yongs su bsngags pa = "consecrates with mantra" (abhimantr-); the homograph "praises" is excluded by the la-marked object and "with his own mantra".
Issue: ar+g+haM transliterates Skt. arghaṃ, given as *argha*; nang par = early in the morning, at daybreak.
Check: in-context, no finding. After the MITRA cross-check, the obligative of "mchod par bya'o" now also governs "visualize" in the second sentence (te-chain; changed).

### U04
HEADER: U04 · prose · instruction with embedded first-person liturgy (confession, rejoicing, refuge, generating the mind; the four immeasurables) · academic · not a citation
TEXT:
In its presence one should perform the confession of wrongdoing and so on, as follows: "I confess all wrongdoing. I rejoice in all the virtue of noble ones and ordinary beings. Until I reach the seat of awakening, I go for refuge to the Buddha; I go for refuge to the Dharma; I go for refuge to the Saṅgha. To benefit all sentient beings and make them happy, to have them attain the three kinds of awakening according to their lot, I generate the mind set on unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening." Then one should cultivate love that wishes all sentient beings to attain supreme happiness. Then, with compassion, one should remove all their suffering. Then one should make them inseparable from joy and happiness. Then [comes] the equanimity that brings them mundane and supramundane virtue.
NOTES:
Q: so sor bshags = "confess" (so sor mirrors Skt. prati-); alt. "confess each one individually" (MITRA).
Q: the two purpose clauses (… bya ba'i phyir | … bya ba'i phyir) stand side by side without dang: coordinate, or the second specifying the first; rendered without a connective to keep both.
Q: skal ba ji lta bar = "according to their lot"; alt. "according to their capacities" (MITRA "capacity").
Q: nye bar gtod pa = "brings [to them]" (upanaya; RY "entrustment"); alt. "directs toward them", "dedicates to them" (MITRA).
Q: the last sentence is verbless: "[comes]" supplied; alt. "one should cultivate" carried over from the sentence on love (MITRA "one should practice").
Q: bgyi'o (humble future) rendered as performative present ("I confess", "I generate"); alt. "I shall confess …".
Alt: byang chub kyi snying po = "seat of awakening" (bodhimaṇḍa); lit. "essence of awakening" (MITRA keeps the literal).
Issue: register shifts to first-person liturgy at "bdag gis" and back to instruction at "de nas"; the formulas are set in quotation marks (no zhes close in the Tibetan). de'i mdun du = in the presence of the maṇḍala visualized in U01.
Check: in-context, 1 minor: a bracketed "[my]" ("I confess all [my] wrongdoing") narrowed sdig pa thams cad; removed. MITRA also adds "my"; not followed.

### U11
