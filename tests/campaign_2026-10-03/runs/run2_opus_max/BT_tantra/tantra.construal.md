UNIT        U01 (prose; one period that runs on into U02 via `cing`)
FORM        prose, narrative frame
GENRE       narrative
REGISTER    narrator to reader; plain, dignified scriptural narrative
SOURCE      de nas rnal 'byor ma bdag med ma la sogs pa'i lha mo thams cad la 'di lta ste / spyan ma dang /

            parNNa ri khrod ma dang / 'og zhal ma dang / de dag la sogs pa ri rab kyi rdul phra rab dang

            beginning with · 'di lta ste = namely · names: spyan ma Locana, mA ma kI Mamaki, gos dkar mo
            Pandaravasini, sgrol ma Tara, khro gnyer can Bhrikuti, tsun dA Cunda, parNNa ri khrod ma

            these and others · ri rab kyi rdul phra rab dang mnyam pa'i = equal (in number) to the finest
            particles of Mount Meru · mchog tu ngo mtshar bar gyur = became supremely amazed
CLAUSES     2  (1) verb=thos (hear) tr/invol, past; frame ERG agent rnal 'byor ma rnams kyis, ABS object
               tshig 'di dag; hon=plain.  (2) verb=gyur (became) intr; subject same yoginis;
               complement ngo mtshar bar (amazed), adverbial mchog tu (supremely); past.

RELATIONS   de nas = narrative sequence (then) · la sogs pa'i = modification (headed by) · lha mo thams
            cad LA = see Q (topic/"among") · 'di lta ste = apposition (namely) · dang ×8 = list

            then were amazed) · cing = coordination, continues into U02
NEG/QUANT   NEG: none (tool counted 8: all are the feminine suffix -ma, false positives) · thams cad =
            all (the goddesses) · rnams = plural · mchog tu = supremely (degree, content)
SPINE       English: one sentence for U01 (heard … and were amazed), with the run-on carried into U02.
CONSTRUAL   Then all the goddesses headed by the yogini Nairatmya, namely Locana, Mamaki,

            the other yoginis, as many as the finest particles of Mount Meru, heard these words, and they

UNIT        U03 (verse; the Vajra Bearer's address, introduced by bstod pa in U02)
FORM        verse 7 × 4; not a citation in this text (it is the root tantra's own verse)
GENRE       address + announcement of teaching
REGISTER    the Vajra Bearer to the goddesses; warm, authoritative, second person (vocative + imperative)
SOURCE      1 sa dang chu dang rlung byin za'i / 2 lha mo snod can khyed nyid la / 3 gang zhig sus kyang
            mi shes pa / 4 de nyid nga yis spro yi nyon /
WORDS       byin za = fire (hutAzana, "eater of oblations") · snod can = having the vessel-quality, a fit
            recipient (cf. snod ldan) · khyed nyid = you (nyid emphatic/metrical) · sus kyang mi shes =
            no one knows · de nyid = reality, tattva (MITRA first sense) / correlative "that very" ·
            spro = set forth, unfold (tr. here: ERG nga yis); MITRA first sense "be willing" is the
            intransitive verb and does not fit the ERG frame · yi after a verb = clause connective
            ("I will unfold, and …") · nyon = listen! (imperative of nyan)
CLAUSES     3  (1) verb=mi shes tr, present; ERG agent sus (kyang) "anyone"; ABS object gang zhig (relative
               head, "that which").  (2) verb=spro tr, future/intent; ERG agent nga yis; ABS object de
               nyid; LA-DON recipient khyed nyid la (with lha mo snod can and pada 1 in apposition).
               (3) verb=nyon imperative; subject = the goddesses (addressees).
RELATIONS   'i (pada 1) = genitive: goddesses OF the four elements · dang ×2 = list · la = recipient (to
            you) · gang zhig … de nyid = relative–correlative (what … that very) · yi = connective
            between "I will unfold" and "listen" · nyon = command
NEG/QUANT   mi (pada 3) scopes over shes: "no one knows"; sus kyang = anyone at all → "no one at all"
VERSE-NOTES nyid in khyed nyid = metrical/emphatic, not rendered as "yourselves"; four elements in
            enumeration, keep in one run; final command closes the stanza
SPINE       one period: address (padas 1–2) → announcement (padas 3–4) → command
CONSTRUAL   Goddesses of earth, water, wind and fire, you who are fit vessels: to you I will set forth
            that very reality which no one at all knows. Listen!
TERMS       de nyid = reality · snod can = worthy vessels · lha mo = goddesses
DOUBTS      Q: snod can — "worthy vessels" (fit recipients of the teaching, as snod ldan); alt. "vessels"
               of the four elements (the goddesses as the container world, snod).
            Q: de nyid — "reality" (tattva) vs the plain correlative "that very thing" (gang zhig … de
               nyid); English "the reality that no one knows" carries both.
            Q: order — (revised at style pass) Tibetan order kept: "I will set forth …", then
               "Listen!" last; the connective yi becomes a full stop before the command.

FORM        verse 7 × 4 (pada 1 = speech introducer, folds into prose); root text, not a citation
GENRE       doctrinal statement (the Blessed One's answer)
REGISTER    the Blessed One to the goddesses; plain, declarative, aphoristic
SOURCE      1 bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal pa / 2 sems can rnams ni sangs rgyas nyid / 3 'on kyang glo

            de nyid = "that very" (the stains); MITRA first sense "reality" is wrong here · bsal =
            removed, cleared away
CLAUSES     4  (1) bka' stsal pa: ERG agent bcom ldan 'das kyis; speech follows.  (2) copula elided:
               sems can rnams (topic ni) = sangs rgyas nyid.  (3) verb=bsgribs tr past; ERG/instr agent

               object de nyid (the stains); main clause copula elided: (they are) sangs rgyas nyid.

NEG/QUANT   none · rnams = plural (beings)

CONSTRUAL   The Blessed One said: Beings are just buddhas. But they are obscured by adventitious

DOUBTS      Q: sangs rgyas nyid — emphatic "buddhas indeed / none other than buddhas" (nyid = eva) vs

CLAUSES     4  (1) gsol pa: ERG agent lha mo(s); speech follows.  (2) copula/assent: (it is) de de

RELATIONS   s = agent · vocative bcom ldan 'das · te = continuative/explanatory (it is so: …) · zhing =
            coordination · yang … yang = "neither … nor" (also/even on each member) · so = final
NEG/QUANT   NEG 2 (tool count 2, both rows present): ma mchis scopes over the existence of truth; ma
            mchis scopes over the existence of falsehood. Existential, not copular: "there is no

SPINE       frame + one English sentence (assent, colon, the two negated existentials)
CONSTRUAL   The goddess said: Blessed One, it is just so: there is no truth, and there is no falsehood

TERMS       bden pa = truth · brdzun = falsehood · lha mo = the goddess (singular)
DOUBTS      Q: lha mos — singular "the goddess" (Nairatmya as spokeswoman, implied) vs collective "the

            Q: de de bzhin te — assent ("it is just so") followed by its explanation; alt.: "that being

               difficulty, which the Blessed One answers in U07); no condition particle (na), so the
               assent reading is kept.

UNIT        U07 (verse: speech frame + 4 content padas — the five-pada stanza)
FORM        verse 7 × 5 (pada 1 = speech introducer, folds into prose); root text, not a citation
GENRE       doctrinal answer by simile (vehicle of the simile completed by de bzhin in U08)
REGISTER    the Blessed One to the goddess; plain, explanatory
SOURCE      1 bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal pa / 2 gang gis mi shes 'jig rten pa / 3 btsan dug zos pas
            brgyal bar 'gyur / 4 rmongs spangs de nyid yid kyis ni / 5 de yis de nyid yongs su gcod /
WORDS       gang gis = relative ERG "who" · mi shes = does not know (object elided) · 'jig rten pa =
            worldly people · btsan dug = deadly poison · zos pas = because (they) have eaten · brgyal
            bar 'gyur = faint (same verb as U02 brgyal ba) · rmongs = confusion (kept distinct from gti
            mug, delusion) · spangs = abandoned, free of · de nyid (pada 4) = "that very [thing]" =
            the poison (anaphoric; see Q) · yid = mind · de yis = "by that", resuming yid kyis ni ·
            de nyid (pada 5) = that very [poison] · yongs su gcod = cut off entirely (gcod = cut, sever,
            destroy; yongs su = pari-, not an English adverb on every verb); MITRA compound
            "determine, discern" = pariccheda (see Q).
            Tool slips: "shes" tagged QUOT (it is the verb, negated by mi); spurious COMPOUND "gang gis
            mi = human being", "nyid yid = afflicted mentality" ignored.
CLAUSES     4  (1) bka' stsal pa: ERG agent bcom ldan 'das; speech follows.  (2) relative: verb=mi shes
               tr present; ERG agent gang (gis) = 'jig rten pa (head); object elided.  (3) verb=zos (eat)
               tr past, cause clause (pas); main verb='gyur intr: 'jig rten pa brgyal bar 'gyur (they
               faint).  (4) verb=gcod tr present; instrument yid kyis (topicalized by ni, resumed by de
               yis); modifiers rmongs spangs (free of confusion), de nyid (regarding that very poison);
               agent elided (generic "one"); ABS object de nyid (the poison).
RELATIONS   gang gis … 'jig rten pa = relative clause (worldly people who do not know) · pas = cause
            (from eating poison → faint) · ni (pada 4) = contrastive topic (but, with a mind …) · kyis
            … de yis = instrument resumed (with that mind, by it) · simile vehicle: completed by
            de bzhin in U08 (likewise)
NEG/QUANT   NEG 1 (tool count 1; row present): mi scopes over shes — "do not know" · yongs su =
            entirely (one adverb, once)
VERSE-NOTES ni = topic/contrast, not "indeed"; de nyid ×2 = anaphora to the poison, keep "that very
            poison" once; the stanza's close (pada 5) lands the point
SPINE       frame + two sentences: S1 padas 2–3 (relative + cause + main verb); S2 padas 4–5
            (instrument topic + main verb)
CONSTRUAL   The Blessed One said: Worldly people who do not know faint because they have eaten a
            deadly poison. But with a mind free of confusion about that very poison, by that mind one
            cuts that very poison off entirely.
TERMS       'jig rten pa = worldly people · btsan dug = deadly poison · brgyal ba = faint · rmongs =
            confusion · yid = mind
DOUBTS      Q: de nyid (padas 4, 5) — read as "that very [poison]" (anaphoric), so the knower destroys
               the poison; alt. de nyid = reality (tattva): "with a mind of reality free of confusion,
               one fully ascertains reality" (yongs su gcod = pariccheda, "determine"). The simile

            Q: gang gis … de yis — read as instrument resumption (yid kyis ni … de yis); alt. relative–
               correlative (the same worldly person who fainted, once free of confusion, cuts it off).
