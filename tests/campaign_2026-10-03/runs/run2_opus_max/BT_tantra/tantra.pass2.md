# tantra — Pass 2 faithful draft (before beats.py and style pass)

### U01
Then all the goddesses headed by the yogini Nairatmya — Locana, Mamaki, Pandaravasini, Tara, Bhrikuti, Cunda, Parnashabari and the Downward-Facing One — these and the other yoginis, as many as the finest particles of Mount Meru, heard these words and were utterly amazed.

### U02
They fainted, were terrified, fell to the ground and trembled. Then the Vajra Bearer looked upon all these goddesses and, to raise them up, praised them:

### U03
Goddesses of earth and water,
of wind and fire, you worthy vessels,
listen, and I will unfold to you that reality,
which no one knows at all.

### U04
When they heard the Blessed One's words as if in a dream, all of them came back to life.

### U05
The Blessed One said:
Beings are none other than buddhas,
but adventitious stains obscure them.
Once the stains are cleared away, they are buddhas.

### U06
The goddess said: Blessed One, it is just so; there is neither truth nor falsehood.

### U07
The Blessed One said:
Worldly people who do not know
faint from eating deadly poison.
But with a mind free of confusion about it,
one destroys that very poison entirely.

### U08
Likewise, if one knows the means of pacification
and finds rest in Hevajra,
one does not cling to ignorance and the rest
and is not bound by delusion and the rest.

### U09
Through one's own realization, by oneself,
there is no being, not a single one,
who is not a buddha.
Hell beings, hungry ghosts and animals,
gods, asuras and humans,

### U10
the worms in excrement and the like:
though they do not know the bliss
even of gods and asuras,
they are always blissful by nature.

### U11
Nowhere at all in the world realms
will a buddha be found elsewhere.
The mind is none other than the perfect buddha;
no buddha elsewhere can be shown.

### U12
Outcastes, bamboo-workers and the like,
those who think of killing as their aim:
if they know Hevajra, they will attain accomplishment;
of this there is no doubt.
