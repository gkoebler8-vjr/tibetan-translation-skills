## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U07 "ascertains that very reality" for destroying the very poison: the poison simile that U08 "likewise" depends on is lost. Cause: construal fork (wrong branch chosen, then confirmed by the MITRA cross-check).
2. U12 "if one knows them as Hevajra, one will gain accomplishment": the outcastes become objects, not the ones who succeed. Cause: construal fork (a revision after MITRA moved it the wrong way).
3. U03 "you worthy vessels": the goddesses as vessels of the elements become "worthy vessels". Cause: dictionary sense (snod can taken as snod ldan).
4. U10 causal pas rendered "though". Cause: skill rule misapplied (relation changed to make the sense cohere).
5. U09 "realizing oneself by oneself": an object is invented for rtogs pa. Cause: grammar misread (rang dang rang gis = each by himself).
