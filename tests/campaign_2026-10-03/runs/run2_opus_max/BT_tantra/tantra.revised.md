# tantra — after the fidelity check revision (one round) and the MITRA cross-check

### U01
HEADER: U01 · prose, one narrative period running on into U02 · narrative, plain and dignified · seasoned practitioner · root text, Hevajra Tantra (Toh 417/418) part 2 ch. 4; not a citation
TEXT:
Then all the goddesses headed by the yogini Nairatmya — Locana, Mamaki, Pandaravasini, Tara, Bhrikuti, Cunda, Parnashabari and the Downward-Facing One — these and other yoginis, as many as the finest particles of Mount Meru, on hearing these words, were utterly amazed.
NOTES:
Issue: the Tibetan period runs on into U02 (cing); split at the seam, subject restated there.
Q: lha mo thams cad la — la read as marking the topic and merged with the subject (the goddesses are the yoginis who heard); alt.: the yoginis heard words addressed to all the goddesses.
Q: 'og zhal ma has no dictionary entry; the name is translated ("the Downward-Facing One") rather than given an unattested Sanskrit form (MITRA gives Ahomukha; Adhomukha is also possible).
Alt: gos dkar mo, "the White-Clad One", given in its established Sanskrit form Pandaravasini (MITRA: Pandara).
Check: in-context; 1 minor (nas rendered "and"), changed to "on hearing these words".

### U02
HEADER: U02 · prose, completion of the U01 period + speech introducer · narrative, plain and dignified · seasoned practitioner · root text, Hevajra Tantra (Toh 417/418) part 2 ch. 4; not a citation
TEXT:
They fainted, were terrified, fell to the ground and trembled. Then, having looked upon all these goddesses, the Vajra Bearer praised them in order to raise them up:
NOTES:
Q: rdo rje can (vajrin) rendered "the Vajra Bearer", kept distinct from rdo rje 'chang (Vajradhara); MITRA reads Vajrapani (phyag na rdo rje), which the Tibetan does not say.
Q: 'gyur ro (present/future stem) read as narrative past; MITRA reads the future ("they will faint").
Alt: bslang ba'i phyir, "to raise them up" (they had fallen to the ground); also "to rouse them" from the faint.
Q: yang read as narrative "then"; alt. "again".
Issue: yang dag par bstod pa (a calque of the Sanskrit preverb sam-) rendered plainly, "praised them".
Check: in-context; 1 minor (nas rendered "and"), changed to "having looked upon … praised them in order to raise them up".

### U03
HEADER: U03 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 1-2-4-3 (padas 1–2 interleaved) · address and announcement, warm and authoritative · seasoned practitioner · root text, Hevajra Tantra (Toh 417/418) part 2 ch. 4; not a citation
TEXT:
Goddesses of earth and water,
of wind and fire, you worthy vessels,
I will set forth for you the very reality,
which no one knows at all. Listen!
NOTES:
Q: snod can — "worthy vessels" (fit recipients of the teaching, as snod ldan); alt.: vessels of the four elements (the goddesses as the container world).
Q: de nyid — "the very reality" (tattva); alt. the plain correlative of gang zhig, "that very thing which no one knows" (so MITRA).
Q: lha mo … khyed — plural, the goddesses of U02; MITRA reads one goddess "who possesses the vessels of earth, water and wind" (and drops fire).
Q: spro — "set forth" (speech: the command to listen follows); MITRA "emanate".
Alt: byin za, "eater of oblations", is simply "fire".
Check: in-context; 2 minor (connective yi before the command became a full stop; defining relative set off by a comma), both kept: English has no connective between a statement and a command, and the head is already definite.

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose, speech frame + reply · dialogue, deferential and plain · seasoned practitioner · root text, Hevajra Tantra (Toh 417/418) part 2 ch. 4; not a citation
TEXT:
The goddess said: Blessed One, it is just so; there is neither truth nor falsehood.
NOTES:
Q: bden yang ma mchis … — existential, "there is neither truth nor falsehood" (ma mchis = med); MITRA reads it as copular, "it is neither true nor false".
Q: lha mos — singular "the goddess" (Nairatmya as spokeswoman, implied); alt. the goddesses collectively.
Q: de de bzhin te — assent, "it is just so", explained by what follows; alt. "that being so, there is neither truth nor falsehood" (the goddess drawing a consequence or raising a difficulty, answered in U07).
Check: in-context; no findings.

### U07
HEADER: U07 · verse: citation mode, rising, 4–5 beats; padas 7 × 5 (pada 1 = speech frame, set as prose), four verse lines, order 1-2-3-4-5 · doctrinal answer (image of poison), plain · seasoned practitioner · root text, Hevajra Tantra (Toh 417/418) part 2 ch. 4; not a citation
TEXT:
The Blessed One said:
Worldly people who do not know
faint from eating deadly poison.
But with a mind free of confusion about reality,
one fully ascertains that very reality.
NOTES:
Q: de nyid (lines 3 and 4) read as reality: "free of confusion about reality … ascertains that very reality" (yongs su gcod = determine, ascertain); revised after the MITRA cross-check, which reads the same: de nyid yid is a modifier and its noun (a mind undeluded about reality), and yongs su gcod with reality as its object means "ascertain". Alt.: de nyid = "that very poison" and gcod = "destroy": "with a mind free of confusion about it, one destroys that very poison entirely", making these lines the vehicle of the simile closed by "likewise" in U08.
Q: gang gis … de yis — read as the instrument resumed ("with a mind …", "by that"); alt. a relative–correlative pair: the same worldly person, once free of confusion, ascertains it.
Q: mi shes — the object of "know" is not stated; on this reading it is reality (lines 3–4).
Issue: "But" makes explicit the contrast carried by the topic marker ni.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (a recorded doubt had dropped from the notes), restored. Revised after the MITRA cross-check (reading of de nyid and yongs su gcod); the changed lines re-checked in-context: no findings.

### U10
HEADER: U10 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3 beats; padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 1-3-2-4 · doctrinal statement, plain · seasoned practitioner · root text, Hevajra Tantra (Toh 417/418) part 2 ch. 4; not a citation
TEXT:
the worms in excrement and the like:
though they do not know the bliss
even of gods and asuras,
they are always blissful by nature.
NOTES:
Q: gang phyir … pas is causal in form ("because they do not know …"); a causal link to "always blissful by nature" does not hold, so it is rendered as concessive "though" (verb + instrumental: though, while); alt. causal.
Q: lha dang lha ma yin gyi yang — "the bliss even of gods and asuras" (genitive on bliss); alt. a genitive of agent: "this bliss, which even gods and asuras do not know".
Q: bde ba gang — "the bliss"; alt. "whatever bliss there is".
Q: MITRA keeps "because" and turns bde ba can into "a nature that is suffering" (the Tibetan says blissful), and reads line 2 as "up to the gods and asuras", the top of the list; kept.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (a recorded doubt had dropped from the notes), restored.

### U12
HEADER: U12 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats (closing formula 2); padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 1-2-3-4 · doctrinal promise, plain, emphatic close · seasoned practitioner · root text, Hevajra Tantra (Toh 417/418) part 2 ch. 4; not a citation
TEXT:
Outcastes, bamboo-workers and the like,
those who think of killing as their aim:
if one knows them as Hevajra, one will gain accomplishment;
this is beyond doubt.
NOTES:
Q: gsod don don du sems pa po — "those who think of killing as their aim"; alt. "who think of killing for gain" (don = profit).
Q: line 2 read as apposition to the outcastes and bamboo-workers (no dang); alt. a further class, "and those intent on killing".
Q: de rnams kye rdo rjer shes na — revised after the MITRA cross-check: the unmarked de rnams is the object and -r means "as": "if one knows them as Hevajra"; alt. de rnams as the agent with the ergative dropped for the metre: "if they know Hevajra, they will gain accomplishment".
Q: 'grub 'gyur has no stated subject: "one will gain accomplishment" (the one who knows them so); alt. "accomplishment will come", or "they will be accomplished".
Check: in-context; 1 minor (term: glossary had "attain accomplishment"), glossary aligned to "gain accomplishment". Revised after the MITRA cross-check (role of de rnams); the changed line re-checked in-context: no findings.
