U01: Then all the goddesses headed by the yogini Nairatmya — Locana, Mamaki, Pandaravasini, Tara, Bhrikuti, Cunda, Parnashabari and the Downward-Facing One — these and other yoginis, as many as the finest particles of Mount Meru, on hearing these words, were utterly amazed.
U02: They fainted, were terrified, fell to the ground and trembled. Then, having looked upon all these goddesses, the Vajra Bearer praised them in order to raise them up:
U03: Goddesses of earth and water,
U03: of wind and fire, you worthy vessels,
U03: I will set forth for you the very reality,
U03: which no one knows at all. Listen!
U04: When they heard the Blessed One's words as if in a dream, all of them came back to life.
U05: The Blessed One said:
U05: Beings are none other than buddhas,
U05: but adventitious stains obscure them.
U05: Once the stains are cleared, they are buddhas.
U06: The goddess said: Blessed One, it is just so; there is neither truth nor falsehood.
U07: The Blessed One said:
U07: Worldly people who do not know
U07: faint from eating deadly poison.
U07: But with a mind free of confusion about reality,
U07: one fully ascertains that very reality.
U08: Likewise, if one knows the means of pacification
U08: and finds rest in Hevajra,
U08: one does not cling to ignorance and the rest
U08: and is not bound by delusion and the rest.
U09: Through realizing oneself by oneself,
U09: there is no being who is not a buddha,
U09: not even a single one.
U09: Hell beings, pretas and animals,
U09: gods, asuras and humans,
U10: the worms in excrement and the like:
U10: though they do not know the bliss
U10: even of gods and asuras,
U10: they are always blissful by nature.
U11: Nowhere at all in the world realms
U11: will any buddha be found elsewhere.
U11: The mind is none other than the perfect buddha;
U11: no buddha elsewhere can be shown.
U12: Outcastes, bamboo-workers and the like,
U12: those who think of killing as their aim:
U12: if one knows them as Hevajra, one will gain accomplishment;
U12: this is beyond doubt.
