### U01
The leaders of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas were four bodhisattva great beings: the bodhisattva great being called Viśiṣṭacāritra, the bodhisattva great being called Anantacāritra, the bodhisattva great being called Viśuddhacāritra, and the bodhisattva great being called Supratiṣṭhitacāritra. These four bodhisattva great beings were the leaders of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas.

### U02
Then those four bodhisattva great beings, standing in front of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas, joined their palms before the Blessed One and said to him, “Blessed One, are your troubles slight? Are your ailments few? Are you living at ease? Blessed One, are the beings in your care well-disposed? Do they understand well? Are they easy to train? Are they easy to purify? Do they cause you no weariness?”

### U03
Then those four bodhisattva great beings addressed these verses to the Blessed One:
Protector of the World, Maker of Light,
are you living at ease?
Are you free from harm?
Faultless One, are you well in body?

### U04
Are the beings in your care well-disposed?
Are they easy to train and easy to purify?
Protector of the World, when you teach them,
do they cause you no weariness?

### U05
Then the Blessed One said to the four bodhisattvas, the leaders of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas, “Noble sons, it is so: I am living at ease. My troubles are slight. My ailments are few. These beings in my care are well-disposed. They understand easily. They are easy to train. They are easy to purify. When I purify them, they cause me no weariness. Why is that? Noble sons, it is because I myself purified these beings in the time of the perfectly awakened buddhas of the past. Noble sons, even just by seeing me and hearing me, they gain faith and enter the wisdom of the buddhas—except those who have made themselves thoroughly familiar with the level of the hearers. Now I have led even them into the wisdom of the buddhas, and I have brought them to hear the ultimate meaning.”

### U06
Then, at that time, those bodhisattva great beings spoke these verses:
Great Hero, excellent, excellent!
That the beings in your care are well-disposed,
easy to train and easy to purify,

### U07
and, Guide, that those who listen
to this profound wisdom of yours
gain faith once they have heard it
and come to enter the Guide’s wisdom—
we rejoice in this.

### U08
When they had said this, the Blessed One gave his approval to those four bodhisattva great beings, the leaders of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas: “Noble sons, excellent, excellent! Noble sons, your praise of the Tathagata, too, is excellent, excellent!”

### U09
At that time the bodhisattva great being Maitreya and other bodhisattvas too, as many hundreds of thousands of millions of billions as there are grains of sand in eight Ganges rivers, had this thought: “This great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas have emerged from within the earth. Standing in the presence of the Blessed One, they honor the Blessed One, revere him, and please him. Since we have never seen them or heard of them before, where have these bodhisattva great beings come from?”

### U10
Then the bodhisattva great being Maitreya knew that he himself had fallen into doubt and uncertainty. With his mind he also understood the thoughts in the minds of those bodhisattva great beings, as many hundreds of thousands of millions of billions as there are grains of sand in eight Ganges rivers. Having understood them, he joined his palms at that time and asked the Blessed One about this matter in verse:

### U11
Bodhisattvas who have never appeared before,
many hundreds of thousands of them,
and tens of millions without end—
Supreme among Humans, please explain!
