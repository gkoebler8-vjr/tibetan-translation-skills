### U01
HEADER: U01 · prose (list period; statement, four names, restatement) · narrative, plain sutra narration · new practitioner · Toh 113 (Saddharmapuṇḍarīka), ch. 14, the sutra's own text; not a citation
TEXT:
The leaders of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas were four bodhisattva great beings: the bodhisattva great being called Viśiṣṭacāritra, the bodhisattva great being called Anantacāritra, the bodhisattva great being called Viśuddhacāritra, and the bodhisattva great being called Supratiṣṭhitacāritra. These four bodhisattva great beings were the leaders of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas.
NOTES:
Q: gang dag byung ba read as existential ("the leaders … were four bodhisattva great beings"); alt. "the four bodhisattva great beings who had appeared" (MITRA reads it so).
Q: names given in Sanskrit with IAST (english.md: Indian names in Sanskrit; the brief's no-diacritics rule covers common words); alt. their English meanings in the body (Distinguished Conduct, Boundless Conduct, Pure Conduct, Steadfast Conduct) or Sanskrit without diacritics — editor's call; meanings kept in the glossary.
Q: "great gathering and great multitude": dang joins two names for one group; alt. apposition ("that great gathering, that great multitude of bodhisattvas").
Issue: the ring repetition (statement, four names, restatement) is reproduced; the house style does not authorize compressing stock repetition.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the construal's paraphrase still gave the English names; synced); body unchanged.

### U02
HEADER: U02 · prose (narrative frame + direct speech, eight courtesy questions) · narrative and dialogue, courteous · new practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Then those four bodhisattva great beings, at the head of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas, joined their palms before the Blessed One and said to him, “Blessed One, are your troubles slight? Are your ailments few? Are you living at ease? Blessed One, are the beings in your care well-disposed? Do they understand well? Are they easy to train? Are they easy to purify? Do they cause you no weariness?”
NOTES:
Q: "the beings in your care" unpacks khyod kyi sems can ("your beings"); alt. the literal "your beings".
Q: the third-person reference to the addressee (bcom ldan 'das la, first and last questions) is rendered "you" after the vocative; alt. keep "the Blessed One" ("Do they cause the Blessed One no weariness?").
Q: tshul mdzes "well-disposed"; alt. "of good character" or "well-behaved" (MITRA).
Q: mdun du 'dug leaves the posture unstated: "at the head of"; alt. "standing in front of" or "seated before" (MITRA "having sat").
Q: legs par go ba "understand well"; MITRA "easy to understand" — kept (legs par is adverbial "well").
Issue: the greeting formula gnod pa chung, snyun nyung rendered "troubles slight, ailments few" (MITRA intensifies to "free from"; chung/nyung mean slight/few).
Check: in-context; 1 minor (posture "standing" replaced by posture-neutral "at the head of").

### U03
HEADER: U03 · prose frame + verse: free verse (house style), unmetred; padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 1-2-3-4 · verse greeting with vocative epithets · new practitioner · not a citation (the sutra's own verse)
TEXT:
Then those four bodhisattva great beings addressed these verses to the Blessed One:
Protector of the World, Maker of Light,
are you living at ease?
Are you free from harm?
Faultless One, are you well in body?
NOTES:
Q: sdig pa mi mnga' read as a vocative epithet ("Faultless One", cf. Sanskrit anagha); alt. a predicate question ("You are without evil? Are you well in body?").
Alt: line 3 "Are you wholly free from harm?" if rnam par rab is read as intensifiers (MITRA "completely liberated"); taken as preverb mirrors, plain verb (english.md failure 16).
Q: the verse's "free from harm" (gnod las … grol) is stronger than the prose greeting's "troubles slight" (U02); kept distinct, as the Tibetan has them.
Issue: 'od mdzad pa rendered "Maker of Light", not "Bringer of Light" (an unwanted Lucifer echo in English).
Check: in-context; 1 minor (intensifier) resolved as a construal point; body unchanged.

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose (speech frame + the Buddha's reply: statements, a why-question, reason, consequence with exception, close) · dialogue, plain and warm · new practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Then the Blessed One said to the four bodhisattvas, the leaders of that great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas, “Noble sons, it is so: I am living at ease. My troubles are slight. My ailments are few. These beings in my care are well-disposed. They understand easily. They are easy to train. They are easy to purify. When I purify them, they cause me no weariness. Why is that? Noble sons, it is because I myself purified these beings in the time of the perfectly awakened buddhas of the past. So, noble sons, even just by seeing me and hearing me, they gain faith and enter the wisdom of the buddhas—except those who have made themselves thoroughly familiar with the level of the hearers. Now I have led even them into the wisdom of the buddhas, and I have brought them to hear the ultimate meaning.”
NOTES:
Q: the text as given reads nyan thos kyis la, which does not construe; read nyan thos kyi sa la (one tsheg lost), "the level of the hearers"; alt. "the way of the hearers" if a different word dropped out (MITRA also reads "the stage of the śrāvakas").
Q: 'bab cing 'dug read as continuous aspect ("they … enter"); alt. lexical "enter the wisdom of the buddhas and remain in it".
Q: yongs su sbyang ba byas "purified" (keeps the thread of "easy to purify", "when I purify them"); alt. "trained".
Q: sangs rgyas is unmarked for number: "the perfectly awakened buddhas of the past" and "the wisdom of the buddhas"; alt. "a perfectly awakened buddha of the past", "the Buddha's wisdom" (MITRA).
Q: thos pa read as "hearing me" (nga governs both verbs); alt. "hearing the teaching".
Q: mos pa "faith"; alt. "conviction" or "devotion" (MITRA "become devoted").
Q: 'dris par byas pa read as causative ("made themselves thoroughly familiar"); alt. "have become thoroughly habituated" (MITRA).
Q: MITRA makes the addressees "the great assembly and the four leaders" (dang outside the genitive); kept one phrase under de'i, as in U01 and U08.
Q: MITRA reads "no weariness arises in me"; kept transitive skyed with the beings as agent ("they cause me no weariness"), mirroring the question in U02.
Issue: "So," makes explicit the te that links the reason to its consequence.
Check: in-context; 2 minor: the te seam had become a bare full stop (restored as "So,"); yongs su as a plain verb confirmed and the construal updated.

### U09
HEADER: U09 · prose · narrative with reported thought · new practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
At that time the bodhisattva great being Maitreya and other bodhisattvas too, as many hundreds of thousands of millions of billions as there are grains of sand in eight Ganges rivers, had this thought: “This great gathering and great multitude of bodhisattvas have emerged from within the earth and, having taken their place before the Blessed One, are honoring him, revering him, and pleasing him. NOTES:

Q: the number string is rendered by magnitude ("hundreds of thousands of millions of billions"), not unit by unit (koti, nayuta, hundred thousand).
Q: spyan sngar 'dug "having taken their place before"; alt. "standing before" or "having sat before" (MITRA).
Q: dgyes par byed "pleasing"; alt. "gladdening" or "bringing joy" (MITRA).
Issue: the double thought frame ('di snyam du sems par gyur te … snyam sems so) is rendered once.
Check: in-context; 2 minor: the te between "emerged" and "taken their place" had become a full stop (now "and"); "standing" replaced by posture-neutral "having taken their place".
