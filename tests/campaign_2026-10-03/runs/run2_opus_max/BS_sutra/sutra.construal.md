# sutra.construal.md — Lotus Sutra (Toh 113), ch. 14, page B-S, units U01–U11

Brief (Pass 0, given): audience new practitioner · purpose publication (84000 Reading Room-style reader edition) ·
common Buddhist words anglicized without diacritics; technical terms in English where a good term exists; no
brackets/Wylie/locators in body · verse free, line for line, one English line per pada, unmetred · no contractions ·
speaker formulas plain · doubts as Q: for the editor · glossary started (sutra.glossary.tsv).
Source context: Saddharmapundarika, ch. 14, the bodhisattvas who emerged from the earth; the four leaders greet the
Buddha; the Buddha replies; Maitreya and the assembly wonder where these bodhisattvas came from.
Page-wide observation: all verse padas on this page are 7 syllables (brief said 9 or 11; counted 7 throughout).

UNIT        U01
FORM        prose (narrative list period; ring structure: statement – four names – restatement)
GENRE       narrative (sutra narration)
REGISTER    narrator to reader; plain, formulaic sutra narration; no elevation
SOURCE      1 byang chub sems dpa'i tshogs chen po dang byang chub sems dpa'i phung po chen po de'i gtso bo /
            2 byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po bzhi gang dag byung ba 'di lta ste /
            3 byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po spyod pa khyad par can zhes bya ba dang /
            4 … spyod pa mtha' yas zhes bya ba dang / 5 … spyod pa rnam par dag ces bya ba dang /
            6 … spyod pa brtan pa zhes bya ba ste /
            7 byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po bzhi po de dag ni byang chub sems dpa'i tshogs chen po dang
              byang chub sems dpa'i phung po chen po de'i gtso bor gyur to //
WORDS       tshogs chen po = great gathering (gana); phung po chen po = great mass/multitude (rasi), NOT "aggregate";
            gtso bo = chief, leader (pramukha); byung ba = came to be / there were (existential, renders "were"),
            alt. "appeared"; 'di lta ste = namely, as follows; zhes bya ba = called;
            names (84000/MITRA): spyod pa khyad par can = Visistacaritra; spyod pa mtha' yas = Anantacaritra;
            spyod pa rnam par dag = Visuddhacaritra; spyod pa brtan pa = Supratisthitacaritra
CLAUSES     2 finite + 1 relative
            c1 (rel.) verb=byung (pa. of 'byung) intr/invol; ABS subj = byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po bzhi;
               gang dag … byung ba = "the four … who were (there)"; whole NP [de'i gtso bo = bzhi gang dag byung ba]
               is topic of 'di lta ste (namely) → list
            c2 verb=gyur (pa. of 'gyur) intr/invol; ABS subj = bzhi po de dag (ni = topic);
               LA-DON gtso bo-r = complement "as leaders"; past, narrative; hon=plain
RELATIONS   1 'i (byang chub sems dpa'i ×2) = modification (gathering OF bodhisattvas)
            2 dang (tshogs chen po dang phung po chen po) = coordination of two designations of ONE group
            3 de'i = genitive on the whole coordinated NP ("of that …"; de = anaphoric, the host already introduced)
            4 'di lta ste = namely (introduces the list)
            5 zhes bya ba = naming; dang ×3 = coordination of the four names; ste = closes the list, leads to restatement
            6 ni = topic; -r on gtso bo = complement of gyur
NEG/QUANT   NEG 0. Quantifier: bzhi (four), bzhi po de dag (these four) — number must surface both times.
SPINE       Two English sentences: (1) The leaders of that great gathering … were four bodhisattva great beings:
            [four names]. (2) These four bodhisattva great beings were the leaders of that great gathering ….
            Ring repetition kept (house style does not authorize compression of repetition).
CONSTRUAL   The leaders of that great gathering of bodhisattvas and great multitude of bodhisattvas were four
            bodhisattva great beings, namely: the bodhisattva great being called Viśiṣṭacāritra (Distinguished
            Conduct), the bodhisattva great being called Anantacāritra (Boundless Conduct), the bodhisattva great
            being called Viśuddhacāritra (Pure Conduct), and the bodhisattva great being called
            Supratiṣṭhitacāritra (Steadfast Conduct). These four bodhisattva great beings were the
            leaders of that great gathering of bodhisattvas and great multitude of bodhisattvas.
TERMS       byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po = bodhisattva great being · tshogs chen po = great gathering ·
            phung po chen po = great multitude · gtso bo = leader · names: Sanskrit, IAST (english.md §7 "Indian
            names in Sanskrit"; attested forms, not back-formed): Viśiṣṭacāritra, Anantacāritra, Viśuddhacāritra,
            Supratiṣṭhitacāritra; English meanings kept for the back glossary
DOUBTS      Q: gang dag byung ba read as existential ("who were [the leaders]", Skt. abhuvan); alt. "who had
               appeared/come forth" (the four who emerged) — no change to the list, only to the opening sentence.
            Q: names given in Sanskrit with IAST (english.md §7; the brief's no-diacritics rule covers common words);
               alt. English meanings in the body (Distinguished / Boundless / Pure / Steadfast Conduct) or Sanskrit
               without diacritics — editor's call for the reader edition.
            Q: "dang" between tshogs chen po and phung po chen po: two names for one group (kept as "and");
               alt. apposition ("that great gathering, that great multitude").

UNIT        U02
FORM        prose (narrative frame + direct speech: eight courtesy questions)
GENRE       narrative + dialogue (greeting formula)
REGISTER    frame: narrator, plain; speech: the four leaders to the Buddha, humble-honorific (gsol, lags, bgyid,
            snyun), courteous, not elevated
SOURCE      1 de nas byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po bzhi po de dag byang chub sems dpa'i tshogs chen po dang
              byang chub sems dpa'i phung po chen po de'i mdun du 'dug ste /
            2 bcom ldan 'das kyi spyan sngar thal mo sbyar nas bcom ldan 'das la 'di skad ces gsol to //
            3 bcom ldan 'das la gnod pa chung lags sam / 4 snyun nyung lags sam / 5 bde bar reg pa la gnas lags sam /
            6 bcom ldan 'das khyod kyi sems can rnams tshul mdzes lags sam / 7 legs par go ba lags sam /
            8 gdul sla ba lags sam / 9 rnam par sbyang sla ba lags sam /
            10 bcom ldan 'das la skyo ba skyed par mi bgyid lags sam /
WORDS       mdun du 'dug = be/stand in front of (sthita); spyan sngar = in the presence of (hon.); thal mo sbyar = join
            palms; gsol = say/request (humble, to a superior); gnod pa = harm, trouble (abadha), chung = slight;
            snyun = illness (hon.), nyung = few/little; bde bar reg pa la gnas = live at ease, in comfort
            (sukhasparsavihara); tshul mdzes = of good disposition/manner (svakara); legs par go ba = understand well;
            gdul sla = easy to train ('dul = tame/train, not vinaya); rnam par sbyang sla = easy to purify;
            skyo ba = weariness, being wearied; skyed par bgyid = cause (hon. bgyid); lags sam = polite question
CLAUSES     3 narrative + 8 questions
            c1 'dug intr; ABS subj = bzhi po de dag; LA-DON mdun du = location (in front of that gathering); past;
               'dug = be positioned/stay, posture unspecified (check: not "standing") → "at the head of"
            c2 sbyar (pa. of sbyor) tr; agent = same four (elided ERG); ABS thal mo; spyan sngar = before the Buddha
            c3 gsol (tr, humble); agent = the four (elided); LA-DON bcom ldan 'das la = addressee; 'di skad ces = this
            q1 gnod pa chung (lags) — possessor/locus bcom ldan 'das la (third-person reference to the addressee)
            q2 snyun nyung (lags) — same possessor elided
            q3 bde bar reg pa la gnas (lags) — subject the Buddha (elided)
            q4 tshul mdzes (lags) — subj khyod kyi sems can rnams; vocative bcom ldan 'das
            q5 legs par go ba; q6 gdul sla ba; q7 rnam par sbyang sla ba — subj the same beings (elided)
            q8 skyo ba skyed par mi bgyid (lags) — agent the beings (elided); LA-DON bcom ldan 'das la = the one
               wearied (recipient); NEG mi on bgyid
RELATIONS   de nas = then (sequence after U01) · ste = sequence/accompaniment (standing … and) · nas = sequence
            (having joined palms, then said) · ces = quotation marker · sam ×8 = polite yes/no question
            khyod kyi = possessor of the beings ("your beings" = the beings you are teaching)
NEG/QUANT   NEG 1: mi bgyid in q8 — scope: "cause weariness"; question asks whether they do NOT weary him (courteous
            expectation: they do not). Quantifier: bzhi po de dag (those four); rnams plural.
SPINE       Frame: 1–2 sentences ("Then those four … stood in front of …; joining their palms … they said to the
            Blessed One:"). Speech: eight separate questions, three about the Buddha, five about the beings.
CONSTRUAL   Then those four bodhisattva great beings, standing in front of that great gathering and great multitude of
            bodhisattvas, joined their palms in the presence of the Blessed One and said this to him: "Blessed One,
            is your harm slight? Is your illness slight? Are you living at ease? Blessed One, are your beings of good
            disposition? Do they understand well? Are they easy to train? Are they easy to purify? Do they not cause
            the Blessed One weariness (I trust they do not)?"
TERMS       bcom ldan 'das = the Blessed One · sems can = beings · gdul sla = easy to train · rnam par sbyang sla = easy
            to purify · bde bar reg pa la gnas = living at ease · skyo ba = weariness
DOUBTS      Q: khyod kyi sems can "your beings": rendered "the beings in your care" (unpack of the genitive); alt.
               literal "your beings".
            Q: third-person "bcom ldan 'das la" (q1, q8) referring to the addressee rendered with "you" after the
               vocative; alt. keep "the Blessed One" in the third person.
            Q: tshul mdzes "well-disposed" (good disposition, receptive); alt. "of good character / well-behaved".

UNIT        U03
FORM        mixed: prose speech frame (1 sentence) + verse, 4 padas x 7 syllables (one stanza; not a citation —
            the sutra's own verse, spoken by the four leaders)
GENRE       narrative frame + verse greeting (gatha)
REGISTER    frame plain; verse: humble address to the Buddha, vocative epithets, courteous questions
SOURCE      F  de nas byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po bzhi po de dag gis / bcom ldan 'das la tshigs su bcad pa
               'di dag gsol to //
            1 'jig rten mgon po 'od mdzad pa / 2 bde bar reg la gnas lags sam /
            3 gnod las rnam par rab grol lam / 4 sdig pa mi mnga' sku bde 'am //
WORDS       tshigs su bcad pa = verses (gatha); 'jig rten mgon po = protector of the world (lokanatha);
            'od mdzad pa = one who makes light (prabhamkara); bde bar reg la gnas = live at ease (reg [pa] la, pa
            dropped metri causa); gnod las … grol = free from harm; rnam par rab = utterly, wholly (intensifiers);
            sdig pa mi mnga' = who has no evil/wrongdoing (hon. mnga'), epithet, cf. Skt. anagha;
            sku bde = (your) body is well (hon. sku). Tool slips: botok fused "la tshigs" (= la | tshigs su bcad pa);
            "lam" tagged as noun = grol + 'am (question particle after -l); 'am = question particle.
CLAUSES     F  gsol tr, humble; ERG agent = bzhi po de dag gis; ABS = tshigs su bcad pa 'di dag; LA-DON bcom ldan
               'das la = addressee; past
            p1 vocative (two epithets, no verb)
            p2 gnas (lags sam) intr; subj = the Buddha (elided, = addressee); question
            p3 grol ('am) intr/invol; subj elided (the Buddha); ABL gnod las = from harm; question
            p4 sku bde ('am) — subj sku (your body); sdig pa mi mnga' = epithet (vocative) or predicate; question
RELATIONS   de nas = then · gis = agent of gsol · la = addressee · las (p3) = source/separation (free FROM harm)
            sam / 'am ×3 = polite yes/no questions; the three questions are coordinate (no connective)
NEG/QUANT   NEG 1: mi mnga' (p4) — scope: sdig pa (has no evil) — inside the epithet, not over the question.
            rnam par rab (p3) = preverb mirrors (vi-pra-mukta), not adverbs (english.md §9 #16): plain "free from
            harm" (check, CONSTRUAL? resolved; Alt: "wholly free from harm").
VERSE-NOTES 7-syllable padas; reg la for reg pa la (count); p1 vocative-only line; p4 epithet + question in one
            line. House style: free verse, one English line per pada, unmetred.
SPINE       Frame: 1 sentence. Verse: vocative (p1) attached to question 1 (p2); question 2 (p3); epithet +
            question 3 (p4).
CONSTRUAL   Then those four bodhisattva great beings addressed these verses to the Blessed One: "Protector of the
            world, you who make light, are you living at ease? Are you free from harm? You who are without
            evil, is your body well?"
TERMS       'jig rten mgon po = Protector of the World · 'od mdzad pa = Maker of Light (not "Bringer of Light":
            the Lucifer calque) ·
            bde bar reg la gnas = living at ease (as U02) · sdig pa mi mnga' = faultless one
DOUBTS      Q: sdig pa mi mnga' (p4) read as a vocative epithet ("Faultless one" = anagha); alt. a predicate
               question ("You are without evil? Is your body well?").
            Q: gnod las rnam par rab grol (verse) is stronger than U02's gnod pa chung (prose): "wholly free from
               harm" vs "troubles slight"; kept distinct, as the Tibetan has them.

UNIT        U04
FORM        verse, 4 padas x 7 syllables (second stanza of the same gatha; continues U03's speakers)
GENRE       verse greeting (gatha)
REGISTER    humble address to the Buddha; courteous questions about his disciples-to-be
SOURCE      1 khyod kyi sems can tshul mdzes sam / 2 gdul sla sbyang bar sla lags sam /
            3 'jig rten mgon gyis bshad pa na / 4 skyo ba skyed par mi bgyid dam //
WORDS       tshul mdzes = well-disposed / of good character (MITRA compound: "good character"); gdul sla = easy to
            train; sbyang bar sla = easy to purify (sbyong/sbyang, tr., purify); 'jig rten mgon = Protector of the
            World (short for mgon po, count); bshad pa = teach, explain; skyo ba skyed par mi bgyid = do not cause
            weariness. Tool slip: "dam" glossed "drum" = question particle 'am after -d.
CLAUSES     p1 tshul mdzes (sam) — subj khyod kyi sems can (your beings); question
            p2 gdul sla, sbyang bar sla (lags sam) — subj same beings (elided); two predicates in one question
            p3 bshad (pa na) tr; ERG agent = 'jig rten mgon gyis (the Buddha, named in third person); object elided
               (the teaching / the beings as hearers); temporal-conditional clause
            p4 skyed par mi bgyid (dam) — agent the beings (elided); recipient of weariness elided (the World
               Protector); NEG mi
RELATIONS   kyi = possessor (your beings) · p2 two predicates juxtaposed (implicit "and") · gyis = agent of bshad ·
            na = temporal "when (you) teach" · dam = question · pa-r (skyed par) = complement of bgyid
NEG/QUANT   NEG 1: mi bgyid (p4) — scope "cause weariness"; courteous question expecting "no, they do not".
VERSE-NOTES mgon for mgon po (count); bar sla = bar after open syllable; p3 third-person self-reference to the
            addressee (honorific).
SPINE       Three questions: p1; p2; p3–4 (one sentence with a "when" clause).
CONSTRUAL   "Are your beings well-disposed? Are they easy to train and easy to purify? When the Protector of the
            World teaches, do they not cause him weariness (i.e., do they cause you no weariness)?"
TERMS       khyod kyi sems can = the beings in your care · gdul sla = easy to train · sbyang bar sla = easy to purify
            · 'jig rten mgon = Protector of the World · skyo ba skyed pa = cause weariness
DOUBTS      Q: p3 'jig rten mgon gyis (third person, = the addressee) rendered "you, Protector of the World"; alt.
               keep third person ("When the Protector of the World teaches, do they not weary him?").
            Q: object of bshad pa unstated: "teach them" (the beings) vs "teach (the Dharma)"; reading "teach them".

UNIT        U05
FORM        prose (speech frame + the Buddha's reply: 8 short statements, a why-question, a reason, a consequence
            with an exception, a closing statement)
GENRE       narrative + dialogue (the Buddha's reply; doctrinal kernel at the end)
REGISTER    the Buddha to the four leaders: plain, warm, authoritative; vocative "noble sons" ×3
SOURCE      F  de nas bcom ldan 'das kyis byang chub sems dpa'i tshogs chen po dang byang chub sems dpa'i phung po
               chen po de'i gtso bo / byang chub sems dpa' bzhi la 'di skad ces bka' stsal to //
            1 rigs kyi bu dag de bzhin te bde bar reg pa la gnas so // 2 gnod pa chung ngo // 3 snyun nyung ngo //
            4 nga'i sems can 'di dag tshul mdzes so // 5 go sla'o // 6 gdul sla'o // 7 rnam par sbyang sla'o //
            8 rnam par sbyong ba na nga la skyo ba mi skyed do //
            9 de ci'i phyir zhe na /
            10 rigs kyi bu dag nga nyid kyis sems can 'di dag sngon gyi yang dag par rdzogs pa'i sangs rgyas kyi tshe
               yongs su sbyang ba byas pa'i phyir te /
            11 rigs kyi bu dag nga mthong ba dang thos pa tsam gyis kyang mos par 'gyur la /
            12 sangs rgyas kyi ye shes la 'bab cing 'dug ste /
            13 gang dag nyan thos kyis [read: kyi sa] la yongs su 'dris par byas pa rnams ni ma gtogs so //
            14 de dag kyang ngas da ltar sangs rgyas kyi ye shes la btsud de don dam pa thos par byas so //
WORDS       bka' stsal = said (hon.) → plain "said"; rigs kyi bu = noble son (kulaputra); de bzhin te = it is so;
            go sla = understand easily (easily made to understand); rnam par sbyong ba = purify;
            yang dag par rdzogs pa'i sangs rgyas = perfectly awakened buddha (samyaksambuddha);
            yongs su sbyang ba byas = thoroughly purified / trained (MITRA compound "trained"); mos par 'gyur = come
            to have faith (adhimukti); sangs rgyas kyi ye shes = the wisdom of the buddhas (buddhajnana);
            'bab = descend into, enter (avatarati); nyan thos = hearer (sravaka); yongs su 'dris par byas pa =
            thoroughly accustomed/familiarized (paricita); ma gtogs = except; btsud (pa. of 'dzud) = lead/put into;
            don dam pa = the ultimate (meaning) (paramartha). Tool slips: botok split "de don | dam pa" — correct
            "btsud de | don dam pa"; "do" glossed "two" = final particle; "nyan thos kyis la": see DOUBTS.
CLAUSES     F  bka' stsal tr hon; ERG agent bcom ldan 'das kyis; LA-DON addressee = byang chub sems dpa' bzhi la, in
               apposition with de'i gtso bo (the leaders of that gathering)
            1 gnas intr; subj = I (the Buddha, elided); de bzhin = assent
            2 chung, 3 nyung — stative; possessor = I (elided)
            4 mdzes stative; subj nga'i sems can 'di dag
            5 go sla, 6 gdul sla, 7 rnam par sbyang sla — stative; subj same beings (elided)
            8 skyed tr; agent = the beings (elided); LA-DON nga la = the one (not) wearied; NEG mi; temporal clause
               rnam par sbyong ba na (agent of sbyong = I, object = them, elided)
            9 rhetorical question ("why is that?")
            10 byas tr past; ERG agent nga nyid kyis (I myself); ABS sems can 'di dag; time = sngon gyi … sangs rgyas
               kyi tshe; nominalized + 'i phyir = reason clause; yongs su = pari- mirror → plain "purified"
               (check, CONSTRUAL? resolved; c13 keeps "thoroughly familiar": the depth carries the exception)
            11 'gyur intr; subj = these beings (elided); means/cause = nga mthong ba dang thos pa tsam gyis kyang
            12 'bab intr + cing 'dug (continuous aspect); subj same; LA-DON ye shes la = goal (into)
            13 ma gtogs (except) — the excepted = gang dag … 'dris par byas pa rnams; 'dris par byas = verbal noun +
               par byed, causative (analysis.md §2.10): "who have made [themselves] thoroughly familiar with" the
               hearers' level — agent = gang dag (reflexive), not a passive "became accustomed";
               LA-DON (sa) la = the thing they have familiarized themselves with
            14 btsud tr past; ERG agent ngas; ABS de dag (kyang); LA-DON ye shes la = goal; then thos par byas
               (causative: made them hear); ABS don dam pa; da ltar = now
RELATIONS   de nas = then · kyis = agent · la = addressee · te (1) = assent then statement (colon)
            na (8) = when (temporal) · zhe na (9) = "if one asks" → rhetorical "why?"
            'i phyir (10) = reason ("because I myself purified them") · te (10→11) = continuation introducing the
            consequence (rendered "So," at the sentence seam — check finding: a bare full stop dropped it)
            gyis (11) = means (by seeing/hearing) · dang (11) = coordination (seeing and hearing) · la (11) = "and"
            cing (12) = coordination/continuous aspect · ste (12→13) = links the statement to its exception
            ma gtogs (13) = exception scoping over 11–12 · kyang (14) = "too / even" (those excepted, too)
            de (14, btsud de) = sequence (led into …, then made to hear)
NEG/QUANT   NEG 2: mi skyed (8) — scope "cause weariness to me"; ma gtogs (13) = exception (restrictive, not
            negation of the verb). Restrictive tsam (11) = merely/just; kyang (11) = even. nyid (10) = myself.
            Plural dag on rigs kyi bu, sems can 'di dag; rnams (13).
SPINE       F 1 sentence. Speech: 1 (assent + at ease) · 2–3 · 4 · 5–7 · 8 · 9 question · 10 reason · 11–13 one
            sentence (faith + entering, except …) · 14 closing sentence. ~10 English sentences.
CONSTRUAL   Then the Blessed One said this to the four bodhisattvas, the leaders of that great gathering and great
            multitude of bodhisattvas: "Noble sons, it is so: I am living at ease. My troubles are slight. My
            ailments are few. These beings of mine are well-disposed. They understand easily. They are easy to
            train. They are easy to purify. When I purify them, they cause me no weariness. Why is that? Noble sons,
            it is because I myself purified these beings in the time of the perfectly awakened buddhas of
            the past. So, noble sons, even merely by seeing me and hearing me they gain faith, and they enter the
            wisdom of the buddhas — except those who have made themselves thoroughly familiar with the level of the
            hearers.
            Those too I have now led into the wisdom of the buddhas, and I have brought them to hear the ultimate
            meaning."
TERMS       rigs kyi bu = noble son · nga'i sems can = the beings in my care · go sla = understand easily ·
            yang dag par rdzogs pa'i sangs rgyas = perfectly awakened buddha · mos pa = faith ·
            sangs rgyas kyi ye shes = the wisdom of the buddhas · nyan thos = hearer · don dam pa = the ultimate meaning
DOUBTS      Q: "nyan thos kyis la" is ungrammatical; read "nyan thos kyi sa la" (one tsheg lost) = "the level of the
               hearers"; alt. "the way/vehicle of the hearers" if another word dropped. Reading affects one phrase.
            Q: 'bab cing 'dug read as continuous aspect ("they enter"); alt. lexical "enter … and remain in it".
            Q: yongs su sbyang ba byas "purified" (keeps the sbyong thread of 7–8); alt. "trained" (MITRA compound
               sense).
            Q: sangs rgyas (10) number unmarked: "the perfectly awakened buddhas of the past" (plural) vs "a past
               perfectly awakened buddha".
            Q: thos pa (11) object: "hearing me" (nga governs both) vs "hearing [the teaching]".
            Q: mos pa "faith"; alt. "conviction / devotion".

UNIT        U06
FORM        mixed: prose speech frame + verse, 3 padas x 7 (the sentence runs on into U07: U06 p3 ends in dang;
            U06+U07 = 8 padas, one sentence)
GENRE       narrative frame + verse praise/rejoicing (gatha)
REGISTER    the bodhisattvas to the Buddha: acclamation, then rejoicing; warm, not ornate
SOURCE      F  de nas de'i tshe byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po de dag gis tshigs su bcad pa 'di dag gsol to //
            1 dpa' bo chen po legs so legs / 2 khyod kyi sems kyi tshul mdzes shing / 3 gdul sla rnam par sbyang sla dang /
WORDS       de'i tshe = at that time; dpa' bo chen po = Great Hero (mahavira, epithet of the Buddha); legs so legs =
            excellent, excellent (sadhu); sems kyi tshul mdzes: see DOUBTS; gdul sla / rnam par sbyang sla as U02-U05.
CLAUSES     F  gsol tr humble; ERG agent = byang chub sems dpa' sems dpa' chen po de dag gis (those — the four, or the
               host; "de dag" only); ABS tshigs su bcad pa 'di dag; addressee elided (the Buddha)
            p1 vocative + exclamation (no verb)
            p2 mdzes stative; subj = khyod kyi sems [can] (your beings) — tshul mdzes = well-disposed
            p3 gdul sla, rnam par sbyang sla — stative predicates of the same subject (elided)
