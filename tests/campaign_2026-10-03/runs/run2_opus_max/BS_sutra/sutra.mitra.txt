U01: Regarding that great host of bodhisattvas and that great multitude of bodhisattvas, the leaders were the four bodhisattva mahāsattvas who appeared, namely: the bodhisattva mahāsattva named Viśiṣṭacāritra, the bodhisattva mahāsattva named Anantacāritra, the bodhisattva mahāsattva named Viśuddhacāritra, and the bodhisattva mahāsattva named Supratiṣṭhitacāritra. Those four bodhisattva mahāsattvas were the leaders of that great host of bodhisattvas and that great multitude of bodhisattvas.
U02: Then those four bodhisattva mahāsattvas, having sat before that great assembly of bodhisattvas and that great multitude of bodhisattvas, joined their palms in the presence of the Blessed One and addressed the Blessed One in this way: "Blessed One, are you free from harm? Are you free from illness? Are you dwelling in comfort? Blessed One, are your sentient beings well-behaved? Are they easy to understand? Are they easy to tame? Are they easy to purify? Do they not cause the Blessed One to become weary?"
U03: Then those four bodhisattva mahāsattvas addressed the Blessed One with these verses:  "O Protector of the world, Maker of light, Are you dwelling in comfort? Are you completely liberated from harm? Are you, who possess no negativity, well in your body?"
U04: Are your sentient beings of beautiful conduct? Are they easy to discipline and easy to purify? When the Protector of the World teaches them, do they not cause you weariness?
U05: Then the Bhagavan spoke thus to the great assembly of Bodhisattvas and to the four principal Bodhisattvas of that great mass of Bodhisattvas: "Sons of noble family, it is so; I abide in comfort. I have little harm. I have little illness. These sentient beings of mine are well-behaved. They understand easily. They are easy to discipline. They are easy to purify. As I purify them, no weariness arises in me. For what reason? Sons of noble family, it is because I myself have previously purified these sentient beings during the time of the former perfectly enlightened Buddhas. Sons of noble family, merely by seeing and hearing me, they become devoted and enter into the wisdom of the Buddha, with the exception of those who have become thoroughly habituated to the stage of the śrāvakas. Yet even those I have now established in the wisdom of the Buddha and caused to hear the ultimate truth."
U06: Then, at that time, those bodhisattva mahāsattvas addressed him with these verses: "Excellent, excellent, Great Hero! The disposition of your mind is beautiful, and you are easy to tame and easy to purify."
U07: Those who hear this profound wisdom of yours, and having heard it, develop devotion and enter into the guidance of the leaders—in them, we rejoice.
U08: When they had spoken thus, the Blessed One said to those four bodhisattva mahāsattvas who were the leaders of that great assembly of bodhisattvas and that great multitude of bodhisattvas, "Noble sons, excellent, excellent! Noble sons, it is also excellent, excellent, that you have praised the Tathāgata."
U09: At that time, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Maitreya, along with other bodhisattvas as numerous as the sands of eight Ganges rivers, thought to themselves: "We have never before seen or heard of this great host of bodhisattvas, this great multitude of bodhisattvas, who have emerged from the depths of the earth, and having sat before the Blessed One, show him honor, revere him, and bring him joy. From where have these bodhisattva mahāsattvas come?"
U10: Then, the bodhisattva mahāsattva Maitreya, knowing his own doubt and uncertainty, and also perceiving with his mind the mental reflections of those hundreds of thousands of millions of billions of bodhisattvas mahāsattvas, as numerous as the sands of eight Ganges Rivers, joined his palms and, at that time, asked the Blessed One about this matter in a melody of verses.
U11: The Bodhisattvas who have appeared in the past, numbering many hundreds of thousands and infinite tens of millions, I beseech the Best of the Two-Footed to reveal.
