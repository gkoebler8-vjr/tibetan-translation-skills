### U01
HEADER: U01 · prose · narrative · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Earlier, when Rinpoche was with the Dharma Lord Phagmodrupa, the former lord said of him, “He is a great bodhisattva abiding on the tenth bhumi,” and this became known everywhere.
NOTES:
Q: zhabs drung du may set the scene for the renown rather than for the statement ("in Phagmodrupa's circle everyone knew that the former lord had called Rinpoche …"); same facts, different focus.
Q: rje gong mas — ergative read as the agent of an implied "said"; alternatively the source of the renown ("through the former lord, Rinpoche became known everywhere as …").
Q: sa bcu la gnas pa is literally "abiding on the ten bhumis"; read as the tenth bhumi because U02 and U04 say sa bcu pa (MITRA keeps "ten levels").
Q: rin po che la read as the person spoken of (X la … zhes grags = X is known as …); MITRA reads him as the addressee ("declared to Rinpoche") — kept.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (invention: "at the feet of" brought in the English idiom's discipleship sense), revised to "was with".

### U02
HEADER: U02 · prose · narrative with dialogue (honorific question) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation

NOTES:
