U01: Formerly, in the presence of the Lord of Dharma Phagmodrupa, the high lord declared to Rinpoche that he was a great bodhisattva abiding on the ten levels, and this became renowned everywhere.
U02: Since the Lord Rinpoche does not acknowledge himself to be on the tenth bhūmi, I asked, "Even if one is on the tenth bhūmi, is there something that one does not know?"
U03: Because the spiritual friend Tashi Gangpa perceived, through many reasons, that the Precious Lord was the venerable Lord of Secrets, Vajrapāṇi, he asked, "Is it indeed so?"
U04: Regarding both of them, in accordance with the speech, what need is there for me to be the tenth-stage [bodhisattva] or the venerable Guhyapati alone?
U05: Realizing this Mahāmudrā of my own mind, these two—the ultimate bodhicitta and the generation of conventional bodhicitta—are equal to all the buddhas of the three times; therefore, they fulfill the intent of all the buddhas.
U06: Because [he/it] is equal to all sentient beings of the three realms, [he/it] causes all sentient beings to ripen and be liberated, and it is said that [he/it] is the state of equality with all of samsara and nirvana.
U07: And within the mandala of the assembly of the Sangha, he repeatedly proclaimed the lion’s roar, saying, "I myself am the essence of the primordial wisdom of the enlightened body, speech, and mind of all the Buddhas of the three times," and...
U08: I also, at the time of passing into complete nirvāṇa, ask: O Lord, having departed from here, into which buddha-field will you depart? I request that you speak of the buddha-field where we should offer our prayers, and the manner in which you will reside there.
U09: Since there is no single buddhafield that is exclusively "this one," and because [the Buddha] gave an extensive teaching on abiding eternally in the suchness (*tathatā*) of all cyclic existence (*saṃsāra*) and transcendence (*nirvāṇa*), [therefore...]
U10: He is the teacher of the three worlds, the lord of all families, the turner of the wheel of Dharma, the sixth Bhagavan, the Great Vajradhara.
U11: And how is that? It is the vajra of wisdom, in which the body, speech, and mind of all the buddhas of the three times are non-dual; it is the nature that unites cyclic existence and nirvana into equality; it is the joyful indulgence in the taste of co-emergent great bliss; it is the unwavering meditative equipoise upon the suchness of all phenomena; and because it pervades all of cyclic existence and nirvana, it remains eternally without interruption.
