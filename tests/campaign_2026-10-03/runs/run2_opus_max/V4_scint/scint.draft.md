# Quotation convention: Rinpoche's speech U04–U06 is one quotation set as continuous paragraphs (opening mark at each unit, closing mark only at U06).

U01
Earlier, when Rinpoche was with the Dharma Lord Phagmodrupa, the former lord said of him, “He is a great bodhisattva abiding on the tenth bhumi,” and this became known everywhere.

U02
Lord Rinpoche, however, would not accept that he was on the tenth bhumi, so he was asked, “Even though you are on the tenth bhumi, is there something you do not know?”

U03
And Geshe Tashi Gangpa asked, “For many reasons I have come to understand that you, Lord Rinpoche, are the Venerable Lord of Secrets, Vajrapani—is that so?”

U04
To both questions he gave the same answer: “Why should I be only a bodhisattva of the tenth bhumi or the Venerable Lord of Secrets?

U05
“Ultimate bodhicitta—the realization that my mind itself is this mahamudra—and the generation of relative bodhicitta: these two are equal to all the buddhas of the three times. Therefore they fulfill the intent of all the buddhas.

U06
“Because they are equal to all the sentient beings of the three realms, they ripen and liberate all sentient beings; and they are equal to all of samsara and nirvana—sameness itself,” he said.

U07
And in the midst of the assembled sangha, too, he roared the lion’s roar again and again: “I myself am the essence of the wisdom of the body, speech, and mind of all the buddhas of the three times.”

U08
And when he was about to pass into parinirvana, I too asked him, “Now, Lord Rinpoche, when you depart from here, to which buddha field will you go? Please tell us the buddha field to which we should direct our prayers, and how you will dwell there.”

U09
He replied at length that he has no single buddha field that could be called “this one,” and that he always dwells in the suchness of all samsara and nirvana.

U10
So the Lama of the Three Worlds himself is the lord of all buddha families, the universal monarch of the Dharma, the sixth Bhagavan, the great Vajradhara.

U11
If you ask how this is so: he is the wisdom vajra of the inseparable body, speech, and mind of all the buddhas of the three times. He embodies the union of samsara and nirvana in sameness. He revels with delight in the taste of coemergent great bliss.
