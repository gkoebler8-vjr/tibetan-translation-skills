UNIT        U01

CLAUSES     2  (a) elided speech verb (implied by zhes): agent rje gong ma-s (ERG), topic rin po che la (LA-DON: "of/about"), content "[he] is a great bodhisattva abiding on the ten bhumis" (yin, copula, ABS-ABS)  (b) grags (intr., invol.) subject = that statement (the zhes clause), locus thams cad du; nas + 'dug = resultative, past

RELATIONS   1 zhabs drung du = locative scene (in the presence of)  2 rje gong ma-s = ERG agent of the implied statement  3 rin po che la = LA-DON, topic/referent ("said of Rinpoche")  4 sa bcu la = locus (abiding on)  5 pa'i = modification (bodhisattva who abides …)  6 zhes = quotative (content of the statement)  7 thams cad du = locus/extent of grags  8 nas = sequence/resultative with 'dug
