1. U05 S3: the master, not the disciple, made the one who "has received the irreversible consecration". Cause: grammar misread (thob pas taken as an ergative restating the agent instead of a cause/means clause about the recipient); a construal fork that was recorded, but C took the wrong branch.
2. U08 S5: the positive definition of the fourth consecration (external seal + objectless-focus yoga = "what is called the fourth") is lost, and "there is … there is … These do not become" is invented scaffolding. 4. U05 S2/S4 and U06 S1: calqued phrasing ("bringing about the four joys in their own nature from the action seal", "counts for nothing", "made firm through conviction"). Cause: register (academic literalism carried into the English).
5. U11 line 2: one overloaded line carrying both qualifiers. Cause: verse constraint (free line-for-line with no redistribution of the qualifiers).
