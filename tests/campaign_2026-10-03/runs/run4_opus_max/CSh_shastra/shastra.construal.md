UNIT        U01
FORM        prose; commentary gloss on a root lemma, ending on the lead-in to a citation (de yang ji skad du → U02)
GENRE       exposition / commentary gloss
REGISTER    commentary gloss: impersonal, plain, lemma–"means"–gloss
SOURCE      1 sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa la /  2 sdom pa ni dbang bskur ba'o //  3 de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste /  4 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no //  5 de yang ji skad du /
WORDS       sdom pa = vow (saṃvara; root lemma) · dbang bskur ba = consecration (abhiṣeka) · dbye ba = division (bheda; root lemma) · rab tu dbye ba = classification (prabheda) · 'chad par 'gyur ba = [that] will be explained (future) · mtshan nyid can = having the defining characteristics · ji skad du = as [it] says
CLAUSES     1 no verb: lemma frame "sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa" + la (topic: regarding)
            2 copula (equational, 'o): sdom pa [lemma] ni = dbang bskur ba [gloss]
            3 copula: de'i rab tu dbye ba [gloss] ni = dbye ba [lemma] — gloss-first order; ste links to 4
            4 copula/predicative: [elided subj = the classification] 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no — "has the characteristics that will be explained"; future
            5 lead-in, verb deferred: the quotation (U02) closes with ces gsungs pa yin no
RELATIONS   sdom pa'i = gen (divisions OF the vow) · la sogs pa = "and so on" (lemma continues) · la = topic, "regarding" · de'i = gen, "its" (de = the consecration = the vow) · ste = semi-final, attaches 4 to 3 (which…) · 'gyur ba'i = relative on mtshan nyid · de yang = connective "and that [is]" · ji skad du = "as [it] says"
NEG/QUANT   none; la sogs pa
SPINE       3 sentences: (1) lemma + gloss of "vow"; (2) gloss of "division" + relative "whose characteristics will be explained"; (3) lead-in "As it is said:"
CONSTRUAL   Regarding [the root's words] "the divisions of the vow" and so on: "vow" means the consecration; "division" means its [the consecration's] classification, which has the defining characteristics that will be explained [later]. And this is as [the tantra] says:
TERMS       sdom pa = vow (saṃvara) · dbang bskur ba = consecration · dbye ba = division · rab tu dbye ba = classification · mtshan nyid = defining characteristic
CITATION    lemma from the root tantra (not run through identify: a 3-word lemma); the lead-in introduces the verse in U02
DOUBTS      Q: 3 read gloss-first ("division" = its classification); alt "its classification is the division" — same sense
            Q: sdom pa: "vow" (saṃvara); the gloss equates it with consecration; alt keep Sanskrit saṃvara (also "binding", the tantra's own title word)
            Q: 4 'chad par 'gyur ba'i: "will be explained" — later in the tantra (cf. U04) or later in this commentary; alt "to be explained"

UNIT        U02
FORM        verse, 7×4 padas (equal count) + closing frame "ces gsungs pa yin no"; citation (frame opened in U01: de yang ji skad du … ces gsungs)
GENRE       root-tantra verse quoted as authority (etymological definition)

SOURCE      1 sems can rnams la phan pa'i phyir /  2 dbang bskur ba ni bzhi ru bsgrags /  3 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur bas /  4 de phyir dbang bskur zhes byar brjod /  frame: ces gsungs pa yin no //

            2 bsgrags (tr., past/perfect) frame: ERG agent elided (the Teacher) / ABS dbang bskur ba (ni = topic) / -ru terminative "as four" → passive in English: "consecration is proclaimed to be fourfold"

RELATIONS   la = recipient (for beings) · pa'i phyir = purpose · ni = topic · ru = terminative (fourfold) · yis = instrumental/means · shing = coordination (and) · bas = cause (since) · de phyir = consequence (therefore) · zhes = quotative (the name) · ces = quotative (closing the citation)
NEG/QUANT   none; numeral bzhi (four)

CONSTRUAL   For the benefit of beings, consecration has been proclaimed to be of four kinds. Because by this [rite] one is washed and consecrated, it is therefore called "consecration." So it has been taught [in the tantra].
TERMS       dbang bskur ba = consecration (bound; also for the verbal use in 3: "consecrated") · sems can = beings · phan pa = benefit

NEG/QUANT   NEG: mi dge ba (lexical, non-virtuous) · phyir mi ldog pa (lexical, irreversible) · la ni mi bya'o (clausal: the designation is NOT applied to S3's consecration) · ci yang ma yin (clausal: the statement is nothing at all). skad cig ma = noun, not NEG. CONSTRUAL   In this yoginī tantra, [one is called] "master" because one engages in conduct far removed from evil, non-virtuous states; for that very [conduct] is his vow. The consecration by the master is, in its nature, what is meant by "bringing about, from the action seal, through the four moments, the four joys in their own nature." But [the term] is not applied to the consecration conferred by a master who has received the irreversible consecration that is common to the kriyā tantras and the rest. Therefore the statement "[through that one] also becomes fit to teach, to hear, and so on, all the yoga tantras, yoginī tantras and the rest" counts for nothing. Therefore, first of all, in order to authorize [the disciple] to hear, meditate on, and so on the yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, [the master] must consecrate [him] with precisely the master consecration. It should further be understood that this [consecration] is indicated, in accordance with the abhisamaya, by the defining characteristics of the master, secret, wisdom and fourth [consecrations].

DOUBTS      Q: S1 ring du gyur pa kun du spyod pa: object ("engages in what is far removed from …") vs state ("being far removed from …, he engages in conduct"); rendered "conduct far removed from"
