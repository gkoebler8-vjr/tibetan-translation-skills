## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U07 "he begets him, too, as a son": de yang is read as an absolutive object (the father) instead of "he too", which invents a doctrine of the father reborn as his son's son. Cause: grammar misread (an over-strict ergative test: an unmarked agent is ruled out).
2. U06 emptiness = "an exalted death, and the stainless body … of the gandharva": the bardo-body stage is folded into emptiness. Cause: construal fork, settled by a ni-marker rule that C then drops in the very next item.
3. U01 "union with that prajna": the vague de yi sbyor bas is made specific as the consort. Cause: construal fork (antecedent supplied from the contrast in pada 3).
4. U09 "the enjoyment of the young women": the la object of longs spyod pa becomes an ambiguous English genitive, which blurs who enjoys whom. Cause: grammar misread / compression into a nominal.
5. U05 "The intermediate directions … belong to Mohavajri": the locative su is turned into a subject. Cause: omission under compression (a list template, "X is/are Y", applied to a locative item).
