# tantra — construal (Caṇḍamahāroṣaṇa Tantra, Toh 431, ch. 15; units U01–U12)

Brief (from the prompt, held): seasoned practitioner · study aid · technical terms kept where the tradition uses them, anglicized, no diacritics · no brackets or Wylie in the body · verse metred per tibetan-verse, citation mode, one line per pada where the sense allows · no contractions · doubts as Q: lines.
Speakers: U01–U02 the Blessed One (Caṇḍamahāroṣaṇa = Acala) instructing; U03 the Blessed Lady asks (gsol), the Blessed One answers (bka' stsal); U04–U12 the Blessed One's answer.
Names: anglicized phonetically, no diacritics (c → ch, ś/ṣ → sh): Achala, Dveshavajri, Mohavajri, Akshobhya, Mamaki, Amoghasiddhi, Prajnaparamita.

UNIT        U01
FORM        verse 7×4; not a citation (root-tantra verse, continuing the Blessed One's instruction from before the span)
GENRE       instruction (practice)
REGISTER    the Blessed One to the Blessed Lady, about the yogi; plain, authoritative
SOURCE      1 de yi sbyor bas rnal 'byor pas / 2 myur du dngos grub the tshom med / 3 yang na shes rab dang bral ba'i / 4 shin tu mnyam gzhag bsgom par bya
WORDS       sbyor ba = union (prayoga/yoga; here union with the consort) · shes rab = prajna, the female consort (contrast with pada 1) · shin tu mnyam gzhag = very deep meditative equipoise · bsgom par bya = should cultivate (gerundive)
CLAUSES     2  (a) verb ELIDED (gain/attain, implied by the ergative agent) frame=ERG rnal 'byor pas / ABS dngos grub / means de yi sbyor bas / manner myur du; the tshom med = certainty adjunct; tense general/future
               (b) verb=bsgom (tr, vol) bsgom par bya = obligative "should cultivate"; agent ELIDED = the yogi (pada 1); ABS shin tu mnyam gzhag, modified by shes rab dang bral ba'i
RELATIONS   de yi = genitive (union with her/that) · sbyor bas = instrumental means (through) · rnal 'byor pas = ergative agent · myur du = adverbial (swiftly) · yang na = alternative (or else) · dang bral = privative (without) · ba'i = modification
NEG/QUANT   the tshom med: no doubt (scope: the swift siddhi) · bral: without a prajna · shin tu: intensifier (deep, very)
VERSE-NOTES yi = long genitive for the count; main verb of S1 elided
SPINE       2 sentences: padas 1–2 (verb supplied), padas 3–4 (bsgom par bya)
CONSTRUAL   Through union with her [the prajna of the preceding verses], the yogi swiftly gains siddhi; of that there is no doubt. Or else, without a prajna, he should cultivate very deep meditative equipoise.
TERMS       rnal 'byor pa = yogi · dngos grub = siddhi · shes rab (consort) = prajna · mnyam gzhag = meditative equipoise · sbyor ba = union
DOUBTS      Q: de yi sbyor bas — "union with her" (the prajna, antecedent before the span; the contrast "or else, without a prajna" supports it) or "by applying that [practice]".
            Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i — modifies the equipoise ("equipoise without a prajna") or describes the yogi ("a yogi without a prajna"); same practical sense; rendered "without a prajna" adverbially.

UNIT        U05
FORM        prose; 16 identity statements (X ni Y'o) + closing frame; not a citation
GENRE       exposition (viśuddhi list: each part of the mandala and what it is in reality)
REGISTER    the Blessed One to the Blessed Lady; plain, definitional; no elevation
SOURCE      1 de la gru bzhi pa ni tshad med pa'i gnas bzhi'o / 2 sgo bzhi ni bden pa bzhi'o / 3 rta babs bzhi ni bsam gtan bzhi'o / 4 ka ba brgyad ni 'phags pa'i lam yan lag brgyad do / 5 'phar ma gcig pa ni sems rtse gcig pa nyid do / 6 pad+ma ni skye gnas so / 7 kha dog sna tshogs pa ni sprul pa sna tshogs pa'o / 8 lha dgu ni gsung rab yan lag dgu'o / 9 phyogs dmar po ni 'dod chags chen po'o / 10 mtshams su dkar po dang ser po dang ljang gu dang nag po rnams ni bram ze dang rje rigs dang rgyal rigs dang dmangs rigs rnams nyid do / 11 zla ba dang nyi ma ni khu ba dang rngul lo / 12 dbus kyi ral gri ni mi g.yo ba nag po'i mtshan ma'o / 13 gri gug ni zhe sdang rdo rje ma'o / 14 shar la sogs pa'i phyogs rnams ni mi g.yo ba dkar po la sogs pa rnams so / 15 me la sogs pa'i mtshams rnams su gti mug rdo rje ma la sogs pa rnams kyi'o / 16 zhes pa ni dkyil 'khor gyi rnam par dag pa'o
WORDS       gru bzhi pa = square (four-cornered) · tshad med pa'i gnas bzhi = the four immeasurables · rta babs = archway (toraṇa) · 'phar ma = not attested as an architectural term in the tools ("additional, extra, double, manifold"); taken as a raised layer/tier of the palace · skye gnas = womb, place of birth (MITRA: womb or vulva) · sprul pa = emanation · gsung rab yan lag dgu = the nine branches of scripture · rje rigs = vaiśya (MITRA, Duff = rje'u rigs; RY also "nobles"); yellow is the vaiśya colour, which confirms it · da mangs rigs = dmangs rigs, śūdra (orthographic variant; botok split it "da mang s") · rngul = sweat (Skt sveda; no other sense in the tools) · mtshan ma = emblem, sign · gri gug = curved knife (kartari) · me (in me la sogs pa'i mtshams) = the fire quarter, i.e. the southeast
CLAUSES     16 nominal identity predicates, copula elided, closing particle 'o/do/so/lo; no finite verb; tense none; agent of the whole = the Blessed One explaining (from U04)
            15: genitive-final kyi'o — head elided ("[those] of Mohavajri…": the corners belong to them)
            16: zhes pa ni = "what has been said thus" — the closing summary of the list
RELATIONS   de la = topic opener (here, in that: the purity announced in U04) · ni = topic of each identity · 'i (tshad med pa'i, 'phags pa'i, nag po'i, sogs pa'i, dkyil 'khor gyi) = modification/genitive · dang = coordination (lists in 10 and 11, respective: white–brahmin, yellow–vaiśya, green–kṣatriya, black–śūdra; moon–semen, sun–sweat) · su (mtshams su, mtshams rnams su) = locus · la sogs pa = "and the others / beginning with" · kyi'o = possession, head elided
NEG/QUANT   NEG: 3 counted by the tool, none real: tshad med (lexical, "immeasurable"), mi g.yo ×2 (the name Achala); "rdo rje ma la" = Mohavajri + la sogs pa, botok slip (ma = feminine suffix, not NEG) · numerals four/four/four/eight/one/nine kept in position · rnams = plural · nyid (10) = emphatic, not rendered · gcig (5) = single, carries the match to one-pointedness
SPINE       16 short identity sentences; 9 contrasts phyogs (cardinal directions) with 10 mtshams (intermediate directions); 14–15 the same contrast for the deities
CONSTRUAL   Here, the square is the four immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the eight branches of the noble path. The single layer is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the womb. The many colours are the many emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red of the cardinal directions is great passion. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are the brahmins, vaishyas, kshatriyas and shudras. The moon and the sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the centre is the emblem of Black Achala. The curved knife is Dveshavajri. The cardinal directions, beginning with the east, are White Achala and the others. The intermediate directions, beginning with the southeast (fire), belong to Mohavajri and the others. That is the purity of the mandala.

DOUBTS      Q: 'phar ma gcig pa — "the single layer" (taken; 'phar ma = "double, manifold, additional" in the tools, no architectural sense attested); could be a single tier, ledge or frieze of the palace.

            Q: rje rigs — vaishyas (taken: MITRA, Duff, and the yellow colour) or "nobles" (RY).

FORM        prose; 9 sentences (narrative of conception, rebirth and the deities' birth, as purity); not a citation
GENRE       exposition in narrative form
REGISTER    the Blessed One; plain, matter-of-fact (the subject is charged; the diction is not)
SOURCE      1 hU~M yig ni ma dang pha'i dbus su dri za'i sems so / 2 ma bskyod pa ni pa'o / 3 mA ma kI ni ma'o / 4 phan tshun du rjes su chags pa mthong nas pha la rnam par zhe sdang zhing ma la rjes su chags nas gti mug pas sems can bzhin du 'jug go / 5 pad+mar zhugs pa'i bu yis pha bsad nas de'i go 'phang 'thob bo / 6 ma bzung nas skye ba mtha' med pa'i bag chags kyi bde ba khyad par 'phags pas de yang bur skye bar byed do / 7 bu mo ni yang mi g.yo ba dkar po la sogs pa rnams dang gti mug rdo rje ma la sogs pa yang ngo / 8 bu rnams kyang pha bsad nas gzhan la bgrod par byed de / 'di nyid dgra bo zhes de gsod par byed do / 9 bu mo rnams kyang skye ba mtha' med pa'i bag chags kyi bde ba khyad par 'phags pas 'dod par byed do

CONSTRUAL   The syllable HUM is the mind of the gandharva between the mother and the father. Akshobhya is the father. Mamaki is the mother. Seeing the two in passion for each other, he hates the father and desires the mother; then, through delusion, he enters as sentient beings do. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and attains his position. Having taken the mother, through the exalted bliss of the habitual tendencies of countless births, he begets him, too, as a son. The daughters, again, are White Achala and the others, and Mohavajri and the others as well. The sons, too, kill the father and go to others; thinking "This is the enemy," they kill him. The daughters, too, feel desire, through the exalted bliss of the habitual tendencies of countless births.

            Q: gzhan la bgrod pa — "go to others" (taken literally); bgrod pa can mean sexual approach: "go to other women".
            Q: de gsod — "him" = the father, the killing restated with its motive (taken) or the "other" of 8a.
            Q: 'dod par byed — object elided: "feel desire" (taken), "desire him" (the father?), or causative "arouse desire".
            Q: gti mug pas — "through delusion" (taken; 'jug is intransitive, so not an agent) or "deluded".
