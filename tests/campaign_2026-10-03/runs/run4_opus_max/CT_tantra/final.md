### U01
HEADER: U01 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7×4, four lines, order 1-2-3-4 · instruction (the Blessed One on the yogi's practice), plain · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text: Chandamaharoshana Tantra, Toh 431, ch. 15)
TEXT:
Through union with that prajna, the yogi
will surely and swiftly gain siddhi.
Or else, without a prajna,
he should cultivate deep meditative equipoise.
NOTES:
Q: de yi sbyor bas — "union with that prajna", the consort of the verses before this span (taken; the contrast "or else, without a prajna" supports it) or "through applying that practice" (MITRA's reading).
Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i — "without a prajna" read adverbially with the yogi (taken) or as the equipoise's attribute; MITRA's "equipoise devoid of wisdom" takes shes rab as wisdom, against the contrast with line 1.
Issue: de yi ("of her, of that") rendered "that prajna", naming the antecedent; the tshom med ("there is no doubt") rendered "surely".
Check: in-context; no finding. MITRA differs (practice; wisdom); kept.

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose (sixteen identity statements, one English sentence each) · exposition (the purity of the mandala), the Blessed One, plain · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text)
TEXT:
Here, the square is the four immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the eight branches of the noble path. The single layer is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the womb. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red of the cardinal directions is great passion. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are the brahmins, vaishyas, kshatriyas and shudras. The moon and the sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of Black Achala. The curved knife is Dveshavajri. The cardinal directions, beginning with the east, are White Achala and the others. The intermediate directions, beginning with the southeast, belong to Mohavajri and the others. That is the purity of the mandala.
NOTES:
Q: 'phar ma gcig pa — "the single layer" (taken; the dictionaries give only "double, manifold, additional"); could be a tier, ledge or frieze of the palace; MITRA has "projection".
Q: phyogs dmar po — "the red of the cardinal directions" (taken; set against the intermediate directions) or "the red direction", one quarter only (MITRA).
Q: rje rigs — vaishyas (taken; yellow is the vaishya color) or "nobles".
Q: rngul — "sweat" as written; paired with the sun, it stands where U06 has "the red".
Q: kyi'o in the last item — "belong to Mohavajri and the others" (taken) or "[the emblems] in the intermediate directions are those of Mohavajri and the others".
Issue: me ("fire") rendered "the southeast", the fire quarter.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the southeast unpack, now noted). MITRA differs (projection; red direction); kept.

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose (announcement and five identities) · exposition (the purity of the meditation), plain · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text)
TEXT:
I shall describe the purity of the meditation: first, the offering is the exalted act of accumulating merit. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom, an exalted death, and the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva. The culmination of the palace is the level of buddhahood. The lotus is the womb. The moon and the sun are the white and the red.
NOTES:
Q: bsod nams kyi tshogs khyad par 'phags pa'i las — "the exalted act of accumulating merit" (taken) or "the act of the exalted accumulation of merit"; las could also be karma, matching "an exalted death".
Q: "the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva" — still said of emptiness (taken; no new topic marker) or a separate item, "the stainless body is the body of the gandharva".
Q: gzhal yas khang gi mthar thug pa — "the culmination of the palace" (taken after the MITRA cross-check: gi governs mthar thug pa) or "the palace is the level of ultimate buddhahood"; the culmination may be the palace's completion or its summit.
Alt: gandharva — the being of the intermediate state, between death and rebirth.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (ste flattened to a full stop; the colon restores "namely"). MITRA differs (the palace's ultimate limit); changed (reason: gi before the noun mthar thug pa governs it, and "mthar thug pa sangs rgyas" has no 'i, so it cannot be "ultimate buddhahood").

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose (nine sentences) · exposition in narrative form (conception and birth as purity), plain, matter-of-fact · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text)
TEXT:
The syllable HUM is the mind of the gandharva between the mother and the father. Akshobhya is the father. Mamaki is the mother. Seeing their passion for each other, the gandharva hates the father and desires the mother; then, through delusion, he enters as sentient beings do. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and then attains his position. Having taken the mother, he begets him, too, as a son, through the exalted bliss of the habitual tendencies of countless births. The daughters, again, are White Achala and the others, and Mohavajri and the others as well. The sons, too, kill the father and then go to others; thinking, "This is the enemy," they kill him. The daughters, too, feel desire through the exalted bliss of the habitual tendencies of countless births.
NOTES:
Q: de yang bur skye bar byed — "he begets him (the father) too as a son" (taken: verbal noun + bar byed is causative, de is its object, as de'i in the sentence before) or "he too begets sons"; MITRA's "he is born as a son" drops the causative.
Q: "The daughters, again, are White Achala and the others, and Mohavajri and the others" — as written; the sentences that follow (the sons kill the father, the daughters desire) suggest the sons are the Achalas and the daughters Mohavajri and the others.
Q: gzhan la bgrod pa — "go to others" (taken) or, with bgrod pa as sexual approach, "go to other women".
Q: de gsod — "kill him": the father, the killing restated with its motive (taken), or the "other" they go to.
Q: 'dod par byed — object elided: "feel desire" (taken), "desire him", or causative "arouse desire".
Q: gti mug pas — "through delusion" (taken; the verb "enter" takes no agent) or "deluded".
Alt: ma bskyod pa — spelt so here for mi bskyod pa, Akshobhya.
Check: in-context; 2 minor (two nas sequences flattened to "and", now "and then"). MITRA differs ("is born as a son"); kept "begets".

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose (eight statements) · exposition (the purity of the attributes, gaze and posture; the four maras), plain · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text)
TEXT:
The sword is wisdom, and the noose is method. Or else, the noose is wisdom and the sword is method. That the two have become one taste is the threatening forefinger. The left eye gazing down protects the seven underworlds. The right eye gazing up protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the ground protects the earth. Striking with the right foot terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma, the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara, the mara of the Lord of Death is Vishnu, and the mara of the son of the gods is Indra.
NOTES:
Q: "the left … gazing down", "the right … gazing up" — the noun is not in the Tibetan; taken as the eyes (bltas pa, "looking"); could be the hands or the face; MITRA's "looking downward to the left" takes g.yon pa as a direction, which would be g.yon du.
Q: sa skyong bar byed pa — "protects the earth" (taken) or "acts as the earth's protector", the king; U09 opens by explaining "the earth".
Q: yang dag par bsnun pa — "striking" (taken; yang dag par mirrors a preverb) or "striking squarely" (MITRA: "stamping firmly").
Issue: shes rab rendered "wisdom" here, the partner of method; "prajna" in U01, where it is the consort.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the term note). MITRA differs (to the left); kept.

### U09
HEADER: U09 · prose (ten identities) · exposition (the seat, the couple, the colors as the aggregates), plain · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text)
TEXT:
The earth is the enjoyment of the young women of the whole world. The seat is a lotus because one remains long in the young woman. Birth in the womb is the seat of moon and sun. The man born from semen and sweat is existence. The woman is nonexistence. Black is consciousness, white is form, yellow is feeling, red is perception, and green is Amoghasiddhi.
NOTES:
Q: pa'i phyir — cause, "because one remains long in the young woman" (taken) or purpose, "the lotus seat is so that one may remain long in the young woman".
Q: skye gnas la skyes pa — "birth in the womb" (taken) or "the one born in the womb".
Q: "green is Amoghasiddhi" as written; the series of aggregates leads one to expect formations, Amoghasiddhi's aggregate.
Q: gzhon nu ma — plural in the first sentence; taken as singular ("the young woman", the partner) in the second; could be plural there too.
Check: in-context; no finding. MITRA agrees.
