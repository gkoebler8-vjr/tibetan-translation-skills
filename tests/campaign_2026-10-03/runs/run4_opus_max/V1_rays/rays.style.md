# rays.style.md — Pass 3 (seasoned practitioner, publication): the text the fidelity check reads

### U01
For his own benefit there is the dharmakaya, which does not shift or change: the naturally pure element, in which the adventitious stains have been stilled. Uncompounded by causes and conditions and free of elaboration, it abides like space within the equality of samsara and nirvana.

### U02
It also possesses qualities such as the ten powers and the four fearlessnesses.

### U03
For the benefit of others, the sambhogakaya, endowed with the five certainties, appears as the foremost guide of retinues of bodhisattvas who are his regents.

### U04
From the cloud of his loving great compassion, the supreme nirmanakaya pervades the realms of beings without partiality.

### U05
It displays the twelve deeds and, with the rain of the sacred dharma of peace and bliss, moistens the mindstreams of disciples whose potential has truly awakened, and sets them free. With respect in body, speech and mind, I bow to him, the Lion of the Shakyas, chief of the sugatas.

### U06
Accordingly, in the *Sublime Continuum* it is said:

With great compassion the Knower of the World
looks upon the whole world,
and then, without moving from the dharmakaya,
through emanations of many kinds,

### U07
he takes birth after birth,
descends from the Tushita heaven,
enters the womb and is born,
delights in the company of queens,

### U08
renounces the world, performs austerities,
goes to the seat of awakening,
defeats the hosts of Mara,
attains complete awakening,
turns the wheel of dharma,

### U09
and passes into nirvana: he displays these deeds
in realms that are not pure
for as long as existence lasts.

### U10
Second. The supreme Sage is victorious over the four maras: the aggregates, the lord of death, the son of the gods and the afflictions. His scriptures are free of the extremes of permanence and annihilation, and as immeasurable as the expanse of the ocean. This text was condensed from these scriptures, the sutras and the tantras, without the stains of not understanding, misunderstanding and doubt. It was condensed as well from the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga and others, and from the meaning of the profound instructions of the great Dharma Lord Drikungpa. Those instructions deserve to be honored like the crown jewel of the naga king. Just as everything one needs and wishes for comes forth when one prays to the vaidurya, supreme among jewels, so it is a treatise that fulfills every aim, temporary and ultimate. It is the essence, presented as the three—ground, path and fruition—and this is what I shall explain.

### U11
Now, of the four topics—the purpose and the rest—the subject matter is the three: ground, path and fruition.

### U12
The purpose is to understand these three through study and reflection. The ultimate purpose is that, through meditation on their meaning, the fruition becomes manifest. The connection is that each depends on the one before it, and in succession they are connected as means and what arises from the means.
