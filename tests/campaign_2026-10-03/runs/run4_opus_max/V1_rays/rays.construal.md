# rays.construal.md — construal objects, page V1 (rays), U01–U12

Context (from the brief): Ayang Thubten Rinpoche, nyi ma'i 'od zer, commentary on Zhedang Dorje's theg chen bstan pa'i snying po. Page = the commentary's opening: homage (U01–U05, ONE Tibetan period: the only finite verb is 'dud do in U05; U01–U05 are a relative chain headed by shAkya seng ge), citation of rgyud bla ma II.53–56 (U06–U09, one sentence over 15 padas as given), commitment to explain (U10–U12).
Mode: seasoned practitioner, publication (reader edition). Construal written after the restart (2026-10-04 13:59); the dictionary passes were re-run, see runlog.md.

PERIOD SPINE U01–U05 (shared by U01–U05):
  [rang don du] chos sku … bzhugs shing (U01) / … yon tan ldan pa dang (U02) / [gzhan don du] longs sku … gtso bor snang ba dang (U03) / … sprul skus … khyab cing (U04) / mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te … grol bar mdzad pa'i (U05) → bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shAkya seng ge la (head, recipient) → [bdag gis] lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do (main clause).
  Everything before 'mdzad pa'i' is relative to Shakyasimha; the three kayas are the three aspects of him (own benefit: dharmakaya U01–U02; benefit of others: sambhogakaya U03, supreme nirmanakaya U04–U05).
  English plan: several sentences across the units, the connective of each seam kept (shing/dang/cing = "and"; te = sequence/means). Shakyamuni is named only in U05, so U01–U04 refer ahead to him ("his own benefit", "he"); the referent must be unmistakable by U05.

UNIT        U01
FORM        prose; first member of the U01–U05 period; ends in converb shing
GENRE       homage (mchod brjod), describing the dharmakaya
REGISTER    the commentator, homage to Shakyamuni; elevated (homage), unhurried; hon. verb bzhugs
SOURCE      a rang don du chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa / b khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa la / c glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing / d rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa / e spros pa dang bral ba / f nam mkha' ltar srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing
WORDS       rang don = one's own benefit (pair with gzhan don U03; dharmakaya = own benefit, form kayas = others' benefit) · 'pho 'gyur med pa = without transition or change (RY: unchanging) · khams = the element (dhatu, the buddha element of the rgyud bla ma) · rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa = completely pure by nature (natural purity) · glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains · rab tu zhi = wholly stilled/pacified (rab tu = preverb, not "thoroughly") · rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa = not compounded by causes and conditions · spros pa dang bral ba = free of elaboration · srid zhi mnyam pa nyid = the equality of samsara and nirvana (srid zhi: existence and peace) · ngang = state · bzhugs = (hon.) abides, rests
CLAUSES     6  1 chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa — attributive (existential neg.): the dharmakaya has no transition or change
               2 khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa — verb=dag (intr., state) ABS khams; gyis = manner: the element is completely pure by nature; NP in apposition to chos sku
               3 glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi — verb=zhi (intr. invol.) ABS dri ma; perfective/state: the adventitious stains are wholly stilled
               4 rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa — verb='dus byas (negated passive participle), INSTR rgyu rkyen = what would compound it: uncompounded by causes and conditions
               5 spros pa dang bral ba — verb=bral (intr.), dang = "from": free of elaboration
               6 … ngang du bzhugs — verb=bzhugs (intr. vol., hon.) ABS subject = chos sku (the dharmakaya of the one praised); LA-DON ngang du = the state abided in; nam mkha' ltar = manner (like space)
            elided: the person (Shakyasimha, named in U05); no ergative agent anywhere in U01
RELATIONS   rang don du = role/purpose ("for his own benefit", the dharmakaya as the own-benefit kaya) · rang bzhin gyis = manner · la (after rnam par dag pa) = locus ("in which") or the additive connective ("and moreover"): the two purities, natural purity and purity from adventitious stains · zhing = and · rgyu rkyen gyis = means/agent of compounding · dang (bral) = from · ltar = simile · kyi = genitive (state of the equality) · du = locus · shing = and (the chain continues into U02)
NEG/QUANT   'pho 'gyur med pa: no transition, no change (med pa negates both members) · 'dus ma byas: NOT compounded (ma scopes over byas) · bral: privative (free of) · tool NEG 2 = 'gyur med pa, 'dus ma byas (dri ma correctly a noun) · no quantifier
SPINE       no finite verb; bzhugs shing is the first of six coordinated predicates of the relative chain; English: 2 sentences possible (the dharmakaya's attributes; its abiding), "and" kept into U02
CONSTRUAL   For his own benefit [he is] the dharmakaya, which has no transition and no change. It is the element that is completely pure by nature, and in it the adventitious stains are wholly stilled. It is not compounded by causes and conditions. Free of elaboration, like space, it abides in the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana, and …
TERMS       chos sku = dharmakaya · rang don = his own benefit · khams = the element · glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains · 'dus ma byas = uncompounded (not compounded by causes and conditions) · spros pa dang bral ba = free of elaboration · srid zhi mnyam pa nyid = the equality of samsara and nirvana
DOUBTS      Q: subject of bzhugs: the dharmakaya (ABS chos sku, my reading: "his dharmakaya abides") vs the Buddha himself abiding "as the dharmakaya" (role reading; same doctrine, different grammatical subject).
            Q: la after rnam par dag pa: locus ("the element pure by nature, in which the adventitious stains are stilled") vs additive connective ("pure by nature and, besides, with the adventitious stains stilled"); both express the twofold purity.
            Q: nam mkha' ltar: with bzhugs ("abides like space") vs with spros pa dang bral ba ("free of elaboration like space") or with mnyam pa nyid ("equal like space"); taken with the abiding, the space simile covering the whole clause.

UNIT        U05
FORM        prose; last member of the period + the main clause (the only finite verb of U01–U05: 'dud do)
GENRE       homage: the nirmanakaya's deeds, then the act of homage
REGISTER    as U01; the commentator speaks in the first person (elided bdag); hon. mdzad (×2), gshegs
SOURCE      a mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te / b zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud brlan te / c grol bar mdzad pa'i bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shAkya seng ge la / d lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do
WORDS       mdzad pa bcu gnyis = the twelve deeds · bstan = (past of ston) displayed/shows · zhi bde = peace and bliss · dam pa'i chos = the sacred dharma · char = rain (tool split cha + r: fused) · rigs = potential (gotra, the spiritual family/disposition) · yang dag par sad pa = properly awakened · gdul bya = disciples (those to be trained) · rgyud = mindstream (NOT tantra, the tool's first sense) · brlan = moisten (rlan, fut./past stem) · grol bar mdzad = (hon.) liberates, sets free · bde bar gshegs pa = sugata · gtso bo = chief · shAkya seng ge = Lion of the Shakyas (Shakyasimha), the Buddha · gus pa = respect, devotion · 'dud = bow (Hackett: Erg. Obl.) · do = final particle (NOT "two")
CLAUSES     4  1 bstan — verb=ston (tr. vol.) ERG agent = the supreme nirmanakaya (U04) ; LA-DON mdzad pa bcu gnyis su = the form displayed ("as the twelve deeds"); past stem, but inside the relative chain: habitual/general
               2 brlan — verb=rlan (tr.) same ERG agent; INSTR char gyis = means; ABS object = gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud
               3 grol bar mdzad — causative (verbal noun + r + mdzad: "makes free"), hon.; same agent; object = the disciples (elided, from 2)
               4 'dud do — main verb (tr. vol.; Erg. Obl.): ERG agent elided = I (the commentator); OBL recipient = … shAkya seng ge la; manner = lus ngag yid gsum gus pas
RELATIONS   su = role/form · te (after bstan) = sequence/manner (displays the deeds and …) · dam pa'i, chos kyi = genitives (rain of the sacred dharma) · gyis = means (with the rain) · par = manner (properly) · sad pa'i = relative (disciples whose potential is awakened) · rnams kyi = genitive (their mindstreams) · te (after brlan) = sequence (moistens, then frees) · bar = causative complement · mdzad pa'i = relative: the whole chain U01–U05 modifies the head gtso bo shAkya seng ge · rnams kyi = genitive (chief of the sugatas) · la = recipient of the homage · gus pas = manner/means (with respect) · do = final
NEG/QUANT   none (tool NEG 0) · bcu gnyis = twelve: keep · lus ngag yid gsum: summation count "the three", may be absorbed in the three-item list · rnams: plural (disciples; sugatas); "all" not in the Tibetan
SPINE       the period's single finite verb is here. English plan: (1) the deeds + rain + moistening + freeing as one or two sentences with "he"/"it" carried from U04; (2) the homage sentence naming him: "With respect in body, speech and mind I bow to …". No fronted object ("to him I bow").
CONSTRUAL   [His supreme nirmanakaya] displays the twelve deeds, and with the rain of the sacred dharma of peace and bliss it moistens the mindstreams of the disciples whose potential has been properly awakened, and then sets them free. To him who does this, the chief of the sugatas, the Lion of the Shakyas, I bow with respect of body, speech and mind.
TERMS       mdzad pa bcu gnyis = the twelve deeds · dam pa'i chos = the sacred dharma · gdul bya = disciples · rgyud = mindstream · rigs = potential · bde bar gshegs pa = sugata · shAkya seng ge = the Lion of the Shakyas
DOUBTS      Q: zhi bde dam pa'i chos: "the sacred dharma of peace and bliss" (zhi bde + dam pa'i chos, my reading) vs "the dharma of sublime peace and bliss" (zhi bde dam pa as one phrase).
            Q: te after brlan: plain sequence ("moistens … and frees them", my reading) vs means ("by moistening … frees them").
            Q: shAkya seng ge: translated as "the Lion of the Shakyas" (epithet, not "Shakya Senge", which a Drikung reader may take for Guru Rinpoche's form of that name); Alt: Shakyasimha.
