## U07 prose, same register. HUM = gandharva's mind between mother and father; Akshobhya=father; Mamaki=mother. Seeing mutual attachment (phan tshun rjes su chags pa mthong nas, nas=sequence) [he, the gandharva] hates father (la rnam par zhe sdang zhing) and attached to mother (nas) enters through delusion (gti mug pas, cause) like a sentient being. Son who has entered lotus (pad mar zhugs pa'i bu yis, agent), having killed father (nas), attains his position. Having taken mother (ma bzung nas), through supreme bliss of imprints of endless births (pas, cause) de yang bur skye bar byed do (that too causes birth as son). Daughters again are white Acala etc and Vajra Lady of Delusion etc. Sons also, having killed father, go to others (gzhan la bgrod), (and) saying 'this very one is the enemy' kill him. Daughters too, through supreme bliss..., desire.
Q: subject and sense of 'de yang bur skye bar byed do'. Q: referent of de in 'de gsod' (the father / the one called enemy). Q: bu mo are Acalas and ladies (as given). Q: gzhan la bgrod = go to other women.
