# Construal file: Kāṇha, Jewel Garland of Yoga (Toh 1183), I.1, 1.1.98-1.1.109. Audience academic; register throughout: commentary gloss / exposition (scaffold: root words, "means", gloss), neutral-scholarly.

## U01 prose, gloss
CLAUSES: (1) "sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa la": lemma quoted, frame "la ... " = as to. (2) sdom pa ni dbang bskur ba 'o: X = consecration. (3) de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste: its detailed classification is "division". (4) 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no: (it) has the characteristic of what will be explained. (5) de yang ji skad du: lead-in.
RELATIONS: ste = sequence/restatement; 'gyur ba'i = modification (what is to be explained).
CONSTRUAL: As to "the divisions of saṃvara, and so on": saṃvara is consecration. Its detailed classification is the "division"; that is, it is characterized by being something that will be explained. And [of this] it is said:
Q: de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba = "its detailed classification is [what is called] division" (kept); alt: "its subdivision is 'division'". Q: 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can: "is defined by what will be explained [later]" (kept) vs "has the definition (to be given) in the exposition". ('de yang' rendered as "And of this").
CITATION: lemma sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa is the root pada (Hevajra); not run (a lemma, not a quotation to identify).

## U02 verse (citation), 4 padas 9/9/9/9 (+ frame "ces gsungs pa yin no")
CLAUSES: sems can rnams la phan pa'i phyir (purpose, la-don + phyir) ... dbang bskur ba ni bzhi ru bsgrags (consecration, topic; fourfold, ru = terminative; verb bsgrags = proclaimed, pass.). 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur bas (agent/means 'di yis = by this; bkrus shing = washed, sequence; bas = cause) de phyir (therefore) dbang bskur zhes bya r brjod (is called "consecration").
RELATIONS: phyir purpose; bas cause; shing sequence; de phyir consequence; zhes bya r brjod = naming.
SPINE: 2 sentences: (1) for beings' benefit consecration is proclaimed as fourfold. (2) because one is washed and consecrated by this, it is therefore called consecration. Frame: "ces gsungs pa yin no" = "this is what was spoken."
FORM: tantra verse; honorific gsungs: spoken by the Bhagavān (elided).
CONSTRUAL: For the sake of benefiting beings, consecration is proclaimed as fourfold. Because by this one is washed and consecrated, for that reason it is called "consecration." So it was taught.
CITATION: dm.py identify: verbatim only in Toh 1183 itself (26 syll.) plus later works quoting this commentary; canonical source not located. Source: not located.
VERBATIM U08 quotes the pada as 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba (not bskur bas): a reading difference between this verse and the lemma in U08.
Q: bkrus shing dbang bskur bas: "washed and consecrated" (kept, both participles under one bas) vs "consecrated after washing". Q: "bzhi ru bsgrags": "fourfold" (ru terminative) vs "as four".

## U04 root padas (7/7) + gloss frame
CLAUSES: quote (slob dpon gsang ba shes rab dang / de ltar de bzhin bzhi pa yang) zhes bya ba la sogs pas ... 'chad par 'gyur ba yin no: "will be explained by [the lines] beginning with ...". 
SPINE: one sentence. Subject elided: the four consecrations / the classification (from U03).
CONSTRUAL: The classification of the four consecrations will be explained by the lines beginning with: "Master, secret, wisdom, and so too, likewise, the fourth."
CITATION: dm.py identify: verbatim 14 syll. in Toh 1190 (quotes it) and 1183; Toh 418 (Hevajra, D 17a-12/13) has the same two padas with a different second pada: "slob dpon gsang ba shes rab dang / bzhi bde yang de bzhin no". The commentator's second pada (de ltar de bzhin bzhi pa yang) is a variant; U08 comments on "de ltar de bzhin yang".
Q: de ltar de bzhin: "thus, likewise" (both kept, glossed in U08 as de = wisdom-gnosis and de bzhin = suchness) vs "just so". 

## U05 prose (exposition), 9 sentences
S1 "rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud 'dir ni ... slob dpon te ... sdom pa yin pas so": topic 'dir ni = in this yoginī tantra; slob dpon [is so called/ is X] because (phyir) one fully practises (kun du spyod pa) what is far (ring du gyur pa) from (las) sinful, non-virtuous things; de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas = because that very [practice] is its saṃvara. ācārya etymology (ācāra). 
S2 "slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba'i ngo bo gang ... zhes bya ba yin gyi,": the essence of consecration by the master is what is called "completely accomplishing the very own-nature of the four joys through four moments from the karmamudrā"; yin gyi = "is X; [it is not Y]" 
   Y: "yang bya ba'i rgyud la sogs pa dang thun mong du gyur pa'i phyir mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba thob pas slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o": that, because it (the master consecration) is shared with Kriyā tantras etc., one who has got the irreversible consecration is [thereby] consecrated by the master's consecration and so consecration is not [further] to be done. NEG: mi ldog pa (irreversible), mi bya'o (not to be done), 
S3 "de lta bas na ... zhes bya ba'i tshig 'di ni ci yang ma yin no": therefore this statement ["One becomes endowed also with the fortune for explaining and hearing all yoga and yoginī tantras"] is nothing at all. NEG ma yin.
S4 "de lta bas na dang por re zhig ...dbang bskur bar bya'o": therefore, first, in order to have mastery (dbang bar bya ba'i phyir) in hearing and meditating on yoginī tantras such as Hevajra, one is to be consecrated by the master's consecration itself.
S5 "yang de ni mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du ... nye bar mtshon pa yin par rig par bya'o": moreover, it is to be understood that it is indicated (nye bar mtshon) by the characteristics of master, secret, wisdom, fourth, as in [per] the realization (mngon par rtogs pa).
CONSTRUAL (full): In this yoginī tantra, [one is] a master (ācārya) because one practises fully what is far from sinful, non-virtuous things; for that very [practice] is its saṃvara. The essence of the consecration the master gives is what is called "the full accomplishment of the very own-nature of the four joys through four moments, from the karmamudrā." It is not [the case] that, because [the master's consecration] is shared with the Kriyā tantras and so on, one who has obtained the irreversible consecration is consecrated by the master's consecration and so is not [further] to be consecrated. Therefore, the statement "So one also becomes endowed with the fortune to explain, hear, and so on, all the yoga and yoginī tantras" is of no account. Therefore, first, in order to be empowered to hear, meditate on, and so on, the yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, one should be consecrated by the master's consecration itself. Moreover, you should understand that it is indicated by the characteristics of the master, the secret, wisdom and the fourth, as in the realization (abhisamaya).
Q1: "slob dpon te, de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas so": (kept) that very [practice] is the master's saṃvara; alt: "because that very [consecration] is its saṃvara"; also whether "te" = "that is, the master" vs. continuing 'is the master because'. 
Q2: "yin gyi ... mi bya'o": see above; alt: "...is not to be done [with this common consecration]" (the common consecration is not what is meant); the sense of "dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o" (to the consecrated, not to be done / for consecration, not to be done) uncertain.
Q3: mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du: "as in the realization (abhisamaya)" vs the title/text Abhisamaya; kept "as in the abhisamaya".
Q4: dgyes pa'i rdo rje = Hevajra (Tibetan of the name; kept Skt. name).
Q5: de (S5) = the consecration (master's) (kept) vs. the fourfold consecration set.

## U06 prose (exposition/instruction), 3 sentences
S1: de lta bu'i slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba thob pa'i dbang po rtul po rnams la ni: to those of dull faculties who have received such a master's consecration; mos pas nges par bzung nas: having grasped it firmly through conviction [elided agent: the guru / the disciples]; las kyi phyag rgya dang spros pa'i phyag rgya bsgom pa bstan par bya ba yin no: meditation on karmamudrā and the mudrā of elaboration is to be taught (yin no, obligation).
S2: de bzhin du rdzogs pa'i rim pa'i phyogs la yang: likewise, on the completion-stage side too; gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis: by the guru's instruction itself, the jewel dwelling in secret; skad cig ma bzhi'i rang bzhin dga' ba bzhi po nyams su myong ba de ni: experiencing the four joys whose nature is the four moments; gsang ba'i dbang bskur ba zhes bya bar brjod de: is called "secret consecration"; ... bstan par bya ba ma yin pa'i phyir ro: because it is not to be taught to yogins engaged in practising elaboration (spros pa bsgom pa la sbyor ba).
S3: 'dir dbang bskur ba'i dbang po 'bring po rnams la ni dam tshig gi phyag rgya bsgom pa nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o: here (in this tantra? at this point), to those of middling faculties [for consecration], meditation on the samayamudrā is to be taught and explained.
NEG: ma yin pa. 
Q1: "spros pa'i phyag rgya" = mudrā of elaboration (kept); "spros pa bsgom pa la sbyor ba'i rnal 'byor pa" = yogins engaged in meditation on elaboration. Q2: "gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu" in apposition to the instruction (kept) vs the jewel as the object experienced (secret place). Q3: "ro" after phyir = the closing particle "ro" (not "taste"). Q4: the agent of "bzung" elided (the disciples; kept as passive "after [it] has been firmly grasped"). Q5: dbang bskur ba'i dbang po 'bring po: "those of middling faculties [among those to be consecrated]" (kept).

## U07 prose (exposition), 4 sentences
S1: de bzhin du khyad par can gyi ye shes ni shes rab ste: likewise, the distinguished gnosis is wisdom; chos thams cad sems tsam nyid du shes pa 'o: [namely] knowing all phenomena as mind-only. 
S2: de rab tu bsgrub pa'i phyir dbang bskur ba ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba 'o: the consecration for the sake of accomplishing that is the wisdom-gnosis consecration.
S3: yang de ni ... shes rab ye shes yin par rig par bya'o: one should understand wisdom-gnosis as: that which, through the guru's instruction, indicates correctly the outer mudrā and the moments, in the form-bearing jewel of the heart (gzugs can snying gi nor bu) that gathers the essence of the three consciousnesses (dependent, imagined, transformed).
S4: dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab rnams la ni sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin gyis chos kyi phyag rgya nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o: by that consecration, for those of superior faculties, the dharmamudrā is to be taught and explained by means of the illusion-like samādhi.
NEG: none real (sgyu ma lta bu = simile).
CONSTRUAL: as drafted.
Q1: gzugs can snying gi nor bur: "form-bearing jewel of the heart" (kept) vs "the jewel of the heart that has a form". Q2: yongs su gyur pa: transformed vs perfected. Q3: khyad par can gyi ye shes: "distinguished gnosis" vs "gnosis that is special". Q4: structure of S3: "gang ... mtshon pa ni shes rab ye shes" (that which indicates ... is wisdom-gnosis; kept) vs "indicated". Q5: dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab: "those of superior faculties [who have received] that consecration" (kept).

## U08 prose (gloss; lemmas "de ltar de bzhin yang" and "'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba")
S1: de bzhin du bzhi pa yang yin te: likewise, the fourth also exists; de ltar de bzhin yang zhes bya ba ste: [as] "thus likewise too".
S2: de zhes pa ni shes rab ye shes so: "that" means wisdom-gnosis.
S3: de bzhin zhes bya ba ni de bzhin nyid de: "likewise/thus" means suchness; yang dag pa'i mtha' dang chos kyi dbyings zhes bya ba rnams la don gyi khyad par yod pa ma yin no: [for] "the true limit" and "dharmadhātu" have no difference in meaning.
S4: dbang bskur ba gang gis de'i ngo bo nyid mthong ba'am rab tu bsgrub pa ste, de bzhin zhes bya ba'i sgras brjod par bya ba yin no: the consecration through which one sees its very essence or accomplishes it is what the word "likewise (suchness)" expresses.
S5: yang zhes bya ba'i sgra ni ... sbyin par bya'o zhes bya ba'i don to: "too" means "it should also be given after the wisdom-gnosis consecration."
S6: yang de ni ... [the fourth consecration]: the external mudrā and the perfection of the moment (characterised by not being delimited object by object through the guru's instruction), apprehended by the yoga of apprehending without apprehending: this is to be called the fourth consecration; the instruction that is to be called "fourth consecration" does not become the fourth consecration merely by being taught.
S7: gzhan du na ...: otherwise, if the Blessed One, by the lines beginning "washed by this and consecrated", intends consecration here as the washing away of stains, how could it come about by merely teaching an instruction?
NEG: yod pa ma yin no (no difference); ma yin pa (not delimited); med par dmigs pa; ma yin no (does not become); dri ma bkru.
Q1: "de zhes pa ni": the pun (de = that / thus) lost: rendered "that" with Wylie. Q2: de bzhin: "likewise" in root, "suchness" in gloss (the gloss reads it as tathatā). Q3: dmigs pa med par dmigs pa'i rnal 'byor: "yoga of apprehending without apprehending" (kept) vs "apprehending the object-less". Q4: S6 syntax: "gang ... can" relative attaches to skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa (kept). Q5: the lemma 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba vs U02 "dbang bskur bas": a reading difference of the root text; rendered as lemma. Q6: the last sentence: "yin par 'gyur" closing question, a rhetorical question.
CITATION: lemma = U02 verse (source not located).

## U09 prose (debate/exposition), objection and reply
S1: gal te bzhi pa'i bdag nyid can gyi de kho na nyid bstan na der ji ltar mi 'gyur zhe na de ni bden mod: if [it is asked]: "if reality, which has the fourth's nature, is taught, how would it not become [the fourth consecration] there?": that is true.
S2: 'on kyang de kho na nyid ni ngag gi spyod yul ma yin pa'i phyir brjod par nus pa ma yin la, thos pas bsgrub par yang mi nus so: but because reality is not the domain of speech, it cannot be expressed, nor can it be accomplished by hearing.
S3: de yang 'di skad du: lead-in.
NEG: mi 'gyur (rhetorical question), ma yin, ma yin, mi nus.
Q1: "bzhi pa'i bdag nyid can": "which has the nature of the fourth" (kept) vs "whose self is the fourth". Q2: "der": there (in that case; kept) vs "to it". Q3: 'de yang' rendered "of this too".
RELATIONS: na = if; mod = concession (true, but); 'on kyang = but; phyir = because; la = and.

## U10 verse (citation), 7×4 (last pada 8)
FORM verse 4 padas. Frame: introduced by U09 "'di skad du".
CLAUSES: nang na gsal ba de nyid ni: topic, "that very thing clear within"; ngag las mngon sum thos mthong yin: copula yin; ngag las = from speech; mngon sum = directly; thos mthong = heard and seen; 'on kyang = however; sgra la rnam rtog gi gzugs brnyan 'byung bar 'gyur ba yin: in sound a reflection of conceptual thought comes to arise.
SPINE: 2 sentences.
CONSTRUAL: That very reality which is clear within is, [as conveyed] from speech, directly heard and seen. However, in the sound there comes to arise a reflection of conceptual thought.
Q1: "ngag las mngon sum thos mthong yin": kept "is, from speech, directly heard and seen" vs "is direct perception (mngon sum), heard and seen, [rather than ?] from speech" / "from speech comes only seeing and hearing". Q2: nang na gsal ba = "clear within" vs "clear in the interior".
CITATION: identify: verbatim 28 syll. only in Toh 1183 (and the later works quoting it). Source not located.

## U11 verse (citation) 7×4 + frame "zhes bstan pa yin no"
CLAUSES: rang gi rnam rtog de de rgyas / brtags pa'i de nyid bsgoms pa yis: by meditating on the reality imputed [by] (one's own conceptual thought expanding, this and that) ...; rgyud la rnam rtog 'khrug 'gyur na: if conceptual thoughts are stirred up in the mind-stream; de nyid rtog par ji ltar 'gyur: how could reality be [discerned].
RELATIONS: yis = means; na = condition; ji ltar 'gyur = rhetorical question.
CONSTRUAL: If, by meditating on a reality imputed through the expansion of one's own conceptual thoughts, this and that, conceptual thoughts are stirred up in the mind-stream, how could reality be discerned? This is what is taught.
Q1: rang gi rnam rtog de de rgyas brtags pa'i: "imputed by expanding one's own thoughts, this and that" (kept) vs "(reality) that is imputed, as one's own thoughts, that and that expand". Q2: rtog par: "discerned" vs "realized" / "conceived". Q3: rgyud = mind-stream (kept) vs tantra (rejected: rgyud la ... 'khrug).
CITATION: identify: verbatim only in Toh 1183. Source not located.

## U12 prose (debate), objection and reply
S1: gal te dbang bskur bas de kho na nyid mngon sum du rtogs par 'gyur zhing dri ma ma lus pa dag par 'gyur na, de ltar na de'i skad cig nyid la phyag rgya chen po'i dngos grub ci'i phyir 'grub par mi 'gyur zhe na: if [it is asked]: "if through consecration reality comes to be directly realized and all stains come to be purified, then why would the siddhi of mahāmudrā not be accomplished in that very moment?"
S2: dbang po rnon po brtson 'grus 'bar ba rnams kyis 'grub pa nyid yin no: it is indeed accomplished by those of sharp faculties whose diligence blazes. (agent kyis)
S3: gang zhig sus kyang mi 'dod cing ma mthong bas de med par 'gyur ram ci: would something not exist because no one wants it and no one has seen it?
S4: de yang ji skad du: lead-in.
NEG: ma lus pa (all, "without remainder"), mi 'gyur (rhetorical), mi 'dod, ma mthong, med par.
Q1: 'dod: wants vs accepts. Q2: "gang zhig ... de med par 'gyur ram ci": "something that no one wants or has seen — does it thereby not exist?" (kept as question: "does it cease to exist because ...") Q3: 'grub pa nyid yin no: "it is indeed accomplished" (nyid = emphatic; the answer is a flat affirmation for the sharp) vs "only by those of sharp faculties" (rejected: no restrictive marker).
CITATION: lead-in only.
