## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U01 "pure by the nature of the expanse": khams read as the genitive owner of rang bzhin rather than in apposition with rang bzhin gyis as an adverb ("the element, pure by nature"). Cause: grammar misread.
2. U06 'jig rten mkhyen made a finite verb ("he knows the world") instead of the epithet "Knower of the World", which also moves "great compassion" from the looking to the knowing. Cause: construal fork (recorded, wrong branch chosen).
3. U05 "whose family has awakened": yang dag par dropped and rigs taken as "family" instead of potential/gotra. Cause: omission under compression / dictionary sense.
4. U10 "prays to lapis lazuli": a gem sense where the wish-granting vaidurya is meant. Cause: term choice.
5. U01 rab tu ("completely") cut from the pacified stains by the checker as a calque, losing the doctrinal "fully". Cause: skill rule misapplied.
