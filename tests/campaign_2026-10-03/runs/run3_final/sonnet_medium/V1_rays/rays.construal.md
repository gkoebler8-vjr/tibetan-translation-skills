# rays construal (U01-U12). Assumptions: seasoned practitioner, publication, EGS-style reader edition, Sanskrit anglicized, no brackets/Wylie in body, no contractions.

UNIT U01-U05  FORM prose (one homage period)  GENRE liturgical homage (commentator's own)  REGISTER homage: elevated only as the Tibetan is
SPINE  One sentence. Finite verb only at end of U05: 'dud do (I bow). Everything before is a chain of relative/converb clauses all modifying the addressee Shakyamuni (named last, U05). Subject of bowing = commentator "I" (elided).
U01  clauses: [rang don du chos sku ...] 'pho 'gyur med pa (NEG: unchanging) / khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa (pure by nature of the khams) / la = converb "and"(or locative) / glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing (stains pacified; zhing=and) / rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa (NEG: not compounded; gyis = agent) / spros pa dang bral ba (free of elaboration) / nam mkha' ltar (like space) / srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing (verb bzhugs hon., abides; du = locus; shing = and)
  RELATIONS gyis=by/in virtue of nature; la=converb; zhing/ shing=and; ltar=simile; du=locus. TERMS dharmakaya, expanse, samsara, nirvana.
  Q: nam mkha' ltar modifies "free of elaboration" or "sameness"? Kept as modifying the abiding (adverbial, "like space"). Q: khams rang bzhin gyis = "pure by the nature of the expanse" vs "naturally pure in its element/nature (rang bzhin)"; chosen: by the nature of the expanse. Q: la after dag pa: converb vs locative ("in which the stains..."); chosen converb/absolute.
U02  one clause: [he is] endowed (ldan pa) with qualities such as (la sogs pa'i) the ten powers and four fearlessnesses; dang = and, continues chain. NEG inside mi 'jigs pa (fearlessness).
U03  gzhan don du = for others' benefit; longs sku nges pa lnga ldan = [as] sambhogakaya endowed with the five certainties; rgyal tshab sems dpa'i 'khor rnams kyi = of the retinues of the regent bodhisattvas; 'dren pa'i gtso bo = chief guide (gtso bo, guide); r snang ba = appears as; dang = and.
  Q: rgyal tshab sems dpa' = "regent bodhisattvas" (heirs of the victor) vs "regent(s) and bodhisattvas". Chosen the first. Five certainties not listed.
U04  brtse ba'i thugs rje chen po'i sprin las = from the cloud of loving great compassion (las = source); mchog gi sprul skus = by/through the supreme nirmanakaya (s=gis agent/means); sems can gyi khams la = in/over the realm of beings; phyogs ris med par = without partiality (NEG: med); khyab cing = pervades, and. Finite: khyab.
  Q: sems can gyi khams "realm of beings" vs "dispositions of beings"; chosen realm. Source of sprul sku = cloud (las) kept as "arising from".
U05  mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te = shows the twelve deeds (su=as, te=and); zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis = by the rain of the sublime peaceful dharma (gyis=means); rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud brlan te = moistens the mindstreams of disciples whose family has rightly awakened (rnams plural); grol bar mdzad pa'i = and liberates (honorific mdzad, causative purposive la-don r); bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shakya seng ge la = to Shakya Senge, chief of the sugatas (la=recipient); lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do = bow with body, speech and mind, with devotion (gus pas = manner); 'dud do = I bow, hon-neutral, speaker first person.
  Q: rigs = gotra "family" vs "type"; kept family. Q: shakya seng ge rendered Shakyamuni. 
  ENGLISH SPINE: "He who ... who ... who ... who ... shows ... moistens ... and liberates — to him, Shakyamuni, chief of the sugatas, I bow ...". Object-first resumption in a homage. Q for editor: head-first alternative.

UNIT U06-U09  FORM verse 7x4, 7x4, 7x4, 7x3 + frame; CITATION from Uttaratantra (rgyud bla ma) ; source dm.py identify: Toh 4024 (Tengyur) BO_T06_D4024:64b-23, Toh 4025 (vyakhya) also; verbatim NO as quoted (50-syl run); lacks canonical pada bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang (the Kalapavatara Toh 4464 hit also shows it).
SPINE  One sentence over 15 padas. Finite verb ston (shows) in U09 pada 3; agent = the Buddha (elided; Shakyamuni, antecedent). Padas 1-3: mkhyen (knows; Erg. thugs rje chen pos = out of/with great compassion; 'jig rten = world, object), gzigs nas (having seen all the world; nas = sequence), ma g.yos par (NEG: without stirring from dharmakaya; las = source/locus; par = manner converb), sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis (by emanated natures of manifold kind; kyis = means). Padas 5-15: twelve deeds as a list, each joined by dang, all objects of ston: skye ba mngon par skye ba / dga' ldan gnas nas 'pho ba / lhums su 'jug dang bltams pa (two items one pada) / btsun mo'i 'khor dgyes rol ba / nges 'byung dka' ba spyod pa (two items) / byang chub snying por gshegs pa / bdud sde 'joms / rdzogs par byang chub (split across padas: 'rdzogs par ni' pada 3 of stanza 3, 'byang chub' pada 4) / chos kyi 'khor lo [skor ba elided: turning] / mya ngan 'das par gshegs mdzad rnams (rnams = plural of the deeds) / yongs su ma dag zhing rnams su (locus; NEG ma dag) / srid pa ji srid gnas par (as long as existence lasts) ston.
  VERSE-NOTES: ni after nas and after rdzogs par = filler/topic; rnams = plural marker of deeds, not emphatic; 'chos kyi 'khor lo dang' lacks verb (supplied: turning).
  Count: 12 deeds present in the Tibetan as given (item 1 skye ba mngon par skye ba replaces/duplicates; canonical has bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa).
  Q: pada skye ba mngon par skye ba: probably a corrupt/variant reading (canonical mngon par dga' ldan ...); rendered "taking manifest birth"; bltams pa = being born. Q: yongs su ma dag = "wholly impure" vs "not wholly pure". Q: 'jig rten mkhyen "knows the world" (finite, with Erg. subject) vs epithet "world-knower"; finite chosen. Q: "he" supplied for the Buddha. Order 1-2-3-4 kept; U09 pada 1-3 reworked only for "he shows these deeds" placement (verb brought forward into pada 1 line, order 3-1-2 inside the final sentence portion). 
  FRAME: de yang rgyud bla ma las = "In the Uttaratantra it is said:" (de yang absorbed into formula); ces gsungs so = closing frame "Thus it is said." (gsungs hon. quotation verb).
  Mode: citation, rising, 3-5 beats.

UNIT U10 prose, exposition, one commitment-to-explain period. gnyis pa ni = "The second part" (sa bcad; Q).
  Spine: head noun phrase: [bstan bcos ... snying po] object of bshad par bya'o (I will explain; 1st person future). Everything before is attributive.
  A. gsung rab (words) of the supreme sage (thub pa mchog) who has triumphed over (las rgyal ba) the four maras (aggregates phung po, lord of death 'chi bdag, son of the gods lha'i bu, afflictions nyon mongs pa; ste = namely) ; gsung rab free of (bral ba) the extremes of permanence and annihilation (rtag chad), immeasurable (dpag tu med pa; NEG) like the expanse of the ocean.
  B. las (from) A, and from among (nang nas) [sutras and tantras (rnams), the treatises of Nagarjuna and Asanga etc. (la sogs pa), and the profound meaning of the pith instructions of chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po], all worthy of veneration (bkur 'os pa) like the crown jewel of a naga king (klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar);
  C. [this text 'di] gathered (bsdus pa) without the stain (dri ma med par) of non-understanding (ma rtogs pa), wrong understanding (log rtog), doubt (the tshom) [NEG: ma, med]
  D. like (bzhin) when (na) one makes request/prays to (gsol ba btab) lapis lazuli, supreme of jewels, all needs and wishes (dgos 'dod thams cad) arise ('byung), [so] the treatise accomplishes ('grub par byed pa) all aims (don thams cad) temporary and ultimate (gnas skabs dang mthar thug).
  E. snying po: the essence taught (bstan pa) as three (gsum du): ground, path, fruit.
  Q: head noun fork: "this treatise, the essence taught as ground, path and fruit" (apposition, chosen) vs "the essence of the treatise taught in three". Q: chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po = Jigten Sumgön? (not stated; rendered by title). Q: gnyis pa ni = "The second part" (commitment to explain). Q: 'di refers to Zhedang Dorje's root text (the "essence"); rendered "this treatise". Q: bsdus pa = gathered/condensed. Q: las (from A) and nang nas (from B): both sources, kept both.
  ENGLISH SPINE: S1 A as description of the sage's words (supply copula "are"); S2 B+C: "From these words, from among ... this treatise has been gathered ..."; S3 D simile; S4 "I will explain it, the essence taught as ground, path, fruit."
