# sutra — construal (Toh 147, ch. 2; tathagata activities 21–23: undiminished aspiration, diligence, mindfulness)
Speaker throughout: the Buddha addressing a bodhisattva ("noble son"). Register: sutra exposition, plain, formulaic. Verse = recapitulation (de la 'di skad ces bya'o), not a citation of another work.

## U01
FORM prose; 1 statement + 2 "gang zhe na" Q&A + 8 parallel items (enumeration, one sentence each). GENRE exposition.
CLAUSES 1 rigs kyi bu (VOC) gzhan yang (moreover) | de bzhin gshegs pa'i 'dun pa nyams pa med do — intr. existential neg: the T's aspiration has no decline.
 2 de la 'dun pa gang zhe na — rhetorical Q: "what is aspiration here?" | 'di lta ste dge ba'i chos la 'dun pa'o — namely, aspiration for virtuous qualities (la = object of aspiration).
 3 de yang gang zhe na — "and what is that?" 
 4–11 X la ('dun pa) nyams pa med do ×8: (a) byams pa chen po (T's 'dun pa named) (b) snying rje chen po (T's named) (c) chos ston pa (d) sems can 'dul ba (e) sems can yongs su smin par bya ba (f) rab tu dben pa (g) sems can byang chub yang dag par 'dzin du 'jug pa — causative: lead beings to take up awakening properly (h) dkon mchog gsum gyi rigs rgyun mi gcod pa — not cutting the lineage/continuity of the Three Jewels (double neg: aspiration to keep it unbroken).
RELATIONS la = goal of aspiration (aspiration FOR X) throughout; de la = here/in that context; de yang = and that.
NEG 10 counted: 9 × med do (no decline) + mi gcod (inner neg in item h). All surface.
CONSTRUAL Noble son, moreover, the Tathagata's aspiration never declines. What is aspiration here? It is aspiration for virtuous qualities. And what is that? The Tathagata's aspiration for great love never declines. His aspiration for great compassion never declines. His aspiration to teach the Dharma never declines; to guide beings; to bring beings to maturity; for solitude; to lead beings to take up awakening properly; to keep the line of the Three Jewels unbroken — none declines.
DOUBTS Q: de bzhin gshegs pa'i named only in (a)(b); (c)–(h) bare 'dun pa — same subject assumed. Q: rab tu dben pa — "complete seclusion"; rab tu read as Sanskrit preverb (pra-), rendered "solitude". Q: rigs rgyun — "lineage" vs "continuity"; rendered "line".

## U02
FORM prose; 3 sentences + framing. GENRE exposition → framing.
CLAUSES 1 de bzhin gshegs pa ni 'dun pas 'gro ba ma yin gyi — the T does not go/act by 'dun pa (pas = means/cause; 'dun pas 'gro ba = chanda-gati, acting from desire/bias, cf. U04 p1) | gyi = adversative "not X but". 
 2 de bzhin gshegs pa'i 'dun pa ni ye shes sngon du 'gro ba nyid de — the T's aspiration is precisely preceded by wisdom (nyid emphatic; de semi-final → therefore).
 3 de'i phyir ... 'dun pa nyams pa med ces bya'o — therefore it is called "the T's aspiration never declines".
 4 sems can thams cad la bla na med pa'i 'dun pa yongs su rdzogs par bya ba'i phyir chos kyang ston te — in order to fulfill (bring to completion) unsurpassed aspiration in/for all beings (la = locus/beneficiary), he also teaches the Dharma (kyang = also).
 5 'di ni de bzhin gshegs pa'i de bzhin gshegs pa'i 'phrin las nyi shu rtsa gcig pa'o — this is the T's twenty-first tathagata activity.
 6 de la 'di skad ces bya'o — on this it is said:
RELATIONS pas cause/means; gyi adversative; de semi-final; de'i phyir = therefore; phyir purpose; kyang also; te → seam.
NEG ma yin (cl.1), med (cl.3), bla na med (unsurpassed). 
CONSTRUAL The Tathagata does not act out of desire; rather, his aspiration is preceded by wisdom. That is why the Tathagata's aspiration is said never to decline. He also teaches the Dharma so that all beings may bring unsurpassed aspiration to completion. This is the twenty-first tathagata activity of the Tathagata. On this it is said:
DOUBTS Q: 'dun pas 'gro ba — "act out of desire/partiality" (chanda-gati) vs "move by aspiration"; same word as the topic 'dun pa; play on 'gro ba. Q: sems can thams cad la — "in all beings" (their aspiration fulfilled) vs "toward all beings" (his own aspiration fulfilled). Took beings' aspiration. Q: de bzhin gshegs pa'i ×2: "tathagata activity of the Tathagata".

## U03 (verse 15×4; recap of U01–02)
p1 rgyal ba'i 'dun pa rtag tu dge ba'i chos kyi tshul la 'dod cing dga' — the Victor's aspiration always wishes for and delights in the way of virtuous qualities ('dod cing dga': two verbs, cing coordination; subject = 'dun pa, metonymy for the Victor).
p2 sems can rab tu thar bar bya phyir byams dang snying rje'i chos kyang sbyin — to liberate beings (phyir purpose) he also gives (sbyin) the Dharma of love and compassion (kyang also). Agent elided (the Victor).
p3 sems can yongs smin bya dang nags tshal dga' la mos pa nyams pa med — his eagerness (mos pa) to bring beings to maturity and for delight in the forest never declines. dang coordinates two objects of mos pa (la applies to both). nags tshal dga' = delight in forest = solitude (U01 f).
p4 rab tu mang po byang chub 'dzin 'jug dkon mchog gsum gyi rgyun mi gcod — he leads very many to take up awakening, and does not cut the stream of the Three Jewels. Two predicates, asyndetic.
FILLER: rab tu (p2) intensifier of thar ba (pra-mokṣa) — "liberate". kyang (p2) = also.
NOTE U01 items (c) teach, (d) guide not separately in verse; verse = p2 giving Dharma.
DOUBTS Q: p3 mos pa = eagerness/interest (adhimukti?), rendered "eagerness". Q: p1 subject — the aspiration (metonymy) vs the Victor. Q: p4 rab tu mang po — "very many [beings]" (object of 'dzin 'jug).

## U04 (verse 15×4)
p1 'dod dang zhe sdang dang ni 'jigs dang gti mug gis ni 'gro ma yin — he does not go (act) through desire, hatred, fear or delusion (the four wrong courses; gis = cause/means; ni filler ×2).
p2 khyad par gyur nas mkhas de shes pas byas pa'i chos kun rtogs par 'gyur — having become distinguished (nas sequence), that wise one, through knowledge (pas = means), comes to realize all conditioned things (byas pa'i chos = made/compounded dharmas).
p3 rgyal ba sems can 'dun bral le lo can dag mthong nas 'dun la sbyor — the Victor, having seen (nas) beings who lack aspiration and are lazy, engages them in aspiration (sbyor = join, apply; la = goal).
p4 gang gis 'gro ba 'dun pa'i las ni mtshungs pa med pa'i shes pas rig — the one by whom (gang gis) beings' actions of aspiration are known with peerless knowledge (pas means). Relative without explicit correlative: = he who knows / he knows.
NEG ma yin (p1), bral (p3, "without"), mtshungs pa med (peerless).
DOUBTS Q: p2 "shes pas byas pa'i chos" — alt "all things done/made by knowledge" (shes pas as agent of byas pa) vs "through knowledge realizes all conditioned things". Took the latter. Q: p2 khyad par gyur nas — "having become distinguished/exceptional". Q: p4 'dun pa'i las — "the work of aspiration" vs "beings' aspirations and karma". Q: p4 gang gis — relative "he by whom" read as "he who"; alt "by which [knowledge]".

## U05
FORM prose; 5 sentences. GENRE exposition with narrative illustration.
CLAUSES 1 rigs kyi bu (VOC) gzhan yang ... brtson 'grus nyams pa med do — the T's diligence never declines.
 2 de la de bzhin gshegs pa'i brtson 'grus ni 'di yin te 'di lta ste — here the T's diligence is this, namely: (a) 'dul ba'i sems can rnams yal bar mi 'dor ba'i brtson 'grus — diligence that never neglects (lets drop) beings to be guided; dang (b) chos nyan pa dag la brnyas pa med pa'i brtson 'grus so — diligence with no contempt for those who listen to the Dharma.
 3 gal te ... rnyed na (condition): if the T (pas ERG of rnyed) finds a vessel of the Dharma, a listener (gang = relative: any listener) who would not tire of listening to the Dharma even for an eon (bskal par yang; skyo bar mi 'gyur, pas = nyan pas cause: tire from listening) | de bzhin gshegs pa yang chos ston — then the T too teaches the Dharma (yang = for his part/also).
 4 kha zas dang bral ba'i rgyud du gyur kyang (concession) bskal par yang mi ldang ngo — even if his body (rgyud = person/system) were deprived of food, he would not rise [from his seat] even for an eon.
 5 de bzhin gshegs pa ni sems can la lta bas (cause: out of regard for beings) | tha na gang na sangs rgyas kyis 'dul ba'i sems can gcig gi phyir yang (even for the sake of a single being to be guided by a buddha, wherever he may be) | sangs rgyas kyi zhing gang ga'i klung gi bye ma snyed 'da' zhing bzhud de — passes through and travels across buddha fields as numerous as the sands of the Ganges | de bzhin gshegs pa la sku mnyel ba'am gsung mnyel ba'am thugs mnyel ba med do — and the T has no fatigue of body, speech or mind (hon.). de = semi-final, sequence/"and yet".
RELATIONS na condition; kyang concession; pas cause (lta bas); phyir purpose ("for the sake of"); tha na ... yang = "even"; zhing coordination ('da' + bzhud); de seam.
NEG mi 'dor, brnyas pa med, mi 'gyur (skyo), mi ldang, mnyel ba med ×1 (scope over all three, 'am = or).
CONSTRUAL Noble son, moreover, the Tathagata's diligence never declines. Here it is this: diligence that never neglects beings who are to be guided, and diligence that holds no contempt for those who listen to the Dharma. If the Tathagata finds a vessel for the Dharma, a listener who would not tire of hearing the Dharma even for an eon, the Tathagata will teach the Dharma to him. Even if he had to go without food, he would not rise from his seat for an eon. Out of regard for beings, the Tathagata will cross buddha fields as many as the grains of sand in the Ganges, even for the sake of one single being to be guided by a buddha, wherever that being may be. Yet he feels no fatigue in body, speech or mind.
DOUBTS Q: bskal par yang chos nyan pas skyo bar mi 'gyur — "even for an eon" attaches to the listener's not tiring (taken) vs to the teaching. Q: kha zas dang bral ba'i rgyud — rgyud = body/person ("his frame"); "even without food". Q: "mi ldang" — "would not rise [from his seat]" supplies "from his seat" (ldang = get up). Q: gang na — "wherever" (taken) vs "where there is". Q: de bzhin gshegs pa yang chos ston — yang "too/then"; present tense generic rendered "will teach".

## U06
FORM prose; 1 period + numbering + framing. GENRE exposition.
CLAUSES 1 de bzhin gshegs pa ni (a) sems shin tu sbyangs pa (b) brtson 'grus brtsams pa (c) brtson 'grus kyi bsngags pa smra ba ste — the T is (a) one whose mind is thoroughly trained/pliant (b) who has taken up diligence (c) who speaks in praise of diligence; ste → elaboration.
 2 yang dag par sbyor ba'i brtson 'grus gang gis sems can rnams kyis 'phags pa'i rnam par grol ba thob par 'gyur ba'i brtson 'grus de sems can rnams la bsngags par byed de — he praises to beings (la recipient) that diligence of right application by which (gang gis ... de, correlative; gis means) beings (kyis ERG of thob) will attain noble liberation ('gyur future).
 3 'di ni ... 'phrin las nyi shu rtsa gnyis pa'o — 22nd. 4 de la 'di skad ces bya'o.
RELATIONS ste elaboration; gang gis means; la recipient.
CONSTRUAL The Tathagata has a thoroughly trained mind, has taken up diligence, and speaks in praise of diligence: he praises to beings the diligence of right application, the diligence through which beings will attain noble liberation. This is the twenty-second tathagata activity of the Tathagata. On this it is said:
DOUBTS Q: sems shin tu sbyangs pa — "thoroughly trained mind" vs "mental pliancy" (prasrabdhi, shin tu sbyangs pa). Q: brtson 'grus brtsams pa — "has taken up / is energetic".

## U07 (verse 11×4)
p1 brtson 'grus gang gis mi yi seng ge 'phags 'gyur ba — the diligence through which (gang gis means) the Lion among Humans became exalted ('phags 'gyur ba; ba = nominalizer, relative head brtson 'grus).
p2 brtson 'grus stobs de'i bsngags pa rtag tu rjod par byed — he always speaks the praise of that power of diligence (de = correlative of gang gis). Agent elided.
p3 brtson 'grus stobs ni gang du'ang nyams pa yod min te — the power of diligence nowhere/in no respect declines (gang du'ang + yod min).
p4 snod du gyur pa rnyed nas chos kyang ston par byed — having found (nas) those who are vessels, he also teaches the Dharma (kyang = also).
DOUBTS Q: p1–2 alt "The Lion among Humans constantly praises the power of diligence through which [beings] become noble" (cf. U06 prose: diligence through which beings attain noble liberation): Lion as agent of p2 with ergative dropped. Took Lion as subject of p1 relative (absolutive, nearest verb). Q: gang du'ang — "anywhere/in any way".

## U08 (verse 11×4)
p1 bde bar gshegs kyi brtson 'grus gnas pa yod min te — the Sugata's diligence has no standing still (gnas pa = abiding/rest; never comes to rest).
p2 lus dang ngag dang sems ni gang du'ang skyo ba med — in body, speech and mind there is weariness nowhere (ni topic).
p3 ji ltar rang byung brtson 'grus kun du sdig med pa — just as (ji ltar) the self-arisen diligence is free of wrongdoing in every way (kun du).
p4 de ltar blo sbyangs sems can yang dag 'dzin du 'jug — so (de ltar) he trains [their] minds and leads beings to take [it] up properly (blo sbyangs: train minds; 'dzin du 'jug causative, cf. U01 g).
RELATIONS ji ltar ... de ltar simile/correlation; te seam p1–p2.
DOUBTS Q: p1 gnas pa yod min — "never rests/stands still" (taken) vs "does not dwell on anything". Q: p3 rang byung — adjective of brtson 'grus ("self-arisen diligence") vs epithet "the Self-Arisen's diligence". Q: p4 blo sbyangs sems can — "beings whose minds are trained" vs "he trains the minds of beings"; object of 'dzin unstated (awakening per U01 g, or the diligence).

## U09
FORM prose; long, ~14 clauses; split as two Q&A blocks. GENRE exposition.
CLAUSES 1 rigs kyi bu (VOC) gzhan yang dran pa ni thams cad kyi thams cad rnam pa thams cad kyi thams cad du nyams pa med do — the T's mindfulness never declines at all, in any way at all (stock emphatic "sarvena sarvam sarvatha sarvam").
 2 de ci'i phyir zhe na — why? | de bzhin gshegs pa la bsnyel ba med do — the T has no forgetfulness.
 3 rigs kyi bu (VOC) de bzhin gshegs pas ... mngon par rdzogs par sangs rgyas ma thag tu (as soon as he fully awakened to unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening) 'das pa dang ma 'ongs pa dang da ltar byung ba'i sems can rnams kyi sems kyi rgyud la rnam par gzigs kyang (although he surveys the mind-streams of past, future and present beings — hon. gzigs) | de la de bzhin gshegs pa bsnyel ba shin tu med do — in that the T has no forgetfulness whatsoever (shin tu + med = not at all).
 4 sems can gyi spyod pa ji lta ba bzhin du mkhyen kyang (concession: although he knows beings' conduct exactly as it is) | de la de bzhin gshegs pas ye shes yang gtong ba med do — in that, the T does not let go of wisdom either (yang = also/either).
 5 de bzhin gshegs pa ni [phung po gsum du rnam par dgod pa dang | sems can gyi dbang po la 'jug pa dang | sems can gyi bsam pa la 'jug pa dang | sems can gyi spyod pa la rnam par gzigs pa]-s dran pa nyams pa med pa nyid de — through (s = means/cause, scoped over the four coordinated nominals) arranging [beings] into three groups, entering their faculties, entering their inclinations, and surveying their conduct, his mindfulness is undiminished (nyid emphatic). de seam.
 6 de bzhin gshegs pa ni dran par byas sam bsams sam rnam par brtags te sems can rnams la chos ston pa med do — the T does not teach beings the Dharma after having recalled, or reflected, or examined (te = sequence; negation scopes over the whole: there is no teaching-after-recollecting).
 7 ston kyang nyams pa med do — although (kyang) he teaches [thus], there is no lapse.
 8 de ci'i phyir zhe na — why? | de bzhin gshegs pa'i dran pa ni ji ltar nyams pa med do — because the T's mindfulness in no way declines.
 9 de ji ltar bdag dran zhing bsnyel ba med pa de ltar sems can rnams la chos ston te — just as he himself remembers (bdag = self, reflexive) and does not forget, so he teaches beings the Dharma (ji ltar ... de ltar correlative). te seam.
 10 'di ni ... nyi shu rtsa gsum pa'o — 23rd. 11 de la 'di skad ces bya'o.
RELATIONS kyang concession (cl.3, 4, 7); s means/cause (cl.5); te sequence (cl.6) inside negation scope; ji ltar ... de ltar simile (cl.9); ma thag tu "as soon as".
NEG 1, 2, 3 (shin tu med), 4 (gtong ba med), 5 (nyams pa med), 6 (ston pa med — scope over recall-then-teach), 7, 8, 9 (bsnyel ba med) + bla na med; ma thag, ma 'ongs not negations.
CONSTRUAL Noble son, moreover, the Tathagata's mindfulness never declines in any way whatsoever. Why? Because the Tathagata has no forgetfulness. Noble son, as soon as the Tathagata fully awakened to unsurpassed, perfect and complete awakening, he surveyed the mind-streams of beings past, future and present; yet he forgets nothing of them at all. Although he knows the conduct of beings exactly as it is, he does not let go of wisdom in doing so. Because he arranges beings into three groups, enters into their faculties, enters into their inclinations and surveys their conduct, his mindfulness never declines. The Tathagata does not first call to mind, reflect or examine and then teach beings the Dharma; and yet when he teaches, nothing lapses. Why? Because the Tathagata's mindfulness never declines in any way. Just as he himself remembers and does not forget, so he teaches beings the Dharma. This is the twenty-third tathagata activity of the Tathagata. On this it is said:
DOUBTS Q: cl.3 gzigs kyang — tense: "surveys" (generic) vs "surveyed" (after awakening); ma thag tu attaches to gzigs. Q: cl.4 ye shes yang gtong ba med — "does not let go of wisdom" vs "his wisdom does not lapse". Q: cl.5 phung po gsum — three groups of beings (those certain of the right, of the wrong, and the undetermined) assumed; not stated in text. Q: cl.5 s — cause ("because") vs means ("through"). Q: cl.8 ji ltar — "in no way" (taken) vs "just as it is". Q: cl.7 ston kyang nyams pa med — "nothing in his teaching lapses" vs "his mindfulness does not lapse".

## U10 (verse 9×4)
p1 ston pa'i dran pa gang yang nyams med de — the Teacher's mindfulness never declines at all (gang yang + med); de seam.
p2 rgyal ba'i dran pas dran par bya ba med — for/by the Victor's mindfulness there is nothing that has to be called to mind (pas = means/agent; bya ba = to be done).
p3 rgyal bas gang tshe byang chub sangs rgyas nas — from (nas) the time (gang tshe) the Victor awakened to awakening (bas ERG),
p4 'gro ba'i sems ni ma lus rnam par mkhyen — he has known the minds of beings without exception (ma lus quantifier; hon. mkhyen).
RELATIONS de seam (p1→p2 = explanation); gang tshe ... nas temporal.
DOUBTS Q: p2 — "his mindfulness has nothing it must recall" vs "there is no need to recollect by mindfulness". Q: p3–4 tense: "since he awakened, he knows".

## U11 (verse 9×4)
p1 bsam pa dbang po sems can spyod pa dang — beings' inclinations, faculties and conduct (dang ends list; sems can genitive elided),
p2 phyis yang rnam par shes par bya ba med — he has no need to come to know them again later (phyis yang = later/again; bya ba med = nothing to be done).
p3 lhun gyis grub pa'i bya bar gnas nas ni — abiding (nas) in effortless activity,
p4 rgyal ba'i dbang po 'gro la chos ston to — the Lord of Victors teaches beings the Dharma.
VERSE-NOTES sems can genitive dropped (p1); ni filler (p3).
DOUBTS Q: p2 phyis yang — "afterward, again" (taken: no later act of finding out) vs "moreover". Q: p1 dang — could link to p2 as "together with": list taken.
