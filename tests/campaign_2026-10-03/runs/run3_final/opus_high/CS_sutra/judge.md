## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U04 line 2 "realize all conditioned things through knowledge": shes pas is detached from byas pa'i chos, which adds a doctrine. Cause: construal fork (C took the weaker branch it had recorded).
2. U06 "mind is thoroughly trained" for shin tu sbyangs pa (pliancy). Cause: dictionary sense.
3. U08 "hold to what is right": yang dag made the object, which breaks the bound phrase from U01 and garden-paths the English. Cause: grammar misread / bound-term inconsistency.
4. U09 the fourfold "thams cad" intensifier is reduced to "in any way at all". Cause: omission under compression.
5. U07 "became exalted" for 'phags 'gyur, which loses the ārya/"noble" link to U06. Cause: term choice.
(Also: U01 'dul ba as "guide", a glossary choice, i.e. skill rule applied; U03 mos pa as "devotion", term choice.)
