# tantra — construal (Candamaharosana Tantra, Toh 431, ch. 15; units U01–U12)
Brief: seasoned practitioner · study aid · Sanskrit terms anglicized, no diacritics · no brackets/Wylie · verse metred, citation mode · no contractions · doubts as Q:
Speakers: U01–U02 Acala (the Blessed One, continuing); U03 the Blessed Lady (bcom ldan 'das ma) asks, frame "then the Blessed One said" introduces U04; U04–U12 the Blessed One.

UNIT U01
FORM verse 7x4 (root tantra, not a citation)
GENRE instruction (practice), REGISTER tantra speaking of the yogi, third person, plain
SOURCE 1 de yi sbyor bas rnal 'byor pas / 2 myur du dngos grub the tshom med / 3 yang na shes rab dang bral ba'i / 4 shin tu mnyam gzhag bsgom par bya
WORDS sbyor ba = union (yoga/prayoga; in this tantra union with the consort); shes rab = prajna in the sense "female partner" (the tantra's usage); shin tu = intensifier on mnyam gzhag
CLAUSES 2. (a) verb elided (gains/attains) — ERG agent rnal 'byor pas; object dngos grub; means de yi sbyor bas (instr); adv myur du; the tshom med = without doubt (assertion modifier). (b) bsgom par bya: obligative/future "should meditate on/cultivate"; ABS object shin tu mnyam gzhag; agent elided (the yogi).
RELATIONS sbyor bas = means ("through"); yang na = alternative ("or else"); bral ba'i = modification of mnyam gzhag
NEG the tshom med (no doubt); bral ba (without prajna) scope = the equipoise
VERSE-NOTES yi = metrical genitive; verb of pada 2 elided
CONSTRUAL Through that union the yogi swiftly gains siddhi, without doubt. Or else he should cultivate a very deep equipoise that is without a prajna.
DOUBTS Q: de yi sbyor ba = "union with her" (the consort of the preceding verses) or "that practice/yoga" — rendered "that union". Q: bral ba'i modifies the equipoise (grammar) though in sense it is the yogi who is without a prajna. Q: shes rab here = consort (prajna) vs "wisdom"; consort reading from the tantra's usage.

UNIT U02
FORM verse 7x2 (root tantra)
SOURCE 1 mi g.yo bsgom pa yongs rdzogs na / 2 byang chub rgyal srid yang dag 'thob
CLAUSES (a) yongs rdzogs: intr, "is complete"; subject mi g.yo bsgom pa (meditation on Acala). (b) 'thob: attains; subject elided (the yogi); object byang chub rgyal srid
RELATIONS na = condition/time ("when"); bsgom pa object relation: mi g.yo = Acala as object of meditation
NEG mi g.yo is the name Acala (Immovable), not a negation of the clause
CONSTRUAL When the meditation on Acala is complete, he truly attains the kingdom of awakening.
DOUBTS Q: mi g.yo bsgom pa = "meditation on Acala" vs "unwavering meditation". Q: yang dag = "truly/fully attains" vs "perfect (awakening)".

UNIT U03
FORM prose frame + verse 7x4 + prose frame
GENRE framing + request (honorific), REGISTER the goddess to the Lord, humble (gsol), vocatives 'dren pa, gtso bo
SOURCE frame: de nas bcom ldan 'das mas gsol pa / 1 lha yi rnam par dag pa gang / 2 'dren pa bdag ni nyan par 'tshal / 3 sngon du gsungs nyid dkyil 'khor rnams / 4 gtso bo bdag la rnam dag gsungs / frame: de nas bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal pa
CLAUSES gsol pa (humble speech, ERG bcom ldan 'das mas); (1) question, copula elided: what is the purity of the deity/deities; (2) 'tshal = wish/request, bdag ni topic: I wish to hear; vocative 'dren pa "Guide"; (3)-(4) gsungs (imperative, hon.): explain to me (bdag la recipient) the purity (rnam dag) of the mandalas that (you) yourself taught before (sngon du gsungs nyid = relative clause on dkyil 'khor rnams; nyid emphatic); vocative gtso bo "Lord"; final frame bka' stsal (hon.) "the Blessed One said"
RELATIONS de nas = sequence; la = recipient; relative clause sngon du gsungs → dkyil 'khor
CONSTRUAL Then the Blessed Lady asked: "What is the purity of the deities? Guide, I wish to hear it. Lord, explain to me the purity of the mandalas you yourself taught before." Then the Blessed One said:
DOUBTS Q: lha singular/plural (deity / the deities). Q: nyid = "you yourself" vs "those very mandalas".

UNIT U04
FORM verse 7x2
SOURCE 1 de nas kun gyi sbyong byed pa'i / 2 rnam dag bdag gis rab bshad bya
CLAUSES bshad bya (future/intent) ERG bdag gis; object rnam dag modified by kun gyi sbyong byed pa'i "which purifies all"
RELATIONS de nas = sequence ("now/next"); kun gyi = genitive object of the agent noun (purifier of all)
CONSTRUAL Now I shall explain the purity that purifies everything.
DOUBTS Q: rab bshad: rab rendered by the plain verb (no "fully").

UNIT U05
FORM prose, a list of equations X ni Y'o (mandala symbolism)
GENRE exposition (definition), REGISTER plain, impersonal
CLAUSES ~16 equations, copula = final particle; de la = "here, in this"
ITEMS gru bzhi pa = the square (shape) = tshad med pa'i gnas bzhi four immeasurables; sgo bzhi four doors = bden pa bzhi four truths; rta babs bzhi four archways (torana) = bsam gtan bzhi four dhyanas; ka ba brgyad eight pillars = eightfold noble path; 'phar ma gcig pa single tier = sems rtse gcig pa nyid one-pointedness of mind; pad+ma lotus = skye gnas womb; kha dog sna tshogs pa various colors = sprul pa sna tshogs various emanations; lha dgu nine deities = gsung rab yan lag dgu nine branches of scripture; phyogs dmar po red directions = 'dod chags chen po great passion; mtshams su white, yellow, green, black (in the intermediate directions) = bram ze, rje rigs (vaishya), rgyal rigs (kshatriya), da mangs rigs (= dmangs rigs, shudra) "themselves" (nyid); zla ba dang nyi ma = khu ba dang rngul (semen and sweat); dbus kyi ral gri sword in center = emblem (mtshan ma) of Black Acala; gri gug curved knife = Dveshavajri; shar la sogs pa'i phyogs rnams directions beginning with east = White Acala and the rest; me la sogs pa'i mtshams rnams su intermediate directions beginning with fire (southeast) = [those] of Mohavajri and the rest (genitive kyi'o: belong to); closing zhes pa ni dkyil 'khor gyi rnam par dag pa'o = this is the purity of the mandala
NEG none real (tool counted ma of rdo rje ma, nyi ma, mi g.yo ba = name Acala; tshad med = immeasurable)
CONSTRUAL as items; colors and castes matched in order.
DOUBTS Q: 'phar ma gcig pa = "single tier" (of the wall/plinth), architecture term uncertain. Q: phyogs dmar po = "the red directions" vs "the red of the directions". Q: rngul "sweat" stands where one expects the female fluid (rakta); rendered as written. Q: kha dog sna tshogs pa may be the lotus's colors (the preceding item) or the mandala's.

UNIT U06
FORM prose
GENRE exposition, REGISTER plain
SOURCE bsgom pa'i rnam par dag pa brjod par bya ste / dang po mchod pa ni bsod nams kyi tshogs khyad par 'phags pa'i las so / stong pa nyid ni ye shes kyi tshogs 'chi ba khyad par 'phags pa dri ma med pa'i lus te dri za'i lus so / gzhal yang (=yas) khang gi mthar thug pa sangs rgyas kyi sa'o / pad+ma ni skye gnas so / zla ba nyi ma ni dkar dmar ro
CLAUSES brjod par bya (future/obligative, agent elided = the speaker) "I shall state"; ste introduces; las = karma/act (tool slip: tagged ABL)
RELATIONS ste = namely; te = apposition "that is"; gi = genitive
CONSTRUAL I shall state the purity of the meditation. First, the offering is the exceptional act of the accumulation of merit. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom, an exceptional death; it is the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva. The culmination of the celestial palace is the level of buddhahood. The lotus is the womb. The moon and sun are the white and the red.
DOUBTS Q: khyad par 'phags pa'i qualifies las (exceptional act) or tshogs (exceptional accumulation). Q: 'chi ba khyad par 'phags pa = "exceptional death" (parallel to the offering clause) vs "death; the exceptional stainless body". Q: gzhal yas khang gi mthar thug pa: strict genitive "the culmination of the palace"; alternatively gi for ni, "the palace is the ultimate level of buddhahood".

UNIT U07
FORM prose
GENRE exposition (narrative of conception, symbolic), REGISTER plain
CLAUSES (1) hum yig = gandharva's mind, ma dang pha'i dbus su (between mother and father). (2) Akshobhya = father (pa, i.e. pha). (3) Mamaki = mother. (4) mthong nas (having seen) mutual passion → pha la zhe sdang (hatred toward father) zhing (and) → ma la chags nas (attached to mother, then) → gti mug pas (through delusion: cause/means) sems can bzhin du 'jug (enters just as a sentient being). Subject elided: the gandharva. (5) pad+mar zhugs pa'i bu yis (ERG) pha bsad nas (having killed) → de'i go 'phang 'thob (attains his = father's rank). (6) ma bzung nas (having taken the mother) → bde ba khyad par 'phags pas (cause: through the exceptional bliss of the latent tendencies of endless births) → de yang bur skye bar byed (he too brings about birth as a son / causes [a son] to be born). (7) bu mo ni yang (daughters again) = White Acala etc. and Mohavajri etc. too. (8) bu rnams kyang (the sons too) pha bsad nas → gzhan la bgrod par byed (go to another) de → 'di nyid dgra bo zhes (thinking "this is the enemy") de gsod par byed (kill him). (9) bu mo rnams kyang (daughters too) through the same bliss 'dod par byed (feel desire).
RELATIONS nas = sequence (x4); zhing = coordination; pas = cause/means (gti mug pas; bde ba ... pas x2); kyang = also
NEG mtha' med pa (endless) x2; tool's other NEG rows are ma "mother", mi g.yo ba name, ma bskyod pa name Akshobhya
DOUBTS Q: de yang bur skye bar byed — revised after MITRA: bur = terminative "as a son", byed auxiliary → "he too is born as a son"; alternative causative "brings about the birth of a son". Q: bu mo "daughters" paired with White Acala (male) — possibly "sons" or "children" intended; rendered as written. Q: gzhan la bgrod = go to another (sexual approach, gamana) ; "him" in "kill him" = the father or the other.

UNIT U08
FORM prose
GENRE exposition (iconographic symbolism), REGISTER plain
CLAUSES sword = wisdom (shes rab), la (connective) noose = method (thabs); yang na: noose = wisdom (text: "ses rab", scribal for shes rab), sword = method; gnyis ka ro gcig tu gyur pa = sdigs mdzub (threatening forefinger); g.yon pa 'og tu bltas pa (left [eye] looking down) = protects the seven underworlds; g.yas pa steng du bltas pa = protects the seven eggs of Brahma; bus mo (= pus mo knee) g.yon pa sa la btsugs pa = protects the earth; g.yas pa'i rkang pas yang dag par bsnun pa (striking/stamping with the right foot) = frightens all maras (mdzad hon.); four maras = Brahma (aggregates), Ishvara (afflictions), Vishnu (Lord of Death), Indra (lha'i dbang po; son of the gods)
RELATIONS la = clause connective "and"; yang na = alternative; rkang pas = instrument
DOUBTS Q: head noun of g.yon pa / g.yas pa bltas pa elided — eyes (gaze) supplied. Q: yang dag par bsnun = "stamping down firmly". Q: ses rab read as shes rab.

UNIT U09
FORM prose
CLAUSES sa ni (the earth, taking up U08's "protects the earth") = enjoying (longs spyod) the young women (gzhon nu ma) of the whole earth; gzhon nu ma la yun ring du gnas pa'i phyir (because [one] dwells long in the young woman) → pad+ma'i gdan (lotus seat); skye gnas la skyes pa (birth in the womb) = moon-and-sun seat; khu ba dang rngul las skyes pa'i skyes bu (man born from semen and sweat) = dngos po (the existent); bud med (woman) = dngos po med pa (the nonexistent); black = consciousness, white = form, yellow = feeling, red = perception, green = Amoghasiddhi
RELATIONS phyir = cause; las = source
NEG dngos po med pa (nonexistent); bud med = "woman" (not neg)
DOUBTS Q: skyes pa = "being born" (not "man"). Q: green = Amoghasiddhi where the pattern leads one to expect formations; rendered as written. Q: la in gzhon nu ma la = "in" (locus) vs "with".

UNIT U12
FORM verse 7x4 (root tantra)
SOURCE 1 ston pa rgyal ba gcig pu nyid / 2 lnga yi tshul gyis yang dag gnas / 3 shes rab pha rol phyin nyid kyang / 4 rnam lnga'i tshul gyis yang dag gnas
CLAUSES 2 sentences; gnas (intr) x2; subjects ston pa rgyal ba gcig pu nyid; shes rab pha rol phyin nyid
RELATIONS tshul gyis = manner ("as, in the mode of"); kyang = also (additive)
VERSE-NOTES yi = metrical; nyid emphatic/filler on both subjects; anaphora/parallelism of padas 2 and 4 kept
CONSTRUAL The Teacher, the Victor, the one alone, truly abides as five; Prajnaparamita too truly abides in five aspects.
DOUBTS Q: gcig pu nyid = "the one alone" (implying a contrast one/five), no concessive particle in the Tibetan.
