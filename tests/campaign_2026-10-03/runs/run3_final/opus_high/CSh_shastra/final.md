### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose · exposition (definition and refutation) · academic · not a citation (contains a quoted claim of an unnamed party)
TEXT:
In this Yoginī Tantra, [one is called] "master" (*ācārya*) because one constantly conducts oneself far removed from evil, unwholesome things; for this is the *saṃvara* of that very [master]. The nature of the master consecration is what is called "the accomplishment, from the *karmamudrā*, of the very own-nature of the four joys by means of the four moments." It is not the case, however, that one who has obtained the irreversible consecration, which is shared with the Kriyā Tantra and the rest, is thereby to be treated as consecrated with the master consecration. Therefore the statement "[Through it] one also comes to have the good fortune to explain, hear, and so on, all the Yoga and Yoginī Tantras and the rest" amounts to nothing. Therefore, first of all, in order to authorize [disciples] to hear, meditate on, and so on, the Yoginī Tantras such as the Hevajra, one should consecrate [them] with the master consecration itself. Moreover, it should be understood that, in accordance with the *abhisamaya*, this [consecration] is designated by the characteristics of master, secret, wisdom, and fourth.
NOTES:
Issue: kun du spyod pa ("conducts oneself") plays on the etymology of *ācārya* (ā-√car).
Q: de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas = "for this is the *saṃvara* of that very [master]". Alt: "because that itself is its [the tantra's] *saṃvara*" (linking to U01).
Q: thun mong du gyur pa'i phyir mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba is read as a relative clause on "irreversible consecration" (phyir mi ldog pa = irreversible). Alt: phyir as cause, "because it is shared with the Kriyā Tantra and the rest, one who has obtained the irreversible consecration…". The sense is close either way.
Q: slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya = "is not to be treated as consecrated with the master consecration". Alt: "the term 'consecrated with the master consecration' does not apply".
Q: mngon par rtogs pa = the *abhisamaya*, probably the ritual or sādhana manual. Alt: "in accordance with realization".
Q: rab tu bsgrub pa = "accomplishment". Alt: "bringing about"/"realization". dbang bar bya = "authorize". Alt: "make competent".
Alt: ci yang ma yin = "amounts to nothing" (lit. "is nothing at all").

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose · exposition (prescriptive) · academic · not a citation
TEXT:
To those of dull faculties who have obtained such a master consecration, having determined [them] by [their] inclination, one is to teach meditation on the *karmamudrā* and on the mudrā of elaboration. Likewise, in the domain of the completion stage too, there is the experience, through the guru's instruction itself, of the four joys, whose nature is the four moments, [in] the jewel located in the secret [place]. That is called the secret consecration, because it is not to be taught to yogins engaged in meditation with elaboration. Here, to the consecrated who are of middling faculties, one should teach and explain meditation on the *samayamudrā*.
NOTES:
Q: mos pas nges par bzung nas = "having determined [them] by [their] inclination". Alt: "having held [it] firmly through devotion" (MITRA reads it this way).
Q: gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu bla ma'i man ngag. Taken with an elided locative, "[in] the jewel located in the secret [place]", as in U07 snying gi nor bur. Alt (also MITRA): apposition, "the guru's instruction, the jewel kept secret".
Q: dbang bskur ba'i dbang po 'bring po = "the consecrated who are of middling faculties". Alt: "those of middling faculties for consecration".
Q: spros pa'i phyag rgya = "mudrā of elaboration" (prapañca). The exact referent is unclear.
Issue: the dictionary segmented "yang gsang" ("most secret") wrongly. The correct division is phyogs la yang / gsang bar gnas pa'i.

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose · exposition (definition) · academic · not a citation
TEXT:
Likewise, the special gnosis (*jñāna*) is wisdom (*prajñā*): knowing all phenomena to be mind only. The consecration for accomplishing it is the wisdom-gnosis consecration. Moreover, it should be understood that wisdom-gnosis is this: in the heart-jewel, whose form condenses the nature of the three consciousnesses—the dependent, the imagined, and the transformed—the external mudrā and the moments are correctly indicated through the guru's instruction itself. To those of superior faculties who have been consecrated with that consecration, one should teach and explain the *dharmamudrā* through the illusion-like *samādhi*.
NOTES:
Q: yongs su gyur pa = "the transformed" (pariṇata?), where the three-nature scheme normally has yongs su grub pa ("the perfected", pariniṣpanna). This may be a variant or an error. MITRA silently gives "perfected".
Q: gzugs can may qualify the heart-jewel ("embodied") or the condensed nature ("having the form of"). The second reading is taken.
Q: dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab = "those of superior faculties consecrated with that consecration". Alt: "by that consecration, to those of superior faculties for consecration".
Alt: mtshon pa = "indicated". Alt: "identified", "exemplified".
Check: the locative "in the heart-jewel" now attaches to the act of indicating, not to "the moments".

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose · commentary gloss on U04 pada 2 + debate · academic · contains an inline citation of U02 pada 3 (same source as U02)
TEXT:
Likewise, there is also the fourth; that is, "in that way, thus … as well." "That" means wisdom-gnosis. "Thus" is thusness (*tathatā*); there is no difference in meaning between it and what are called "the reality-limit" (*bhūtakoṭi*) and "the dharma-sphere" (*dharmadhātu*). The consecration through which its nature is seen or accomplished is what the word "thus" denotes. The word "as well" means "it is to be conferred also immediately after the wisdom-gnosis consecration." Moreover, that [fourth] does not become the fourth consecration merely through the imparting of this instruction: "What, with the external mudrā and through the guru's instruction itself, focuses on the perfection of the moment, characterized by not being definitively delimited into distinct objects, by the yoga of focusing without focus, is to be called the fourth consecration." Otherwise—since, by the words "Because one is washed and consecrated by this" and so on, the Blessed One holds consecration here to be the washing away of stains—how could it come about merely through the teaching of an instruction?
NOTES:
Issue: the pun de bzhin / de bzhin nyid (tathā / tathatā) is carried by "thus / thusness". "That" is the commentator's lemma drawn from de ltar ("in that way").
Q: skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa = "the perfection of the moment". Alt: "the perfect moment(s)".
Q: where the quoted instruction begins. Phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang may stand outside it: "that [fourth], [involving] the external mudrā, and what, through the guru's instruction…". MITRA folds the external mudrā into "not delimited by", which goes against the shad after dang.
Q: na in bzhed pa yin na is read as "since" (a given premise). Alt: "if".
Issue: the dictionary mis-segmented "de yang | dag pa'i mtha'" and "la don", and glossed don as a name. The text reads de | yang dag pa'i mtha' and la | don gyi khyad par.
