## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U10 pada 2 "is heard and seen directly from speech": the stanza asserts what the commentary denies. Cause: construal fork (literal declarative kept against the argument, although a Q was recorded).
2. U08 the definition of the fourth consecration is made into the quoted content of the "instruction", which hides the commentator's own claim. Cause: construal fork (scope of the gang … de ni clause and of the genitive -'i man ngag).
3. U11 "a reality imagined as one's own conceptual thoughts spread this way and that" garbles the relation (imagined by, not as), and the rgyas/brgyas variant is never considered. Causes: grammar misread and verse constraint.
4. U05 "the saṃvara of that very [master]" attaches the saṃvara to the wrong referent, against the U01 equation saṃvara = consecration. Cause: construal fork.
5. U04–U08 calque register ("in that way, thus, … as well", "the very own-nature", "wisdom-gnosis", bracket-heavy clauses in U06), which leaves most prose units at 3. Cause: register / skill rule misapplied (lemma-literalism carried beyond the gloss points).
