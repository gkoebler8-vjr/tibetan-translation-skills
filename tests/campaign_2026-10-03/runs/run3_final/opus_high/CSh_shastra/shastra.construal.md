## U01
FORM prose; commentary gloss. GENRE gloss. REGISTER commentary gloss, impersonal.
SOURCE 1 sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa la/ 2 sdom pa ni dbang bskur ba'o/ 3 de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste/ 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no/ 4 de yang ji skad du/
CLAUSES 1 lemma "sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa" + la = "regarding" (topic of gloss). 2 copular gloss: saṃvara = consecration. 3 copular: its prabheda = (lemma) dbye ba; ste + mtshan nyid can: [it] has the characteristics that will be explained (future 'chad par 'gyur ba). 4 lead-in: de yang ji skad du = "that, moreover, [is] as stated:" — quotation follows (U02).
RELATIONS 'i (sdom pa'i dbye ba) genitive; la sogs pa = "and so on"; de'i = of that (saṃvara); ste = apposition/continuation.
NEG none. QUANT none.
CONSTRUAL On "the divisions of the saṃvara and so on": saṃvara is consecration. "Division" is its classification, which has characteristics that are going to be explained. That, moreover, is as [the tantra] says:
TERMS sdom pa = saṃvara (kept; glossed by the text as consecration); dbang bskur ba = consecration; dbye ba = division; rab tu dbye ba = classification; mtshan nyid = characteristic.
DOUBTS Q: sdom pa as "vow" would hide the gloss saṃvara→abhiṣeka; kept Sanskrit. Q: 'chad par 'gyur ba = "will be explained [below in the tantra/commentary]".

## U02
FORM verse 7×4 + frame "ces gsungs pa yin no". Citation (ji skad du … ces gsungs).
SOURCE 1 sems can rnams la phan pa'i phyir/ 2 dbang bskur ba ni bzhi ru bsgrags/ 3 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur bas/ 4 de phyir dbang bskur zhes byar brjod/
CLAUSES S1 verb bsgrags (past of sgrog, tr.; agent elided = [the Buddha/tantra]); abs. dbang bskur ba; bzhi ru = result "as fourfold". S2: 'di yis (instr. means, = this consecration) bkrus (washed) shing (coord) dbang bskur (consecrated) + bas (cause) → de phyir (therefore) … zhes byar brjod (is called).
RELATIONS phyir (pada 1) purpose; shing coordination; bas cause; de phyir consequence.
VERSE-NOTES ni filler/topic; ru short form.
CONSTRUAL For the benefit of beings consecration has been proclaimed to be fourfold. Because by this [one] is washed and consecrated, therefore it is called "consecration". — So it has been said.
CITATION dm.py identify: VERBATIM yes — but the only canonical hit is Toh 1183 itself (BO_T02_D1183:6a-15); parallels: 0. Root presumably Hevajra Tantra (cf. II.3.9, Toh 418), not matched verbatim. → source not located verbatim.
DOUBTS Q: pada 3 dbang bskur renders a verb of sprinkling in the Indic etymology of seka/abhiṣeka; Tibetan repeats dbang bskur, kept as "consecrated".

## U04
FORM verse 2 padas 7×2 (root lemma) + prose frame. SOURCE 1 slob dpon gsang ba shes rab dang/ 2 de ltar de bzhin bzhi pa yang/ 3 zhes bya ba la sogs pas 'chad par 'gyur ba yin no/

RELATIONS dang coord; la sogs pas = means; yang "also/again" (glossed in U08).
CONSTRUAL "Master, secret, and wisdom, and in that way, thus, the fourth as well": by this and what follows [the divisions] will be explained.
CITATION identify: verbatim canonical in Toh 1190 (Suviśadasaṃpuṭa) and Toh 2255; Kangyur Toh 418 (Hevajra, part II) at D 17a reads pada 2 "bzhi bde yang de bzhin no" (run 7) → root quoted in a variant form; Toh 418 = quoted loosely.

## U05
FORM prose, 5 sentences. GENRE exposition/debate. REGISTER exposition, impersonal.
SOURCE S1 rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud 'dir ni sdig pa mi dge ba'i chos rnams las ring du gyur pa kun du spyod pa'i phyir slob dpon te/ de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas so/
 S2 slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba'i ngo bo gang las kyi phyag rgya las skad cig ma bzhis dga' ba bzhi'i rang gi ngo bo nyid rab tu bsgrub pa zhes bya ba yin gyi/
 S3 yang bya ba'i rgyud la sogs pa dang thun mong du gyur pa'i phyir mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba thob pas slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o/
 S4 de lta bas na rnal 'byor dang rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud la sogs pa thams cad bshad pa dang nyan pa la sogs pa'i skal pa dang yang ldan par 'gyur ro zhes bya ba'i tshig 'di ni ci yang ma yin no/
 S5 de lta bas na dang por re zhig dgyes pa'i rdo rje la sogs pa rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud rnams mnyan pa dang bsgom pa la sogs pa la dbang bar bya ba'i phyir slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba nyid kyis dbang bskur bar bya'o/
 S6 yang de ni mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du slob dpon dang gsang ba dang shes rab dang bzhi pa'i mtshan nyid kyis nye bar mtshon pa yin par rig par bya'o/
CLAUSES S1 phyir = cause (etymology ā-√car: kun du spyod pa = conduct); "slob dpon te" = [is called] master; pas so = because: de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin = "it is the saṃvara of that very one". S2 copular: ngo bo (nature of the master consecration) = "what is called the accomplishment (rab tu bsgrub pa), from (las) the karmamudrā, of the own-nature of the four joys by means of (s) the four moments"; gyi = adversative "but". S3 yang = however; thun mong du gyur pa'i = modifies "phyir mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba" (irreversible consecration, COMPOUND), NOT a phyir-cause (botok split phyir); thob pas = by obtaining; "slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya" = one is not [thereby] to be treated as consecrated with the master consecration. S4 de lta bas na therefore; quoted claim zhes bya ba'i tshig; ci yang ma yin = is nothing/baseless. S5 de lta bas na therefore; dang por re zhig first of all; phyir purpose: to authorize (dbang bar bya) [disciples] for hearing, meditating etc. the Yoginī Tantras such as Hevajra; main verb dbang bskur bar bya (one should consecrate), instrument slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba nyid kyis. S6 yang moreover; de = this [consecration]; mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du = in accordance with the abhisamaya; mtshan nyid kyis nye bar mtshon pa = is designated by the marks of master, secret, wisdom, fourth; rig par bya = should be understood.
RELATIONS S1 las source (far from); phyir cause; pas cause. S2 las source; -s means; gyi adversative. S3 phyir-mi-ldog = compound; thob pas means/cause; -s instrument. S4 de lta bas na consequence. S5 de lta bas na consequence; phyir purpose; kyis instrument. S6 bzhin du accordance; kyis means.
NEG mi dge (unwholesome, lexical); phyir mi ldog (irreversible, lexical); "la ni mi bya'o" — scopes over treating the irreversible-consecration recipient as master-consecrated; "ci yang ma yin" — the statement is nothing. skad cig ma = "moment" (tool NEG false positive).
QUANT bzhi (four moments, four joys); thams cad (all the Yoga and Yoginī Tantras etc.); nyid restrictive/emphatic on slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba (the master consecration itself).
CONSTRUAL In this Yoginī Tantra [one is called] "master" because one constantly conducts oneself far from evil, unwholesome things; for this is the saṃvara of that very [master]. What the nature of the master consecration consists in is what is called the accomplishment, from the karmamudrā, of the very own-nature of the four joys through the four moments. But it is not the case that by obtaining the irreversible consecration, which is common to the Kriyā Tantra and the rest, one is to be treated as consecrated with the master consecration. Therefore the statement "[by it] one also comes to have the good fortune of explaining, hearing, and so on, all the Yoga and Yoginī Tantras and the like" is baseless. Therefore, first of all, to authorize [disciples] to hear, meditate on, and so on, the Yoginī Tantras such as the Hevajra, one should consecrate [them] with the master consecration itself. Moreover, it should be understood that this [consecration], in accordance with the abhisamaya, is designated by the characteristics of master, secret, wisdom, and fourth.
DOUBTS Q: S1 "de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas" — "because it is the saṃvara of that very [master]" vs "because that itself is its (the tantra's) saṃvara". Q: S2 "rab tu bsgrub pa" — accomplishment vs "realization/bringing about". Q: S3 alt: "one is not to be called 'consecrated by the master' [merely] because one has obtained the irreversible consecration". Q: S6 mngon par rtogs pa = the abhisamaya (ritual/sādhana manual) vs "realization". Q: dbang bar bya = "authorize/make competent".

## U06

## U07
FORM prose, 4 sentences. REGISTER exposition/definition.
SOURCE S1 de bzhin du khyad par can gyi ye shes ni shes rab ste/ chos thams cad sems tsam nyid du shes pa'o/
 S2 de rab tu bsgrub pa'i phyir dbang bskur ba ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'o/
 S3 yang de ni gzhan gyi dbang dang kun brtags dang yongs su gyur pa rnam par shes pa gsum gyi ngo bo bsdus pa'i gzugs can snying gi nor bur gang bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang skad cig ma rnams yang dag par mtshon pa ni shes rab ye shes yin par rig par bya'o/
 S4 dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab rnams la ni sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin gyis chos kyi phyag rgya nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o/
CLAUSES S1 definition: special gnosis = wisdom (the root lemma shes rab); ste = namely: knowing all phenomena as mind only. S2 phyir purpose: the consecration for accomplishing it = wisdom-gnosis consecration. S3 yang moreover; de ni topic; relative "gang … mtshon pa": that which, in the heart-jewel (r locative) — embodied, the form condensing the nature of the three consciousnesses (dependent, imagined, transformed) — correctly indicates, through the guru's instruction itself, the external mudrā and the moments; = wisdom-gnosis; rig par bya. S4 to those of superior faculties consecrated with that consecration, the dharmamudrā is to be taught and explained through (gyis) the illusion-like samādhi.
RELATIONS S2 phyir purpose. S3 'i chains; r locative; kyis means; dang coord. S4 des instr. (consecrated by that); gyis means; cing coord.
NEG none real (cig ma, sgyu ma = tool false positives). QUANT thams cad (all phenomena); tsam restrictive (mind only); gsum (three consciousnesses); rnams (moments).
CONSTRUAL Likewise, the special gnosis is wisdom: knowing all phenomena to be mind only. The consecration for accomplishing it is the wisdom-gnosis consecration. Moreover, it should be understood that wisdom-gnosis is the correct indication, through the guru's instruction itself, of the external mudrā and the moments in the heart-jewel, an embodied form condensing the nature of the three consciousnesses — the dependent, the imagined, and the transformed. To those of superior faculties consecrated with that consecration one should teach and explain the dharmamudrā through the illusion-like samādhi.
DOUBTS Q: yongs su gyur pa "transformed" where the three-nature scheme has yongs su grub pa (perfected); possibly a variant/error, or deliberate (pariṇata). Q: gzugs can "embodied" may qualify the heart-jewel or modify ngo bo bsdus pa ("having the form of the condensed nature"). Q: "dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab" — consecrated by that consecration vs "by that consecration, to those of superior faculties for consecration". Q: mtshon pa = indicate/identify/characterize.

## U08
FORM prose, gloss on pada "de ltar de bzhin bzhi pa yang", 6 sentences + rhetorical question; inline quotation of U02 pada 3.
SOURCE S1 de bzhin du bzhi pa yang yin te/ de ltar de bzhin yang zhes bya ba ste/ de zhes pa ni shes rab ye shes so/

 S3 dbang bskur ba gang gis de'i ngo bo nyid mthong ba'am rab tu bsgrub pa ste/ de bzhin zhes bya ba'i sgras brjod par bya ba yin no/
 S4 yang zhes bya ba'i sgra ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'i rjes thogs su yang sbyin par bya'o zhes bya ba'i don to/
 S5 yang de ni phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang/ gang bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis yul so sor nges par yongs su bcad pa ma yin pa'i mtshan nyid can skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa la dmigs pa med par dmigs pa'i rnal 'byor gyis dmigs par byed pa de ni dbang bskur ba bzhi pa zhes bya bar brjod par bya ba'i man ngag nye bar bstan par byas pa tsam gyis ni dbang bskur ba bzhi par 'gyur ba ma yin no/
 S6 gzhan du na/ 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba/ zhes bya ba la sogs pas bcom ldan 'das kyis dbang bskur ba ni 'dir dri ma bkru bar bzhed pa yin na/ de ji lta na man ngag bstan pa tsam gyis yin par 'gyur/
CLAUSES S1 likewise there is the fourth too: that is "in that way, thus … as well"; "that" = wisdom-gnosis. S2 "thus" = thusness (tathatā); de = and; no difference of meaning among it and the terms reality-limit, dharma-sphere (la = among; NEG yod pa ma yin). (Tool slip: botok "de yang | dag pa"; correct "de | yang dag pa'i mtha'"; "la don" slip = la + don.) S3 the consecration by which (gang gis) its nature is seen or accomplished — that is what the word "thus" denotes. S4 the word "as well" means "it is to be conferred also immediately after the wisdom-gnosis consecration" (don = meaning; tool "Don (name)" slip). S5 yang moreover; de ni = that [fourth]; nested: instruction stating "That which — with the external mudrā and through the guru's instruction itself — focuses, by the yoga of focusing without focus, on the perfection of the moment characterized by not being definitely delimited into distinct objects, is to be called the fourth consecration"; merely (tsam) imparting that instruction does not make it the fourth consecration (NEG scopes over 'gyur ba, restricted by tsam: "merely by … not"). S6 gzhan du na otherwise; [since] with "by this one is washed and consecrated" etc. the Blessed One (hon. bzhed) holds consecration here to be the washing away of stains (na = given that/if); then how could it come about merely by teaching an instruction? (rhetorical: it could not).
RELATIONS S1 te/ste apposition; S3 gis instrument; 'am alternative; S4 rjes thogs su immediately after; yang also; S5 dang with; kyis means; gyis means; tsam gyis restrictive means; S6 gzhan du na otherwise; pas means; kyis agent; na condition; ji lta na how.
NEG S2 yod pa ma yin (no difference); S5 nges par yongs su bcad pa ma yin pa'i (not delimited); dmigs pa med par (without object); 'gyur ba ma yin (does not become). "dri ma" = stain (tool NEG false positive). S6 rhetorical question = implied negation (keep as question).
CONSTRUAL Likewise there is the fourth as well: that is "in that way, thus … as well." "That" means wisdom-gnosis. "Thus" is thusness; there is no difference in meaning between it and what are called "the reality-limit" and "the dharma-sphere". The consecration through which its nature is seen or accomplished is what is denoted by the word "thus". The word "as well" means "it is to be conferred also immediately after the wisdom-gnosis consecration." Moreover, that [fourth] does not become the fourth consecration merely through imparting the instruction "What, with the external mudrā and through the guru's instruction itself, focuses — by the yoga of focusing without focus — on the perfection of the moment characterized by not being definitely delimited into distinct objects, is to be called the fourth consecration." Otherwise — since with the words "by this one is washed and consecrated" and so on the Blessed One holds that consecration here is the washing away of stains — how could it come about merely through teaching an instruction?
DOUBTS Q: S5 skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa — "the perfection of the moment" (kṣaṇa-saṃpad?) vs "the perfect moment(s)". Q: S5 the scope of the quoted instruction (where it begins: "phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang" may sit outside, = "that [fourth], with the external mudrā …"). Q: "de bzhin" pun de bzhin / de bzhin nyid (tathā / tathatā) carried by "thus / thusness". Q: S6 na read as "since" (given) vs "if".

## U11
