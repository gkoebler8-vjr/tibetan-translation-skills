# rays.construal.md — nyi ma'i 'od zer, opening (homage, citation, commitment)

BRIEF (given): audience seasoned practitioner; purpose publication (reader edition, Garchen Stiftung manner); Sanskrit anglicized w/o diacritics; Tibetan names phonetic; no brackets/Wylie/locators in body; verse citations in citation mode; no contractions; em dashes allowed; doubts as Q: lines. Speaker: Ayang Thubten Rinpoche (commentator), first person in U05 ('dud do) and U10 (bshad par bya'o).

PERIOD A = U01–U05 (one Tibetan sentence; finite verb 'dud do in U05; object Shakya seng ge la; U01–U05 are a chain of relative/converbal clauses describing him by the three kayas).

---
UNIT U01
FORM prose; GENRE homage (liturgical-prose); REGISTER elevated homage, commentator to the Buddha (3rd person description)
SOURCE rang don du chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa / khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa la / glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing / rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa / spros pa dang bral ba nam mkha' ltar / srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing
CLAUSES 1 finite-ish: bzhugs (intr, vol, HON) frame ABS subj = elided Buddha (Shakyamuni, named U05); LA-DON ngang du = locus. shing = coord ("and", continuing the period). Elided subject filled from U05.
  sub: chos sku (as dharmakaya) in apposition/adverbial with "rang don du" (purpose/domain: for his own sake);
  'pho 'gyur med pa — attribute of chos sku (unchanging);
  khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa — the element pure by nature (gyis = manner/means: "by nature");
  la — connective after nominalized verb: "and also", links the two purities;
  glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi — adventitious stains completely pacified (zhi intr.); zhing coord;
  rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa — not compounded by causes and conditions (gyis = agent/means);
  spros pa dang bral ba — free from elaboration; nam mkha' ltar — simile, like space (scopes over "free from elaboration", possibly over the whole description).
RELATIONS du = purpose/domain ("for his own sake"); gyis (rang bzhin) = manner; la = additive connective; zhing = coord; gyis (rgyu rkyen) = agent; ltar = simile; kyi = gen; du (ngang du) = locus.
NEG/QUANT 'pho 'gyur med pa (no change/transition); 'dus ma byas (not conditioned); bral (free of). rab tu = completely (intensive preverb; render by verb choice).
SPINE subject elided → in the dharmakaya, he abides in the state of equality of samsara and nirvana; attributes of the dharmakaya stacked before.
CONSTRUAL For his own sake, as the dharmakaya — unchanging; the element that is pure by its nature and in which, moreover, the adventitious stains are completely stilled; not conditioned by causes and conditions; free from elaboration like space — he abides in the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana, and ...
TERMS rang don = his own sake/benefit; chos sku = dharmakaya; khams = element (dhatu); glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains; rgyu rkyen = causes and conditions; 'dus ma byas = unconditioned/not compounded; spros pa = elaboration; srid zhi = samsara and nirvana; mnyam pa nyid = equality.
DOUBTS
 Q: la after rnam par dag pa — additive connective (twofold purity: natural + freed from adventitious stains) vs locative ("in that pure element the stains are stilled"). Taken: additive.
 Q: nam mkha' ltar — scopes over "free from elaboration" only, or over "abides ... like space". Taken: with "free from elaboration".
 Q: rang don du — "for his own sake" (two benefits scheme, contrasted with gzhan don du U03). Taken.

UNIT U03
FORM prose; GENRE homage; REGISTER as U01
SOURCE gzhan don du longs sku nges pa lnga ldan / rgyal tshab sems dpa'i 'khor rnams kyi 'dren pa'i gtso bor snang ba dang
CLAUSES snang (intr, invol): subject elided Buddha as sambhogakaya; gtso bor = terminative/essive "as the chief".
RELATIONS du = purpose ("for the sake of others"); 'i/kyi gen; r (gtso bor) = essive; dang coord → continues.
CONSTRUAL For the sake of others, as the sambhogakaya endowed with the five certainties, he appears as the foremost guide of his retinues of bodhisattvas, the regents of the Victor, and ...
TERMS gzhan don = the sake of others; longs sku = sambhogakaya; nges pa lnga = five certainties; rgyal tshab = regents (heirs to the Victor's office); sems dpa' = bodhisattvas; 'dren pa = guide; gtso bo = chief/foremost.
DOUBTS

 Q: 'dren pa'i gtso bo — "chief of guides" vs "foremost guide". Taken: foremost guide (of the retinue).

UNIT U04
FORM prose; GENRE homage; REGISTER as U01
SOURCE brtse ba'i thugs rje chen po'i sprin las / mchog gi sprul skus / sems can gyi khams la phyogs ris med par khyab cing
CLAUSES khyab (Hackett Erg.Abs; here sprul sku-s ERG/means: "as/through the supreme nirmanakaya") subject elided Buddha; LA-DON khams la = locus/object pervaded; cing = coord → continues.
RELATIONS las = source (from the cloud); gyis (sprul skus) = agent/means; la = locus; par = adverbial (impartially); cing coord.
NEG phyogs ris med par = without partiality.
CONSTRUAL From the cloud of his loving great compassion, as the supreme nirmanakaya he pervades the realms of beings without partiality, and ...
TERMS brtse ba = love; thugs rje chen po = great compassion; mchog gi sprul sku = supreme nirmanakaya; sems can gyi khams = realm(s) of beings; phyogs ris med par = without partiality, impartially.
VERSE-NOTES (prose) image: compassion as cloud, nirmanakaya as what comes forth from it (anticipates rain of dharma in U05) — keep physical.
DOUBTS
 Q: sprul skus — agent ("the supreme nirmanakaya pervades") vs means ("by means of"). Taken: he, as the supreme nirmanakaya (agentive identity).
 Q: sems can gyi khams — "the realm(s) of beings" (sattvadhatu) vs "beings' element/disposition". Taken: realms of beings.

UNIT U05
FORM prose; GENRE homage, main verb; REGISTER as U01, first-person homage at the end
SOURCE mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te / zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis / rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud brlan te / grol bar mdzad pa'i / bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shakya seng ge la / lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do
CLAUSES (1) bstan (ston, past, tr): subject Buddha (nirmanakaya); object elided (himself / the deeds); mdzad pa bcu gnyis su = as/in twelve deeds; te = sequence/manner.
 (2) brlan (tr, fut/pres stem of rlan): subject Buddha; instrument char gyis; object rgyud (mindstreams) of gdul bya; te = sequence → (3).
 (3) grol bar mdzad (HON caus.: liberates) — object the disciples; nominalized 'i → relative clause on bde bar gshegs pa'i gtso bo shakya seng ge.
 (4) 'dud do (tr, vol, final): agent = I (commentator, elided); la = recipient (Shakya Simha); gus pas = manner/cause (with respect); lus ngag yid gsum = with/by the three (instrument, unmarked).
RELATIONS su = essive ("in the form of"); te = sequence; gyis = instrument (rain); pa'i (sad pa'i) = modification; kyi gen; te = sequence/cause (moistens and so); pa'i = relative clause; rnams kyi gtso bo = chief among; la = recipient; pas = manner; do = final.
CONSTRUAL Displaying the twelve deeds, he moistens with the rain of the sublime dharma of peace and happiness the mindstreams of disciples whose potential has been rightly awakened, and so liberates them: to him, Shakya Simha, chief of the sugatas, I bow with respect in body, speech and mind.
TERMS mdzad pa bcu gnyis = the twelve deeds; zhi bde = peace and happiness; dam pa'i chos = sublime dharma; char = rain; rigs = potential (spiritual family/lineage); yang dag par sad pa = rightly/fully awakened; gdul bya = disciples (those to be tamed); rgyud = mindstream; brlan = moisten; grol bar mdzad = liberate; bde bar gshegs pa = sugata; shakya seng ge = Shakya Simha (Lion of the Shakyas); lus ngag yid = body, speech and mind; 'dud = bow.
DOUBTS
 Q: rigs yang dag par sad pa'i — "whose potential has been rightly awakened" (disciples ready) vs "in whom he rightly awakens the potential" (Buddha's act). Taken: state of the disciples (pa'i relative, past).
 Q: zhi bde — "peace and happiness" (dvandva) vs "peaceful bliss". Taken: dvandva.
 Q: shakya seng ge — "Shakya Simha" (name kept as Sanskrit form) vs "Lion of the Shakyas" (translated). Taken: Shakyasimha (Indian name kept in Sanskrit, english.md §7); Alt Lion of the Shakyas.
 Q: bstan te — object: "displaying (himself) in twelve deeds" vs "teaching through the twelve deeds". Taken: displaying the twelve deeds.

PERIOD B = U06–U09 (citation; one sentence, finite verb ston in U09; frame de yang rgyud bla ma las ... ces gsungs so).

UNIT U06–U09 (verse, citation)
FORM verse, 7-syllable padas: 4+4+4+3 = 15 padas (canonical has 16; missing pada bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang after U07 pada 3); citation (las ... ces gsungs so).
GENRE citation in homage context; REGISTER treatise verse, descriptive, elevated-neutral.
CITATION Uttaratantra (rgyud bla ma), Toh 4024, D 64b (BO_T06_D4024:64b-12); dm.py identify VERBATIM MATCH yes, canonical (42 syllables). Also in Toh 4025 (vyākhyā). The citing text itself (BO_EGS_0002) appears in the index.
SOURCE
 6.1 thugs rje chen pos 'jig rten mkhyen / 6.2 'jig rten kun la gzigs nas ni / 6.3 chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par / 6.4 sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis /
 7.1 skye ba mngon par skye ba dang / 7.2 dga' ldan gnas nas 'pho ba dang / 7.3 lhums su 'jug dang bltams pa dang / 7.4 btsun mo'i 'khor dgyes rol ba dang /
 8.1 nges 'byung dka' ba spyod pa dang / 8.2 byang chub snying por gshegs pa dang / 8.3 bdud sde 'joms dang rdzogs par ni / 8.4 byang chub chos kyi 'khor lo dang /
 9.1 mya ngan 'das par gshegs mdzad rnams / 9.2 yongs su ma dag zhing rnams su / 9.3 srid pa ji srid gnas par ston / + ces gsungs so
CLAUSES (a) 'jig rten mkhyen: read as epithet lokavid ("Knower of the World") = subject; thugs rje chen pos = by/through great compassion (instrument/cause), governing gzigs.
 (b) gzigs (HON see, tr) object 'jig rten kun la; nas = sequence ("having beheld").
 (c) ma g.yos par = adverbial negation ("without moving from the dharmakaya"); las = source.
 (d) sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis = means ("through manifold forms whose nature is emanation").
 (e) FINAL ston (tr, pres): he displays; object = the deeds listed 7.1–9.1, headed by gshegs mdzad rnams ("the deeds ... up to passing into nirvana"); LA-DON zhing rnams su = locus; srid pa ji srid gnas par = duration ("as long as existence remains").
RELATIONS pos = instrument/cause; la = object of seeing; nas = sequence; las = source (not moving from); par = manner; kyis = means; dang ×n = coordination of list; nas (gnas nas) = source (from Tushita); su (lhums su) = goal; r (snying por) = goal; rdzogs par ... byang chub = adverbial + noun (split by ni, metrical); su (zhing rnams su) = locus; ji srid = duration; ces = quotative; gsungs = HON said.
NEG/QUANT kun (all the world); ma g.yos (never moving); ma dag (impure); rnams (plural of deeds, collects the list); ji srid (as long as).
VERSE-NOTES ni (6.2, 8.3) filler; rdzogs par ni / byang chub = "perfect awakening" split across padas; chos kyi 'khor lo — verb (turning) elided; enumerative run — one deed per line, keep order; 12 deeds with "skill in the arts" missing in this Tibetan.
SPINE one sentence; main verb ston at the very end; subject = the Knower of the World / Buddha.
CONSTRUAL Through his great compassion the Knower of the World, having beheld the whole world, without moving from the dharmakaya, through emanations of various kinds, displays — taking birth, transferring from the abode of Tushita, entering the womb, being born, delighting in the company of queens, leaving home, practicing austerities, going to the seat of awakening, vanquishing the hosts of mara, perfect awakening, the wheel of dharma, and passing into nirvana: these deeds — in impure realms for as long as existence remains.
TERMS 'jig rten mkhyen = Knower of the World; chos kyi sku = dharmakaya; sprul pa = emanation; dga' ldan = Tushita; byang chub snying po = seat of enlightenment (bodhimanda); bdud = mara; rdzogs par byang chub = perfect enlightenment; chos kyi 'khor lo = wheel of dharma; mya ngan 'das pa = nirvana; ma dag zhing = impure realms; srid pa = existence.
DOUBTS
 Q: 6.1 'jig rten mkhyen — epithet lokavid (subject) vs predicate "with great compassion he knows the world". Taken: epithet.
 Q: 7.1 skye ba mngon par skye ba — "taking birth" (one item, rebirth among the Tushita gods / birth in general) vs two items "births and rebirths". Taken: one item, "taking birth".
 Q: missing pada (bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang, skill in the arts) — translate as given (omitted) and note.
 Q: 9.1 gshegs mdzad rnams — "deeds" heads whole list (taken) vs "the passing into nirvana" only.

FRAME de yang rgyud bla ma las — de yang = elaborating connective ("in this regard"); English short title *Sublime Continuum* (Uttaratantra); ces gsungs so = closing quotative, absorbed by the citation formula.

PERIOD C = U10 (one period; finite verb bshad par bya'o: "I shall explain"; first person commentator).

UNIT U10
FORM prose; GENRE exposition / framing (commitment to explain, sa bcad "second"); REGISTER exposition, elevated by simile, commentator to reader.
SOURCE gnyis pa ni / phung po dang 'chi bdag dang lha'i bu dang nyon mongs pa ste bdud bzhi las rgyal ba'i thub pa mchog gi gsung rab / rtag chad kyi mtha' dang bral ba / rgya mtsho'i klong ltar dpag tu med pa las / mdo sde dang rgyud sde rnams dang / klu sgrub dang thogs med la sogs pa'i bstan bcos dang / chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po'i man ngag zab mo'i don / klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i nang nas / ma rtogs par dang log par rtog pa dang the tshom gyi dri ma med par bsdus pa 'di ni / rin chen rnams kyi mchog baidurya la gsol ba btab na dgos 'dod thams cad 'byung ba bzhin / gnas skabs dang mthar thug gi don thams cad 'grub par byed pa'i bstan bcos / gzhi dang lam dang 'bras bu gsum du bstan pa'i snying po / bshad par bya'o
CLAUSES
 (a) rgyal ba'i (rgyal intr: victorious over; las = from/over) → modifies thub pa mchog.
 (b) gsung rab: attributes rtag chad mtha' dang bral ba; dpag tu med pa (rgya mtsho'i klong ltar = simile).
 (c) las (after dpag tu med pa) = partitive source: out of these scriptures → the sutras and tantras.
 (d) list in nang nas: [mdo sde dang rgyud sde rnams] dang [klu sgrub ... bstan bcos] dang [chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po'i man ngag zab mo'i don]; bkur 'os pa'i modifies the list (or the last item).
 (e) bsdus pa (sdud, past, tr): agent elided (Zhedang Dorje, the root author); "condensed from among these, without the stains of ..."; 'di ni = this [text], topic.
 (f) conditional simile: baidurya la gsol ba btab na (condition) dgos 'dod thams cad 'byung ba (result) bzhin (as).
 (g) 'grub par byed pa'i (caus.: accomplishes) → modifies bstan bcos; object don thams cad (gnas skabs dang mthar thug gi).
 (h) bstan pa'i snying po: gzhi lam 'bras bu gsum du = as three; bstan pa = taught/presented (also pun on title theg chen bstan pa'i snying po).
 (i) FINAL bshad par bya'o: agent I (commentator), object the snying po / bstan bcos; future/intentional.
RELATIONS ste = apposition (namely) for the four maras; las (bdud bzhi las) = over (victory from); gi gen; kyi gen; dang bral = free from; ltar = simile; las = partitive source; dang ×n coord; pa'i gen/mod; ltar = simile (crown jewel); nang nas = from among; par = manner (without stains); ni topic; la = recipient of prayer; na = condition; bzhin = as/just as; r ('grub par) = purpose/result of byed; pa'i = relative; du = essive (as three); pa'i = mod; r (bshad par) + bya = intention.
NEG/QUANT dpag tu med = immeasurable; ma rtogs pa = not understanding; med par = without; thams cad ×2 = all/every.
SPINE Tibetan: one sentence. English: 4–5 sentences; seams: "out of these" (las), "from among these" (nang nas), "just as ... so" (bzhin), apposition for the head.
CONSTRUAL Second. The scriptures of the Supreme Sage, victorious over the four maras — the aggregates, the Lord of Death, the son of the gods and the afflictions — are free from the extremes of permanence and annihilation and immeasurable like the depths of the ocean. Out of them come the sutras and the tantras. From among these, the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga and others, and the meaning of the profound pith instructions of the great Dharma Lord Drikungpa — worthy of honor like the jewel on the naga king's crown — this text has been condensed, without the stains of non-understanding, misunderstanding and doubt. Just as every need and wish arises when one prays to the vaidurya, best of jewels, this treatise accomplishes every aim, temporary and ultimate. I shall explain it: the essence of the teachings, presented as the three — ground, path and fruit.
TERMS thub pa mchog = the Supreme Sage; bdud bzhi = the four maras; phung po = aggregates; 'chi bdag = Lord of Death; lha'i bu = son of the gods; nyon mongs pa = afflictions; gsung rab = scriptures; rtag chad kyi mtha' = extremes of eternalism and nihilism; mdo sde / rgyud sde = sutras / tantras; klu sgrub = Nagarjuna; thogs med = Asanga; bstan bcos = treatise; chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po = the great Dharma Lord Drikungpa (Jigten Sumgön); man ngag = pith instructions; klu rgyal = naga king; gtsug gi nor bu = crown jewel; baidurya = vaidurya; gnas skabs dang mthar thug = temporary and ultimate; gzhi lam 'bras bu = ground, path and fruit; snying po = essence.
DOUBTS
 Q: head of the period — apposition "this treatise, the essence (of the teachings) presented as ground, path and fruit" vs genitive "the essence of this treatise". Taken: apposition, with the title pun (theg chen bstan pa'i snying po) carried by "the essence of the teachings".
 Q: bkur 'os pa'i — modifies Drikungpa's instructions only vs all three sources. Taken: Drikungpa's instructions (nearest; the naga-king crown-jewel image fits the lineage master's word).
 Q: first las (dpag tu med pa las) — partitive (sutras and tantras out of the scriptures) vs a fourth parallel source. Taken: partitive.
 Q: bsdus pa 'di — agent elided: the root author Zhedang Dorje (the root text "this compendium"). Not stated in English.
 Q: ma rtogs par dang log par rtog pa — "not understanding and misunderstanding" (two) — taken.

UNIT U12
FORM prose; GENRE exposition; REGISTER exposition, plain.
SOURCE dgos pa ni / thos bsam gyis de rtogs pa'o / dgos pa'i dgos pa ni / de'i don bsgoms pas 'bras bu mngon du 'gyur ba'o / 'brel ba ni / snga ma la brten te phyi ma rim gyis thabs dang thabs las byung ba'i tshul gyis 'brel pa'o
CLAUSES (1) rtogs (tr): means thos bsam gyis; object de (= ground, path, fruit); agent general (the student). (2) bsgoms pas (cause) → 'bras bu mngon du 'gyur (intr: becomes manifest). (3) 'brel (intr): subject phyi ma (the later); snga ma la brten te = relying on the earlier; rim gyis = gradually/in sequence; tshul gyis = in the manner of means and what arises from means.
RELATIONS gyis = means; pas = cause; la = object of reliance; te = manner/sequence; rim gyis = adverbial; las = source (arisen from the means); tshul gyis = manner.
CONSTRUAL The purpose: to understand these through study and reflection. The ultimate purpose: through meditating on their meaning, the fruit becomes manifest. The connection: relying on what comes before, each later one is connected in sequence in the manner of means and what arises from the means.
TERMS dgos pa = purpose; dgos pa'i dgos pa = ultimate purpose; thos bsam = study and reflection; bsgom = meditate; 'bras bu = fruit; mngon du 'gyur = become manifest; 'brel ba = connection; thabs = means.
DOUBTS
 Q: snga ma / phyi ma — the four points themselves (subject → purpose → ultimate purpose; standard reading) vs ground → path → fruit. Taken: generic "each later one relies on the one before" (leaves both open); alternative: name the four points.
 Q: de rtogs pa — de = the subject matter (ground, path, fruit). Taken.
