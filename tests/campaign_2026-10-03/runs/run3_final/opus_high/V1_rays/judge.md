## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U07 "taking birth": the two canonical items (jātaka, and the birth in Tushita) are collapsed into one that duplicates "being born". Cause: construal fork (recorded, settled against the Sanskrit).
2. U10 "the essence of the teachings, set out as three": bstan pa is used twice, adding a genitive the phrase does not carry. Cause: term choice (title pun forced into the line).
3. U10 bkur 'os pa'i is limited to Drikungpa's instructions when it more naturally qualifies all the sources before nang nas. Cause: construal fork (nearest-head attachment).
4. U09: the closing locus and duration lines dangle five lines after the verb "he shows", so the citation's main clause is hard to follow. Cause: verse constraint (the verb fronted, the list hung from it).
5. U01–U05: the single homage period is cut into a string of "he" sentences, which removes the build-up to "I bow" and makes U01 choppy. Cause: register (prose sentence-splitting rule applied to liturgical homage).
