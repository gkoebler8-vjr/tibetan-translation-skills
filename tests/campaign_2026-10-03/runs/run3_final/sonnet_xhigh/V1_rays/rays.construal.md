UNIT        U01
FORM        prose (one converb clause, ends bzhugs shing; first of 5 units of the homage period)
GENRE       liturgy-in-prose: expression of worship (commentary opening)
REGISTER    commentator addressing the Buddha through the reader; elevated but current, unhurried; no archaism; the period is split in English, cataphoric "he"
SOURCE      rang don du chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa la / glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing / rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa / spros pa dang bral ba nam mkha' ltar / srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing /
WORDS       chos sku = dharmakaya · 'pho 'gyur med pa = without change · khams = basic element (dhatu; RGV sense) · rnam par dag pa = (utterly) pure · glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains · rab tu zhi = completely pacified · 'dus ma byas pa = not made up/compounded by causes and conditions (unconditioned) · spros pa = elaboration · srid zhi = samsara and nirvana (srid pa / zhi ba) · mnyam pa nyid = being equal · ngang = state
CLAUSES     1 verb=bzhugs (intr., voluntary, hon.) frame=ABS subject elided (= Shakyasimha, U05); chos sku is what he abides as, with all the attributes below; tense=present/habitual; hon=honorific
            attribute chain on chos sku (verbless, nominal predicates): 'pho 'gyur med pa / khams ... rnam par dag pa / glo bur gyi dri ma ... zhi / 'dus ma byas pa / spros pa dang bral ba
RELATIONS   rang don du: du = beneficiary/purpose ("for his own benefit")
            gyis (khams rang bzhin gyis) = means/manner ("by nature")
            la (after rnam par dag pa) = connective, concessive-simultaneous "while" (alt: locus; alt: "and")
            zhing = coordination/sequence to U02 (and)
            gyis (rgyu rkyen gyis) = agent/means of 'dus byed (negated)
            dang (spros pa dang) = comitative with bral ba (free from)
            ltar = simile (like space)
            kyi = genitive (state of the equality of samsara and nirvana); du = locus (in that state)
NEG/QUANT   'pho 'gyur med pa (no change) · 'dus ma byas pa (not made up; scope = causes and conditions are not what compound it) · bral ba (free of elaboration). Dictionary NEG hit "dri ma rab" is false (dri ma = stain). rab tu = intensifier/prefix (completely)
SPINE       1 Tibetan clause; English: one sentence, finite verb "abides" first, appositive list after colon
CONSTRUAL   For his own benefit (his = Shakyasimha's, the head noun of U05) the dharmakaya abides in the state in which samsara and nirvana are equal. It does not change; its basic element is pure by its own nature while its adventitious stains are completely pacified; it is not made by causes and conditions, and it is free of elaboration, like space. [REVISED after the MITRA flag: the bare noun chos sku is the subject of bzhugs; was: "he abides as the dharmakaya".]
TERMS       chos sku = dharmakaya · khams = basic element · glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains · spros pa = elaboration · srid zhi = samsara and nirvana
DOUBTS      Q: chos sku — "the dharmakaya abides" (taken, after the MITRA flag: bare chos sku / longs sku are subjects, sprul skus is the ergative agent of khyab (Erg. Abs. frame); the three kayas are Shakyasimha's, the owner named last) vs. "he abides as the dharmakaya" (Shakyasimha as the one subject of the whole relative chain).
            Q: khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa — "its basic element is pure by nature" (taken) vs. "naturally pure in the nature of the element" vs. "the naturally pure element".
            Q: la after rnam par dag pa — "while the adventitious stains are completely pacified" (taken) vs. locus "in what is pure by nature the stains are pacified" vs. plain "and".
            Q: nam mkha' ltar — "free of elaboration, like space" (taken, set last in the list so it may read with the whole) vs. "like space, abides in the state ...".
            Q: head noun / subject of period = Shakyasimha (U05) — cataphoric "he" in English U01-U04.

UNIT        U05
FORM        prose (converb chain, ends 'dud do: main verb of the whole period)
GENRE       liturgy-in-prose: homage, expression of worship (closing the one period)
REGISTER    as U01; the main verb is first person (the commentator bows)
SOURCE      mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te / zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis / rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud brlan te / grol bar mdzad pa'i / bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shakya seng ge la / lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do /
WORDS       mdzad pa bcu gnyis = the twelve deeds · bstan = display, show · zhi bde = peace and happiness · dam pa'i chos = the sublime Dharma · char = rain (tibdict split cha+r) · rigs = lineage/family/gotra → potential (RGV/lam rim sense) · sad pa = awaken · gdul bya = disciples · rgyud = mindstream (NOT tantra) · brlan = moisten · grol ba = liberation · mdzad = hon. do · bde bar gshegs pa = sugata · gtso bo = chief · shakya seng ge = Shakyasimha (epithet "Lion of the Shakyas") · lus ngag yid gsum = body, speech, and mind · gus pa = reverence · 'dud = bow
CLAUSES     1 bstan te (tr., voluntary; honorific mdzad pa'd): [he] shows the twelve deeds — te = sequence
            2 brlan te (tr.): [he] moistens the mindstreams, agent chos kyi char gyis (means: with the rain of the Dharma) — te = sequence
            3 grol bar mdzad pa'i (honorific "makes/does to be liberated"): [he] brings them to liberation; pa'i = relative clause modifying the head (gtso bo shakya seng ge): the whole chain U01-U05 is one relative-modifier of "Shakyasimha"
            4 MAIN: 'dud do (tr., voluntary; imperative/present; closing particle do): I bow; agent gus pas = manner/means (in reverence), lus ngag yid gsum = by body, speech and mind; recipient la = to Shakyasimha
RELATIONS   su (mdzad pa bcu gnyis su) = la-don "as/in the form of": display the twelve deeds
            te = sequence (displaying, then moistening, then liberating)
            gyis (chos kyi char gyis) = means: with the rain of the Dharma
            kyi (zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char) = genitive/apposition: rain of the Dharma; 'i = modification (sublime)
            pa'i (rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya) = relative: disciples whose potential has awakened; kyi (gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud) = genitive: their mindstreams
            la (shakya seng ge la) = recipient; s (gus pas) = manner
NEG/QUANT   no negation; rnams = plural disciples/sugatas; gsum = the three (body, speech, mind) all engaged; yang dag par = properly/fully (prefix)
SPINE       English: S1 (he displays ... and moistens ... and brings to liberation) + S2 (I bow ... to him, Shakyasimha) with main verb in S2
CONSTRUAL   He shows the twelve deeds and, with the rain of the sublime Dharma of peace and happiness, moistens the mindstreams of the disciples whose potential has awakened, and brings them to liberation. In reverence I bow, with body, speech, and mind, to him, Shakyasimha, chief of the sugatas.
TERMS       mdzad pa bcu gnyis = twelve deeds · bde bar gshegs pa = sugata · shakya seng ge = Shakyasimha · rigs = potential (gotra) · rgyud = mindstream
DOUBTS      Q: rigs yang dag par sad pa — "whose potential has awakened" (taken; rigs = gotra, buddha-family) vs. "family/lineage" literal vs. "fitting kind (suitable vessels)".
            Q: mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan — "shows the twelve deeds" (taken) vs. "shows himself as/in the form of the twelve deeds".
            Q: shakya seng ge — "Shakyasimha" (taken, the attested epithet) vs. "the Lion of the Shakyas"; brief names Shakyamuni, text does not (no thub pa).
            Q: subject shift: U01-U04 have the three kayas as subjects (taken), U05's converbs (bstan te, brlan te, mdzad pa'i) take Shakyasimha as subject, since mdzad pa'i modifies him; rendered "he" (cataphoric to the name at the end).
            Q: head of the relative chain = shakya seng ge, not "sugatas" — grol bar mdzad pa'i ... gtso bo: "Shakyasimha, chief of the sugatas" taken (apposition), vs "chief of the sugatas, Shakyasimha" order only.

UNIT        U06
FORM        verse: 7x4 as given, citation (frame de yang rgyud bla ma las ...); frame in prose
GENRE       citation in a commentary (supporting the twelve deeds of U05)
REGISTER    quoted authoritative verse; exposition-plus-homage temperature, plain and dignified
SOURCE      de yang rgyud bla ma las / thugs rje chen pos 'jig rten mkhyen / 'jig rten kun la gzigs nas ni / chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par / sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis /
WORDS       thugs rje chen po = great compassion · mkhyen = know (hon.) · gzigs = look on (hon.) · chos kyi sku = dharmakaya (same as chos sku) · ma g.yos pa = not stirring, unmoved (tibdict listed g.yos pa as "trembling"; it is the verb "to stir/move") · sprul pa'i rang bzhin = emanations (nirmanatman; rang bzhin = nature, absorbs as "natures") · sna tshogs = manifold, of many kinds
CLAUSES     p1: verb=mkhyen (tr., voluntary, honorific) frame=ERG thugs rje chen pos (means/cause) / ABS 'jig rten / subject elided (the Buddha)
            p2: verb=gzigs nas (tr., hon.; converb "having looked") frame=obj with la (kun la = on all); ni = filler/topic (metrical)
            p3: ma g.yos par = negated verb with terminative par = adverbial "without stirring"; source las = from the dharmakaya
            p4: kyis = means/instrument ("by/through emanations of many natures"); goes with the finite verb ston (U09)
RELATIONS   pos (thugs rje chen pos) = means/cause ("with great compassion"); nas = sequence (having looked, then ...); las = source/separation ("from the dharmakaya"); par = manner; kyis = means; p1-p2 have no explicit connective (mkhyen stands bare): "and" supplied
NEG/QUANT   ma g.yos pa (not stirring from the dharmakaya: the dharmakaya is not left); kun (all the world)
VERSE-NOTES filler: ni in p2 (count); par = terminative (not "to"); pos = ergative of the short form (count); anaphora 'jig rten x2 kept in English (the world / the whole world)
CONSTRUAL   With great compassion he (the Buddha) knows the world. Having looked on the whole world, without stirring from the dharmakaya and by emanations of many natures, he shows (U09) the deeds listed in U07-U09, in the impure realms, for as long as existence lasts. Frame: "And in the Uttaratantra it is said:" (de yang = and; las = in; the work named; ces gsungs so closes the quotation at U09).
TERMS       chos kyi sku = dharmakaya · thugs rje chen po = great compassion · sprul pa = emanation
CITATION    see block above: Uttaratantra, Toh 4024; verbatim NO only because one pada is missing here
DOUBTS      Q: mkhyen is bare (no connective): "he knows the world and, having looked ..." (taken; two actions of the one subject) vs. "he who knows the world through great compassion, having looked ..., shows ..." (relative/appositive).
            Q: subject number: "he" (Shakyasimha, continuing U05; taken) vs. "they" (the buddhas generally, as in the Uttaratantra).
            Q: sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis — "through emanations of many natures" (taken; rang bzhin absorbed as "natures", Skt nirmanatman) vs. "emanated forms of many kinds".
            Q: de yang read as the connective "and" (this too is said ...) rendered in the frame "And in the Uttaratantra it is said:".
