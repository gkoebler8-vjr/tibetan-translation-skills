## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U06 "With great compassion he knows the world / and, having looked ...": the epithet 'jig rten mkhyen (lokavid) becomes a finite verb, and the compassion is attached to knowing rather than to looking. Cause: construal fork (resolved toward the surface word order, without the Sanskrit).
2. U07 "the taking of birth": skye ba / mngon par skye ba (Jatakas plus the taking of birth) is collapsed into one item. Cause: omission under compression (verse constraint, with one noun phrase per line).
3. U01 "its basic element ... its adventitious stains": the twofold purity of the element is recast as things the dharmakaya possesses, and "his" dangles cataphorically. Cause: grammar misread when the attributive chain was split into predications.
4. U05 "whose potential has awakened": yang dag par is dropped. Cause: omission under compression (the adverb was "absorbed").
5. U10/U12 calqued scaffolding: "the three of ground, path, and result", "It comes from within", and "the latter is connected in stages, in the manner of a means ..."; snying po is not marked as the root text's title. Cause: register (literalism surviving the style pass).
