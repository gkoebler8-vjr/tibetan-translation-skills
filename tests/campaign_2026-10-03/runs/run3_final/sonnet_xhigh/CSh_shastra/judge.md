## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U05: the reported saying is scoped back over the commentator's own yin gyi ... mi bya'o clause, with the invented frame "But there is this saying". Diagnosis: construal fork (grammar misread of yin gyi as a break, not a contrastive continuation).
2. U07: snying gi nor bur (locus) is read as an essive, and the attributive bsdus pa'i gzugs can becomes a finite "it gathers", so prajñājñāna becomes the heart-jewel. Diagnosis: grammar misread.
3. U06: "the jewel abiding in the secret [place]" is made an epithet of the guru's instruction, which erases the practice content of the secret empowerment. Diagnosis: dictionary sense / term choice (the technical sense of nor bu and gsang ba is missed).
4. U10: ngag las is read as a comparative ("more direct than speech"), which inverts the concession that mirrors U09. Diagnosis: grammar misread under argument-fit pressure (construal fork resolved the wrong way).
5. U11: reading A was adopted on MITRA's flag, and the probable rgyas/brgya corruption was not considered. Diagnosis: skill rule misapplied (pada-alignment heuristic outranked sense).
