## U01
UNIT U01 | FORM prose (commentary gloss) | GENRE exposition | REGISTER neutral-scholarly commentary gloss; quoted lemma, then explanation
SOURCE sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa la / sdom pa ni dbang bskur ba'o // de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste / 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no // de yang ji skad du
WORDS sdom pa=vow (not "restraint"; glossed here as empowerment); dbye ba=division; rab tu dbye ba=subdivision/classification; mtshan nyid can=having the characteristics; 'chad par 'gyur ba=what will be explained (future, passive sense)
CLAUSES 5 | (1) frame: la = locus "in/as for [the words] ..."; (2) sdom pa ni dbang bskur ba'o: copular gloss lemma=gloss; (3) de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste: topic=gloss "its subdivision", predicate=lemma "division"; ste = sequence/explicative; (4) [subject elided: the divisions] 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no = "having characteristics that will be explained"; (5) de yang ji skad du = lead-in, verb of saying elided
RELATIONS la=locus(frame); ni=topic; ste=sequence (clause 3->4); 'i=genitive (de'i = of the vow/empowerment); yang=additive; du=la-don; ji skad du=as-said (formula, quotation follows outside span)
NEG/QUANT none; la sogs pa = "and so on" (the lemma is an incipit of a root passage)
SPINE 4 English sentences; last = lead-in formula, end on it
CONSTRUAL In the words "the divisions of the vow, and so on": the "vow" is empowerment. Its subdivision is the "division", [namely divisions] having the characteristics that will be explained. And as is also said of this: [quotation follows].
TERMS sdom pa=vow; dbang bskur ba=empowerment; dbye ba=division; rab tu dbye ba=subdivision
CITATION lemma "sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa" (7 syll.): dm.py identify -> verbatim only in Toh 1183 itself (BO_T02_D1183:6a-10); other hits run 4-5 syllables. Source: root-text incipit; not located outside the commentary.
DOUBTS Q1: in "de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste" the topic is the gloss and the predicate the lemma (reverse of clause 2); alt: "its subdivision" is the lemma and "division" the gloss. Rendered with the scaffold '"Division" means its subdivision' (the equation is symmetric); the literal order 'Its subdivision is the "division"' is the alternative.
       Q2: de in "de yang" = the division just defined (taken), alt: the whole preceding explanation; de'i = of the vow (=empowerment).

## U02
UNIT U02 | FORM verse, 4 padas x 7 syll. (7/7/7/7), followed by the closing frame "ces gsungs pa yin no" | GENRE root verse quoted in commentary (etymology of abhiṣeka) | REGISTER definitional; neutral
SOURCE sems can rnams la phan pa'i phyir // dbang bskur ba ni bzhi ru bsgrags // 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur bas // de phyir dbang bskur zhes byar brjod // ces gsungs pa yin no
WORDS bsgrags=past of sgrog "proclaim"; bkrus=past of 'khrud "wash, cleanse"; shing = connective "and" (tibdict's "great tree" is the slip); bskur = past of skur "bestow" (the etymology plays on dbang + bskur)
CLAUSES 2 sentences | S1 (pada 1-2): finite verb bsgrags (past, tr.; agent elided: the speaker of the root text, cf. U08 bcom ldan 'das kyis), object dbang bskur ba (topic ni), bzhi ru = terminative "as four / in four [kinds]"; phan pa'i phyir = purpose, la = recipient (sems can rnams la)
        S2 (pada 3-4): [one] is washed (bkrus) and empowered (dbang bskur) by this ('di yis = instrument), bas = cause; de phyir = therefore; [it] is called (zhes byar brjod, passive, agent elided) "dbang bskur"
RELATIONS phyir=purpose (p1->p2); yis=instrument; shing=coordination; bas=cause (p3->p4); de phyir=consequence; zhes byar brjod = naming
VERSE-NOTES no filler to mark beyond ni/ru/r; byar = bya+r (fused); order kept
SPINE 2 sentences: purpose + proclamation; cause + naming
CONSTRUAL For the benefit of sentient beings, empowerment is proclaimed as fourfold. Since one is washed and empowered by this, it is therefore called "empowerment". [closing frame:] This is what was said (gsungs, honorific; speaker not named; cf. U08: the Bhagavān).
TERMS sems can=sentient beings; dbang bskur=empowerment; bkrus=washed
CITATION identify: 28-syllable verbatim run only in Toh 1183 itself (BO_T02_D1183:6a-15) -> the commentary is the only canonical witness found; root source not located by identify. (The brief says the commentator quotes root padas.)
DOUBTS Q1: agent of bsgrags/gsungs unnamed; U08 shows the Bhagavān is the root speaker; kept unnamed in the verse, bracketed nowhere. Q2: 'di yis = "by this" (the ablution/empowerment), antecedent unstated; kept "this". Q3: bzhi ru = "as four [kinds]" (terminative) vs "in four [places]"; taken as four kinds (cf. U03 "bzhi po").

## U04
UNIT U04 | FORM verse, 2 padas x 7 syll., quoted root padas + commentary frame | GENRE root verse (incipit) + gloss frame | REGISTER neutral
SOURCE slob dpon gsang ba shes rab dang // de ltar de bzhin bzhi pa yang // zhes bya ba la sogs pas 'chad par 'gyur ba yin no
WORDS slob dpon=master; gsang ba=the secret; shes rab=wisdom; de ltar=in that way; de bzhin=likewise (glossed by the commentary in U08 as a term, tathatā); bzhi pa=the fourth; yang=also (glossed in U08)
CLAUSES padas: verbless enumeration (elided: empowerment); frame: finite 'gyur ba yin = future "will be explained"; pas = instrument/means "by [the words]"; subject elided (the four empowerments / their subdivisions, from U03)
RELATIONS dang=coordination; yang=also; zhes bya ba la sogs pa = "[the words] ..., and so on"; r=terminative (par 'gyur)
SPINE verse block (2 lines) + one frame clause
CONSTRUAL [The four empowerments] will be explained by the words that begin "master, secret, and wisdom, and in that way, likewise, the fourth also", and so on.
TERMS as above; "de ltar"="in that way" kept so that U08's "'that' means prajñājñāna" has an anchor
CITATION identify: first pada verbatim (14-syll. run) in Toh 1190 (Tengyur, 'rab tu gsal ba'i kha sbyor' commentary, D 163a) and Toh 2255; in the root Hevajra Tantra, Toh 418 (Kangyur) D 17a the pair reads "slob dpon gsang ba shes rab dang / bzhi bde yang de bzhin no" (7-syll. run only): the commentary's second pada is a variant of Toh 418. 
DOUBTS Q1: second pada differs from Toh 418 (variant); the commentary's reading is translated and glossed in U08. Q2: subject of 'chad par 'gyur ba elided: supplied "[the empowerments]" (alt: "[their subdivisions]").

## U05
UNIT U05 | FORM prose, 8 sentences | GENRE exposition with reported objection (debate) | REGISTER neutral-scholarly exposition; objection reported; register shifts to instruction/prescription at "de lta bas na ... bya'o"
SOURCE (sentences) 1 rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud 'dir ni ... de'i sdom pa yin pas so // 2 slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba'i ngo bo gang ... zhes bya ba yin gyi / yang bya ba'i rgyud la sogs pa dang thun mong du gyur pa'i phyir mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba thob pas slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o // 3 de lta bas na ... 'gyur ro zhes bya ba'i tshig 'di ni ci yang ma yin no // 4 de lta bas na dang po rer zhig ... dbang bskur bar bya'o // 5 yang de ni mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du ... rig par bya'o
WORDS slob dpon=master; kun du spyod pa=to practise/conduct oneself in every way (the etymological gloss of slob dpon, ā-cāra); ring du gyur pa=far removed; sdig pa mi dge ba'i chos=sinful, nonvirtuous phenomena; ngo bo=essence; dga' ba=joy; skad cig ma=moment; rab tu bsgrub pa=accomplishing (rab tu not rendered as adverb); thun mong du gyur pa=shared in common; mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba=irreversible empowerment; skal pa=fortune; dbang bar bya ba=to gain authority/entitlement (dbang ba, adhikāra-sense); mngon par rtogs pa=realization (abhisamaya?); nye bar mtshon pa=indicated
CLAUSES S1: finite yin (copular, pas=cause) "since that is the vow of that"; 'gyur-less: kun du spyod pa 'i phyir = reason for naming slob dpon; subject elided (the master/ the one empowering). S2: yin gyi = concessive-contrastive "is ..., but"; second part reports the opponent's claim: mi bya'o (neg. gerundive, impersonal). S3: 'gyur ro = opponent's conclusion; tshig 'di ni ci yang ma yin no = main clause: "this statement is nothing at all" (neg. copular). S4: bya'o prescriptive gerundive, impersonal: "[one] is to be empowered". S5: rig par bya'o prescriptive: "is to be known".
RELATIONS S1 phyir=reason; te=namely (explicative); kyi=gen; pas so=cause. S2 las=source (karmamudrā as source: "from the karmamudrā"); bzhis=instrument (by the four moments); 'i=gen; gyi=contrastive "but" after yin; yang=however/further; dang=coordination; phyir=cause (common with Kriyā); pas=cause/sequence (thob pas); gyis=agent (master); bas=instrument; la=locus/respect. S3 de lta bas na=therefore (opponent's conclusion); zhes bya ba'i tshig 'di=quote frame (closing). S4 de lta bas na=therefore; phyir=purpose; nyid kyis=instrument "by the master empowerment itself"; r bya'o=prescription. S5 yang=moreover; ji lta ba bzhin du=just as; kyis=instrument; par=terminative.
NEG/QUANT mi ldog pa (irreversible: negation inside the compound); mi bya'o (prescriptive negation: "is not to"); ci yang ma yin no ("nothing at all": ci yang + ma yin = total denial); thams cad (all the yoga and yoginī tantras); la sogs pa (and so on) x3
SPINE S1: 1 sentence (naming + reason). S2: 1 long sentence -> English 2 sentences (definition; "But" + reported claim). S3: tail of reported claim + verdict. S4: 1. S5: 1.
CONSTRUAL
 S1. In this yoginī tantra, [the word] "master" [is used] because he practises, in every way, being far removed from sinful, nonvirtuous phenomena -- that is, because that is the vow of that very [person/empowerment].
 S2. The essence of empowerment by the master is what is called "accomplishing, from the karmamudrā, by means of the four moments, the intrinsic nature of the four joys". But [the claim runs]: "Because [this] is shared in common with the Kriyā tantras and the rest, and because [one] has obtained the irreversible empowerment, [one] is not to [confer/receive empowerment] by the master's empowerment as the empowerment. (S3) Therefore one becomes endowed with the fortune to expound, hear and so on all the yoga and yoginī tantras" -- this statement is nothing at all.
 S4. Therefore, first of all, in order to gain authority to hear, meditate on and so on the yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, [one] is to be empowered by the master's empowerment itself.
 S5. Moreover, that [empowerment] is to be known as indicated by the characteristics of the master, the secret, wisdom and the fourth, just as [set out] in the realization.
TERMS slob dpon=master; dbang bskur ba=empowerment; las kyi phyag rgya=karmamudrā; skad cig ma=moment; dga' ba=joy; bya ba'i rgyud=Kriyā tantra; mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur=irreversible empowerment; rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud=yoginī tantra; dgyes pa'i rdo rje=Hevajra
DOUBTS Q1 (S1) "de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas": double genitive; taken "that [conduct] is the vow of that [= the master/his empowerment]"; alt: "because it is that very [master]'s vow", or "that very vow of his". Q2 (S1) slob dpon = the person (ācārya) or the empowerment named after him ("master [empowerment]"); the etymology treats the person; the next sentence the empowerment. Q3 (S2) scope of the reported claim: taken to begin at "yang bya ba'i rgyud ... " (after "yin gyi") and to end at "'gyur ro"; alt: it begins at "slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba'i ngo bo". Q4 (S2) "slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o": rendered "is not to be [done]: empowerment by way of the master's empowerment"; the sense is: a further/prior master empowerment is not required (it contradicts S4); alt: "is not to be given to the one who is empowered". Q5 (S2) gang taken as relative ("what is called ..."); alt: part of "gang las kyi phyag rgya" ("whichever karmamudrā"). Q6 (S2) mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba: "irreversible empowerment" taken as the empowerment already received in the lower tantras. Q7 (S5) mngon par rtogs pa: generic "realization" vs a named text (abhisamaya, perhaps Kāṇha's own Hevajra abhisamaya); rendered lower-case "the realization". Q8 (S4) dgyes pa'i rdo rje = Hevajra (the root of this commentary, Toh 1183 'dgyes pa rdo rje'i dka' 'grel'); alt: "Joyous Vajra" as a title.

## U06
UNIT U06 | FORM prose, 3 sentences | GENRE exposition (prescriptive: grading of disciples) | REGISTER neutral exposition with impersonal prescription ("is to be taught")
SOURCE 1 de lta bu'i slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba thob pa'i dbang po rtul po rnams la ni ... bsgom pa bstan par bya ba yin no // 2 de bzhin du rdzogs pa'i rim pa'i phyogs la yang ... gsang ba'i dbang bskur ba zhes bya bar brjod de / ... phyir ro // 3 'dir dbang bskur ba'i dbang po 'bring po rnams la ni dam tshig gi phyag rgya bsgom pa nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o
WORDS dbang po rtul po=dull faculties; 'bring po=middling; mos pa=conviction; nges par bzung nas=having firmly taken hold (object elided); spros pa'i phyag rgya=mudrā of elaboration; phyogs=side/aspect (of the completion stage); nor bu=jewel; gsang bar gnas pa=dwelling in secret; nyams su myong ba=to experience; sbyor ba=to apply oneself; nye bar bstan=set out
CLAUSES S1: bya ba yin no (impersonal gerundive "is to be taught"); object bsgom pa (the meditation on the karmamudrā and the mudrā of elaboration); recipients la = those of dull faculties who have received the master's empowerment; nas = sequence (having firmly taken hold through conviction), elided object. S2: brjod = "is called" (impersonal), subject = the experiencing of the four joys; phyir ro = cause; ma yin = negation "is not to be taught". S3: bya'o impersonal; nye bar bstan cing bshad = two coordinated verbs (cing).
RELATIONS la=recipient; ni=topic/contrast; pas=instrument/cause (mos pas); nas=sequence; dang=coordination; kyis=instrument; de bzhin du=in the same way; la yang=also in; te=sequence/pause; phyir=cause (secret because not to be taught to ...); cing=coordination
NEG/QUANT bstan par bya ba ma yin pa (not to be taught); cig ma bzhi'i = false NEG hit (skad cig ma); rnams plural; bzhi po (all four joys)
SPINE S1 1; S2 -> 2 English sentences (naming, then reason "since"); S3 1
CONSTRUAL 1. To those of dull faculties who have received such a master's empowerment, once they have firmly taken hold [of it] through conviction, the meditation on the karmamudrā and the mudrā of elaboration is to be taught. 2. In the same way, in the case of the completion stage too, the experiencing of the four joys, which are the nature of the four moments, through the guru's pith instruction itself -- [a pith instruction that is] a jewel dwelling in secret -- is called the "secret empowerment", since it is not to be taught to yogins who are applying themselves to meditation on elaboration. 3. Here, to those of middling faculties among the empowered, the meditation on the samayamudrā is to be set out and explained.
TERMS dbang po=faculty; rtul po=dull; 'bring po=middling; rab=superior; phyag rgya=seal; las kyi phyag rgya=karmamudrā; spros pa'i phyag rgya=mudrā of elaboration; dam tshig gi phyag rgya=samayamudrā; rdzogs pa'i rim pa=completion stage; bla ma'i man ngag=guru's pith instruction; gsang ba'i dbang bskur ba=secret empowerment
DOUBTS Q1 mos pas nges par bzung nas: elided object and agent: taken as "[the recipients], having firmly taken hold [of the empowerment] through conviction"; alt: "[the teacher], having firmly taken them [as disciples]". Q2 gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis: taken as apposition "the guru's pith instruction itself, a jewel dwelling in secret" (fits the explanation of "secret"); alt: "the jewel dwelling in secret" is a separate locus/instrument ("in/by the jewel ... through the guru's pith instruction"). Q3 'dir dbang bskur ba'i dbang po 'bring po rnams: "among the empowered, those of middling faculties" (alt: "those of middling faculties for empowerment"); 'dir = "here" (in this empowerment/ this tantra). Q4 spros pa'i phyag rgya: "mudrā of elaboration", Sanskrit not given in the text and not back-formed. Q5 gsang ba'i dbang bskur ba (secret empowerment) is here tied to the completion stage and the samayamudrā is assigned to the middling; the pairing in S3 (samayamudrā with middling) is taken as the next grade, not as the explicit counterpart of "secret empowerment".

## U07
UNIT U07 | FORM prose, 4 sentences | GENRE exposition (gloss of prajñājñāna empowerment) | REGISTER neutral-scholarly
SOURCE 1 de bzhin du khyad par can gyi ye shes ni shes rab ste chos thams cad sems tsam nyid du shes pa'o // 2 de rab tu bsgrub pa'i phyir dbang bskur ba ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'o // 3 yang de ni gzhan gyi dbang dang kun brtags dang yongs su gyur pa rnam par shes pa gsum gyi ngo bo bsdus pa'i gzugs can snying gi nor bur gang bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang skad cig ma rnams yang dag par mtshon pa ni shes rab ye shes yin par rig par bya'o // 4 dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab rnams la ni sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin gyis chos kyi phyag rgya nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o
WORDS khyad par can=special/distinguished; ye shes=awareness; shes rab ye shes=prajñājñāna (the commentary glosses ye shes as khyad par can + shes rab); sems tsam nyid=mind alone; rab tu bsgrub pa=accomplish; gzhan gyi dbang=the dependent; kun brtags=the imagined; yongs su gyur pa=the transformed (possibly for yongs su grub pa "perfected"); rnam par shes pa=consciousness; ngo bo=essence; bsdus pa=gathered; gzugs can=having form/embodied; snying gi nor bu=heart-jewel; yang dag par mtshon pa=to indicate accurately; phyi rol gyi phyag rgya=external mudrā; sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin=illusion-like samādhi; chos kyi phyag rgya=dharmamudrā
CLAUSES S1: copular ni..ste..'o: A (special awareness) is B (wisdom), namely C (knowing all phenomena as mind alone). S2: purpose phyir; copular. S3: rig par bya'o (prescriptive "is to be known as"); the long subject NP: [gang] ... yang dag par mtshon pa = "that which ... indicates"; agent/means bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis; objects phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang skad cig ma rnams; terminative nor bur "as a jewel". S4: bya'o impersonal; instrument des (that empowerment) and ting nge 'dzin gyis; recipients la.
RELATIONS de bzhin du=in the same way; ni=topic; ste=namely; phyir=purpose; kyi=gen; dang=coordination; r=terminative; kyis=means; ni=topic; yin par=terminative (as); des=instrument; la ni=recipient+contrast; cing=coordination
NEG/QUANT chos thams cad (all phenomena); tsam (only/alone); cig ma rnams = false NEG hit; sgyu ma lta bu (not a negation)
SPINE S1 1; S2 1; S3 1 long (split into 2 English sentences); S4 1
CONSTRUAL 1. In the same way, the special awareness is wisdom: knowing all phenomena to be mind alone. 2. In order to accomplish that, the empowerment is the prajñājñāna empowerment. 3. Moreover, that is to be known as follows: that which, as a heart-jewel -- an embodied [thing] in which the essence of the three consciousnesses (the dependent, the imagined, the transformed) is gathered -- accurately indicates, through the guru's pith instruction itself, the external mudrā and the moments, is prajñājñāna. 4. Through that empowerment, to those of the highest faculties among the empowered, the dharmamudrā is to be set out and explained by means of the illusion-like samādhi.
TERMS shes rab=wisdom; ye shes=awareness; shes rab ye shes=prajñājñāna; gzhan gyi dbang=dependent; kun brtags=imagined; chos kyi phyag rgya=dharmamudrā; sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin=illusion-like samādhi
DOUBTS Q1 S1 khyad par can gyi ye shes: "special awareness" vs "the awareness that is distinctive/ excellent"; taken as the awareness named in "shes rab ye shes". Q2 S2 de rab tu bsgrub pa'i phyir: purpose ("in order to accomplish that") vs cause ("because it accomplishes that"); purpose taken. Q3 S3 construction of the long NP: gang taken as relative pronoun, nor bur as terminative "as a jewel", gzugs can as "embodied/having form"; bsdus pa'i gzugs can may be a fixed expression ("having a condensed form"); alt: the guru's instruction is itself the heart-jewel. Q4 S3 yongs su gyur pa: "the transformed", possibly a corruption of yongs su grub pa (the perfected); the three natures read as the three consciousnesses (rnam par shes pa gsum is in apposition). Q5 S3 whose "heart-jewel": the practitioner's heart; unspecified. Q6 S4 dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab: "highest faculties among the empowered" (as in U06 S3); des = "through that" (means), alt "by that empowerment" (agent).

## U08

SOURCE 1 de bzhin du bzhi pa yang yin te / de ltar de bzhin yang zhes bya ba ste // 2 de zhes pa ni shes rab ye shes so // 3 de bzhin zhes bya ba ni de bzhin nyid de / yang dag pa'i mtha' dang chos kyi dbyings zhes bya ba rnams la don gyi khyad par yod pa ma yin no // 4 dbang bskur ba gang gis de'i ngo bo nyid mthong ba'am rab tu bsgrub pa ste / de bzhin zhes bya ba'i sgras brjod par bya ba yin no // 5 yang zhes bya ba'i sgra ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'i rjes thogs su yang sbyin par bya'o zhes bya ba'i don to // 6 yang de ni phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang / gang bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis ... dmigs par byed pa de ni ... dbang bskur ba bzhi par 'gyur ba ma yin no // 7 gzhan du na / 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba zhes bya ba la sogs pas bcom ldan 'das kyis ... bzhed pa yin na / de ji lta na man ngag bstan pa tsam gyis yin par 'gyur
