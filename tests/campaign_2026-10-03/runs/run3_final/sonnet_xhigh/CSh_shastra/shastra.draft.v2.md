### U01
In the words "the divisions of the vow, and so on," "vow" means empowerment. "Division" means its subdivision, which has the defining characteristics that will be explained. As is also said of this:

### U02
For the benefit of sentient beings
empowerment is made known as fourfold.
Since this washes and empowers one,
it is therefore called "empowerment."
This is what was said.

### U03
In order to state the subdivisions of those same four empowerments, [the root text] also says:

### U04
Master, secret, and wisdom,
and likewise the fourth, too.
By these words and those that follow, [the subdivisions] will be explained.

### U05
In this yoginī tantra he is called "master" because he conducts himself in every respect far from sinful, nonvirtuous phenomena—that is, because this is his very vow. The essence of empowerment by the master is what is called "accomplishing, from the *karmamudrā* and by means of the four moments, the intrinsic nature of the four joys." But there is this saying: "Because it is shared in common with the Kriyā tantras and the like, and because one has obtained the irreversible empowerment, one is not to be empowered by the master's empowerment. Therefore one will also be endowed with the fortune to explain, to hear, and so on all the yoga and yoginī tantras and the rest." This saying is nothing at all. Therefore, to begin with, in order to gain authority over the hearing, meditation, and so on of the yoginī tantras such as the *Hevajra*, one is to be empowered by the master's empowerment itself. Moreover, this is to be understood as indicated by the characteristics of the master, the secret, wisdom, and the fourth, just as in the *abhisamaya*.

### U06
To those of dull faculties who have received such a master's empowerment, once they have firmly taken hold [of it] through conviction, the meditation on the karmamudrā and the mudrā of elaboration is to be taught. In the same way, in the case of the completion stage too, the experience of the four joys, which have the nature of the four moments, through the guru's pith instruction itself—a jewel that dwells in secret—is called the "secret empowerment." This is because it is not to be taught to yogins who are applying themselves to meditation on elaboration. Here, to those of middling faculties among the empowered, the meditation on the *samayamudrā* is to be set out and explained.

### U07
In the same way, the special *jñāna* is *prajñā*, which is knowing all phenomena to be mind alone. The empowerment for accomplishing that is the empowerment of *prajñājñāna*. Moreover, *prajñājñāna* is to be understood as follows. As a heart-jewel having form, it gathers the essence of the three consciousnesses—the dependent, the imagined, and the transformed—and, through the guru's pith instruction itself, accurately indicates the external mudrā and the moments. Through that empowerment, to those of the highest faculties among the empowered, the *dharmamudrā* is to be set out and explained by means of the illusion-like *samādhi*.

### U08
In the same way, there is also a fourth, which the root text gives as "thus likewise also" (*de ltar de bzhin yang*). "That" (*de*) means prajñājñāna. "Likewise" (*de bzhin*) means suchness, and "suchness," "the limit of reality," and "the dharma realm" do not differ in meaning. The empowerment by which one sees or accomplishes its intrinsic nature is what the word "likewise" denotes. The word "also" (*yang*) means that [the fourth empowerment] is to be conferred also after the empowerment of prajñājñāna. Moreover, there is the observing of the external mudrā and of the perfection of the moment, which has the characteristic of not being definitively delimited into individual objects. It is done by the yoga of observing without observing, through the guru's pith instruction itself. This does not become the fourth empowerment merely because the pith instruction on what is to be called "the fourth empowerment" has been set out. Otherwise, if the Bhagavān, in the words beginning "this washes and empowers one," holds that empowerment here is the washing away of stains, how could that come about merely through the teaching of a pith instruction?

### U09
It may be objected: "If reality, whose nature is the fourth, is taught, why would [the fourth empowerment] not come about in that case?" That is true. However, since reality is not within the range of speech, it cannot be expressed, and it cannot be accomplished through hearing either. This, too, is said as follows:

### U10
What is clear within, that very thing,
is more direct than speech: heard and seen.
But in sound there comes to arise
a reflection of conceptual thought.

### U11
If, by meditating upon a reality
that one's own thoughts have each imagined,
conceptual thought is stirred in the mindstream,
how can that very reality be realized?
This is what was taught.

### U12
It may be objected: "If, through empowerment, one comes to realize reality directly and the stains are purified without remainder, then why would the *siddhi* of *mahāmudrā* not be accomplished in that very moment?"
