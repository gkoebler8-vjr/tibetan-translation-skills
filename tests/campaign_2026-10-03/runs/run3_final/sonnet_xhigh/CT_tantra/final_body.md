### U01
HEADER: U01 · verse: citation mode, rising, 2-4 beats; padas 7 x 4, four English lines, order 1-2-3-4 · instruction · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
By the practice of that, the yogin
will quickly gain siddhi, undoubtedly.
Or else, meditate on deep equipoise
that is free of wisdom.
NOTES:
Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i is taken as "free of wisdom"; wisdom may here be the consort (prajna), so "without the consort" is the alternative.
Q: sbyor ba is "practice"; alt "union" (with the consort), given the contrast with an equipoise free of wisdom. "That" refers back to before the span.
Q: the first sentence has no verb after dngos grub; "will gain" is supplied (cf. 'thob in U02).
Alt: the tshom med = "undoubtedly" (literally "there is no doubt"), chosen for the line.
Issue: shes rab is bound as "wisdom"; ye shes is "primordial awareness" (glossary).

### U02
HEADER: U02 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3-4 beats; padas 7 x 2, two English lines, order 1-2 · instruction · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
When the meditation on Acala is complete,
you fully gain the kingdom of awakening.
NOTES:
Q: mi g.yo bsgom pa = "meditation on Acala" (the ba dropped for the count); alt "unwavering meditation". MITRA reads "immovability" and no deity; kept, since the chapter is Acala's.
Q: byang chub rgyal srid = "kingdom of awakening" (genitive elided); alt "awakening and kingship".
Q: the Tibetan has no subject; "you" supplied (alt "one").
Alt: yang dag 'thob = "fully gain" (alt "truly", "rightly").

### U03
HEADER: U03 · verse: citation mode, rising, 2-4 beats; four content padas of 7 in two prose frame lines, four English lines, order 3-2-4-5 (pada 3 first) · dialogue, petition · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Then the Blessed Lady asked:
Guide, I wish to hear:
what is the deities' purity?
The very mandalas you spoke of earlier—
Chief, tell me their purity.
Then the Blessed One said:
NOTES:
Q: gtso bo = vocative "Chief" (MITRA also reads "Lord"); alt the adjective "principal" for the mandalas.
Q: the question lha yi rnam par dag pa gang is the object of "I wish to hear"; set as a direct question after a colon (order 3-2).
Q: rnam par dag pa = "purity"; alt "complete purification".
Q: the last request, rnam dag, has no genitive: "their purity" is the mandalas' (the deities' purity is asked about in the question).
Issue: gsol pa "asked" and bka' stsal pa "said": honorifics carried by verb choice; the two frame padas are set as prose lines around the verse.
Check: minor omission of nyid in sngon du gsungs nyid; restored as "very".

### U04
HEADER: U04 · verse: citation mode, rising, 2-3 beats; padas 7 x 2, two English lines, order 2-1 · exposition (the Blessed One's reply) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Then I will expound in full
the purity that purifies all.
NOTES:
Q: kun gyi sbyong byed pa'i rnam dag = "the purity that purifies all"; alt "the purity of the all-purifier". MITRA drops "purity" and makes "that which purifies all" the head; kept.
Q: de nas "then" repeats the frame's "then"; kept as the reply's own sequence marker.
Alt: rab bshad = "expound in full".

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Here, the square is the four abodes of the immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the eight branches of the noble path. The single crossbeam is one-pointedness of mind itself. The lotus is the birthplace. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red direction is great desire. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are none other than the brahmins and the vaishya, kshatriya and shudra castes. The moon and the sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of Black Acala. The curved knife is Hatred Vajri. The directions beginning with the east are White Acala and the others. In the intermediate directions beginning with fire are those of Delusion Vajri and the others. This is the purity of the mandala.
NOTES:
Q: 'phar ma gcig pa = "single crossbeam": uncertain (dictionary: plank, panel; also "extra, double"); MITRA reads "single projection".
Q: da mangs rigs read as dmangs rigs (shudra caste).
Q: the order brahmin, vaishya (rje rigs), kshatriya (rgyal rigs), shudra is the Tibetan's, matched in order with white, yellow, green, black.
Q: mtshams rnams su ... kyi'o = "in the intermediate directions beginning with fire are those of Delusion Vajri and the others" (locative plus genitive predicate, subject elided); alt "the intermediate directions belong to".
Q: rngul = "sweat" (not blood); the sun is paired with sweat, the moon with semen.
Q: mtshan ma = "emblem"; alt "sign".
Alt: tshad med pa'i gnas bzhi = "four abodes of the immeasurables" (MITRA "four immeasurables"); skye gnas = "birthplace" (yoni; alt "womb"); rta babs = "archway" (torana); me la sogs pa'i mtshams = "beginning with fire" (the southeast, unstated).
Issue: the copula is supplied throughout (the Tibetan closes each equation with o/so/do); the Vajri names follow the dictionary equivalents of zhe sdang rdo rje ma and gti mug rdo rje ma (Dveshavajri, Mohavajri) in English form.

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations; rebirth sequence) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The purity of meditation is to be stated: first, offering is the accumulation of merit, the supreme action. Emptiness is the accumulation of primordial awareness. Death is the supreme stainless body, that is, the gandharva body. The ultimate end of the palace is the buddha ground. The lotus is the birthplace. The moon and the sun are white and red.
NOTES:
Q: stong pa nyid ni ye shes kyi tshogs and 'chi ba khyad par 'phags pa dri ma med pa'i lus te dri za'i lus so are read as two sentences; alt one predicate ("emptiness is the accumulation of primordial awareness, the supreme death, the stainless body, the gandharva body"), which is how MITRA reads it. Kept: no ni after 'chi ba is not decisive (the palace sentence has none either).
Q: khyad par 'phags pa modifies las (and lus); alt it modifies tshogs ("the supreme accumulation of merit, an action").
Q: gzhal yang khang read as gzhal yas khang (palace); mthar thug pa = "ultimate end".
Q: brjod par bya = "is to be stated" (impersonal); alt "I will state" (MITRA).
Issue: ste rendered as a colon; "first" is the Tibetan's only ordinal, so none is added after it.

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose · exposition (conception allegory) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The syllable hum is the mind of the gandharva in the midst of the mother and father. Akshobhya is the father. Mamaki is the mother. Seeing their attachment to each other, it feels hatred toward the father, and, becoming attached to the mother, it enters out of delusion, like a sentient being. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and obtains his rank. Having taken the mother, through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births, he also causes that to be born as a son. The daughters too are White Acala and the others, and also Delusion Vajri and the others. The sons too, having killed the father, go on to others, and, saying "This very one is an enemy," kill him. The daughters too feel desire through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births.
NOTES:
Q: "in the midst of the mother and father" (ma dang pha'i dbus su) may attach to the syllable hum or to the gandharva's mind; English keeps the ambiguity.
Q: ma bskyod pa read as mi bskyod pa (Akshobhya) and pa'o as pha'o (father).
Q: the subject of the long chain (mthong nas ... 'jug go) is not stated: taken as the gandharva's mind, "it" (MITRA: "one"); gti mug pas read as cause, alt "the deluded one enters like a sentient being".
Q: ma bzung nas read "having taken the mother" (ma = mother, parallel to pha bsad nas); alt negative "not taking". de yang bur skye bar byed do: "he also causes that to be born as a son" (object elided); alt "he too produces a son", "he himself is born as a son" (MITRA).
Q: bu mo "daughters" are identified with both the Acalas (White Acala and the others) and the Vajri ladies; first yang "too", second "also".
Q: gzhan la bgrod par byed = "go on to others" (alt "go against others"); de in de gsod par byed is the one called an enemy.
Alt: sems = "mind" (MITRA "consciousness"); go 'phang = "rank" (alt "state", "station").

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose · exposition (iconography as symbol) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The sword is wisdom, and the noose is method. Or else, the noose is wisdom, and the sword is method. The two, having become one taste, are the threatening finger. The left one, facing downward, protects the seven underworlds. The right one, facing upward, protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee, planted on the ground, protects the earth. The right foot, stamping down firmly, terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma; the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara; the mara of the Lord of Death is Vishnu; and the mara of the son of the gods is Indra.
NOTES:
Q: ral gri shes rab la zhags pa ni thabs so is read as two equations joined by la ("and"); alt "the noose on the sword of wisdom is method".
Q: g.yon pa and g.yas pa (S4, S5) have no noun: hand, finger or eye; bltas pa "looking" is rendered "facing"; MITRA reads "looking downward to the left", the direction of the gaze.
Q: sdigs mdzub po = "threatening finger" (tarjani, forefinger); the gesture is the union of sword and noose.
Q: yang dag par bsnun pa = "stamping down firmly" (dictionary: striking, hitting); alt "striking".
Q: the identification of the four maras with Brahma, Ishvara, Vishnu and Indra is the Tibetan's; lha'i dbang po = Indra (epithet).
Issue: the nominalized predicates (skyong bar byed pa'o) are rendered as plain finite verbs.

### U09
HEADER: U09 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The earth is the enjoyment of all the young women on the face of the earth. Because of staying long with the young women, it is the lotus seat. What is born in the birthplace is the seat of the moon and sun. The man born from semen and sweat is an entity. The woman is without entity. Black is consciousness. White is form. Yellow is feeling. Red is perception. Green is Amoghasiddhi.
NOTES:
Q: longs spyod pa is a nominal predicate, "is the enjoyment of"; alt "the earth is the one who enjoys".
Q: gnas pa'i phyir read as cause ("because of staying"); alt purpose; the subject (the earth) is elided; la gnas = "with" (alt "among", "in").
Q: skyes pa = "what is born" (arisen); alt "the male". skye gnas la = "in" the birthplace (MITRA "from").
Q: skyes bu = "man" (against bud med "woman"); alt "person"; dngos po = "entity" (MITRA "existence"); dngos po med pa = "without entity".
Q: the first four are aggregates; the fifth gives a name, don yod grub pa = Amoghasiddhi, not 'du byed (formations); alt "accomplishing the meaningful".

### U10
HEADER: U10 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Or else, dark blue is space, white is water, yellow is earth, red is fire, and green is wind. As the Blessed One is, so is the Blessed Lady.
NOTES:
Q: ji lta ba de bzhin du: the comparison of the Blessed Lady with the Blessed One names no respect in the Tibetan; alt "as the Blessed One is, so likewise is the Blessed Lady the same".
Alt: mthing ga = "dark blue"; yang na = "or else" (an alternative to the scheme of U09).

### U11
HEADER: U11 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Or else, dark blue is the primordial awareness of the utterly pure dharmadhatu. White is mirror-like primordial awareness. Yellow is the primordial awareness of equality. Red is discriminating primordial awareness. Green is the primordial awareness of accomplished activity.
NOTES:
Q: chos kyi dbyings rnam par dag pa'i ye shes = "the primordial awareness of the utterly pure dharmadhatu"; alt "the primordial awareness that is the purity of the dharmadhatu".
Alt: bya ba grub pa'i ye shes = "of accomplished activity" (the standard "all-accomplishing"); so sor rtog pa = "discriminating" (alt "individual discernment").
Issue: ye shes bound as "primordial awareness" for all five (glossary).

### U12
HEADER: U12 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3-4 beats; padas 7 x 4, four English lines, order 1-2-3-4 · doctrinal statement, aphoristic · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The Teacher, the Victor, the one alone,
abides fully in a fivefold way.
The perfection of wisdom itself, too,
abides fully in five aspects.
NOTES:
Q: ston pa rgyal ba gcig pu nyid: apposition "the Teacher, the Victor, the one alone" (taken, so that "one alone" may also read as a predicate, as MITRA does); no concessive "though one" is marked.
Q: lnga yi tshul vs rnam lnga'i tshul: "fivefold way" and "five aspects" (tshul absorbed in the second); the five (families or wisdoms) are not named.
Q: yang dag gnas: "abides fully"; alt "abides truly / rightly".
Check: minor omission of nyid in shes rab pha rol phyin nyid kyang; restored as "itself".
