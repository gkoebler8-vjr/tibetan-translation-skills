# tantra.construal.md — Candamaharosana Tantra (Toh 431) ch. 15, test page C-T (U01-U12)
Brief: seasoned practitioner, study aid. Terms kept anglicized w/o diacritics; no brackets/Wylie in body; verse metred, citation mode, one line per pada where sense allows; no contractions; doubts as Q: lines. Glossary: tantra.glossary.tsv (binds).
Speakers: U01-U02 the Blessed One's instruction (continuing); U03 Blessed Lady asks, frame ends with 'then the Blessed One said'; U04 the Blessed One replies and opens the exposition U05-U12.

=== U01 ===
SOURCE      de yi sbyor ba s rnal 'byor pa s myur du dngos grub the tshom med yang na shes rab dang bral ba 'i shin tu mnyam gzhag bsgom pa r bya
UNIT        U01
FORM        verse, 7x4, not a citation (no zhes/ces frame); the tantra's own verse
GENRE       instruction (pith)   REGISTER  Blessed One to the practitioner; plain, directive, "you"/yogin; seasoned practitioner
WORDS       sbyor ba = practice (alt union, with the consort, given the contrast with shes rab dang bral ba'i); dngos grub = siddhi; the tshom med = without doubt (the tshom 'doubt' + med, adverbial); shes rab = wisdom (bound); mnyam gzhag = equipoise; shin tu = deep/intensifier on mnyam gzhag
CLAUSES     2  (1) de yi sbyor bas rnal 'byor pas myur du dngos grub [thob elided; cf. 'thob in U02] the tshom med: agent rnal 'byor pa (ergative pas), means sbyor bas, adverb myur du; verb elided = gain/attain, supplied from sense; tense future/general.
            (2) yang na ... shin tu mnyam gzhag bsgom par bya: bsgom par bya = imperative/obligation "should be meditated"; object mnyam gzhag; agent = the practitioner (elided, addressee).
RELATIONS   bas = means (sbyor bas); pas = agent (yogin); du = adverbial (myur du); yang na = alternative ("or else"); dang = comitative with bral; bral ba'i = modification (relative-clause adjective on mnyam gzhag); par bya = obligation
NEG/QUANT   med = "there is no doubt" (scope: the tshom); bral = "free of" (scope: shes rab). shin tu = intensifier.
VERSE-NOTES 7x4; filler none beyond particles for count. Pada 3 is thin (alternative marker + modifier), pada 4 carries the verb.
SPINE       2 sentences. S1 spans padas 1-2, verb elided (supplied "will gain"). S2 spans padas 3-4, finite verb bsgom par bya at the end of pada 4; pada 3 is its modifier.
CONSTRUAL   By the practice of that, the yogin will quickly gain siddhi, without doubt. Or else, meditate on a deep equipoise that is free of wisdom.
TERMS       sbyor ba = practice; rnal 'byor pa = yogin; dngos grub = siddhi; shes rab = wisdom; mnyam gzhag = equipoise; bsgom = meditate
CITATION    not a citation
DOUBTS      Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i = "free of wisdom" (wisdom as the consort / prajna) vs "without insight"; sbyor ba = "practice" vs "union" (with the consort); "de yi" refers back before the span.
            Q: elided verb of S1 (dngos grub [thob]) supplied as "will gain" (general result, future/habitual).

=== U03 ===
SOURCE      de nas bcom ldan 'das mas gsol pa lha yi rnam pa r dag pa gang 'dren pa bdag ni nyan pa r 'tshal sngon du gsungs nyid dkyil 'khor rnams gtso bo bdag la rnam dag gsungs de nas bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal pa
UNIT        U03
FORM        verse, 7x4 content padas inside two prose-length frame padas (opening: de nas bcom ldan 'das mas gsol pa; closing: de nas bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal pa); not a citation
GENRE       dialogue / request   REGISTER  the Blessed Lady (consort) petitions the Blessed One: honorific, respectful, direct; seasoned practitioner
WORDS       bcom ldan 'das ma (mas = ma + ergative s) = Blessed Lady (Bhagavati; cf. U05 dkyil 'khor gyi rnam par dag pa); sngon du = before/previously; gsungs = spoke (hon.); nyid = emphatic "the very"; nyan par 'tshal = wish to hear; bka' stsal = said (hon.)
CLAUSES     frame1: bcom ldan 'das mas gsol pa (ERG agent = the Blessed Lady; speech introducing P2-P5).

NEG/QUANT   none negated. rnams = plural (mandalas). nyid = emphasis on the mandalas already taught.
VERSE-NOTES Content padas all 7. 'i/yi for count. gsungs in P4 is the participial (relative) form, in P5 the imperative.
SPINE       Frame; S1 question (P2) + wish to hear (P3); S2 (P4-P5) topic = mandalas, request = tell me their purity; closing frame.
CONSTRUAL   Then the Blessed Lady asked: "Guide, I wish to hear what the purity of the deities is. As for the mandalas you spoke of before, Chief, tell me their purity." Then the Blessed One said:
TERMS       bcom ldan 'das ma = Blessed Lady; 'dren pa = Guide; gtso bo = Chief; rnam par dag pa = purity; dkyil 'khor = mandala; lha = deity
CITATION    not a citation
DOUBTS      Q: gtso bo = vocative "Chief" (taken) vs adjective "the principal (mandalas)".
            Q: P2 "what is the purity of the deities" is embedded as object of nyan par 'tshal; set as a direct question after "hear:".
            Q: rnam par dag pa = "purity" (viSuddhi); alt "complete purification / true significance".
            Q: P5 rnam dag has no genitive: "their purity" (the mandalas'); P2 asks about the deities' purity, P4-5 about the mandalas'.

FORM        prose, ~13 sentences; not a citation   GENRE exposition (conception and Oedipal allegory of the five families)   REGISTER definitional-narrative, impersonal, present tense general; seasoned practitioner
WORDS       hum yig = the syllable hum; dri za'i sems = the mind of the gandharva; ma bskyod pa (pa'o = pha'o) = Akshobhya, the father; ma ma ki = Mamaki, the mother; phan tshun du rjes su chags pa = their attachment to each other; rnam par zhe sdang = feels hatred (rnam par preverb, not rendered); gti mug pas = out of delusion (pas cause); sems can bzhin du 'jug = enters like a sentient being; pad+mar zhugs pa'i bu = the son who has entered the lotus; go 'phang = rank; ma bzung nas = having taken the mother (ma noun, not negation); bag chags = habitual tendencies; bde ba khyad par 'phags pa = supreme bliss; bur skye bar byed = causes birth as a son; bgrod pa = go (to/on to); 'dod par byed = feels desire

The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and obtains his rank. Having taken the mother, through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births, he also causes that to be born as a son. The daughters too are White Acala and the others, and also Delusion Vajri and the others. The sons too, having killed the father, go on to others, and, saying "This very one is an enemy," kill him. The daughters too feel desire through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births.
