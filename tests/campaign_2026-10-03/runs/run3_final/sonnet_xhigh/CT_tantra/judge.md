## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U06 "Death is the supreme stainless body, that is, the gandharva body": construal fork (recorded, wrong branch taken); 'chi ba khyad par 'phags pa belongs with emptiness.
2. U07 "kill him" and "causes that to be born as a son": grammar misread; an unmarked agent shift and the subject reading of de yang were missed, so the allegory reads as incoherent.
3. U08 "The left one, facing downward": dictionary sense; bltas pa is the gaze, and C supplies a phantom referent.
4. U01 "equipoise that is free of wisdom": term choice; shes rab as the consort was flagged but not adopted, and the result is doctrinally odd.
5. U05 "single crossbeam" / "the red direction": dictionary sense; 'phar ma is a mandala tier or enclosure, and phyogs is the cardinal directions taken together.
