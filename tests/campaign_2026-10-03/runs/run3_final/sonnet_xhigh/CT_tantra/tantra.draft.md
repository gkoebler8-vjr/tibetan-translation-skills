### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Here, the square is the four abodes of the immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the eight branches of the noble path. The single crossbeam is one-pointedness of mind itself. The lotus is the birthplace. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red direction is great desire. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are none other than the brahmins and the vaishya, kshatriya and shudra castes. The moon and the sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of Black Acala. The curved knife is Hatred Vajri. The directions beginning with the east are White Acala and the others. In the intermediate directions beginning with fire are those of Delusion Vajri and the others. This is the purity of the mandala.

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations; rebirth sequence) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The purity of meditation is to be stated: first, offering is the accumulation of merit, the supreme action. Emptiness is the accumulation of primordial awareness. Death is the supreme stainless body, that is, the gandharva body. The ultimate end of the palace is the buddha ground. The lotus is the birthplace. The moon and the sun are white and red.

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose · exposition (conception allegory) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The syllable hum is the mind of the gandharva in the midst of the mother and father. Akshobhya is the father. Mamaki is the mother. Seeing their attachment to each other, it feels hatred toward the father, and, becoming attached to the mother, it enters out of delusion, like a sentient being. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and obtains his rank. Having taken the mother, through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births, he also causes that to be born as a son. The daughters too are White Acala and the others, and also Delusion Vajri and the others. The sons too, having killed the father, go on to others, and, saying "This very one is an enemy," kill him. The daughters too feel desire through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births.

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose · exposition (iconography as symbol) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The sword is wisdom, and the noose is method. Or else, the noose is wisdom, and the sword is method. The two, having become one taste, are the threatening finger. The left one, facing downward, protects the seven underworlds. The right one, facing upward, protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee, planted on the ground, protects the earth. The right foot, stamping down firmly, terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma; the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara; the mara of the Lord of Death is Vishnu; and the mara of the son of the gods is Indra.

### U09
HEADER: U09 · prose · exposition (symbolic equations) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The earth is the enjoyment of all the young women on the face of the earth. Because of staying long with the young women, it is the lotus seat. What is born in the birthplace is the seat of the moon and sun. The man born from semen and sweat is an entity. The woman is without entity. Black is consciousness. White is form. Yellow is feeling. Red is perception. Green is Amoghasiddhi.
