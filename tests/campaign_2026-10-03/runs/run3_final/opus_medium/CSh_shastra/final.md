### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose · exposition/polemic (etymological gloss, then argument) · neutral-scholarly · academic · not a citation (one opponent's statement quoted inline, source not located)
TEXT:
Here in this yoginī tantra, "master" (*ācārya*) [is so called] because, having become remote from evil, nonvirtuous things, he conducts himself thoroughly (*kun du spyod pa*, ā-√car); for that very [consecration] is his *saṃvara*. The nature of the master consecration is what is called the full actualization, from the karmamudrā and by means of the four moments, of the very own-nature of the four joys. It is not, however, to be taken as "being consecrated with the master consecration through having obtained the irreversible consecration," because that is shared with the kriyā tantras and the rest. Therefore the statement "[by it] one also comes to possess the fortune to explain, hear, and so on, all the yoga and yoginī tantras and the rest" amounts to nothing. Therefore, first of all, in order to have authority to hear, meditate on, and so on, the yoginī tantras such as the *Hevajra*, one should be consecrated with the master consecration itself. Moreover, it should be understood that [consecration] is indicated by the characteristics of master, secret, wisdom, and fourth, as in the *abhisamaya*.
NOTES:
Q: de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas: "for that very [consecration] is his saṃvara"; alt. "because he is the saṃvara of that very [tantra]".
Q: …thob pas slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o: taken as "not to be taken as being consecrated with the master consecration through having obtained the irreversible consecration"; alt. (MITRA) "the master consecration is not to be conferred on those who have already obtained the irreversible consecration"; the phyir clause and the next sentence favour the reading taken.
Q: mngon par rtogs pa = the *abhisamaya* (realization/sādhana texts); alt. "in accordance with clear realization"; the referent of de in the last sentence (consecration in general vs the master consecration) is open.
Alt: ci yang ma yin "amounts to nothing" = is baseless.
Check: in-context; 2 minor fixed ("this" yoginī tantra restored; person unified).

TEXT:

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose · commentary gloss (root words "that, thus, again" glossed) + argument · neutral-scholarly · academic · inline root pada from Hevajratantra (cf. U02 p3), not separately identified
TEXT:
Likewise there is also the fourth: "that, thus, … again." "That" is wisdom-gnosis. "Thus" (*de bzhin*) is thusness (*tathatā*): there is no difference in meaning among [thusness], the real limit (*bhūtakoṭi*), and the dharmadhātu. The consecration by which one sees or actualizes its nature is what the word "thus" expresses. The word "again" means: "it is to be given again, immediately after the wisdom-gnosis consecration." Moreover, as for that [fourth]: with the external mudrā, and through the guru's instruction itself, one focuses, by the yoga of focusing without a reference point, on the perfection of the moment, which is characterized as not being definitely delimited into separate objects. Merely having taught the instruction on what is to be called "the fourth consecration" does not make it the fourth consecration. Otherwise—since, in the words "With this one is washed and consecrated" and so on, the Blessed One holds that consecration here is the washing away of stains—how could it come about by merely teaching an instruction?
NOTES:
Q: the sentence beginning "Moreover, as for that [fourth]": de ni … dmigs par byed pa de ni taken as topic (what the fourth is), the main claim being that merely teaching the instruction does not make it the fourth; alt. one subject, "the instruction for what is to be called the fourth consecration, namely focusing …". MITRA takes the external mudrā and the guru's instruction as agents of "delimited" ("not limited … by external seals or by the guru's instructions"); rejected because gang … de correlates the instruction with dmigs par byed pa.
Q: yin na read as "since" (the Blessed One's view is not hypothetical); alt. "if".
Issue: de bzhin nyid rendered "thusness" (not "suchness") to keep the pun on de bzhin "thus".
Issue: botok slips corrected: de | yang dag pa'i mtha'; rnams la | don gyi khyad par.
Check: in-context; no findings.
