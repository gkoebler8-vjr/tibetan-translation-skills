## U05  prose · exposition/polemic, 5 sentences
SOURCE 1 rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud 'dir ni sdig pa mi dge ba'i chos rnams las ring du gyur pa kun du spyod pa'i phyir slob dpon te / de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas so // 2 slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba'i ngo bo gang las kyi phyag rgya las skad cig ma bzhis dga' ba bzhi'i rang gi ngo bo nyid rab tu bsgrub pa zhes bya ba yin gyi / yang bya ba'i rgyud la sogs pa dang thun mong du gyur pa'i phyir mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba thob pas slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o // 3 de lta bas na rnal 'byor dang rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud la sogs pa thams cad bshad pa dang nyan pa la sogs pa'i skal pa dang yang ldan par 'gyur ro zhes bya ba'i tshig 'di ni ci yang ma yin no // 4 de lta bas na dang por re zhig dgyes pa'i rdo rje la sogs pa rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud rnams mnyan pa dang bsgom pa la sogs pa la dbang bar bya ba'i phyir slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba nyid kyis dbang bskur bar bya'o // 5 yang de ni mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du slob dpon dang gsang ba dang shes rab dang bzhi pa'i mtshan nyid kyis nye bar mtshon pa yin par rig par bya'o
WORDS kun du spyod pa = ā-√car (etymology of ācārya); slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba = master consecration (ācāryābhiṣeka; gyis here read as gyi: "master's"); mi ldog pa'i dbang = irreversible consecration; ci yang ma yin = is nothing, worthless; dbang ba = have authority/mastery over; mngon par rtogs pa = abhisamaya (sādhana manuals); nye bar mtshon pa = indicated/exemplified.
CLAUSES s1: copular "is ācārya" + phyir cause; second clause pas so = because. s2: yin gyi = "is X, but"; mi bya'o = "is not to be done/taken". s3: tshig 'di ni ci yang ma yin. s4: dbang bskur bar bya'o (obligative), purpose phyir. s5: rig par bya'o obligative.
RELATIONS s1 las = source/separation (distant from); phyir = cause; te = gloss link; pas so = cause. s2 las (karmamudrā) = source/means; bzhis = instrument; gyi = adversative; phyir = cause (common to kriyā etc.); thob pas = means/cause; s3 de lta bas na = therefore; zhes bya ba'i tshig = quoted statement; s4 de lta bas na = therefore; phyir = purpose; kyis = instrument; s5 yang = moreover; ji lta ba bzhin du = in accordance with; kyis = means.
NEG mi dge ba (nonvirtuous); mi ldog pa (irreversible); mi bya'o (scope: taking master consecration as the irreversible one); ci yang ma yin (statement = nothing).
QUANT thams cad (all the yoga & yoginī tantras); bzhi ×2; nyid restrictive (slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba nyid kyis = with the master consecration itself).
CONSTRUAL Here in the yoginī tantra, [one is called] "master" (ācārya) because, having become distant from evil, nonvirtuous things, he practises (ā-√car) thoroughly; for that very [consecration] is his saṃvara. The nature of the master consecration is what is called the actualizing, from the karmamudrā and by means of the four moments, of the very own-nature of the four joys; but it is not to be taken as "being consecrated with the master consecration through having obtained the irreversible consecration", because that is shared with the kriyā tantras and the rest. Therefore the statement "[by it] one also comes to possess the fortune for explaining, hearing and so on all the yoga and yoginī tantras and the rest" is worthless. Therefore first of all, in order to have authority to hear, meditate on and so on the yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, one is to be consecrated with the master consecration itself. Moreover it should be understood that this [consecration] is indicated by the characteristics of master, secret, wisdom and fourth, as in the abhisamaya.
DOUBTS Q: de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas: "because that very [consecration] is his (the master's) saṃvara" vs "because he is the saṃvara of that very [tantra]"; Q: s2 mi bya'o: "one should not [construe it as]…" vs "one should not consecrate [with it]"; Q: mngon par rtogs pa = the abhisamaya / sādhana texts vs "clear realization"; Q: "de" in s5 = consecration in general (fourfold) vs the master consecration.

## U06  prose · exposition, 3 sentences
SOURCE 1 de lta bu'i slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba thob pa'i dbang po rtul po rnams la ni mos pas nges par bzung nas / las kyi phyag rgya dang spros pa'i phyag rgya bsgom pa bstan par bya ba yin no // 2 de bzhin du rdzogs pa'i rim pa'i phyogs la yang gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis skad cig ma bzhi'i rang bzhin dga' ba bzhi po nyams su myong ba de ni gsang ba'i dbang bskur ba zhes bya bar brjod de / spros pa bsgom pa la sbyor ba'i rnal 'byor pa rnams la bstan par bya ba ma yin pa'i phyir ro // 3 'dir dbang bskur ba'i dbang po 'bring po rnams la ni dam tshig gi phyag rgya bsgom pa nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o
WORDS mos pas nges par bzung nas = having (them) firmly gripped by conviction/inclination; spros pa'i phyag rgya = mudrā with elaboration (prapañca); phyogs = side/context; gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu = the jewel residing in the secret [place] (botok "yang gsang" merger rejected: phyogs la yang | gsang bar); nyams su myong ba = experience.
CLAUSES s1 bstan par bya ba yin (obligative), recipient la = those of dull faculties; nas = sequence. s2 brjod (is called), subject = the experiencing; de = semi-final linking to reason; phyir ro = because. s3 bstan cing bshad par bya'o obligative; cing coordination.
RELATIONS s1 pa'i (who have obtained) modification; pas = means; nas = sequence. s2 de bzhin du = likewise; la yang = also in; gyi/kyis = means (guru's instruction); 'i rang bzhin = whose nature is; de = and [the reason is]; phyir = cause. s3 'dir = here.
NEG s2 bstan par bya ba ma yin pa (not to be taught; scope: to yogins engaged in elaborated meditation).
CONSTRUAL To those of dull faculties who have obtained such a master consecration, having first firmly secured [them] through conviction, one is to teach the meditation on the karmamudrā and on the mudrā with elaboration. Likewise, also on the side of the completion stage: the experiencing, through the guru's instruction itself, of the four joys whose nature is the four moments at the jewel residing in the secret [place] — that is called the secret consecration, because it is not to be taught to yogins engaged in meditation with elaboration. Here, to those of middling faculties for consecration, one is to teach and explain the meditation on the samayamudrā.
DOUBTS Q: mos pas nges par bzung nas: subject/object unclear ("having ascertained [their] inclination" vs "having firmly grasped [it] through conviction"); Q: gsang bar gnas pa'i nor bu: locative elided ("at the jewel …"; cf. U07 snying gi nor bur) vs apposition "the guru's instruction, a jewel kept secret"; Q: 'dir = "in this [secret consecration]" vs "here"; Q: dbang bskur ba'i dbang po 'bring po = "of middling faculties for consecration" vs "the consecrated who are of middling faculties".

## U07  prose · gloss/exposition, 4 sentences
SOURCE 1 de bzhin du khyad par can gyi ye shes ni shes rab ste / chos thams cad sems tsam nyid du shes pa'o // 2 de rab tu bsgrub pa'i phyir dbang bskur ba ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'o // 3 yang de ni gzhan gyi dbang dang kun brtags dang yongs su gyur pa rnam par shes pa gsum gyi ngo bo bsdus pa'i gzugs can snying gi nor bur gang bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang skad cig ma rnams yang dag par mtshon pa ni shes rab ye shes yin par rig par bya'o // 4 dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab rnams la ni sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin gyis chos kyi phyag rgya nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o
WORDS khyad par can gyi ye shes = special gnosis; sems tsam = mind only; gzhan gyi dbang = dependent (paratantra); kun brtags = imagined (parikalpita); yongs su gyur pa = transformed (not the usual yongs grub); rnam par shes pa gsum = three consciousnesses; ngo bo bsdus pa'i gzugs can = embodiment condensing the nature of; snying gi nor bu = heart-jewel; yang dag par mtshon pa = correctly indicated.
CLAUSES s1 copular, gloss: special gnosis = wisdom, namely knowing all dharmas as mind only. s2 copular. s3 rig par bya'o; subject clause gang … mtshon pa (relative) = wisdom-gnosis. s4 bstan cing bshad par bya'o; des = instrument; gyis = means.
RELATIONS ste = namely; phyir = purpose; yang = moreover; 'i = modification; r (nor bur) = locus; kyis = means; dang = coordination; des = by that; la = recipient; gyis = means.
QUANT thams cad; tsam (mind only); nyid (instruction itself); gsum; rnams.
CONSTRUAL Likewise, the special gnosis is wisdom, that is, the knowledge that all phenomena are mind only. The consecration [given] to actualize it is the wisdom-gnosis consecration. Moreover, what is correctly indicated — the external mudrā and the moments — by the guru's instruction itself, in the heart-jewel, an embodied form that condenses the nature of the three consciousnesses (dependent, imagined and transformed), is to be understood as the wisdom-gnosis. To those of superior faculties consecrated with that consecration, one is to teach and explain the dharmamudrā by means of the illusion-like samādhi.
DOUBTS Q: s1 head: "special gnosis is wisdom" (as written, ni on ye shes) vs "wisdom is special gnosis" (the root word glossed is shes rab); Q: the three: dependent/imagined/transformed consciousness, or three natures + "consciousness" only on the last; yongs su gyur pa (transformed) vs yongs grub (perfected); Q: gzugs can = "embodied form" modifies snying gi nor bu (taken) vs the mudrā; Q: dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab = superior faculties for consecration.

## U08  prose · gloss + argument, 7 sentences
SOURCE 1 de bzhin du bzhi pa yang yin te / de ltar de bzhin yang zhes bya ba ste / 2 de zhes pa ni shes rab ye shes so // 3 de bzhin zhes bya ba ni de bzhin nyid de / yang dag pa'i mtha' dang chos kyi dbyings zhes bya ba rnams la don gyi khyad par yod pa ma yin no // 4 dbang bskur ba gang gis de'i ngo bo nyid mthong ba 'am rab tu bsgrub pa ste / de bzhin zhes bya ba'i sgras brjod par bya ba yin no // 5 yang zhes bya ba'i sgra ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'i rjes thogs su yang sbyin par bya'o zhes bya ba'i don to // 6 yang de ni phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang / gang bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis yul so sor nges par yongs su bcad pa ma yin pa'i mtshan nyid can skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa la dmigs pa med par dmigs pa'i rnal 'byor gyis dmigs par byed pa de ni dbang bskur ba bzhi pa zhes bya bar brjod par bya ba'i man ngag nye bar bstan par byas pa tsam gyis ni dbang bskur ba bzhi par 'gyur ba ma yin no // 7 gzhan du na / 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba / zhes bya ba la sogs pas bcom ldan 'das kyis dbang bskur ba ni 'dir dri ma bkru bar bzhed pa yin na / de ji lta na man ngag bstan pa tsam gyis yin par 'gyur
WORDS de bzhin nyid = thusness/suchness (tathatā; pun on de bzhin "thus"); yang dag pa'i mtha' = the real limit (bhūtakoṭi); chos kyi dbyings = dharmadhātu; rjes thogs su = immediately after; yul so sor nges par yongs su bcad pa = definitely delimited into separate objects; phun sum tshogs pa = perfection/excellence; dmigs pa med par dmigs pa = focusing without a reference point; bzhed pa = hold (hon., agent Bhagavān). botok slips corrected: "de yang dag pa'i" = de | yang dag pa'i; "rnams la don" = rnams la | don (not "la don").
CLAUSES s1 yin te; lemma. s2–3 gloss copulas; s3 yod pa ma yin (no difference). s4 brjod par bya ba yin. s5 don to. s6 'gyur ba ma yin — subject: the fourth; tsam gyis = merely by. s7 gzhan du na = otherwise; bzhed pa yin na = since/if [the Blessed One] holds; rhetorical question ji lta na … 'gyur.
RELATIONS s1 te = namely. s3 de = namely; la = among (no difference among). s4 gang gis = by which (means); 'am = or; ste = that is. s5 rjes thogs su = after; zhes bya ba'i don = means. s6 dang = with (external mudrā and …); kyis = means; gyis (yoga) = means; tsam gyis = merely by (restrictive+means). s7 la sogs pas = by [the words]…etc. (instrument/authority); kyis = agent (Bhagavān); na = condition/since; ji lta na = how.
NEG s3 yod pa ma yin (no difference in meaning); s6 yongs su bcad pa ma yin pa (not delimited); dmigs pa med par (without reference); 'gyur ba ma yin (does not become fourth — scope: "merely by teaching the instruction"); s7 rhetorical: cannot be by mere teaching.
CONSTRUAL Likewise there is also the fourth: "that, thus, … again". "That" is wisdom-gnosis. "Thus" is thusness: between [thusness], the real limit and the dharmadhātu there is no difference in meaning. The consecration by which one sees or actualizes its nature is what the word "thus" expresses. The word "again" means: "it is to be given again, immediately after the wisdom-gnosis consecration." Moreover, as for that [fourth]: with the external mudrā, and through the guru's instruction itself, one focuses, by the yoga of focusing without reference, on the perfection of the moment, which is characterized as not being definitely delimited into separate objects; merely having taught the instruction on what is to be called "the fourth consecration" does not make it the fourth consecration. Otherwise: since, by "With this one is washed and consecrated" and so on, the Blessed One holds that consecration here is the washing away of stains, how could it come about by merely teaching an instruction?
DOUBTS Q: s6 syntax: "de ni … dmigs par byed pa de ni" — taken as topic "that [fourth] is where one focuses …", then main claim "merely teaching the instruction ... does not make it the fourth"; alt: one long subject "the instruction that... is called fourth"; Q: "phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang" — "with the external mudrā" vs "the external mudrā and [the moment]"; Q: yin na — "since" (taken; the Bhagavān's view is not hypothetical) vs "if".

## U10  verse 7×4 (citation)
SOURCE 1 nang na gsal ba de nyid ni / 2 ngag las mngon sum thos mthong yin / 3 'on kyang sgra la rnam rtog gi / 4 gzugs brnyan 'byung bar 'gyur ba yin
CLAUSES p1–2 copular yin: subject de nyid (reality) clear within; predicate: directly heard/seen from speech. p3–4 'byung bar 'gyur: subject gzugs brnyan of rnam rtog; locus sgra la.
RELATIONS na = locus (within); las = source (from speech); 'on kyang = however; la = locus (in sound); gi = genitive.
CONSTRUAL That reality which is clear within is heard and seen directly from speech; yet in sound there arises a reflection of conceptual thought.

## U11  verse 7×4 (citation) + frame
SOURCE 1 rang gi rnam rtog de de rgyas / 2 brtags pa'i de nyid bsgoms pa yis / 3 rgyud la rnam rtog 'khrug 'gyur na / 4 de nyid rtog par ji ltar 'gyur / zhes bstan pa yin no
CLAUSES p1 rgyas (spread/expand): one's own conceptual thought expands that [reflection]; de de = "that, it" (one de as demonstrative of the reflection, the other filler/resumptive). p2 yis = means: by meditating on an imagined reality; p3 'khrug 'gyur: thought is stirred in the continuum; na = if/when; p4 rhetorical question: how will reality be realized?
RELATIONS p2 yis = means; p3 la = locus; na = condition; frame zhes bstan pa yin no.
CONSTRUAL One's own conceptual thought expands that [reflection]; [and] if, by meditating on an imagined reality, conceptual thought becomes agitated in the mind-stream, how will reality be realized? — So it is taught.
DOUBTS Q: de de rgyas: "that [reflection] expands" / "it expands that"; Q: rtog par = realize (reading rtogs) vs "how will it become conceptualized as reality"; Q: p2 may attach to p1 ("…expands, so that one meditates on an imagined reality").
