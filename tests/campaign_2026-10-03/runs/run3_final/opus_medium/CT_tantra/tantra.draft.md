# tantra.draft.md (Pass 2 draft + Pass 3 style, seasoned practitioner)

## U01
The yogin, through union with her,
gains siddhi swiftly, beyond doubt.
Or else, without a prajna,
cultivate profound equipoise.

## U02
When meditation on Acala is complete,
you truly gain the kingdom of awakening.

## U03
Then the Blessed Lady asked:

What is the purification of the deity?
Guide, I wish to hear it.
The mandalas you taught before:
Lord, tell me their purification.

Then the Blessed One said:

## U04
Now I shall set out in full
the purification that purifies all.

## U05
Here, the square is the four immeasurable abodes. The four doors are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the noble eightfold path. The single tier is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the place of birth. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red quarters are great passion. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are the brahmin, vaishya, kshatriya and shudra castes. The moon and sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of Black Acala. The flaying knife is Dveshavajri. The directions beginning with the east are White Acala and the others. The intermediate directions beginning with the southeast belong to Mohavajri and the others. This is the purification of the mandala.

## U06
I shall now state the purification of the meditation. First, the offering is the supreme action of the accumulation of merit. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom: the supreme death, the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva. The culmination of the palace is the level of buddhahood. The lotus is the place of birth. The moon and sun are the white and the red.

## U07
The syllable HUM is the mind of the gandharva between the mother and the father. Akshobhya is the father; Mamaki is the mother. Seeing them in passion with each other, the gandharva feels hatred toward the father and desire for the mother, then, through delusion, enters, just as an ordinary sentient being does. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and takes his place. Having taken the mother, through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of beginningless births, he in turn brings about birth as a son. The daughters, again, are White Acala and the others, and Mohavajri and the others as well. The sons too kill the father and then go on to others; thinking, "This one is the enemy," they kill him. The daughters too, through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of beginningless births, feel desire.

## U08
The sword is prajna and the noose is method. Or else, the noose is prajna and the sword is method. The two becoming one taste is the threatening forefinger. The left gaze, turned downward, protects the seven underworlds. The right gaze, turned upward, protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the ground protects the earth. Stamping firmly with the right foot terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma; the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara; the mara of the Lord of Death is Vishnu; the mara of the son of the gods is Indra.

## U09
The ground is the enjoyment of young women all over the earth. Because he dwells long with young women, there is the lotus seat. Being born in the place of birth is the seat of moon and sun. The man born from semen and sweat is entity; the woman is non-entity. Black is consciousness, white is form, yellow is feeling, red is perception, and green is Amoghasiddhi.

## U10
Or else, blue is space, white is water, yellow is earth, red is fire, and green is wind. As it is with the Blessed One, so it is with the Blessed Lady.

## U11
Or else, blue is the wisdom of the completely pure dharmadhatu, white is mirrorlike wisdom, yellow is the wisdom of equality, red is discriminating wisdom, and green is the wisdom that accomplishes activity.

## U12
The Teacher, the one and only Victor,
truly abides in the mode of five;
Prajnaparamita, too,
truly abides in a fivefold mode.
