# tantra.construal.md — Candamaharosana Tantra (Toh 431) ch. 15, page C-T
Context (assumed from brief): Acala's/Lord's instruction continues; then the Bhagavati asks, the Bhagavan answers with the "purifications" (rnam dag, symbolic equivalences). Speaker of U01-U02: the Blessed One (Acala/Candamaharosana). U03: Bhagavati asks, frame "then the Blessed One said" introduces U04. U04-U12: the Blessed One.
NEG-tool note: tibdict NEG counts in U05-U09 are false positives: ma = mother, ma bskyod pa = Akshobhya, ma ma ki = Mamaki, rdo rje ma = -vajri, gzhon nu ma = young woman, nyi ma = sun. Real negations: U01 med, U02 mi g.yo (name/adj), U05 tshad med, U06 dri ma med, U07 mtha' med (x2), U09 dngos po med.

## U01
FORM verse 7x4, not a citation (root tantra verse). GENRE instruction. REGISTER the Lord to the assembly/practitioner; plain instruction.
SOURCE 1 de yi sbyor bas rnal 'byor pas / 2 myur du dngos grub the tshom med / 3 yang na shes rab dang bral ba'i / 4 shin tu mnyam gzhag bsgom par bya
CLAUSES 2. (a) verb elided ('thob, "gains") agent rnal 'byor pas ERG; object dngos grub; means de yi sbyor bas (INSTR). (b) bsgom par bya: obligative/future-imperative "should cultivate"; object shin tu mnyam gzhag; agent elided = the yogin.
RELATIONS sbyor bas = means ("through union with her"); yang na = alternative ("or else"); bral ba'i = genitive modification of equipoise (equipoise of one without a prajna).
NEG the tshom med = without doubt (scope: the claim of quick siddhi). bral = without.
TERMS sbyor ba = union; rnal 'byor pa = yogin; dngos grub = siddhi; shes rab = prajna (here the female partner); mnyam gzhag = equipoise.
CONSTRUAL Through union with her, the yogin quickly gains siddhi; there is no doubt. Or else, one who is without a prajna should cultivate profound equipoise.
DOUBTS Q: de yi "her" = the consort of the preceding verses (outside the span); could be "that practice". Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i may qualify the equipoise ("an equipoise that is free of prajna") rather than the practitioner; taken as the practitioner without a consort. Q: shin tu "profound/complete" intensifier on mnyam gzhag.

## U02
FORM verse 7x2. GENRE instruction/promise. REGISTER plain.
SOURCE 1 mi g.yo bsgom pa yongs rdzogs na / 2 byang chub rgyal srid yang dag 'thob
CLAUSES 2. (a) bsgom pa yongs rdzogs: intransitive "is complete"; subject mi g.yo bsgom pa. (b) 'thob, agent elided (the practitioner), object byang chub rgyal srid, yang dag = truly/genuinely.
RELATIONS na = condition ("once/when ... is complete").
TERMS mi g.yo = Acala; byang chub = awakening; rgyal srid = kingdom.
CONSTRUAL When the meditation on Acala is complete, one truly gains the kingdom of awakening.
DOUBTS Q: mi g.yo bsgom pa could be "unwavering meditation" (adjectival); taken as the deity Acala given the chapter. Q: yang dag adverb "truly" vs "the true kingdom".

## U03
FORM mixed: prose frame + verse 7x4 + prose frame. GENRE narrative frame + request. REGISTER supplication to the Lord, honorific (gsol pa, 'tshal, gsungs); then honorific bka' stsal.
SOURCE f1 de nas bcom ldan 'das mas gsol pa / 1 lha yi rnam par dag pa gang / 2 'dren pa bdag ni nyan par 'tshal / 3 sngon du gsungs nyid dkyil 'khor rnams / 4 gtso bo bdag la rnam dag gsungs / f2 de nas bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal pa
CLAUSES f1 gsol pa (hon. "asked/said to"), agent bcom ldan 'das ma = the Blessed Lady (Bhagavati). 1 copular question "what is the purification of the deity?" 2 'tshal "I wish", 'dren pa vocative ("Guide"), bdag ni subject. 3-4 gsungs imperative (hon.), gtso bo vocative "Lord"; object: the purification of the mandalas taught before ("sngon du gsungs nyid" = which [you] yourself taught before); bdag la recipient. f2 bka' stsal, agent the Blessed One.
RELATIONS de nas = sequence (x2); la = recipient.
TERMS rnam par dag pa / rnam dag = purification; dkyil 'khor = mandala; lha = deity; 'dren pa = Guide; gtso bo = Lord; bcom ldan 'das ma = the Blessed Lady; bcom ldan 'das = the Blessed One.
CONSTRUAL Then the Blessed Lady asked: "What is the purification of the deity? Guide, I wish to hear it. The mandalas you yourself taught before: Lord, tell me their purification." Then the Blessed One said:
DOUBTS Q: nyid in pada 3 may emphasize "those very mandalas" rather than "you yourself". Q: pada 3-4 may be two requests: "the mandalas taught before, and their purification".

## U05
FORM prose, list of equations (X ni Y'o). GENRE exposition (symbolic correspondence). REGISTER impersonal, plain.
SOURCE (sentences) 1 de la gru bzhi pa ni tshad med pa'i gnas bzhi'o 2 sgo bzhi = bden pa bzhi 3 rta babs bzhi = bsam gtan bzhi 4 ka ba brgyad = 'phags pa'i lam yan lag brgyad 5 'phar ma gcig pa = sems rtse gcig pa nyid 6 padma = skye gnas 7 kha dog sna tshogs pa = sprul pa sna tshogs pa 8 lha dgu = gsung rab yan lag dgu 9 phyogs dmar po = 'dod chags chen po 10 mtshams su dkar ser ljang nag rnams = bram ze, rje rigs, rgyal rigs, dmangs rigs 11 zla ba dang nyi ma = khu ba dang rngul 12 dbus kyi ral gri = mi g.yo ba nag po'i mtshan ma 13 gri gug = zhe sdang rdo rje ma 14 shar la sogs pa'i phyogs rnams = mi g.yo ba dkar po la sogs pa rnams 15 me la sogs pa'i mtshams rnams su = gti mug rdo rje ma la sogs pa rnams kyi'o 16 zhes pa ni dkyil 'khor gyi rnam par dag pa'o
CLAUSES copular equations throughout; 15 genitive predicate "belong to Mohavajri and the others".
RELATIONS de la = "in this regard/therein" (topic setting); 10 order-correspondence (respectively); 16 zhes pa ni = summary frame "This is ...".
NEG tshad med = immeasurable.
QUANT bzhi/brgyad/gcig/dgu numerals; la sogs pa = "and the others".
TERMS gru bzhi = square; tshad med bzhi = four immeasurables; bden pa bzhi = four truths; rta babs = archway (torana); bsam gtan = dhyana; 'phags lam yan lag brgyad = noble eightfold path; 'phar ma gcig pa = single tier/ledge; rtse gcig = one-pointedness; skye gnas = place of birth/womb; sprul pa = emanation; gsung rab yan lag dgu = nine branches of scripture; 'dod chags = passion; castes; khu ba = semen; rngul = sweat; mtshan ma = emblem; gri gug = flaying knife; zhe sdang rdo rje ma = Dveshavajri; gti mug rdo rje ma = Mohavajri; mi g.yo ba nag po = Black Acala; me = southeast (fire quarter).
CONSTRUAL In this mandala, the square is the four abodes of the immeasurables; the four doors are the four truths; the four archways are the four dhyanas; the eight pillars are the noble eightfold path; the single tier is one-pointedness of mind; the lotus is the place of birth; the various colours are the various emanations; the nine deities are the nine branches of scripture; the red quarters are great passion; the white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are the brahmin, vaishya, kshatriya and shudra castes; moon and sun are semen and sweat; the sword in the centre is the emblem of Black Acala; the flaying knife is Dveshavajri; the directions beginning with the east are White Acala and the others; in the intermediate directions beginning with the southeast are [the places] of Mohavajri and the others. This is the purification of the mandala.
DOUBTS Q: 'phar ma gcig pa: "single tier/one ledge" of the palace wall; exact architectural member uncertain. Q: phyogs dmar po: "the red quarters" vs "the red direction" (singular). Q: rngul "sweat": likely stands for the female fluid; kept literal. Q: rje rigs/rgyal rigs order gives white-brahmin, yellow-vaishya, green-kshatriya, black-shudra as listed. Q: 15 kyi'o: "belong to" Mohavajri etc. (their positions).

## U06
FORM prose. GENRE exposition. REGISTER announcement then equations.
SOURCE 1 bsgom pa'i rnam par dag pa brjod par bya ste / 2 dang po mchod pa ni bsod nams kyi tshogs khyad par 'phags pa'i las so / 3 stong pa nyid ni ye shes kyi tshogs 'chi ba khyad par 'phags pa dri ma med pa'i lus te / dri za'i lus so / 4 gzhal yang [yas] khang gi mthar thug pa sangs rgyas kyi sa'o / 5 padma ni skye gnas so / 6 zla ba nyi ma ni dkar dmar ro
CLAUSES 1 brjod par bya future/intent "I shall state" (speaker the Blessed One), ste = sequence/introduces. 2-6 copular equations.
RELATIONS ste (1) = "namely"; 3 te = apposition/explanation ("that is"); genitive chains.
NEG dri ma med = stainless.
TERMS mchod pa = offering; bsod nams kyi tshogs = accumulation of merit; khyad par 'phags pa = supreme; las = action/deed (karma, NOT ablative: tool slip); stong pa nyid = emptiness; ye shes kyi tshogs = accumulation of wisdom; 'chi ba = death; dri za = gandharva (here the intermediate-state being); gzhal yas khang = palace (text spells gzhal yang); mthar thug pa = culmination; sangs rgyas kyi sa = the level of buddhahood; dkar dmar = white and red [elements].
CONSTRUAL I shall now state the purification of the meditation. First, the offering is the supreme action of the accumulation of merit. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom, the supreme death, the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva. The culmination of the palace is the level of buddhahood. The lotus is the place of birth. Moon and sun are the white and red.
DOUBTS Q: 2 khyad par 'phags pa may qualify the accumulation ("the action of the supreme accumulation of merit"). Q: 3 the list may be read: emptiness = accumulation of wisdom; [it is also] the supreme death; the stainless body = gandharva body (two equations). Kept as one appositive series. Q: 4 "the culmination of the palace" vs "the palace, the ultimate, is the level of buddhahood". Q: 1 speaker is the Blessed One (bdag elided).

## U07
FORM prose. GENRE exposition (conception narrative as symbolism). REGISTER impersonal, plain, explicit.
SOURCE 1 hum yig ni ma dang pha'i dbus su dri za'i sems so / 2 mi bskyod pa ni pha'o (text: ma bskyod pa ni pa'o) / 3 ma ma ki ni ma'o / 4 phan tshun du rjes su chags pa mthong nas pha la rnam par zhe sdang zhing / ma la rjes su chags nas gti mug pas sems can bzhin du 'jug go / 5 padmar zhugs pa'i bu yis pha bsad nas de'i go 'phang 'thob bo / 6 ma bzung nas skye ba mtha' med pa'i bag chags kyi bde ba khyad par 'phags pas de yang bur skye bar byed do / 7 bu mo ni yang mi g.yo ba dkar po la sogs pa rnams dang gti mug rdo rje ma la sogs pa yang ngo / 8 bu rnams kyang pha bsad nas gzhan la bgrod par byed de / 'di nyid dgra bo zhes de gsod par byed do / 9 bu mo rnams kyang skye ba mtha' med pa'i bag chags kyi bde ba khyad par 'phags pas 'dod par byed do
CLAUSES 4 mthong (inv. tr.), experiencer elided = the gandharva; zhe sdang (verbal) toward pha la; chags toward ma la; 'jug intr. "enters", gti mug pas cause. 5 bsad tr., agent bu yis ERG; 'thob. 6 bzung (past of 'dzin), agent the son; skye bar byed causative/tr.; cause bde bas. 8 bsad, bgrod par byed; zhes quotative (thinking "this is the enemy"), gsod par byed, object de. 9 'dod par byed "desire", cause bde bas.
RELATIONS 4 nas = sequence ("having seen ..."), zhing = coordination, nas = sequence, pas = cause ("through delusion"), bzhin du = simile ("like a sentient being"). 5 pa'i = modification (the son who has entered the lotus), nas = sequence. 6 nas = sequence, pas = cause (through the supreme bliss), yang = also/in turn. 7 dang/yang = coordination/also. 8 kyang = also; nas sequence; de = sequence/explanation; zhes = quotative/thought. 9 kyang = also; pas = cause.
NEG mtha' med = endless/beginningless (x2).
TERMS hum = HUM; dri za = gandharva; sems = mind; Akshobhya; Mamaki; rjes su chags pa = passion/desire (lust); zhe sdang = hatred; gti mug = delusion; sems can = sentient being; go 'phang = position/rank; bag chags = habitual tendencies; bde ba = bliss; khyad par 'phags pa = supreme; skye ba mtha' med pa = beginningless births.
CONSTRUAL The syllable HUM is the mind of the gandharva between mother and father. Akshobhya is the father; Mamaki is the mother. Seeing them in passion with each other, the gandharva feels hatred toward the father and desire for the mother, and through delusion enters, like an ordinary sentient being. The son, having entered the lotus, kills the father and attains his position. Having taken the mother, through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of beginningless births, he in turn brings about birth as a son. The daughters, again, are White Acala and the others, and also Mohavajri and the others. The sons too kill the father and then go on to others; thinking "this one is the enemy," they kill him. The daughters too, through the supreme bliss of the habitual tendencies of beginningless births, feel desire.
DOUBTS Q: 2 text "ma bskyod pa ni pa'o" = mi bskyod pa ni pha'o (spelling variants). Q: 6 de yang bur skye bar byed: "causes that one too to be born as a son" (object unclear: the mother? himself?); rendered "he in turn brings about birth as a son". Q: 7 "bu mo ... mi g.yo ba dkar po" — expected sons = the Acalas, daughters = the vajris; text as given assigns both to "daughters"; rendered as given. Q: 8 gzhan la bgrod pa "go on to others" vs "approach other [women]"; 'di nyid dgra bo — "this one" = the father (or another rival). Q: mtha' med "endless" vs "beginningless": beginningless is the natural English for past births.

## U08
FORM prose. GENRE exposition (iconography). REGISTER plain.
SOURCE 1 ral gri shes rab la zhags pa ni thabs so / 2 yang na zhags pa shes rab la ral gri thabs so / 3 gnyis ka ro gcig tu gyur pa ni sdigs mdzub po / 4 g.yon pa 'og tu bltas pa ni sa 'og bdun skyong bar byed pa'o / 5 g.yas pa steng du bltas pa ni tshangs pa'i sgo nga bdun skyong bar byed pa'o / 6 pus mo (bus mo) g.yon pa sa la btsugs pa ni sa skyong bar byed pa'o / 7 g.yas pa'i rkang pas yang dag par bsnun pa ni bdud thams cad skrag par mdzad pa'o / 8 phung po'i bdud tshangs pa / nyon mongs pa'i bdud dbang phyug / 'chi bdag gi bdud khyab 'jug / lha'i bu'i bdud lha'i dbang po'o
CLAUSES equations; 4-6 nominalized "the one that protects"; 7 rkang pas INSTR means, mdzad honorific (the deity's act).
RELATIONS la (1,2) = clause connective "and"; yang na = alternative; 7 pas = means.
QUANT bdun seven; thams cad all; gnyis ka both.
TERMS ral gri = sword; zhags pa = noose; shes rab = prajna; thabs = method; ro gcig = one taste; sdigs mdzub = threatening forefinger; sa 'og = underworld; tshangs pa'i sgo nga = Brahma's eggs (worlds); pus mo = knee; bdud = mara; phung po = aggregates; nyon mongs pa = afflictions; 'chi bdag = Lord of Death; lha'i bu = son of the gods; tshangs pa = Brahma; dbang phyug = Ishvara; khyab 'jug = Vishnu; lha'i dbang po = Indra.
CONSTRUAL The sword is prajna and the noose is method. Or else, the noose is prajna and the sword is method. The two becoming one taste is the threatening forefinger. The left one looking downward protects the seven underworlds; the right one looking upward protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the ground protects the earth. Striking firmly with the right foot terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma; the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara; the mara of the Lord of Death is Vishnu; the mara of the son of the gods is Indra.
DOUBTS Q: 4-5 g.yon pa / g.yas pa unspecified: likely the left and right eyes (Candamaharosana's squint); rendered "the left eye ... the right eye"? -> rendered "on the left, gazing downward" kept unspecified. Q: sdigs mdzub po: the gesture with the threatening forefinger. Q: bus mo read as pus mo (knee). Q: 'chi bdag gi bdud "mara of death / of the Lord of Death".

## U09
FORM prose. GENRE exposition. REGISTER plain, explicit.
SOURCE 1 sa ni sa steng mtha' dag gi gzhon nu ma la longs spyod pa'o / 2 gzhon nu ma la yun ring du gnas pa'i phyir padma'i gdan no / 3 skye gnas la skyes pa ni zla ba dang nyi ma'i gdan no / 4 khu ba dang rngul las skyes pa'i skyes bu ni dngos po'o / 5 bud med ni dngos po med pa'o / 6 nag po = rnam par shes pa / dkar po = gzugs / ser po = tshor ba / dmar po = 'du shes / ljang gu = don yod grub pa
CLAUSES equations; 1 longs spyod pa nominalized "enjoying"; 2 gnas pa (abiding) + phyir cause.
RELATIONS 2 phyir = cause ("because ... therefore the lotus seat"); 4 las = source ("born from"); pa'i = modification.
NEG 5 dngos po med pa = non-entity/absence of entity.
QUANT mtha' dag = all/entire.
TERMS sa = the ground (earth); gzhon nu ma = young woman; gdan = seat; skye gnas = place of birth; skyes bu = man/person (male here, contrasted with bud med); dngos po = entity (bhava); dngos po med pa = non-entity; rnam par shes pa = consciousness; gzugs = form; tshor ba = feeling; 'du shes = perception; don yod grub pa = Amoghasiddhi.
CONSTRUAL The ground is the enjoyment of young women throughout the whole earth. Because he dwells long with young women, there is the lotus seat. Being born in the place of birth is the moon and sun seat. The man born from semen and sweat is entity; the woman is non-entity. Black is consciousness; white is form; yellow is feeling; red is perception; green is Amoghasiddhi.
DOUBTS Q: 1 sa "ground/earth" here probably the ground of the mandala or the earth-touching knee of U08; rendered "the earth". Q: 2 subject of gnas pa elided (the deity/yogin). Q: 4 skyes bu "man" (male, contrasted with woman) vs "person". Q: 6 green = Amoghasiddhi where the aggregate (formation) is expected; kept as given.
