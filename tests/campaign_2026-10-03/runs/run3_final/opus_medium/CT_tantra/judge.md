## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U07 "The daughters too ... feel desire": the daughters are the object of desire, not the ones who desire (construal fork, unrecorded).
2. U07 "they kill him": the sons are made to kill the father again, where the likelier reading has the father kill the son as an enemy (construal fork).
3. U12 "the one and only Victor ... abides in the mode of five": making the predicate an epithet loses the one-in-five point (construal fork / verse constraint).
4. U07 "beginningless births" for མཐའ་མེད་པ ("endless"): an interpretive swap of a fixed sense (dictionary sense / term choice).
5. U01 "union with her": a feminine referent is added to a neutral དེ (construal fork, resolved toward context).
