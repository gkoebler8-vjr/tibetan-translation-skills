## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U01, la read as the connective "and" instead of the locative. The naturally pure element becomes "its element", and the unconditioned and free-from-elaboration attributes move to the dharmakaya. Cause: grammar misread / construal fork (recorded as Q but resolved the wrong way).
2. U09, yongs su dropped from "utterly impure realms". Cause: omission under compression / verse constraint.
3. U01–U02, one homage period chopped into flat predications ("his dharmakaya is unchanging", "It is endowed with"). The referent blurs and the devotional register weakens. Cause: skill rule misapplied (sentence splitting) / register.
4. U06–U09, the verse reads as a prose list in lines, only roughly metred, and fronting "displays" to the end of U06 leaves an awkward line. Cause: verse constraint.
5. U10, the crest-jewel simile is split off into its own sentence ("That meaning deserves to be honored ..."). This narrows its scope and adds a full stop the Tibetan does not have. Cause: construal fork / skill rule (period splitting).
