# rays — construal (Rays of Sunlight, opening)
Brief: seasoned practitioner; publication (reader edition, Garchen Stiftung manner). Sanskrit anglicized, no diacritics; phonetic Tibetan names; no brackets/Wylie; no contractions; em dashes ok; Q: for editor.
Spine: U01–U05 one homage period; head = shakya seng ge la ... 'dud do (U05). Structure by the three kayas: rang don du (dharmakaya) U01–U02; gzhan don du longs sku U03; mchog gi sprul sku U04; deeds + liberating U05. All relative clauses modify Shakyamuni.

## U01 (prose, homage; relative clause on Shakyamuni)
- rang don du — purpose/aspect: "for his own sake/as his own benefit" (la-don).
- chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa — dharmakaya, unchanging (apposition: he is the unchanging dharmakaya).
- khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa la — element (dhatu) pure by nature; la after verb = connective "and" (Q: or locus "in the naturally pure dhatu").
- glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing — adventitious stains completely stilled (two purities: natural + from adventitious stains). zhing = and.
- rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa — NEG: not compounded by causes and conditions (unconditioned).
- spros pa dang bral ba — free from elaborations.
- nam mkha' ltar — simile: like space (Q: modifies bral ba or bzhugs; take with what follows/precedes = space-like).
- srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing — finite: bzhugs (hon., intrans.) abides in the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana; shing = and (continues).
- NEG: 'gyur med, 'dus ma byas (2). Honorific bzhugs.
Plain: For his own sake, he is the unchanging dharmakaya: his element is pure by nature and the adventitious stains are completely stilled; unconditioned by causes and conditions, free from elaboration, like space, he abides in the equality of samsara and nirvana, and

## U02 (prose)
- stobs bcu dang mi 'jigs pa bzhi la sogs pa'i yon tan ldan pa dang — possesses qualities such as the ten powers and four fearlessnesses; dang = and. NEG: mi 'jigs (lexical: fearlessness).
Plain: he possesses qualities such as the ten powers and the four fearlessnesses; and

## U03 (prose)
- gzhan don du — for the sake of others.
- longs sku nges pa lnga ldan — sambhogakaya endowed with the five certainties (apposition: as the sambhogakaya).
- rgyal tshab sems dpa'i 'khor rnams kyi 'dren pa'i gtso bo r snang ba dang — appears as the chief guide of the retinues of bodhisattvas, the Victor's regents. Q: rgyal tshab = "regents" (heirs, e.g. Maitreya); construed as apposition to sems dpa'.
Plain: for the sake of others, as the sambhogakaya with the five certainties, he appears as the chief guide of his retinue of bodhisattvas who are his regents; and

## U04 (prose)
- brtse ba'i thugs rje chen po'i sprin las — from the cloud of loving great compassion (las source; image: cloud → rain in U05).
- mchog gi sprul sku s — agent/means: as the supreme nirmanakaya (instr.).
- sems can gyi khams la phyogs ris med par khyab cing — pervades the realm of sentient beings without partiality (NEG: ris med = impartially). cing = and.
Plain: from the cloud of his loving great compassion, as the supreme nirmanakaya he pervades the realm of beings without bias, and

## U05 (prose; main verb)
- mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te — displays the twelve deeds (te = sequence/and).
- zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis — by the rain of the sublime Dharma of peace and bliss (instr. means).
- rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud brlan te — moistens the mindstreams of disciples whose potential (rigs) has been genuinely awakened. Q: alternatively the rain awakens their potential (sad pa'i read as result); grammar: sad pa'i modifies gdul bya.
- grol bar mdzad pa'i — liberates them (hon. mdzad); te = sequence: moisten → liberate.
- bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shakya seng ge la — to Shakya Senge (Lion of the Shakyas), chief of the sugatas. la = addressee of homage.
- lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do — I bow with respect of body, speech and mind (gus pas: manner/means). Speaker: commentator, 1st person elided. Mood: performative homage.
Plain: To the Lion of the Shakyas, chief of the sugatas, who displays twelve deeds and, with the rain of the sublime Dharma of peace and bliss, moistens the mindstreams of disciples whose potential is awakened and so liberates them, I bow with respect in body, speech and mind.

## U06 (frame + verse 7x4, citation)
- de yang rgyud bla ma las — frame: "Accordingly, from the Uttaratantra". Source: Toh 4024, Uttaratantra (Mahayanottaratantrashastra) D 64b; dm.py identify verbatim yes, canonical. Uttaratantra II.53ff.
- thugs rje chen pos 'jig rten mkhyen — Knower of the World (lokavid, epithet = subject), through great compassion (instr.).
- 'jig rten kun la gzigs nas ni — having looked upon all worlds (hon. gzigs; nas sequence; ni filler).
- chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par — NEG: without moving from the dharmakaya (las source).
- sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis — through various emanated forms (instr. means).
- Main verb: ston (U09), "shows/displays". Speech act: description, cited as proof.

## U07 (verse 7x4; list of deeds, objects of ston)
- skye ba mngon par skye ba dang — Q: Skt jataka + upapatti: "former births" and "taking birth"; or one item "manifestly taking birth". Take two-part: (former) births and taking birth.
- dga' ldan gnas nas 'pho ba dang — departing from the Tushita abode (nas source).
- lhums su 'jug dang bltams pa dang — entering the womb, being born.
- btsun mo'i 'khor dgyes rol ba dang — delighting in play amid a retinue of consorts.
- MISSING (canonical): bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa (skill in the arts) — omitted in this witness; not supplied in body.

## U08 (verse 7x4)
- nges 'byung dka' ba spyod pa dang — renunciation, practicing austerities (two deeds; Q: or "austerities out of renunciation").
- byang chub snying por gshegs pa dang — going to the seat (heart) of enlightenment.
- bdud sde 'joms dang — defeating the hosts of mara.
- rdzogs par ni byang chub — perfect enlightenment (ni filler; botok split byang chub/chos wrongly).
- chos kyi 'khor lo dang — the wheel of Dharma (turning supplied as the deed's verb — Q).

## U09 (verse 7x3 + closing frame)
- mya ngan 'das par gshegs mdzad rnams — and the deed(s) of passing into nirvana; rnams pluralizes the whole run of deeds = object of ston.
- yongs su ma dag zhing rnams su — NEG: in impure realms.
- srid pa ji srid gnas par ston — he shows them for as long as existence remains (ji srid: duration). Finite verb ston, present.
- ces gsungs so — closing frame: "so it is said" (hon. gsungs; the text, not a person).

## U10 (prose, one long period; commitment to explain)
- gnyis pa ni — Second (outline heading: the commitment to explain).
- phung po ... nyon mongs pa ste bdud bzhi las rgyal ba'i thub pa mchog — the supreme Sage, victorious over the four maras: aggregates, Lord of Death, son of the gods, afflictions (ste = namely; las = from/over).
- gi gsung rab rtag chad kyi mtha' dang bral ba rgya mtsho'i klong ltar dpag tu med pa las — from his scriptures, free from the extremes of permanence and annihilation, immeasurable like the ocean's expanse (NEG: dpag tu med). las = source.
- mdo sde dang rgyud sde rnams dang — the sutras and tantras (apposition to gsung rab: what the scriptures are; Q).
- klu sgrub dang thogs med la sogs pa'i bstan bcos dang — treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga and others.
- chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po'i man ngag zab mo'i don klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i nang nas — from within [these and] the meaning of the profound pith instructions of the great Dharma Lord Drikungpa (Jigten Sumgön), worthy of honor like the crest jewel of the naga king. nang nas = from among. Q: simile scope — the instructions' meaning only, or all sources.
- ma rtogs par dang log par rtog pa dang the tshom gyi dri ma med par bsdus pa 'di ni — NEG: this, condensed without the stains of non-understanding, wrong understanding and doubt. Agent of bsdus elided (the root text's author, Zhedang Dorje). 'di = the root text.
- rin chen rnams kyi mchog baidurya la gsol ba btab na dgos 'dod thams cad 'byung ba bzhin — just as all needs and wishes arise if one prays to the vaidurya, supreme among jewels (na condition; bzhin simile).
- gnas skabs dang mthar thug gi don thams cad 'grub par byed pa'i bstan bcos — the treatise that accomplishes all aims, temporary and ultimate.
- gzhi dang lam dang 'bras bu gsum du bstan pa'i snying po — the Essence, which teaches in three: ground, path, fruition. Title pun on theg chen bstan pa'i snying po. Q.
- bshad par bya'o — I shall explain (future/intent; speaker the commentator).
Plain: Second. From the supreme Sage's immeasurable scriptures (sutras, tantras), the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga etc., and the meaning of Drikungpa's profound instructions, worthy of honor like the naga king's crest jewel, this text was condensed free from the stains of non-understanding, misunderstanding and doubt. Like the vaidurya that grants all needs and wishes when prayed to, it is a treatise that accomplishes all temporary and ultimate aims: the Essence, taught as ground, path and fruition. I shall explain it.

## U11 (prose)
- de yang dgos sogs kyi chos bzhi las — among the four topics beginning with purpose (las = partitive). de yang = now / in this regard.
- brjod bya ni gzhi lam 'bras bu gsum mo — the subject matter is the three: ground, path, fruition.

## U12 (prose; three definitions)
- dgos pa ni thos bsam gyis de rtogs pa'o — purpose: to understand it (de = subject matter) through study and reflection (instr.).
- dgos pa'i dgos pa ni de'i don bsgoms pas 'bras bu mngon du 'gyur ba'o — ultimate purpose: by meditating on its meaning (pas cause/means), the fruition becomes manifest.
- 'brel ba ni snga ma la brten te phyi ma rim gyis ... — connection: relying on the former, the latter [arises] in sequence; they are connected in the manner of means and what arises from the means (thabs dang thabs las byung ba; tshul gyis manner).
