## U01 prose, exposition, 10 clauses (list)
CLAUSES: (1) 'dun pa nyams pa med do: ABS 'dun pa (gen. de bzhin gshegs pa'i), existential med w/ nyams pa; plain. (2) de la 'dun pa gang zhe na = "what is aspiration here?" (3) 'di lta ste ... 'dun pa'o = namely, aspiration for virtuous qualities (copular 'o). (4) de yang gang zhe na = "and what are those (virtuous qualities)?" (5-12) eight items "X la (de bzhin gshegs pa'i) 'dun pa nyams pa med do": la = object/target of aspiration. Subject tathagata elided from item 3 on (filled from items 1-2).
ITEMS (X): byams pa chen po; snying rje chen po; chos ston pa; sems can 'dul ba; sems can yongs su smin par bya ba; rab tu dben pa; sems can byang chub yang dag par 'dzin du 'jug pa; dkon mchog gsum gyi rigs rgyun mi gcod pa.
NEG: nyams pa med x8 (undiminished) = "does not decline"; mi gcod = "not cutting" (inside the last item, scope: cutting the lineage).
RELATIONS: la = aspiration directed at X; gang zhe na = rhetorical question; 'di lta ste = namely; de yang = and.
TERMS: 'dun pa=aspiration; nyams pa med=does not decline; sems can 'dul ba = taming beings; smin = ripen; rab tu dben pa = seclusion; 'jug pa = inspire/engage (causative of entering).
CONSTRUAL: Moreover, son of noble family, the tathagata's aspiration does not decline. What is this aspiration? Namely aspiration for virtuous qualities. What are these qualities? The tathagata's aspiration for great love does not decline; likewise for great compassion; then (tathagata's) aspiration for teaching the Dharma, taming beings, ripening beings, seclusion, inspiring beings to embrace awakening rightly, not cutting the lineage of the Three Jewels: each does not decline.
DOUBTS: Q: rigs kyi bu = "son of noble family" (kulaputra); Alt noble son. Q: dge ba'i chos = virtuous qualities; alt virtuous practices/phenomena. Q: byang chub yang dag par 'dzin du 'jug pa: yang dag par adverb of 'dzin (embrace rightly; samadapana) vs "perfect awakening" with 'dzin 'jug as hold/engage; kept: inspire beings to embrace awakening rightly. Q: rab tu dben pa = seclusion (alt complete seclusion/solitude).

## U02 prose, exposition, 6 clauses
CLAUSES: (1) de bzhin gshegs pa ni 'dun pas 'gro ba ma yin gyi: ERG 'dun pas (instr: from, out of), 'gro ba = verb "proceed/act" (cf. U04 'dod ... gis 'gro ma yin), ma yin = negated; gyi = contrastive ("but rather"). (2) de bzhin gshegs pa'i 'dun pa ni ye shes sngon du 'gro ba nyid de: ABS 'dun pa topic; ye shes sngon du 'gro ba = wisdom goes before (is preceded by wisdom); nyid emphatic "itself/ just that"; de = and/sequence. (3) de'i phyir ... nyams pa med ces bya'o: phyir = cause "for that reason"; ces bya = "it is called/said". (4) sems can thams cad la bla na med pa'i 'dun pa yongs su rdzogs par bya ba'i phyir chos kyang ston te: phyir = purpose; kyang = also; subject tathagata elided; the unsurpassed aspiration (in/for all beings) is the thing brought to completion. (5) 'di ni ... 'phrin las nyi shu rtsa gcig pa'o: "this is the twenty-first of the tathagata's activities" (de bzhin gshegs pa'i repeated twice in Tibetan: dittography/title-formula; rendered once). (6) de la 'di skad ces bya'o = "concerning this, the following is said" (verse introducer).
NEG: ma yin (scope 'gro ba by 'dun pas); med (nyams pa med); bla na med pa (unsurpassed).
CONSTRUAL: The tathagata does not act out of aspiration; rather the tathagata's aspiration is itself preceded by wisdom. For that reason it is said the tathagata's aspiration does not decline. In order to bring the unsurpassed aspiration to completion in all beings, he also teaches the Dharma. This is the twenty-first of the tathagata's activities. Concerning this, the following is said.
DOUBTS: Q: 'dun pas 'gro ba ma yin: 'dun pa here may be the wrong-course "desire/partiality" (cf. U04 'dod dang zhe sdang ... gis 'gro ma yin); rendered with the same word "aspiration" per bound term; alt: "is not led by desire". Q: 'gro ba = verb (act) not noun (beings): grammar: 'dun pas instr + 'gro ba + ma yin; alt reading would leave 'dun pas unconstrued. Q: sems can thams cad la ... 'dun pa yongs su rdzogs par bya ba: whose aspiration is perfected; kept as the unsurpassed aspiration in all beings (U04 'dun la sbyor supports); alt: his aspiration toward all beings. Q: de bzhin gshegs pa'i de bzhin gshegs pa'i 'phrin las: doubled genitive; kept as one "tathagata's activities". Q: ye shes = wisdom.

## U03 verse, 4 padas x 15 syllables, free verse, 4 lines
P1: rgyal ba'i 'dun pa rtag tu dge ba'i chos kyi tshul la 'dod cing dga' -- subject 'dun pa (gen rgyal ba'i), verbs 'dod and dga' coordinated by cing; la = object (tshul of virtuous qualities); rtag tu = always. = The Victor's aspiration always longs for and delights in the ways of virtuous qualities.
P2: sems can rab tu thar bar bya phyir byams dang snying rje'i chos kyang sbyin -- phyir = purpose (to set beings free); kyang = also; sbyin = give (subject Victor, elided); chos governed by genitive of byams dang snying rje (the Dharma of love and compassion).
P3: sems can yongs smin bya dang nags tshal dga' la mos pa nyams pa med -- (his) devotion (mos pa) to ripening beings and to delighting in forests does not decline. dang = coordinates two targets (smin bya; nags tshal dga').
P4: rab tu mang po byang chub 'dzin 'jug dkon mchog gsum gyi rgyun mi gcod -- rab tu mang po = very many (beings, elided object); byang chub 'dzin 'jug = inspires to embrace awakening; dkon mchog gsum gyi rgyun mi gcod = does not cut the lineage (rgyun) of the Three Jewels. Two verbs/clauses in one pada.
VERSE-NOTES: parallels prose items in U01 (aspiration for virtuous qualities; love, compassion; ripening; seclusion (forests); awakening; Three Jewels). Filler: ni/ cing; 'dzin 'jug elides 'par/ du.
DOUBTS: Q: dge ba'i chos kyi tshul = "ways of virtuous qualities" (alt: way/practice of virtue). Q: byams dang snying rje'i chos = the Dharma of love and compassion (alt: love, compassion, and the Dharma; two items). Q: mos pa governs both smin bya and nags tshal dga' (devotion to ripening beings and to delighting in forests); alt: only forests. Q: rab tu mang po: object of 'jug elided = beings; "very many" kept. Q: rgyun alone (here) = lineage as in U01 rigs rgyun.

## U04 verse, 4 padas x 15 syllables
P1: 'dod dang zhe sdang dang ni 'jigs dang gti mug gis ni 'gro ma yin -- ERG gis over four coordinated (desire, hatred, fear, delusion); 'gro ma yin = does not act/proceed (the four wrong courses). NEG scope: whole.
P2: khyad par gyur nas mkhas de shes pas byas pa'i chos kun rtogs par 'gyur -- nas = sequence; subject mkhas de (that wise one = the Victor); shes pas = by that knowing; byas pa'i chos kun = all the things (dharmas) that have been done/made; rtogs par 'gyur = comes to realize.
P3: rgyal ba sems can 'dun bral le lo can dag mthong nas 'dun la sbyor -- nas = sequence; beings who lack aspiration and are lazy (dag plural); sbyor = engages/applies them to aspiration (la = goal).
P4: gang gis 'gro ba 'dun pa'i las ni mtshungs pa med pa'i shes pas rig -- gang gis = by whom (the Victor) ERG agent of rig; 'gro ba = beings; 'dun pa'i las = the activity (working) of aspiration; mtshungs pa med pa'i shes pa = incomparable knowledge (instr. -s); rig = know.
DOUBTS: Q: khyad par gyur nas = having become distinguished/excellent (alt: having become special, i.e., "in particular"); the whole pada 2 is obscure: alt reading "by knowing that, the wise one, being distinguished, realizes everything that is done". Q: byas pa'i chos kun = all that has been done (alt: all conditioned/made phenomena). Q: 'dun pa'i las = the working of aspiration (alt: karma of aspiration; alt: las as "than": aspiration beyond beings'). Q: gang gis referent: the Victor (agent) vs relative "by which". Q: 'gro ba 'dun pa'i las = 'beings' aspiration' (genitive on beings) vs 'activity of aspiration in beings'.

## U05 prose, exposition, ~9 clauses
S1: ... brtson 'grus nyams pa med do = tathagata's diligence does not decline.
S2: de la ... brtson 'grus ni 'di yin te 'di lta ste: [a] 'dul ba'i sems can rnams yal bar mi 'dor ba'i brtson 'grus = diligence of not abandoning (letting fall away) the beings to be tamed; dang; [b] chos nyan pa dag la brnyas pa med pa'i brtson 'grus = diligence of having no contempt for those who listen to the Dharma. so = copula. NEG: mi 'dor, med.
S3: gal te (if) de bzhin gshegs pas ... rnyed na (condition): finds [chos kyi snod chos nyan pa gang (relative) bskal par yang chos nyan pas skyo bar mi 'gyur ba]: a vessel of the Dharma, a listener who does not become weary of hearing the Dharma even for an eon; then (apodosis) de bzhin gshegs pa yang chos ston = the tathagata too teaches the Dharma (yang = also/correlative). Next: kha zas dang bral ba'i rgyud du gyur kyang = even though he is in a state free of food (kyang concession); bskal par yang mi ldang ngo = he does not rise even for an eon (NEG mi).
S4: de bzhin gshegs pa ni sems can la lta bas (cause: looking upon beings) tha na (even to the extent) gang na (wherever) sangs rgyas kyis 'dul ba'i sems can gcig gi phyir yang (for the sake of even a single being a buddha can tame) sangs rgyas kyi zhing gang gA'i klung gi bye ma snyed 'da' zhing bzhud de = crosses and travels over buddha fields as numerous as the grains of sand of the river Ganges.
S5: de bzhin gshegs pa la sku mnyel ba 'am gsung mnyel ba 'am thugs mnyel ba med do = the tathagata has no fatigue of body, speech or mind. hon. forms.
CONSTRUAL: [as above, in English] Moreover, son of noble family, the tathagata's diligence does not decline. Here is his diligence: it is diligence in not abandoning beings to be tamed, and diligence in having no contempt for those who listen to the Dharma. If the tathagata finds a vessel of the Dharma, a listener who does not grow weary of hearing the Dharma even for an eon, then the tathagata too teaches the Dharma; even if he goes without food he does not rise for an eon. Because he looks upon beings, he goes so far as to cross buddha fields as many as the grains of sand in the Ganges, even for one being a buddha can tame, wherever that being is. The tathagata has no fatigue of body, speech or mind.
DOUBTS: Q: yang chos ston (S3 apodosis) = "too/also teaches" vs "teaches even for an eon" (kept: the tathagata too teaches, matching the listener). Q: subject of mi ldang = tathagata (kept). Q: chos kyi snod chos nyan pa = vessel who listens (apposition), kept as "a vessel of the Dharma, a listener". Q: tha na gang na sangs rgyas kyis 'dul ba'i sems can gcig: "wherever there is one being a buddha can tame" vs "wherever a buddha tames"; kept the former. Q: sangs rgyas kyis = "a buddha" (generic) vs the tathagata himself. Q: yal bar mi 'dor = not abandon/ not let fall away.

## U06 prose, exposition, 4 clauses
Tathagata (topic): sems shin tu sbyangs pa (mind well trained) / brtson 'grus brtsams pa (has undertaken diligence) / brtson 'grus kyi bsngags pa smra ba (speaks praise of diligence) ste; yang dag par sbyor ba'i brtson 'grus gang gis ... (diligence of right application, by which [gang gis instr.] sems can rnams kyis 'phags pa'i rnam par grol ba thob par 'gyur ba = beings come to attain the noble liberation) de sems can rnams la bsngags par byed = he praises that diligence to beings. 'di ni ... nyi shu rtsa gnyis pa'o = twenty-second of the tathagata's activities. de la 'di skad ces bya'o = introducer.
NEG: none.
DOUBTS: Q: 'phags pa'i rnam par grol ba = noble liberation (alt: the liberation of the noble ones). Q: sems shin tu sbyangs pa = mind thoroughly trained (alt: pliant). Q: brtson 'grus brtsams pa = has undertaken diligence (alt: strives). Q: yang dag par sbyor ba = right application (alt: right exertion).

## U07 verse, 4 padas x 11 syllables
P1+P2: one sentence: brtson 'grus gang gis mi yi seng ge 'phags 'gyur ba / brtson 'grus stobs de'i bsngags pa rtag tu rjod par byed: (the diligence by which the lion among men becomes noble [is exalted]) -- that power of diligence's praise he always utters (rjod par byed, subject = the lion among men/the Victor). 
P3: brtson 'grus stobs ni gang du'ang nyams pa yod min te = the power of diligence is in no case/nowhere subject to decline (yod min = there is no).
P4: snod du gyur pa rnyed nas chos kyang ston par byed: having found one who has become a vessel, he also teaches the Dharma (kyang = also).
DOUBTS: Q: 'phags 'gyur ba = is ennobled/exalted (alt: becomes an arya). Q: mi yi seng ge = lion among men = the Buddha. Q: gang du'ang = anywhere (alt: in any way). Q: snod du gyur pa = one who has become a vessel (of the Dharma, cf. U05).

## U08 verse, 4 padas x 11 syllables
P1: bde bar gshegs kyi brtson 'grus gnas pa yod min te = the Well-Gone One's diligence has no abiding (does not come to rest).
P2: lus dang ngag dang sems ni gang du'ang skyo ba med = in body, speech and mind there is no weariness anywhere.
P3-P4: ji ltar ... de ltar: just as his self-arisen (rang byung) diligence is in every way free of wrongdoing (sdig med), so with a trained mind (blo sbyangs) he inspires beings to embrace (awakening) rightly (yang dag 'dzin du 'jug).
DOUBTS: Q: gnas pa yod min = does not come to rest (alt: has no fixed abode/basis). Q: rang byung = self-arisen modifying diligence (alt: the Self-Arisen One's diligence; svayambhu epithet of buddhas). Q: object of 'dzin elided; supplied awakening from U01/U03. Q: blo sbyangs subject = the Well-Gone One.

## U09 prose, exposition, long
S1 dran pa nyams pa med: tathagata's mindfulness does not decline in all respects (thams cad kyi thams cad du; rnam pa thams cad kyi thams cad du = reduplicative totality).
S2 de ci'i phyir zhe na: de bzhin gshegs pa la bsnyel ba med do = why: tathagata has no forgetfulness (la = locus).
S3 rigs kyi bu (vocative) ... ma thag tu (immediately upon awakening to unsurpassed perfect awakening) ... 'das pa dang ma 'ongs pa dang da ltar byung ba'i sems can rnams kyi sems kyi rgyud la rnam par gzigs kyang (concessive: although he looks upon the mindstreams of beings past, future, present) de la ... bsnyel ba shin tu med do = in regard to that he has no forgetfulness at all.
S4 sems can gyi spyod pa ji lta ba bzhin du mkhyen kyang (although knows beings' conduct as it is) de la de bzhin gshegs pas ye shes yang gtong ba med do = he does not let go of wisdom either (ERG pas: agent).
S5 de bzhin gshegs pa ni [A phung po gsum du rnam par dgod pa dang, B sems can gyi dbang po la 'jug pa dang, C bsam pa la 'jug pa dang, D spyod pa la rnam par gzigs pas] dran pa nyams pa med pa nyid de = through A-D, mindfulness is undiminished (pas = cause/means; nyid = emphatic "precisely").
S6 dran par byas sam bsams sam rnam par brtags te sems can rnams la chos ston pa med do = he does not teach the Dharma after calling to mind, thinking, or deliberating (te = sequence; sam = or). NEG med.
S7 ston kyang nyams pa med do = even while teaching, (mindfulness) does not decline. Subject elided: mindfulness.
S8 de ci'i phyir zhe na: de bzhin gshegs pa'i dran pa ni ji ltar nyams pa med do. de ji ltar bdag dran zhing bsnyel ba med pa de ltar sems can rnams la chos ston te = why: mindfulness is undiminished in this way: just as he himself is mindful and without forgetfulness, so he teaches the Dharma to beings.
S9 twenty-third activity; introducer.
DOUBTS: Q: thams cad kyi thams cad ... = reduplicative idiom, rendered "in any way at all, in any respect whatever". Q: phung po gsum = three groups (rasi), not skandhas; object (beings) supplied. Q: ye shes yang gtong ba med: gtong = let go/abandon (alt: exert/apply); yang = either (alt: even). Q: ji ltar nyams pa med do: rendered "undiminished in this way: just as ..."; alt "how is it undiminished? Just as...". Q: ma thag tu ... gzigs kyang tense: present/habitual vs completed; kept as "as soon as he awakened, even as he looks upon". Q: de la (S3, S4) = regarding that / in it. Q: dran par byas = calling to mind (deliberate recollection).

## U10 verse, 4 padas x 7 syllables
P1: ston pa'i dran pa gang yang nyams med de = the Teacher's mindfulness does not decline at all.
P2: rgyal ba'i dran pas dran par bya ba med = the Victor's mindfulness has nothing to recall (dran pas ERG: mindfulness is the agent).
P3-4: rgyal bas gang tshe byang chub sangs rgyas nas / 'gro ba'i sems ni ma lus rnam par mkhyen = when the Victor has awakened to buddhahood, he knows the minds of beings without exception (nas = sequence; ni topic).
DOUBTS: Q: dran pas dran par bya ba med = nothing to recall (alt: there is no deliberate calling to mind through mindfulness). Q: tense of P3-4 (present, general). Q: gang tshe = when (since the moment).

## U11 verse, 4 padas x 7 syllables
P1+P2: bsam pa dbang po sems can spyod pa dang / phyis yang rnam par shes par bya ba med = of beings' dispositions, faculties and conduct there is nothing (more) to be discerned later (phyis yang = later also).
P3+P4: lhun gyis grub pa'i bya bar gnas nas ni / rgyal ba'i dbang po 'gro la chos ston to = abiding in spontaneously accomplished activity, the Lord of Victors teaches the Dharma to beings (nas = sequence; la = recipient).
DOUBTS: Q: rgyal ba'i dbang po = Lord of Victors (genitive) vs "the Victor, the lord" (apposition). Q: phyis yang = later on (alt: again later; even later). Q: bya bar gnas = abiding in activity (la/r terminative: dwelling in).
