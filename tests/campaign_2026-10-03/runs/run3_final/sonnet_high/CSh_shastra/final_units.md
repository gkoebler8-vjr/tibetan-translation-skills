### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose, 5 paragraphs · register: exposition and refutation (argument; one etymology; one instruction “is to be empowered”) · academic mode · source: not a citation; refers to the Hevajra and to an abhisamaya text
TEXT:
In this Yoginī tantra one is a “master” because one fully practises what has become far removed from sinful, non-virtuous phenomena, since that very [practice] is one’s vow.

The essence of the empowerment by the master is what is called “fully accomplishing, through the four moments from the action seal, the very essence of the four joys.” But further, because [it] is shared with the Action tantras and the rest, one who has obtained the irreversible empowerment has been empowered by the master, and so empowerment is not to be performed on the empowered.

Therefore the statement “one will also be endowed with the fortune of explaining, listening to, and so on, all the Yoga and Yoginī tantras and the rest” is nothing at all.

Therefore, first, in order to have authority over listening to, meditating on, and so on, the Yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, one is to be empowered with the master’s empowerment itself. Moreover, [the empowerment] is to be understood as indicated by the characteristics of the master, the secret, the wisdom and the fourth [empowerments], just as in the *abhisamaya*.
NOTES:
Q: “slob dpon te … phyir”: “fully practises what has become far removed from sinful, non-virtuous phenomena” takes ring du gyur pa as the object of practice; MITRA reads it as a predicate of the master (“has become far removed … through his conduct”). The bare juxtaposition ring du gyur pa kun du spyod pa favours the object reading; alt: “because one, having become far removed from …, practises fully”. The etymology is that of ācārya (kun du spyod pa), no Sanskrit asserted.
Q: “de nyid kyi de’i sdom pa yin pas”: “that very [practice] is one’s vow”; alt: “it is the vow of that very [empowerment]” (cf. U01, sdom pa = dbang bskur ba).
Q: “slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba’i ngo bo”: “the empowerment by the master” (ergative); alt: genitive, “the master’s empowerment” (as gyi in later clauses).
Q: the clause “yang … phyir … dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya’o” reads as the position refuted by “ci yang ma yin no”; the Tibetan does not mark it as an objector’s view. The referent of “thun mong du gyur pa” (what is shared with the Action tantras) is not stated; “[it]” kept open. MITRA reads: the master’s empowerment is not to be performed for one who already has the irreversible empowerment; mine: one who has it has been empowered by the master, so empowerment is not to be performed on the empowered. Same upshot; the grammar (thob pas / dbang bskur bas as two causal clauses) kept.
Q: “mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du”: “just as in the abhisamaya”, a ritual manual; alt: “in accordance with the direct realization”. Lower-case italic kept.
Q: “slob dpon dang gsang ba dang shes rab dang bzhi pa’i mtshan nyid”: “[empowerments]” supplied; the characteristics are those of the four empowerments.
Alt: skal pa = “fortune” (alt “share, eligibility”); dgyes pa’i rdo rje = Hevajra, which is also the title word of the commentary.
Issue: “mi ldog pa’i dbang bskur ba” = “irreversible empowerment”; no source identified for the term.
MITRA: differs at S1 (reads ring du gyur pa as predicate of the master; “samaya” for sdom pa), S2 (reads master’s empowerment as what is not performed), S3 (adds “if one has not received this specific empowerment”); kept.
Check: in-context, 0 major; 1 minor (capitalisation of tantra classes made consistent).

### U06

TEXT:

Likewise, on the side of the completion stage also, the experience of the four joys, whose nature is the four moments, through the guru’s own pith instruction, a jewel dwelling in secret: this is called the “secret empowerment”, because it is not to be taught to yogins who are engaged in meditation on elaboration.

Here, to those of middling faculties for empowerment, meditation on the pledge seal is to be taught and explained.
NOTES:
Q: “mos pas nges par bzung nas”: the disciples “have firmly grasped [it] through conviction”; alt: the master, having firmly grasped [them] through their conviction. The object of grasping is elided.
Q: “gsang bar gnas pa’i nor bu bla ma’i man ngag nyid”: “a jewel dwelling in secret” in apposition to the guru’s pith instruction (read; it fits the reason given, that it is not to be taught to the elaboration meditators); alt: the jewel as a separate object (the jewel in the secret [place]). MITRA sets the jewel in apposition to the guru; same family of readings.
Q: “skad cig ma bzhi’i rang bzhin dga’ ba bzhi po” = “the four joys, whose nature is the four moments”; alt: “the nature of the four moments, the four joys”.

Issue: register shifts to instruction at “bstan par bya ba”; kept impersonal (“are to be taught”) as the Tibetan has no “you”.

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose, 3 paragraphs · register: exposition (definitions) · academic mode · source: not a citation
TEXT:
Likewise, the distinctive gnosis is wisdom: the knowing of all phenomena as nothing but mind. The empowerment for the full accomplishment of that is the wisdom-gnosis empowerment.

Moreover, the heart-jewel that has form, gathers the essence of the three consciousnesses (the dependent, the imagined and the transformed) and, through the guru’s own pith instruction, correctly indicates the external seal and the moments: this is to be understood as wisdom-gnosis.

By that empowerment, to those of superior faculties who are empowered, the dharma seal is to be taught and explained through illusion-like samādhi.
NOTES:
Q: “gzhan gi dbang dang kun brtags dang yongs su gyur pa rnam par shes pa gsum”: “the three consciousnesses (the dependent, the imagined and the transformed)”; alt: “the three [natures]: dependent, imagined, perfected” (yongs su gyur for yongs su grub). MITRA reads the latter (paratantra, parikalpita, pariniṣpanna); kept “transformed” as the Tibetan has gyur.
Q: “gzugs can” = “that has form”; alt: “embodied”.
Q: “snying gi nor bu” = “heart-jewel”; unglossed in the span.
Q: “phyi rol gyi phyag rgya” = “external seal”; unglossed in the span.
Issue: “phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang skad cig ma rnams yang dag par mtshon pa”: the seal and the moments are the object of mtshon pa (no instrumental marker), the guru’s instruction the means, and the heart-jewel the agent. First drafted the other way round; changed after the MITRA comparison, on the grammar.
MITRA: differs at U07 S3 (reads the seal and the moments as indicated within the heart-jewel; “perfected” for yongs su gyur pa); changed the first, kept the second.
Check: in-context, 0 major; revision after MITRA touched one relation.

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose, 3 paragraphs · register: commentary gloss on the lemma “de ltar de bzhin yang”, then argument · academic mode · source: lemma = Toh 418 pada 2 abbreviated (cf. U04); “’di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba” = U02 pada 3; speaker of the latter: the Blessed One
TEXT:
Likewise there is also the fourth, [as in] “thus likewise also” (de ltar de bzhin yang). “That” (de) means wisdom-gnosis. “Likewise” (de bzhin) means suchness; there is no difference of meaning among those called “suchness”, “true limit” and “dharma realm.” The empowerment by which one sees its very essence, or fully accomplishes it, is what is to be expressed by the word “likewise.” The word “also” (yang) means that [it] is also to be given following the wisdom-gnosis empowerment.

Moreover, the focusing, by the yoga of focusing without a focus, on the external seal and on the perfection of the moment (the latter having the characteristic of not being definitively delimited, object by object, through the guru’s own pith instruction): this does not become the fourth empowerment merely through being shown the pith instruction that is to be called “the fourth empowerment.”

Otherwise, if [by words] such as “washed by this and empowered” the Blessed One intends empowerment here to be the washing of stains, how could it come about through merely being taught a pith instruction?
NOTES:
Q: the lemma “de ltar de bzhin yang” is shorter than the root pada in U04 (“bzhi pa” missing); the gloss treats “de” as “that” (wisdom-gnosis) and “de bzhin” as “suchness”, which English cannot show without the Wylie, given in brackets.
Q: “de bzhin nyid de yang dag pa’i mtha’ dang chos kyi dbyings”: the “de” read as part of a list of three terms without difference of meaning; alt: “suchness, [that is,] the true limit and the dharma realm”. Same sense.
Q: the long sentence: the first “de ni” is the focusing described; the subject of “does not become” is that focusing; “nye bar bstan par byas pa tsam gyis” = merely through being shown. The relative clause “gang … mtshan nyid can” is read as modifying only “skad cig ma’i phun sum tshogs pa”; alt: both the seal and the perfection of the moment.
Q: “dmigs pa med par dmigs pa’i rnal ’byor” = “the yoga of focusing without a focus”; alt: “yoga that takes the non-objectifiable as its object”.
Issue: the lemma “’di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba” is set inline, not as a verse block, because it is one pada quoted with “la sogs pa”; the full stanza is U02.
Issue: “de bzhin du … yin te” rendered “Likewise there is also the fourth, [as in]”.
MITRA: differs at U08 (swaps the glosses of de bzhin / yang; reads the external seals as what does not delimit; “non-conceptualizing conceptualization” for dmigs pa med par dmigs pa); kept.
Check: in-context, 1 minor (“washing away” made “washing”).
