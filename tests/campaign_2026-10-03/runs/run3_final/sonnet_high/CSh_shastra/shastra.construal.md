## U01  prose, commentary gloss (lemma + glosses), ends on lead-in "de yang ji skad du"
SOURCE sdom pa'i dbye ba la sogs pa la / sdom pa ni dbang bskur ba'o / de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste / 'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no / de yang ji skad du
CLAUSES 1 lemma (la = "regarding"); 2 "sdom pa ni dbang bskur ba'o" (copular gloss: vow = empowerment); 3 "de'i rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba ste" (its classification is 'division'); 4 "'chad par 'gyur ba'i mtshan nyid can no" (having the characteristic of what will be explained; subject = the division); 5 lead-in.
RELATIONS la = topic/"as to" after a lemma; ste = sequence/coordination of glosses; pa'i = modification; yang = "also/and".
NEG none. SPINE 3 short sentences + lead-in. 
CONSTRUAL Regarding the root words "the divisions of the vow, and so on": the "vow" is empowerment. Its classification is the "division", which has the characteristic of what will be explained. And as is said [following].
TERMS sdom pa=vow; dbang bskur ba=empowerment; dbye ba=division; rab tu dbye ba=classification; mtshan nyid=characteristic.
DOUBTS Q: rab tu dbye ba ni dbye ba = "its classification is [what the root calls] 'division'" (read) vs "classification, i.e. division" (equation, same sense).  Q: mtshan nyid can = "having the characteristic of" (read) vs "defined as, namely".

## U02  verse citation 7x4 (+ frame "ces gsungs pa yin no"), Hevajra-type root stanza
SOURCE sems can rnams la phan pa'i phyir / dbang bskur ba ni bzhi ru bsgrags / 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur bas / de phyir dbang bskur zhes byar brjod / ces gsungs pa yin no
CLAUSES (1) bsgrags: past of sgrog, tr.; patient dbang bskur ba (topic ni); ru = terminative "as four"; phyir = purpose, la = beneficiary. Agent elided (the speaker). (2) bkrus shing ... dbang bskur bas: ergative/instrumental 'di yis = means; verbs bkrus (wash) + dbang bskur (empower); bas = cause; elided patient (the one empowered). (3) de phyir = therefore; dbang bskur zhes byar brjod: passive 'it is called'. Frame: ces gsungs pa yin no (honorific).
RELATIONS phyir = purpose (pada 1) ; yis = means; shing = coordination; bas = cause; de phyir = consequence; zhes byar = naming.
VERSE-NOTES rnams plural marker; ni topic; ru = terminative; 'bas' cause + 'de phyir' doubled cause by design (etymology of dbang bskur).
CONSTRUAL To benefit sentient beings, empowerment is proclaimed as four. Because [one] is washed with this and empowered, for that reason it is called "empowerment". Thus [the Blessed One] has said.
CITATION dm.py identify: verbatim only in Toh 1183 itself (D 6a); source of the stanza not located in the index. Hevajra root attribution not confirmed. U08 ascribes the half-line 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba to bcom ldan 'das.
VARIANTS U08 cites pada 3 as "'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba" (no -s).
DOUBTS Q: speaker of gsungs elided; read "the Blessed One" from U08 (bcom ldan 'das kyis ... bzhed pa). Q: 'di yis = by "this" (the rite/water; antecedent unspecified); kept "this". Q: dbang bskur bas read as cause on the passive disciple ("one is washed and empowered"); alt "since this washes and empowers".

## U04  verse (2 padas 7x2) quoted as lemma + gloss; frame "zhes bya ba la sogs pas 'chad par 'gyur ba yin no"
SOURCE slob dpon gsang ba shes rab dang / de ltar de bzhin bzhi pa yang / zhes bya ba la sogs pas 'chad par 'gyur ba yin no
CLAUSES padas = a list (slob dpon, gsang ba, shes rab; dbang bskur elided) + dang; de ltar de bzhin bzhi pa yang = "thus, likewise, the fourth too" (verbless). Gloss: -s (pas) = means; 'chad par 'gyur ba yin = what will be explained; object elided ([the classification]).
CITATION dm.py identify: verbatim in Toh 418 (Kangyur, Kye'i rdo rje mkha' 'gro ma dra ba'i sdom pa'i rgyud kyi rgyal po, D 17a; Sanskrit title not given by the tool) with variant pada 2 "bzhi bde yang de bzhin no"; also quoted in Toh 1190 (rab tu gsal ba'i kha sbyor gyi rgya cher 'grel pa, D 163a), Toh 2255 (Vajrapada, D 177b) and Toh 1183. Recorded as: quoted with a variant of pada 2.
DOUBTS Q: slob dpon gsang ba shes rab = master, secret, wisdom [empowerments] as three coordinate items (read), not "the master's secret wisdom". Q: "zhes bya ba la sogs pas" = "by [the words] beginning X, and so on".

## U05  prose, exposition/argument; 6 sentences (the hard unit)
SOURCE (see u/U05.txt) 
S1 rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud 'dir ni [sdig pa mi dge ba'i chos rnams las ring du gyur pa kun du spyod pa]'i phyir slob dpon te / de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas so.
  CLAUSES etymology of slob dpon (ācārya): purpose/cause phyir with nominalized clause "[one] practises (kun du spyod) what has become far from sin and non-virtue"; pas so = cause (second reason). Topic 'dir ni = in this Yoginī tantra.
  NEG mi dge ba = non-virtuous (lexical).
  DOUBTS Q: de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa = "that very [conduct] is his vow" (read); alt: "it is the vow of that very [empowerment]" (cf U01 sdom pa = dbang bskur ba). Q: kun du spyod pa = "practises/engages in everywhere"; reads ā-√car of ācārya; the "far from" (ring du gyur) glossing dūra is not an Skt. etymology I assert.
S2 slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba'i ngo bo gang [las kyi phyag rgya las skad cig ma bzhis dga' ba bzhi'i rang gi ngo bo nyid rab tu bsgrub pa] zhes bya ba yin gyi / yang [bya ba'i rgyud la sogs pa dang thun mong du gyur pa'i phyir] [mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba thob pas] [slob dpon gyis dbang bskur bas] dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o.
  CLAUSES (a) copular: the essence of the master's empowerment IS what is called [...]; gang = relative; bsgrub pa nominal "fully accomplishing", ergative/means skad cig ma bzhis, source las kyi phyag rgya las. (b) yin gyi = "but"; yang = "moreover"; phyir = cause (shared with Action Tantras); thob pas = cause/sequence (having obtained); dbang bskur bas = cause (empowered by master); main verb mi bya'o = prohibition-like negative "is not to be done", la ni = "to/for".
  NEG mi ldog pa (irreversible, lexical); mi bya'o = "is not to be done" (scope: giving empowerment).
  DOUBTS Q: gyis in slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba'i ngo bo = "empowerment given by the master" (read); alt genitive "master's empowerment" (cf gyi in S4, S6). Q: last clause is the position rejected at S3 (ci yang ma yin no); Tibetan does not mark it as an objector's view. Q: dbang bskur ba la ni mi bya'o = "empowerment is not to be done [for the one already empowered]" (read); alt "[one] does not do [it] for the sake of empowerment". Q: referent of the shared item (thun mong du gyur pa) not stated: the master's/irreversible empowerment of the lower tantras (read, left as bracketed [it]). Q: irreversible empowerment (mi ldog pa'i dbang) read as the Action/Yoga-tantra empowerment shared with this tantra; no source.
S3 de lta bas na [rnal 'byor dang rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud la sogs pa thams cad bshad pa dang nyan pa la sogs pa'i skal pa dang yang ldan par 'gyur ro] zhes bya ba'i tshig 'di ni ci yang ma yin no.
  CLAUSES quoted sentence (zhes bya ba'i) = subject of the final negative copula; ci yang ma yin = "is nothing at all" (NEG, strong).
  NEG ci yang ma yin: scope = the whole statement.
S4 de lta bas na dang po re zhig [dgyes pa'i rdo rje la sogs pa rnal 'byor ma'i rgyud rnams mnyan pa dang bsgom pa la sogs pa la dbang bar bya ba'i phyir] slob dpon gyi dbang bskur ba nyid kyis dbang bskur bar bya'o.
  CLAUSES instruction/obligation: bya'o = "is to be done"; nyid = emphatic "itself"; phyir = purpose; la dbang ba = have authority over.
S5 yang de ni mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du [slob dpon dang gsang ba dang shes rab dang bzhi pa'i mtshan nyid] kyis nye bar mtshon pa yin par rig par bya'o.
  CLAUSES "should be understood that it is indicated by"; ji lta ba bzhin du = "just as"; de = the empowerment (the fourfold).
CONSTRUAL In this Yoginī tantra one is a "master" because one practises everything that has become far from sinful, non-virtuous phenomena, since that very [conduct] is one's vow. The essence of the empowerment by the master is what is called "fully accomplishing, through the four moments from the action seal, the very essence of the four joys"; but, moreover, because [it] is shared with the Action Tantras and the rest, [the person] having obtained the irreversible empowerment, being empowered by the master, is not to be [given] empowerment. Therefore [the claim] "one will also be endowed with the fortune of explaining, listening to, and so on, all the Yoga, Yoginī and other tantras" is nothing at all. Therefore, first, to have authority over listening to and meditating on the Yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, one is to be empowered with the master's empowerment itself. Moreover, [the empowerment] is to be understood as indicated by the characteristics of the master, secret, wisdom and fourth, just as in the abhisamaya.
TERMS slob dpon=master; las kyi phyag rgya=action seal; skad cig ma=moment; dga' ba=joy; bya ba'i rgyud=Action Tantra; mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur=irreversible empowerment; skal pa=fortune; dbang ba=authority.
DOUBTS (above) + Q: mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du = "just as in the abhisamaya [ritual manual]" (read, lower-case) vs "in accordance with direct realization". Q: slob dpon ... bzhi pa'i mtshan nyid = "characteristics of the master, secret, wisdom and fourth [empowerments]" (elided dbang bskur). Q: dgyes pa'i rdo rje = Hevajra (also title word of the commentary).

## U07  prose, exposition
S1 de bzhin du khyad par can gyi ye shes ni shes rab ste / chos thams cad sems tsam nyid du shes pa'o.
  = distinctive gnosis is wisdom, namely knowing all phenomena as mind-only (tsam+nyid restrictive).
S2 de rab tu bsgrub pa'i phyir dbang bskur ba ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'o. = purpose phyir: the empowerment for fully accomplishing that is the wisdom-gnosis empowerment.
S3 yang de ni [gzhan gi dbang dang kun brtags dang yongs su gyur pa rnam par shes pa gsum gyi ngo bo bsdus pa'i gzugs can snying gi nor bu] gang [bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang skad cig ma rnams yang dag par mtshon pa] ni shes rab ye shes yin par rig par bya'o.
  CLAUSES head = snying gi nor bu (heart-jewel) with two modifiers: 'gzugs can' and 'ngo bo bsdus pa' (gathering the essence of three); relative gang = the jewel as agent of mtshon pa (means bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis; OBJECTS phyi rol gyi phyag rgya and skad cig ma rnams, no instrumental on them: revised after MITRA, grammar decided). Predicate yin par rig par bya = "is to be understood as".
  DOUBTS Q: gzhan gi dbang dang kun brtags dang yongs su gyur pa rnam par shes pa gsum = "the three consciousnesses: dependent, imagined, transformed" (read); alt "the three [natures]: dependent, imagined, perfected" (yongs su gyur pa for yongs su grub pa). Q: gzugs can = "having form" (read), alt "embodied/physical". Q: snying gi nor bu = "heart-jewel" (jewel of the heart/centre); no further gloss. Q: phyi rol gyi phyag rgya = "external seal" (as opposed to inner); unglossed.
S4 dbang bskur ba des dbang bskur ba'i dbang po rab rnams la ni sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin gyis chos kyi phyag rgya nye bar bstan cing bshad par bya'o. = with that empowerment, to those of superior faculties who are empowered, the dharma seal is to be taught and explained by means of illusion-like samādhi.
TERMS ye shes=gnosis; shes rab ye shes=wisdom-gnosis; sems tsam=mind-only; ting nge 'dzin=samādhi; chos kyi phyag rgya=dharma seal.

## U08  prose, commentary gloss on the lemma "de ltar de bzhin yang" (and argument)
S1 de bzhin du bzhi pa yang yin te / de ltar de bzhin yang zhes bya ba ste. [lemma = pada fragment, shorter than root pada U04 (bzhi pa omitted)]
S2 'de' zhes pa ni shes rab ye shes so. S3 'de bzhin' zhes bya ba ni de bzhin nyid / de [yang dag pa'i mtha' dang chos kyi dbyings zhes bya ba rnams] la don gyi khyad par yod pa ma yin no. (NEG yod pa ma yin = "there is no difference of meaning")
S4 dbang bskur ba gang gis de'i ngo bo nyid mthong ba'am rab tu bsgrub pa ste / de bzhin zhes bya ba'i sgras brjod par bya ba yin no. (gang gis = by which; ba'am = or)
S5 'yang' zhes bya ba'i sgra ni shes rab ye shes kyi dbang bskur ba'i rjes thogs su yang sbyin par bya'o zhes bya ba'i don to.
S6 yang de ni [phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang / gang bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis yul so sor nges par yongs su bcad pa ma yin pa'i mtshan nyid can skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa la dmigs pa med par dmigs pa'i rnal 'byor gyis dmigs par byed pa] de ni [dbang bskur ba bzhi pa zhes bya bar brjod par bya ba'i man ngag nye bar bstan par byas pa tsam] gyis ni dbang bskur ba bzhi par 'gyur ba ma yin no.
  CLAUSES main: de ... ma yin no = "does not become the fourth empowerment"; gyis = means (merely having had the pith instruction shown); tsam = restrictive "merely"; NEG ma yin no scoped over the whole 'becoming'.
  DOUBTS Q: first de ni = the practice of focusing (read); the second de ni resumes it. Alt: the yoga itself is the instruction "to be called the fourth empowerment" and the unmarked contrast is with its bare statement. Q: relative gang ... = modifies "skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa" only (read); alt both items. Q: dmigs pa med par dmigs pa'i rnal 'byor = "the yoga of focusing without a focus"; alt "yoga that takes the non-objectifiable as object".
S7 gzhan du na / 'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba zhes bya ba la sogs pas bcom ldan 'das kyis dbang bskur ba ni 'dir dri ma bkru bar bzhed pa yin na / de ji lta na man ngag bstan pa tsam gyis yin par 'gyur.
  CLAUSES na = condition ("if/since ... intends"), de ji lta na = how then; rhetorical question "how could it be so by merely being taught"; bzhed pa honorific (the Blessed One).
  VARIANT lemma "'di yis bkrus shing dbang bskur ba" (no -s) vs U02 "dbang bskur bas".
TERMS de bzhin nyid=suchness; yang dag pa'i mtha'=true limit; chos kyi dbyings=dharma realm; dmigs pa=focus.
DOUBTS Q: de bzhin as adverb in the lemma vs noun "suchness" in the gloss: English cannot carry both; lemma rendered "likewise", gloss "suchness". Q: de in "de bzhin nyid de yang dag pa'i mtha'..." = demonstrative "that" (suchness; [for it,] 'true limit' and 'dharma realm') vs the end of nyid-te; sense same.

## U09  prose, objection and reply; ends on lead-in "de yang 'di skad du"
S1 gal te [bzhi pa'i bdag nyid can gyi de kho na nyid] bstan na der ji ltar mi 'gyur zhe na / de ni bden mod.
  CLAUSES objection: condition na (if reality is shown), rhetorical question der ji ltar mi 'gyur ("how would it not come about there?"), zhe na = "if [one] asks"; reply de ni bden mod = "that is true (granted)".
  NEG mi 'gyur = "would not come about" in a rhetorical question (affirmative force).
  DOUBTS Q: der = "in that case/ by this" (read); alt "in that [person]". Q: subject elided of 'gyur: the fourth empowerment.
S2 'on kyang [de kho na nyid ni ngag gi spyod yul ma yin pa'i phyir] brjod par nus pa ma yin la / thos pas bsgrub par yang mi nus so.
  NEG ma yin pa (not the domain of speech), nus pa ma yin (cannot be expressed), mi nus (cannot be accomplished); all three negations scope on de kho na nyid; yang = "also/ and neither".
S3 de yang 'di skad du (lead-in). 
CONSTRUAL "If it is asked, 'If the reality that has the fourth as its nature is shown, how would it not come about in that case?', that is true. However, since reality is not within the range of speech, it cannot be expressed, and it cannot be accomplished by hearing either. And [it is said] in these words:"
TERMS de kho na nyid=reality; spyod yul=range; bsgrub pa=accomplish.

## U10  verse 7x4 (citation inside U09's frame), epistemology of speech/concept
SOURCE nang na gsal ba de nyid ni / ngag las mngon sum thos mthong yin / 'on kyang sgra la rnam rtog gi / gzugs brnyan 'byung bar 'gyur ba yin
CLAUSES (1) de nyid [= that which is clear within] ... yin: copular, ni topic; ngag las = source ("from speech"); mngon sum = direct; thos mthong = heard and seen (verbal pair). (2) 'on kyang = contrast; sgra la = locus (in sound); rnam rtog gi gzugs brnyan = subject (reflection of conceptual thought); 'byung bar 'gyur ba yin = comes to arise.
NEG none. VERSE-NOTES ni filler/topic; yin x2 closing copula.
CITATION identify: verbatim only in Toh 1183 itself (D 7a); source not located.
CONSTRUAL That very [thing] that is clear within is, from speech, directly heard and seen. Yet in sound there comes to arise a reflection of conceptual thought.
DOUBTS Q: ngag las = "from speech" (read); alt las = comparative "more directly than speech". Q: mngon sum thos mthong = "directly heard and seen"; alt "direct perception (and) what is heard and seen". Q: gsal ba = "clarity/ what is clear" (nominal) read as "that very clarity within". Q: gzugs brnyan = reflection (alt image).
TERMS rnam rtog=conceptual thought; gzugs brnyan=reflection; sgra=sound.

## U11  verse 7x4 + frame "zhes bstan pa yin no"
SOURCE rang gi rnam rtog de de rgyas / brtags pa'i de nyid bsgoms pa yis / rgyud la rnam rtog 'khrug 'gyur na / de nyid rtog par ji ltar 'gyur / zhes bstan pa yin no
CLAUSES protasis (na): (a) rang gi rnam rtog de de rgyas (each of one's own conceptual thoughts spreads); (b) brtags pa'i de nyid bsgoms pa yis (by/when meditating on imputed reality; yis = means/occasion); (c) rgyud la rnam rtog 'khrug 'gyur (conceptual thought is stirred up in the mindstream; la = locus). Apodosis: de nyid rtog par ji ltar 'gyur (rhetorical question: how could reality be conceived?). Frame: zhes bstan pa yin no (so it is taught; non-honorific bstan).
RELATIONS na = condition; yis = means; la = locus; ji ltar = how (rhetorical, negative force).
VERSE-NOTES de de = distributive reduplication; yis short instrumental.
CITATION identify: verbatim only in Toh 1183 (D 7a); not located.
CONSTRUAL If each of one's own conceptual thoughts spreads, when one meditates on imputed reality, and conceptual thought is stirred up in the mindstream, how could reality be conceived? So it is taught.
DOUBTS Q: rgyas = intransitive verb in the protasis (read); alt converb/prefix "rgyas brtags" = "imagined extensively": "meditating on reality as extensively imputed by one's own conceptual thoughts". Q: de de = "each" (read); alt "that very". Q: rtog par = "conceived" (read); alt rtogs "realized" (scribal). Q: rgyud = mindstream (read), alt tantra.
TERMS rgyud=mindstream (context-sensitive: also tantra); rtog pa=conceive; brtags pa=imputed.

## U12  prose, objection and reply; ends on lead-in "de yang ji skad du"
S1 gal te [dbang bskur bas de kho na nyid mngon sum du rtogs par 'gyur zhing dri ma ma lus pa dag par 'gyur] na / de ltar na [de'i skad cig nyid la phyag rgya chen po'i dngos grub ci'i phyir 'grub par mi 'gyur] zhe na / dbang po rnon po brtson 'grus 'bar ba rnams kyis 'grub pa nyid yin no.
  CLAUSES objection: na (condition: empowerment -> direct realization + total purification), de ltar na (in that case), ci'i phyir ... mi 'gyur zhe na (why would siddhi not be accomplished at that very moment?); reply: instrumental/agent rnams kyis (those of sharp faculties blazing with diligence) 'grub pa nyid yin = it is in fact accomplished.
  NEG ma lus pa (no remainder) ; mi 'gyur (rhetorical).
S2 gang zhig sus kyang mi 'dod cing ma mthong bas de med par 'gyur ram ci.
  CLAUSES gang zhig = "that which"; sus kyang = by anyone at all; mi 'dod, ma mthong = not wanted, not seen; bas = cause; de med par 'gyur ram ci = rhetorical question "would it thereby become non-existent?".
  DOUBTS Q: 'dod = "wants" (read); alt "accepts (as a thesis)". Q: reading of the rhetorical question: 'because no one wants it or has seen it, would it not exist?' (read); alt 'would what no one wants...' with de = the siddhi. Q: ram ci = rhetorical particle pair; rendered by the question.
S3 de yang ji skad du (lead-in).
TERMS dngos grub=siddhi; phyag rgya chen po=great seal; 'grub pa=be accomplished.
