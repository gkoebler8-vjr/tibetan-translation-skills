### U01
On “the divisions of the vow, and so on”: the “vow” is empowerment. Its classification is the “division”, which has the characteristic of what will be explained. And as is said:

### U02
To benefit sentient beings,
empowerment is proclaimed as four.
Since by this one is washed and empowered,
it is for that reason called “empowerment.”

Thus [the Blessed One] has said.

### U03
And in order to state the classification of those very four empowerments, [it says] as follows:

### U04
The master, the secret, the wisdom,
and thus likewise the fourth, too.

[The classification] will be explained by these lines and those that follow.

### U05
In this Yoginī tantra one is a “master” because one fully practises what has become far removed from sinful, non-virtuous phenomena, since that very [practice] is one’s vow.

The essence of the empowerment by the master is what is called “fully accomplishing, through the four moments from the action seal, the very essence of the four joys.” But further, because [it] is shared with the Action tantras and the rest, one who has obtained the irreversible empowerment has been empowered by the master, and so empowerment is not to be performed on the empowered.

Therefore the statement “one will also be endowed with the fortune of explaining, listening to, and so on, all the Yoga and Yoginī tantras and the rest” is nothing at all.

Therefore, first, in order to have authority over listening to, meditating on, and so on, the Yoginī tantras such as the Hevajra, one is to be empowered with the master’s empowerment itself. Moreover, [the empowerment] is to be understood as indicated by the characteristics of the master, the secret, the wisdom and the fourth [empowerments], just as in the *abhisamaya*.

### U06
Those of dull faculties who have received the empowerment of such a master are to be taught meditation on the action seal and the elaboration seal, once they have firmly grasped [it] through conviction.

Likewise, on the side of the completion stage also, the experience of the four joys, whose nature is the four moments, through the guru’s own pith instruction, a jewel dwelling in secret: this is called the “secret empowerment”, because it is not to be taught to yogins who are engaged in meditation on elaboration.

Here, to those of middling faculties for empowerment, meditation on the pledge seal is to be taught and explained.

### U07
Likewise, the distinctive gnosis is wisdom: the knowing of all phenomena as nothing but mind. The empowerment for the full accomplishment of that is the wisdom-gnosis empowerment.

Moreover, the heart-jewel that has form, gathers the essence of the three consciousnesses (the dependent, the imagined and the transformed) and, through the guru’s own pith instruction, correctly indicates the external seal and the moments: this is to be understood as wisdom-gnosis.

By that empowerment, to those of superior faculties who are empowered, the dharma seal is to be taught and explained through illusion-like samādhi.

### U08
Likewise there is also the fourth, [as in] “thus likewise also” (de ltar de bzhin yang). “That” (de) means wisdom-gnosis. “Likewise” (de bzhin) means suchness; there is no difference of meaning among those called “suchness”, “true limit” and “dharma realm.” The empowerment by which one sees its very essence, or fully accomplishes it, is what is to be expressed by the word “likewise.” The word “also” (yang) means that [it] is also to be given following the wisdom-gnosis empowerment.

Moreover, the focusing, by the yoga of focusing without a focus, on the external seal and on the perfection of the moment (the latter having the characteristic of not being definitively delimited, object by object, through the guru’s own pith instruction): this does not become the fourth empowerment merely through being shown the pith instruction that is to be called “the fourth empowerment.”

Otherwise, if [by words] such as “washed by this and empowered” the Blessed One intends empowerment here to be the washing of stains, how could it come about through merely being taught a pith instruction?

### U09
If it is asked, “If the reality whose nature is the fourth is taught, how would [the fourth empowerment] not come about in that case?”: that is true. However, since reality is not within the range of speech, it cannot be expressed, and it also cannot be accomplished through hearing. And as to that, [it is said] in these words:

### U10
That very clarity within
is directly heard and seen from speech.
Yet in sound there comes to arise
a reflection of conceptual thought.

### U11
If each of one’s own conceptual thoughts spreads,
when one meditates on imputed reality,
and conceptual thought is stirred up in the mindstream,
how could reality be conceived?

Thus it is taught.

### U12
