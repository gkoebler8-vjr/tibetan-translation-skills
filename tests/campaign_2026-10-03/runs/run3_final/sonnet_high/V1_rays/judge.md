## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage
1. U05 (with U01–U05): the homage's relative chain onto the Lion of the Shakyas is broken. The liberating agent becomes the nirmanakaya, and the bow is moved to U01. Cause: construal fork / skill rule misapplied (the order rule and the "kaya as subject" revision overrode the pa'i relative).
2. U06: the epithet 'jig rten mkhyen (lokavid) is made a finite verb, and compassion is moved from the looking to the knowing. Cause: grammar misread (construal fork; the Sanskrit was not consulted).
3. U10: the supplied "They include" puts the śāstras and Drikungpa's instructions inside the Buddha's own scriptures. Cause: omission under compression (las and nang nas were collapsed into one "From among them").
4. U07: "taking manifest birth" calques skye ba mngon par skye ba (jAtaka + upapatti). Cause: dictionary sense.
5. U01: khams is made a possession of the dharmakaya ("its basic element") rather than an apposition. Cause: grammar misread. A related weakness is the U09 adverbials stranded four lines from their verb (verse constraint).
