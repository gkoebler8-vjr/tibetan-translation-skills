### U01
HEADER: U01 · prose, homage period (U01–U05 are one Tibetan sentence) · homage (liturgical, elevated as the Tibetan is) · seasoned practitioner, publication (reader edition) · not a citation
TEXT:
I bow with reverence in body, speech, and mind to the Lion of the Shakyas, chief of the sugatas. For his own benefit, his dharmakaya abides, like space, in the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana. It is unchanging, its basic element naturally pure, its adventitious stains pacified, not compounded by causes and conditions, and free of elaboration.
NOTES:
Issue: order. The Tibetan chain ends in the verb of U05 ('dud do, I bow); the English names the object of homage first, so the bow clause stands here, and U01–U05 then run as finite clauses whose subjects are the three kayas. No relation is lost; U05 carries no bow clause.
Issue: the descriptions of the dharmakaya are set after the verb as a second sentence; "his" is supplied (the kayas belong to the Lion of the Shakyas of U05).
Q: subject is the dharmakaya itself (bare noun before an intransitive verb, parallel to U03 and U04) rather than the Buddha "abiding as" it; MITRA reads the latter ("abides in the state of the dharmakaya").
Q: "la" after "dag pa": connective (read) or locus ("in the naturally pure element the stains are pacified"; MITRA: "where adventitious stains are completely pacified").
Q: "like space" goes with "abides" (read) or with "free of elaboration".
Alt: "khams" = basic element (dhatu); alternatives sphere, realm. "mnyam pa nyid" = equality (alternatives evenness, sameness). "'pho 'gyur med pa" = unchanging (literally without transition or change). The preverb of "rab tu zhi" is not rendered separately (pacified, not "utterly pacified").
Check: in-context; no major finding; MITRA differs on the subject and on "la" as locus; subject changed to the kaya on the grammar, "la" reading kept and recorded.

Issue: "rang bzhin" in "sprul pa'i rang bzhin" is absorbed into "varied emanations" (sna tshogs = varied).

### U10
HEADER: U10 · prose, one long Tibetan period (the commitment to explain) split into seven English sentences · exposition with elevated praise, first person at the close · seasoned practitioner, publication (reader edition) · not a citation
TEXT:
Second: The Supreme Sage is the conqueror of the four maras — the aggregates, the lord of death, the son of the gods, and the afflictions. His teachings are free of the extremes of eternalism and nihilism and are immeasurable like the expanse of the ocean. They include the sutras and tantras, the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga, and others, and the meaning of the profound pith instructions of the Great Drikungpa, Lord of Dharma, all of them worthy of veneration like the crown jewel of the naga king. From among them this compendium has been condensed. It is free of the stains of non-understanding, wrong understanding, and doubt. Just as every need and wish arises when one makes supplication to the beryl, the supreme of jewels, so it accomplishes all aims, temporary and ultimate. I shall explain this treatise, the essence taught as the three: ground, path, and fruit.
NOTES:
Q: head noun of the period: "bstan bcos ... gzhi dang lam dang 'bras bu gsum du bstan pa'i snying po" = "this treatise, the essence taught as the three" (apposition, read) or "the essence of the treatise" (MITRA: "the heart of the treatise"); "bstan pa'i snying po" may echo the title of the root text (theg chen bstan pa'i snying po).
Q: scope of "klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i" = the whole list of teachings (read) or only the Great Drikungpa's pith instructions.
Q: "chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po" = the Great Drikungpa, Lord of Dharma; the person is not named in the Tibetan and no name is supplied (Jigten Sumgon is likely; the editor decides).
Q: "bsdus pa 'di" = this compendium, the root text under comment (read; Zhedang Dorje's text); "bsdus pa" = condensed (read) or compiled.
Q: "man ngag zab mo'i don" = the meaning of the profound pith instructions (read) or the profound meaning of the pith instructions.
Issue: "gnyis pa ni" = "Second:" (section marker); the editor may set it as a run-in heading.
Issue: split. The relative material on the Supreme Sage becomes two sentences; "las" and "nang nas" are rendered once, as "From among them", after a list sentence ("They include ...", supplied); the manner clause "dri ma med par" becomes "It is free of the stains".
Issue: "rtag chad" = eternalism and nihilism; "ste" is rendered as a dash (namely); "bdud bzhi" = the four maras, each named.
Check: in-context; 1 minor ("both temporary and ultimate": "both" removed). MITRA reads the sentence differently at "snying po" (see the first Q) and joins the Supreme Sage's teachings and the list; kept and recorded.
