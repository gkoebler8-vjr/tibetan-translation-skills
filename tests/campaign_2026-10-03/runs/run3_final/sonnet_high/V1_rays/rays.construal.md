## U01
UNIT        U01 (homage period, part 1 of U01-U05)
FORM        prose
GENRE       liturgy-in-prose (expression of worship)
REGISTER    commentator addresses homage to Shakyamuni; elevated only as the Tibetan is
SOURCE      rang don du chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa la / glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa / spros pa dang bral ba nam mkha' ltar srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing /
CLAUSES     1 chain, finite verb=bzhugs (hon., intr.) ; SUBJECT = chos sku (bare noun before an intransitive verb = absolutive subject; a role would take the terminative, cf. gtso bor in U03); du = purpose (rang don du: for his own benefit); ngang du = locus (in the state of ...)
            descriptive predicates all modify the chos sku: 'pho 'gyur med pa (unchanging); khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa (its basic element naturally pure); glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi (adventitious stains pacified); 'dus ma byas pa (not compounded); spros pa dang bral ba (free of elaboration)
            the possessor of the kayas = the object of homage shakya seng ge (U05); 'his' is supplied (grammar: the kaya is the Buddha's)
RELATIONS   du=purpose (rang don du) ; gyis(1)=by nature (rang bzhin gyis, adverbial) ; la=connective/loc after 'dag pa' ; gyi=gen (glo bur gyi dri ma) ; zhing=coordination ; gyis(2)=agent/cause (rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas: not made by causes and conditions) ; dang=comit (spros pa dang bral) ; ltar=simile (like space) ; kyi=gen ; du=locus ; shing=continuation into U02
NEG/QUANT   'pho 'gyur med pa = no change; 'dus ma byas pa = NOT compounded by causes and conditions (ma scopes byas; gyis marks the cause); spros pa dang bral ba = free of elaboration
SPINE       Tibetan: one chain to the main verb 'dud do (U05). English: head-first. The bow clause (I bow to ... ) is placed first, in U01, so that the object of homage is named before the relative material; U01-U05 then continue as finite clauses with the three kayas as subjects. U05's bow clause is therefore rendered in U01 (position only; no relation lost).
CONSTRUAL   For his own benefit, the dharmakaya abides, like space, in the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana. The dharmakaya is without change; its basic element (khams) is pure by its own nature; its adventitious stains are pacified; it is not compounded by causes and conditions; it is free of elaboration. [Possessor: the Lion of the Shakyas.]
TERMS       chos sku = dharmakaya; srid zhi = samsara and nirvana; mnyam pa nyid = equality; spros pa = elaboration; khams = basic element; glo bur = adventitious; zhi = pacified; bzhugs = abides
DOUBTS      Q: subject = the dharmakaya (read; bare noun, parallel U03/U04) vs the Buddha 'abiding as' the dharmakaya (MITRA's reading).
            Q: la after 'dag pa' = connective (read) vs locus ("in the naturally pure dharmakaya the stains are pacified" / "abiding in the naturally pure element").
            Q: 'nam mkha' ltar' scopes 'bral ba' (free of elaboration like space) or 'bzhugs' (abides like space); rendered with 'abides'.
            Q: khams rang bzhin gyis = 'its basic element naturally pure' (read) vs 'pure by the nature of the sphere'.
            Q: mnyam pa nyid = 'equality' (read); 'sameness/evenness' alternative.
            Issue: bow clause moved from U05 to U01 (head first); descriptors set after the verb, as a second sentence.

## U05
FORM        prose; REGISTER homage closes (main verb 'dud do)
SOURCE      mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud brlan te grol bar mdzad pa'i bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shakya seng ge la lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do /
CLAUSES     (a) bstan te: displays the twelve deeds (transitive; subject = the nirmanakaya of U04, continued; su=la-don: in/as the twelve deeds); (b) brlan te: moistens the mindstreams of the disciples (ERG chos kyi char gyis = with the rain of the sublime dharma of peace and happiness; zhi bde = peace and happiness); (c) grol bar mdzad pa: brings them to liberation; (a)-(c) are relative clauses on the object of homage: shakya seng ge, bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo ; (d) MAIN: 'dud do (tr.), agent = I (commentator, elided, 1st person), LA shakya seng ge = object of homage; gus pas = with reverence ; lus ngag yid gsum = in body, speech, mind
            rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya = disciples whose rigs (gotra: disposition) is rightly awakened
RELATIONS   su=la-don (in/as) ; te(1)=sequence/manner (displaying ...) ; 'i=gen ; kyi=gen ; char gyis=means (rain) ; yang dag par=adverbial (preverb) ; 'i (sad pa'i)=modification ; rnams kyi=gen ; te(2)=sequence (moisten, then liberate; NOT stated as consequence) ; r=terminative (grol bar mdzad pa: bring to liberation) ; pa'i=modification of the object of homage ; la=object of homage ; pas=manner (gus pas)
NEG/QUANT   none; rnams plural (disciples; sugatas); gsum = body speech mind (three)
SPINE       Tibetan: main verb last. English: main clause carried in U01 (head first); U05 English = the final relative clauses as one sentence with 'the nirmanakaya' as subject.
CONSTRUAL   Displaying the twelve deeds, the nirmanakaya moistens the mindstreams of the disciples whose disposition is rightly awakened with the rain of the sublime dharma of peace and happiness, and brings them to liberation. [The kayas belong to the chief of the sugatas, the Lion of the Shakyas; to him I bow with reverence in body, speech, and mind (rendered in U01).]
TERMS       mdzad pa bcu gnyis = twelve deeds; bde bar gshegs pa = sugata; shakya seng ge = Lion of the Shakyas; rigs = disposition (gotra); gdul bya = disciples; rgyud = mindstream; zhi bde = peace and happiness
DOUBTS      Q: rigs yang dag par sad pa = 'whose disposition (gotra) is rightly awakened' (read) vs 'lineage/family'.
            Q: bstan te = 'displaying the twelve deeds' (read) vs 'teaching in twelve deeds'.
            Q: zhi bde = 'peace and happiness' (read) vs 'peaceful bliss'.
            Q: te after rgyud brlan = sequence (read; MITRA: 'thereby bringing about').
            Q: subject of (a)-(c) = the nirmanakaya (read) vs the Buddha (relative clause on the object of homage).
            Issue: bow clause rendered in U01.
## U06
UNIT        U06 (frame + 4 padas 7x4)
FORM        verse 7x4, citation (de yang rgyud bla ma las ... ces gsungs so frame, closing frame in U09)
GENRE       treatise verse (Uttaratantra II.53-56 area: Toh 4024 D 64b)
REGISTER    commentator quotes; the verse is exposition of the Buddha's deeds; framing minimal
SOURCE      1 thugs rje chen pos 'jig rten mkhyen / 2 'jig rten kun la gzigs nas ni / 3 chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par / 4 sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis /
CLAUSES     U06-U09 one sentence. Final verb ston (U09 pada 3: shows), agent = the Buddha (elided; Shakyamuni of U05). Before it: mkhyen (knowing, converb-like, bare stem), gzigs nas (having looked; nas = sequence), ma g.yos par (without stirring; ma = neg, par = manner)
            case frames: thugs rje chen pos = ERG means; 'jig rten = ABS object of mkhyen; 'jig rten kun la gzigs = LA object of gzigs (honorific verb of looking); chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par = source/separation (not moving away from the dharmakaya); sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis = means (through emanation-natures of many kinds)
RELATIONS   pos=means (through great compassion) ; nas=sequence (having looked) ; ni=filler/topic after nas ; las=source (from the dharmakaya) ; par=manner ; 'i=gen/modification (nature of emanation) ; kyis=means
NEG/QUANT   ma g.yos par = without stirring; kun = all
VERSE-NOTES 'ni' after gzigs nas = filler; 'rang bzhin' in 'sprul pa'i rang bzhin' marks the emanation as the nature of the display (absorbed); sna tshogs = manifold
SPINE       one English sentence: knows ... (finite), having looked ..., without stirring ..., he shows his deeds: [list in U07-U09] -- in the impure realms, as long as samsara remains. Predicate 'ston' (pada 15) is brought forward to line 4 (order: 1-2-3-15-4... declared as 'verb forward') and 'mdzad ... rnams' (deeds) is brought forward with it as the object-head.
CONSTRUAL   Through great compassion he knows the world; and, having looked on all the world, without stirring from the dharmakaya, through emanations of many kinds he shows his deeds: taking birth, leaving Tushita, entering the womb, being born, delighting with the queens' retinue, renouncing and practicing austerities, going to the seat of awakening, defeating mara's host, becoming perfectly awakened, turning the wheel of dharma, passing into nirvana -- in the impure realms, for as long as samsara remains.
CITATION    dm.py identify on the four stanzas: whole run 50 syll verbatim in Toh 4024 (Mahayanottaratantrashastra), D 64b-17..29 and Toh 4025 (vyakhya); VERBATIM MATCH 'NO' only because the page lacks the canonical pada 'bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang' (skill in the crafts) between 'lhums su 'jug dang bltams pa dang' and 'btsun mo'i 'khor dgyes rol ba dang' (D 64b-22). Source: quoted with one pada lacking from Toh 4024.
VARIANTS    canon D4024 64b-23 'dgyes rol pa dang' vs page 'dgyes rol ba dang' (orthography only); canon 64b-20 'dga' ldan gnas nas' (Toh 4025: 'dga' ldan nas ni') -- no effect on sense.
DOUBTS      Q: de yang = 'as for this too' folded into the framing formula ("In the Uttaratantra it is said:").
            Q: mkhyen = converb-like participle (read) vs finite 'he knows' at pada end; rendered finite for the line, no effect.
            Q: 'ston' agent elided = the Buddha of U05 (supplied 'he').
            Q: 'deeds' supplied as the object head from 'mdzad rnams' (U09), so that 'shows' has a governing noun in line 4.

## U10
UNIT        U10 (one long period: the commitment to explain, with sa bcad marker 'gnyis pa ni')
FORM        prose; GENRE commentary: framing/commitment ; REGISTER exposition with elevated praise; first person at the end
SOURCE      gnyis pa ni / phung po dang 'chi bdag dang lha'i bu dang nyon mongs pa ste bdud bzhi las rgyal ba'i thub pa mchog gi gsung rab rtag chad kyi mtha' dang bral ba rgya mtsho'i klong ltar dpag tu med pa las / mdo sde dang rgyud sde rnams dang klu sgrub dang thogs med la sogs pa'i bstan bcos dang / chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po'i man ngag zab mo'i don klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i nang nas / ma rtogs par dang log par rtog pa dang the tshom gyi dri ma med par bsdus pa 'di ni / rin chen rnams kyi mchog bai DUrya la gsol ba btab na dgos 'dod thams cad 'byung ba bzhin / gnas skabs dang mthar thug gi don thams cad 'grub par byed pa'i bstan bcos / gzhi dang lam dang 'bras bu gsum du bstan pa'i snying po bshad par bya'o /
CLAUSES     Finite: bshad par bya'o ('I shall explain', 1st person, commitment; elided agent = commentator). Object: snying po (the essence), in apposition with bstan bcos (head noun fork).
            Nonfinite: bsdus pa 'di ni = 'this one, which has been gathered/condensed' (topic); the zhing/ste chain: bdud bzhi las rgyal ba'i thub pa mchog = the Supreme Sage victorious over the four maras (ste = namely: lists the four maras); gsung rab (his teachings) free of the extremes (bral ba) and immeasurable (dpag tu med pa); 'grub par byed pa'i bstan bcos = the treatise that accomplishes all aims
            ma rtogs par dang log par rtog pa dang the tshom gyi dri ma med par = without the stains of non-understanding, wrong understanding, doubt (med par = manner, 'without', governs all three)
RELATIONS   gnyis pa ni = sa bcad marker (second [part]) ; ste = namely (four maras) ; las (bdud bzhi las rgyal ba) = over/from (victorious over) ; 'i = gen/modification ; las (dpag tu med pa las) = from among ; nang nas = from among ; dang = coordination (aggregates ...; sutras ...) ; ltar = simile (like the ocean's expanse; like the naga king's crown jewel) ; na = condition (if one prays) ; bzhin = like/just as ; 'i = modification ; du = in (gsum du bstan: taught as three)
NEG/QUANT   dpag tu med pa = immeasurable ; bral ba = free of (extremes) ; ma rtogs pa / dri ma med par = without stains of ... ; thams cad x2 = all needs and wishes / all aims ; thogs med = Asanga (name, not neg.); la sogs pa = and so forth
SPINE       English: 5 sentences, connectives kept: S1 sage conquered four maras (namely...). S2 his teachings free of extremes, immeasurable. S3 from among these (list worthy of veneration) this compendium was condensed. S4 it is free of the stains.. S5 just as ... so it accomplishes ... S6 I shall explain this treatise, the essence taught as three.
CONSTRUAL   The Supreme Sage is the conqueror of the four maras: the aggregates, the lord of death, the son of the gods, and the afflictions. His teachings are free of the extremes of eternalism and nihilism and are immeasurable like the expanse of the ocean. From among these teachings -- the sutras and tantras, the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga and others, and the profound meaning of the pith instructions of the Great Drikungpa, Lord of Dharma, all worthy of veneration like the crown jewel of the naga king -- this compendium has been condensed. It is free of the stains of non-understanding, wrong understanding, and doubt. Just as every need and wish arises when one makes supplication to the beryl, the supreme of jewels, so it accomplishes all aims, temporary and ultimate. I shall explain this treatise, the essence taught as the three: ground, path, and fruit.
TERMS       thub pa mchog = the Supreme Sage; bdud bzhi = four maras; phung po = aggregates; 'chi bdag = lord of death; lha'i bu = son of the gods; nyon mongs pa = afflictions; rtag chad = eternalism and nihilism; gsung rab = teachings; mdo sde / rgyud sde = sutras / tantras; bstan bcos = treatise; man ngag = pith instructions; chos rje = Lord of Dharma; gnas skabs = temporary; mthar thug = ultimate; gzhi lam 'bras bu = ground, path, and fruit; snying po = essence
DOUBTS      Q: head noun of the period: bstan bcos ... bstan pa'i snying po = apposition 'this treatise, the essence' (read) vs 'the essence OF the treatise'; also 'bstan pa'i snying po' may echo the root-text title (theg chen bstan pa'i snying po).
            Q: scope of 'klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i': whole list (read) vs only Drikungpa Chenpo's pith instructions.
            Q: chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po = 'the Great Drikungpa, Lord of Dharma': identity (Jigten Sumgon?) not stated; no name supplied.
            Q: 'di ni = 'this (root text)' = the treatise being commented on (the Mahayana root text by Zhedang Dorje); rendered 'this compendium'.
            Q: bsdus pa = 'condensed' (read) vs 'gathered/compiled'.
            Q: 'las ... nang nas' double 'from among': rendered once.
            Issue: gnyis pa ni = 'Second:' (section marker); editor may set as run-in heading.
            Issue: rtag chad = eternalism and nihilism (term swap from 'permanence and annihilation').

## U12
UNIT        U12
FORM        prose; GENRE commentary (the remaining three points)
SOURCE      dgos pa ni / thos bsam gyis de rtogs pa'o / dgos pa'i dgos pa ni / de'i don bsgoms pas 'bras bu mngon du 'gyur ba'o / 'brel ba ni / snga ma la brten te phyi ma rim gyis thabs dang thabs las byung ba'i tshul gyis 'brel pa'o /
CLAUSES     (1) rtogs pa'o: to realize (de = the subject matter of U11), means thos bsam gyis (instr.) ; (2) 'gyur ba'o: the fruit becomes manifest ('bras bu = subject; mngon du 'gyur = become manifest), cause de'i don bsgoms pas (by meditating on its meaning) ; (3) 'brel pa'o: is connected, la brten te = relying on the earlier; rim gyis = in sequence; tshul gyis = in the manner of (means and what arises from means)
RELATIONS   gyis = means ; pas = cause ; te = sequence/manner ; dang = coordination ; las = source (from the means) ; 'i = modification ; gyis = manner
SPINE       three short sentences
CONSTRUAL   The purpose is to realize it through hearing and reflection. The ultimate purpose is that, by meditating on its meaning, the fruit becomes manifest. The connection is that, relying on the earlier, the later ones are linked in sequence, in the manner of a means and what arises from that means.
TERMS       dgos pa = purpose; dgos pa'i dgos pa = ultimate purpose (literal: purpose of the purpose); 'brel ba = connection; thos bsam = hearing and reflection; bsgom = meditate
DOUBTS      Q: snga ma / phyi ma = earlier / later among subject matter, purpose, ultimate purpose (read) vs earlier/later texts or stages.
            Q: de (U12 line 1) = the subject matter (ground, path, fruit) (read).
            Issue: dgos pa'i dgos pa = 'ultimate purpose'; literal 'the purpose of the purpose'.
            Issue: thabs ... tshul gyis rendered with an explicit clause, not a nominal chain.
