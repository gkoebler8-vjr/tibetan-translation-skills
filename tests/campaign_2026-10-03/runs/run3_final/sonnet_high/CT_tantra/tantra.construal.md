# tantra.construal.md  (Candamaharosana Tantra ch.15, span 15.4-15.15; audience seasoned practitioner, study aid)
Register legend: speaker is the Blessed One (Acala) instructing; U03 opens with the Blessed Lady's request.

UNIT U01
FORM verse 7x4, not a citation (no zhes frame); GENRE tantric instruction; REGISTER teacher to practitioner, plain-warm
SOURCE de yi sbyor bas rnal 'byor pas / myur du dngos grub the tshom med / yang na shes rab dang bral ba'i / shin tu mnyam gzhag bsgom par bya
CLAUSES 2: (1) [yogi] ERG yogi, ABS siddhi, verb elided (thob, supplied "gains"), quick, no doubt. (2) imperative bsgom par bya, ABS equipoise, addressee "you"
RELATIONS bas = means/cause ("through the practice of that"); pas = agent (yogi); du = adverbial (myur du); yang na = alternative ("or else"); dang bral ba'i = modification ("free of wisdom" on equipoise); par bya = obligation/imperative
NEG/QUANT the tshom med = "without doubt" (adverbial); bral = privative; shin tu = intensifier on mnyam gzhag
VERSE-NOTES padas 1-2 one sentence with verb elided in 2; 3-4 second sentence; de = the foregoing practice (before span)
CONSTRUAL By the practice of that, the yogi swiftly gains siddhi, without doubt. Or else, meditate on an equipoise that is total and free of wisdom.
TERMS dngos grub=siddhi; shes rab=wisdom; mnyam gzhag=equipoise; rnal 'byor pa=yogi
CITATION none
DOUBTS Q: shes rab = wisdom (prajna) or the consort (prajna as a woman); "free of wisdom" = practice without a consort. Q: shin tu = "total/complete" vs "very deep"; scope of bral ba'i: on the equipoise (taken) vs on the meditator.

UNIT U02
FORM verse 7x2, not citation; GENRE instruction/promise; REGISTER plain
SOURCE mi g.yo bsgom pa yongs rdzogs na / byang chub rgyal srid yang dag 'thob
CLAUSES 1 (conditional): verb 'thob (intr. involuntary, ABS kingdom), subject elided = practitioner ("you"); na = condition on yongs rdzogs
RELATIONS na = condition (when/if complete)
NEG/QUANT mi g.yo is name/epithet, not a negation of an action
CONSTRUAL When meditation on Acala is complete, you truly gain the kingdom of awakening.
TERMS mi g.yo=Acala; byang chub rgyal srid=kingdom of awakening
DOUBTS Q: mi g.yo bsgom pa = meditation on Acala (context: Acala speaks) or unwavering meditation. Q: yang dag = truly vs fully (Alt: fully gain).

UNIT U03
FORM verse 7x4 inside two frames; GENRE petition then frame; REGISTER devoted request (addressee superior)
SOURCE [frame] de nas bcom ldan 'das mas gsol pa / lha yi rnam par dag pa gang / 'dren pa bdag ni nyan par 'tshal / sngon du gsungs nyid dkyil 'khor rnams / gtso bo bdag la rnam dag gsungs / [frame] de nas bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal pa
CLAUSES frame1: bcom ldan 'das ma + s (ERG) gsol pa. V1: gang = interrogative "what", predicate (is) elided: what is the purity of the deities; 'tshal (I wish) with nyan par (to hear), agent bdag; V2: gsungs imperative ("tell"), vocative gtso bo, recipient bdag la, object rnam dag; sngon du gsungs nyid = relative clause on dkyil 'khor (the very ones you spoke of before). frame2: bcom ldan 'das kyis ERG bka' stsal pa (introduces next unit)
RELATIONS yi = gen (lha yi); par/ r (terminative) in rnam par dag pa = fixed phrase; la = recipient (bdag la); nyid = emphasis on the previously spoken mandalas
NEG/QUANT none; rnams plural mandalas
VERSE-NOTES padas 1-2 one sentence (gang embedded as direct question); 3-4 second sentence, order 4-3 in English; 'dren pa and gtso bo vocatives (bdag ni topic)
CONSTRUAL Then the Blessed Lady requested: What is the purity of the deities? Guide, I wish to hear it. Chief, tell me the purity of the mandalas you spoke of before. Then the Blessed One said (next unit).
TERMS bcom ldan 'das ma=the Blessed Lady; 'dren pa=Guide; gtso bo=Chief; rnam dag=purity
DOUBTS Q: bcom ldan 'das ma (+s) read as "the Blessed Lady" (consort), not "mother"; confirmed by the Blessed One then answering. Q: gtso bo = vocative "Chief" (parallel with 'dren pa) or adjective "principal" on the mandalas. Q: gsungs = imperative "tell" (taken) vs past "you told". Q: rnam dag of the mandalas (taken) vs "purely".

UNIT U04
FORM verse 7x2; GENRE announcement; REGISTER teacher, plain
SOURCE de nas kun gyi sbyong byed pa'i / rnam dag bdag gis rab bshad bya
CLAUSES 1: bya (future/intent) with bdag gis ERG agent, object rnam dag modified by relative "kun gyi sbyong byed pa'i"
RELATIONS de nas = sequence ("next"); gyi = gen/object of purification (all); pa'i = modification (the purifier); gis = agent
CONSTRUAL Next I will explain the purity that purifies all.
TERMS rnam dag=purity; sbyong byed=purify
DOUBTS Q: kun gyi = of everything (taken) vs of all beings; head noun: rnam dag "the purity that purifies" vs "the purity of the purifier of all". rab = preverb, not rendered.

UNIT U05
FORM prose (15 short identification sentences, closing particles 'o/so); GENRE exposition/commentary-style symbolism; REGISTER definition
SOURCE (see page file) each sentence "X ni Y'o"
CLAUSES 15 copular identities, no finite verbs but the closing particles. Elided: none that matter. S12 mtshams rnams su ... rnams kyi'o = genitive-final: "belong to"
RELATIONS ni = topic; la sogs pa = "and the others"; dang = coordination; su = locus (intermediate directions); kyi'o = belonging; zhes pa ni = summary ("this/what has been said")
NEG/QUANT mtshams su "in the intermediate directions"; rnams nyid = "none other than" (emphasis)
CONSTRUAL Here the square is the four abodes of the immeasurables; the four doors, the four truths; the four archways, the four dhyanas; the eight pillars, the eight branches of the noble path; the single crossbeam, one-pointedness of mind; the lotus, the womb; the various colors, various emanations; the nine deities, the nine branches of scripture; the red direction, great desire; the white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are none other than the brahmin, merchant, warrior and commoner castes; the moon and sun, semen and sweat; the sword in the center, the emblem of black Acala; the curved knife, Hatred Vajra Lady; the directions beginning with east, white Acala and the others; the intermediate directions beginning with fire belong to Delusion Vajra Lady and the others. This is the purity of the mandala.
TERMS see glossary
DOUBTS Q: de la = "Here/In this" (mandala just asked about); no antecedent in the span. Q: 'phar ma gcig pa = single crossbeam (uncertain architectural term; Alt lintel). Q: rta babs = archways (Alt porticoes/pediments). Q: ད་མངས་རིགས = dmangs rigs (commoner caste; text spelling). Q: rngul = sweat (dictionary), possibly standing for the red element; also in U09. Q: S12 su ... kyi'o: "belong to" vs "in the intermediate directions are those of". Q: zhes pa ni = "this is" summarizing everything above (taken) vs only the last item.

UNIT U06
FORM prose; GENRE exposition (the purity of meditation); REGISTER definition
SOURCE bsgom pa'i rnam par dag pa brjod par bya ste / dang po mchod pa ni ...
CLAUSES: brjod par bya (I will state; agent = speaker), ste = sequence/colon; then 7 identities
RELATIONS pa'i = modification; ste = and then; kyi = gen; te (dri ma med pa'i lus te dri za'i lus so) = apposition ("that is")
NEG/QUANT dri ma med = stainless
CONSTRUAL I will now state the purity of meditation: First, offering is the accumulation of merit, the supreme action. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom, death, the supreme, stainless body, the body of a gandharva. [REVISED after MITRA cross-check, 20:24] The palace's culmination is the buddha ground. The lotus is the womb. The moon and sun are the white and the red.
DOUBTS Q: 'chi ba: (now taken as appositive chain; the earlier reading, now the alternative: subject of the next identity: "Death is the supreme stainless body, the gandharva's body") vs. appositive of the wisdom accumulation ("emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom, death, ..."). Q: gzhal yang khang gi mthar thug pa: "gzhal yang" probably gzhal yas khang (palace); "culmination of the palace is the buddha ground" vs "the palace ... the ultimate". Q: khyad par 'phags pa = supreme (twice; attaches to action, to body). Q: brjod par bya agent supplied as "I".

UNIT U07
FORM prose; GENRE exposition (symbolism of conception/rebirth, patricide/incest image); REGISTER exposition, blunt
SOURCE hum yig ni ma dang pha'i dbus su dri za'i sems so ...
CLAUSES: identities (HUM, Akshobhya, Mamaki); 'jug (he enters; agent gandharva); 'thob (obtains, son); skye bar byed (causes to be born); identities (daughters); bgrod par byed (go); gsod par byed (kill); 'dod par byed (desire)
RELATIONS nas = sequence (mthong nas; chags nas; bsad nas; bzung nas); zhing = and; pas = cause/means (gti mug pas; bde ba khyad par 'phags pas); la = object (pha la hatred); bzhin du = comparison; yis = agent (bu yis); zhes = quotation/naming (dgra bo zhes)
NEG/QUANT mtha' med pa'i = endless; rnams = plural
CONSTRUAL The letter HUM is the mind of the gandharva between mother and father. Akshobhya is the father. Mamaki is the mother. Seeing them attached to each other, he feels hatred for the father and, becoming attached to the mother, enters through delusion as sentient beings do. Having killed the father, the son who has entered the lotus obtains his position. Having taken the mother, through the supreme bliss of the habitual imprints of endless births, he causes that too to be born as a son. The daughters are also white Acala and the others, and Delusion Vajra Lady and the others as well. The sons also, having killed the father, go to another; calling this very one "the enemy", they kill him. The daughters also feel desire through the supreme bliss of the habitual imprints of endless births.
DOUBTS Q: referent of phan tshun du rjes su chags pa: the parents attached to each other (taken) vs the gandharva's own attachment. Q: de yang bur skye bar byed: "that" = the mother? the bliss? the process; and who is born as son. Q: gti mug pas cause (taken) vs "the deluded one". Q: gzhan la bgrod = "go to another (woman)"; euphemism for sex. Q: 'di nyid dgra bo zhes de gsod: whom they kill (the next man, not the father already dead). Q: 'dod par byed: object not stated. Q: ma bskyod pa spelled for mi bskyod pa; taken as Akshobhya.

UNIT U08
FORM prose; GENRE exposition (iconography); REGISTER definition
SOURCE ral gri shes rab la zhags pa ni thabs so ...
CLAUSES identities; "X-pa ni Y byed pa'o" = this posture means this function
RELATIONS la (after shes rab) = and; yang na = or else; ni...pa'o = posture signifies function; kyis = means (rkang pas, with the foot); par mdzad = causative honorific
CONSTRUAL The sword is wisdom and the noose is method. Or else the noose is wisdom and the sword is method. Both having become one taste is the threatening finger. The left, directed downward, means it protects the seven underworlds. The right, directed upward, means it protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the earth means it protects the earth. Pressing down fully with the right foot means he terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma; the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara; the mara of the lord of death is Vishnu; the mara of the son of the gods is the lord of the gods.
DOUBTS Q: g.yon pa / g.yas pa = left / right (hand or finger, noun elided); bltas pa = directed. Q: bus mo = knee (variant of pus mo). Q: yang dag par bsnun = pressing down fully (Alt: firmly, trampling). Q: lha'i dbang po = lord of the gods (Alt Indra).

UNIT U09
FORM prose; GENRE exposition (symbolism); REGISTER definition
SOURCE sa ni sa steng mtha' dag gi gzhon nu ma la longs spyod pa'o ...
CLAUSES identities; phyir = cause (because remains long in the young woman); skyes pa = nominalized "what is born"
RELATIONS la = locus (gnas pa la); phyir = cause; las = source (semen and sweat); ni = topic
NEG/QUANT mtha' dag = all; dngos po med pa = non-entity
CONSTRUAL The earth is the enjoyment of all the young women on the surface of the earth. Because he remains for a long time in the young woman, it is the lotus seat. What is born in the womb is the seat of the moon and sun. The man born of semen and sweat is the entity. The woman is the non-entity. Black is consciousness. White is form. Yellow is feeling. Red is perception. Green is Amoghasiddhi.
DOUBTS Q: sa = earth (taken) vs ground/stage. Q: agent of gnas pa ("he") elided. Q: don yod grub pa = Amoghasiddhi (name) in place of the fifth skandha, formations. Q: rngul = sweat. Q: skyes bu = man (pair with bud med).

UNIT U10
FORM prose; GENRE exposition; REGISTER definition
SOURCE yang na mthing ga ni nam mkha'o ...
CLAUSES 5 identities + a comparison: ji lta ba de bzhin du
RELATIONS yang na = alternative; ji lta ba ... de bzhin du = as ... so

UNIT U11

UNIT U12
FORM verse 7x4, not a citation; GENRE exposition in verse; REGISTER plain
SOURCE ston pa rgyal ba gcig pu nyid / lnga yi tshul gyis yang dag gnas / shes rab pha rol phyin nyid kyang / rnam lnga'i tshul gyis yang dag gnas
CLAUSES 2 parallel: verb gnas (abides), subjects ston pa rgyal ba gcig pu (Teacher, sole Victor) and shes rab pha rol phyin (perfection of wisdom); gyis = manner
RELATIONS gyis = manner/means; kyang = also (parallel second subject); yi / 'i = gen
NEG/QUANT gcig pu = sole; nyid on gcig pu = emphasis ("the one alone"); nyid in phyin nyid = nominalizing/emphatic
VERSE-NOTES two parallel sentences kept in position; yang dag abides truly
CONSTRUAL The Teacher, the sole Victor, abides truly in a fivefold way. The perfection of wisdom, too, abides truly in five aspects.
DOUBTS Q: ston pa rgyal ba apposition (taken: "the Teacher, the sole Victor") vs "Teacher and Victor". Q: tshul = way (twice) with lnga "fivefold way" and rnam lnga "five aspects". Q: yang dag gnas = abides truly (Alt: is properly established). Q: nyid after phyin: part of the term, not rendered.
