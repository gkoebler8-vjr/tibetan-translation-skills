### U01
HEADER: U01 · verse (citation mode, rising, 3-4 beats; padas 7 x 4, four lines, order 2-1 then 3-4) · instruction · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The yogi swiftly gains siddhi
through the practice of that, without doubt.
Or else, meditate on equipoise
that is total and free of wisdom.
NOTES:
Q: shes rab as "wisdom" (taken) or as the consort (practice without a consort); shin tu as "total" (taken) vs "very deep"; whether "free of wisdom" belongs to the equipoise (taken) or the meditator.
Issue: the verb of pada 2 is elided in Tibetan; "gains" supplied.

### U02

TEXT:

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose · exposition (identifications) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Here, the square is the four abodes of the immeasurables. The four doors are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the eight branches of the noble path. The single crossbeam is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the womb. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red direction is great desire. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are none other than the brahmin, merchant, warrior and commoner castes. The moon and sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of black Acala. The curved knife is Hatred Vajra Lady. The directions beginning with the east are white Acala and the others. The intermediate directions beginning with fire belong to Delusion Vajra Lady and the others. This is the purity of the mandala.
NOTES:
Q: de la as "Here" (no antecedent in the span except the mandalas of U03).
Q: 'phar ma gcig pa as "single crossbeam" (uncertain architectural term; Alt lintel). Alt: rta babs as porticoes or pediments.
Q: the text's spelling of the fourth caste (dmangs rigs, "commoner") is corrupt.
Q: rngul as "sweat" (dictionary sense); it may stand for the red element, as the moon and sun usually do.
Q: the last identification as "belong to" (taken) vs "in the intermediate directions are those of".
Q: zhes pa ni as "This is" covering everything above.
Issue: Hatred Vajra Lady and Delusion Vajra Lady are translated names; no Sanskrit back-form given.

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose · exposition (identifications) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
I will state the purity of meditation. First, offering is the accumulation of merit, the supreme action. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom, death, the supreme, stainless body, the body of a gandharva. The culmination of the palace is the buddha ground. The lotus is the womb. The moon and sun are the white and the red.
NOTES:
Q: 'chi ba as an appositive in the chain after emptiness (taken, by parallel with the first identification, which also gives two predicates) vs the subject of a separate sentence ("Death is the supreme stainless body, the body of a gandharva"); khyad par 'phags pa may go with death or with the body.
Q: gzhal yang khang is probably gzhal yas khang (palace); the "culmination of the palace" vs "the palace, the ultimate ...".
Q: the agent "I" of brjod par bya is supplied from the speaker; khyad par 'phags pa as "supreme" on the action and on the body.
Check: in-context, 0 major, 1 minor ("now" removed: ste is sequence, not an adverb).
Check: MITRA read 'chi ba as appositive; changed to MITRA's chain after reconsidering the parallel with the first identification.

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose · exposition (symbolism) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The letter HUM is the mind of the gandharva between the mother and the father. Akshobhya is the father. Mamaki is the mother. Seeing them attached to each other, he feels hatred for the father and, becoming attached to the mother, enters through delusion as sentient beings do. Having killed the father, the son who has entered the lotus obtains the father's position. Having taken the mother, through the supreme bliss of the habitual imprints of endless births, he causes that too to be born as a son. The daughters are also white Acala and the others, and Delusion Vajra Lady and the others as well. The sons also, having killed the father, go to another; calling this very one "the enemy," they kill him. The daughters also feel desire, through the supreme bliss of the habitual imprints of endless births.
NOTES:
Q: phan tshun du rjes su chags pa as the parents' attachment to each other (taken) vs the gandharva's.
Q: de yang bur skye bar byed: "that" could be the mother, the bliss, or the process; who is born as a son is not stated.
Q: gti mug pas as "through delusion" (cause) vs "the deluded one".
Q: gzhan la bgrod as "go to another", a euphemism for sexual approach; whom they kill after "this very one is the enemy" (the next man, not the father already dead).
Q: the object of 'dod par byed is unstated.
Issue: ma bskyod pa (text) taken as Akshobhya.
Check: in-context, 0 major, 1 minor ("his position" made "the father's position").

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose · exposition (iconography) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The sword is wisdom and the noose is method. Or else the noose is wisdom and the sword is method. Both having become one taste is the threatening finger. The left, directed downward, means he protects the seven underworlds. The right, directed upward, means he protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the earth means he protects the earth. Pressing down fully with the right foot means he terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma; the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara; the mara of the lord of death is Vishnu; the mara of the son of the gods is the lord of the gods.
NOTES:
Q: g.yon pa and g.yas pa as "the left" and "the right" (hand or finger; the noun is elided).
Q: bus mo as "knee" (variant of pus mo).
Alt: yang dag par bsnun pa as "firmly pressing down" or "trampling".
Alt: lha'i dbang po as "Indra"; tshangs pa'i sgo nga as "cosmic eggs".
Check: in-context, 0 major, 1 minor (subject made uniformly "he").

### U09
