### U01
HEADER: U01 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four English lines, order 2-1-3-4 (padas 1–2 recast as one sentence) · instruction · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text, Toh 431, ch. 15)
TEXT:
The yogin will gain siddhi quickly
through that union, without a doubt.
Or else, without a consort,
he should meditate in deep equipoise.
NOTES:
Q: de yi sbyor bas: "that union" may mean union with her (the consort of the preceding verses) or "that practice". "That union" leaves both open.
Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i: grammatically this qualifies the equipoise ("an equipoise without a consort"). It could also describe the yogin ("one who lacks a consort"). The English attaches it to the act of meditating, and the sense is the same.
Q: MITRA reads shes rab as "wisdom" ("equipoise devoid of wisdom"). I kept "consort", because in this tantra the prajna of the union is the female partner and the line gives the alternative to practising with her.
Issue: shes rab (wisdom, prajna) here means the female partner, so it is rendered "consort".
Q: after the agentive "yogin" the verb of padas 1–2 is left out. I supplied "will gain".
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the note on where "without a consort" attaches contradicted the construal); note corrected.

### U03
HEADER: U03 · mixed: prose frame + verse, citation mode, rising, 2–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four English lines, order 1-2-4-3 · framing and request (the Blessed Lady to the Blessed One) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Then the Blessed Lady asked:
What is the purity of the deities?
Guide, I wish to hear it.
Lord, please explain to me the purity
of the mandalas you taught before.
Then the Blessed One said:
NOTES:
Q: lha: either "the deities" (of the mandala) or "the deity" (Acala). I took the plural.
Q: lha yi rnam par dag pa gang is an embedded question ("I wish to hear what the purity … is"). It is set here as a direct question followed by "I wish to hear it".
Issue: gsol pa (humble "said") is rendered "asked", and bka' stsal pa (honorific) plainly as "said". The honorific imperative gsungs is rendered "please explain".
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the honorific imperative had been rendered as a bare imperative); "please" added, so the line now has four beats.

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose · exposition (purity list) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Here, the square is the four immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the noble eightfold path. The single tier is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the womb. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red in the cardinal directions is great passion. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are the brahmins, vaishyas, kshatriyas and shudras. The moon and sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of Black Acala. The curved knife is Dveshavajri. The cardinal directions, beginning with the east, are White Acala and the others. The intermediate directions, beginning with the southeast, belong to Mohavajri and the others. This is the purity of the mandala.
NOTES:
Q: 'phar ma gcig pa: it is not certain which part of the building this is (a single tier, course or ledge of the palace). Rendered "the single tier" (MITRA: "single projection").
Q: phyogs dmar po: read as the red of the cardinal directions, set against the colors "in the intermediate directions" that follow. "The red quarter" (one direction) is also possible, and MITRA reads it that way.
Q: rngul means "sweat". One would expect the female fluid as the counterpart of the sun, but the Tibetan has sweat (again in U09).
Q: me la sogs pa'i mtshams rnams su … kyi'o: either "belong to Mohavajri and the others" or "are the places of Mohavajri and the others".
Issue: me ("fire", Agni's quarter) is rendered "the southeast".
Issue: zhe sdang rdo rje ma and gti mug rdo rje ma are given by their Sanskrit names, Dveshavajri ("Vajri of Hatred") and Mohavajri ("Vajri of Delusion"). MITRA gives the first as Vajradveshi; I kept the form that follows the Tibetan.
Check: in-context; no findings.

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose · exposition (purity of the meditation) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
I shall state the purity of the meditation. First, the offering is the accumulation of merit; it is the exceptional action. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom; it is the exceptional death and the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva. The culmination of the celestial palace is the level of buddhahood. The lotus is the womb. The moon and sun are the white and the red.
NOTES:
Q: the speaker of "I shall state" is not named (the verb is a bare future). I assumed the Blessed One, as in U04. An impersonal "The purity of the meditation will now be stated" is also possible.
Q: the offering sentence can read "the accumulation of merit, the exceptional action" (apposition, parallel to the emptiness sentence) or "the action of the exceptional accumulation of merit" (my first draft). I changed it after the MITRA cross-check on grammatical grounds: khyad par 'phags pa'i is the adjective placed before las, as in the parallel construction ye shes kyi tshogs + 'chi ba.
Q: the emptiness sentence may be two statements ("emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom and death; the exceptional stainless body is the body of the gandharva"). The single subject follows the Tibetan punctuation.
Q: gzhal yas khang gi mthar thug pa: either "the culmination of the celestial palace" or "the celestial palace is the culmination, the level of buddhahood".
Issue: the text's gzhal yang khang is read as gzhal yas khang (celestial palace).
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the doubt about the unnamed speaker was missing from the notes); Q added. MITRA cross-check: offering sentence changed, with the reason recorded in the construal.

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose · exposition (purity of conception and birth) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The syllable HUM is the mind of the gandharva between the mother and the father. Akshobhya is the father, and Mamaki is the mother. Seeing their passion for each other, the gandharva feels hatred for the father and, becoming attached to the mother, enters the womb through delusion, just as sentient beings do. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and then attains his position. Having taken the mother, he in turn begets a son through the exceptional bliss of the imprints of endless births. The daughters, moreover, are White Acala and the others, and also Mohavajri and the others. The sons too kill the father and go on to another; thinking, "This one is the enemy," they kill him. Through the exceptional bliss of the imprints of endless births, the daughters too feel desire.
NOTES:
Q: "between the mother and the father" may place either the HUM or the gandharva's mind there. The sense is the same.
Q: gti mug pas: either "through delusion" (the third poison after hatred and attachment) or "the deluded one" as the agent.
Q: where the gandharva enters is not stated. "The womb" is supplied from the next sentence ("entered the lotus").
Q: de yang bur skye bar byed is read as causative, "he in turn begets a son". MITRA reads it intransitively ("he is born as a son"). I kept the causative, because bar byed with an agent is causative.
Q: bu mo ("daughters") is equated both with White Acala and the others and with Mohavajri and the others. The text probably means the children (sons = White Acala and the others, daughters = Mohavajri and the others), but the Tibetan says daughters.
Q: gzhan la bgrod par byed: either "go on to another" (whom they then kill) or, taking "going" in its sexual sense, "approach another woman".
Q: 'dod par byed: the daughters' desire has no stated object (presumably the father). Left unstated.
Issue: the text's pa'o is read as pha'o ("is the father").
Check: in-context; 2 minor (a missing Q on where "between" attaches; chags nas had been rendered as a state, now "becoming attached"); both fixed.

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose · exposition (purity of Acala's attributes and posture) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The sword is wisdom and the noose is means. Or else, the noose is wisdom and the sword is means. The two becoming one taste is the threatening forefinger. The left eye looking downward protects the seven underworlds. The right eye looking upward protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the ground protects the earth. Stamping down with the right foot terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma, the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara, the mara of the Lord of Death is Vishnu, and the mara of the sons of the gods is Indra.
NOTES:
Q: g.yon pa / g.yas pa ("the left one", "the right one") have no noun. "Eye" is supplied from "looking" (the gaze, one eye up and one down); "hand" is possible. MITRA reads them as directions ("looking downward to the left"). I kept my reading, because g.yon pa is unmarked and is the subject of bltas pa; a direction would need g.yon du.
Alt: yang dag par bsnun pa is rendered "stamping down"; literally "striking properly".
Q: dbang phyug is rendered Ishvara, as the Tibetan gives it; Maheshvara (Shiva) is meant.
Issue: lha'i dbang po ("lord of the gods") is rendered by the name Indra. The text's ses rab and bus mo are read as shes rab (wisdom) and pus mo (knee).
Check: in-context; 2 minor (a missing Q on Ishvara; glossary form "son"/"sons of the gods"); both fixed.

### U09
HEADER: U09 · prose · exposition (purity of seats and colors) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The earth means enjoying all the young women on earth. The seat is a lotus because he dwells a long time with the young women. Being born in the womb is the seat of moon and sun. The man born from semen and sweat is being. The woman is non-being. Black is consciousness. White is form. Yellow is feeling. Red is perception. Green is Amoghasiddhi.
NOTES:
Q: phyir in the lotus-seat sentence can mean cause ("because he dwells") or purpose ("so that he may dwell"). I took cause. The subject "he" is supplied.
Q: gzhon nu ma la: either "with the young women" or "in the young woman".
Q: green is Amoghasiddhi, where the series of aggregates would lead one to expect formations. Kept as the Tibetan gives it.
Q: skye gnas la skyes pa is read as "being born in the womb". skyes pa can also mean "the man" (the next sentence uses skyes bu for "man").
Issue: the first sentence uses "means" because "is" would let the young women be read as the ones enjoying.
Check: in-context; 2 minor ("the enjoyment of the young women" left the roles unclear; a Q on skyes pa was missing); both fixed.

### U11
HEADER: U11 · prose · exposition (purity of colors as the five wisdoms) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Or else, blue is the wisdom of the pure dharmadhatu, white is mirror-like wisdom, yellow is the wisdom of equality, red is discriminating wisdom, and green is the wisdom that accomplishes activity.
NOTES:
Q: chos kyi dbyings rnam par dag pa'i ye shes: either "the wisdom of the pure dharmadhatu" or "the wisdom of the dharmadhatu's purity".
Alt: the last wisdom is usually called "all-accomplishing wisdom", but the Tibetan has no "all".
Issue: ye shes (jnana) and shes rab (prajna, U08) are both "wisdom", as practitioners use them; the context keeps them apart.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (missing Q); added.

### U12
HEADER: U12 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four English lines, order 1-2-3-4 · exposition in verse · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The Teacher, the one and only Victor,
truly abides in a fivefold way.
Prajnaparamita, too,
truly abides in five aspects.
NOTES:
Q: "fivefold" presumably means as the five buddhas, with Prajnaparamita as the five consorts (the colors and wisdoms of U09–U11). The text does not say so, and nothing is added.
Q: MITRA reads gcig pu nyid as a predicate ("the Victor is unique, and abides …"). I kept it as an attribute, since the only finite verb is gnas. A concessive sense ("though one, he abides as five") is possible.
Check: in-context; 1 minor (missing Q); added.
