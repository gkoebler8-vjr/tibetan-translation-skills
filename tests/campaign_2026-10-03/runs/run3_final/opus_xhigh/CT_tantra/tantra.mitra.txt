U01: By means of that practice, the yogi will swiftly attain accomplishment without doubt; or alternatively, one should meditate in deep meditative equipoise that is devoid of wisdom.
U02: When the meditation on immovability is perfected, one truly attains the sovereignty of enlightenment.
U03: Then the Blessed One [female] asked: "What is the purity of the deities? Guide, I wish to hear. The mandalas previously taught— Lord, speak to me of their purity." Then the Blessed One replied:
U04: Then, I shall thoroughly explain that which purifies all.
U05: In this regard, the square is the four immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four entrance arches are the four concentrations. The eight pillars are the eightfold noble path. The single projection is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the place of birth. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of the excellent teaching. The red direction is great desire. The white, yellow, green, and black in the intermediate directions are the Brahmin, Vaishya, Kshatriya, and Shudra castes themselves. The moon and the sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the symbol of Black Acala. The curved knife is Vajradveṣī. The cardinal directions, such as the east, are White Acala and the others. The intermediate directions, such as the fire direction, are those of Mohavajrī and the others. This is the purification of the maṇḍala.
U06: I shall explain the purification of meditation. First, the offering is the sublime activity of the accumulation of merit. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom, the sublime death, the stainless body, that is, the gandharva body. The ultimate limit of the celestial palace is the stage of a Buddha. The lotus is the place of birth. The moon and sun are the white and red [elements].
U07: The *hūṃ* syllable is the consciousness of the *gandharva* between the mother and the father. Akṣobhya is the father; Māmakī is the mother. Seeing their mutual passion, one feels hatred toward the father, and, being attached to the mother, one enters through delusion just as a sentient being does. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and attains his status. Having taken the mother, through the sublime bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births, he is born as a son. The daughters are *Mi gyo ba dkar po* (Acalā) and the others, and also *Gti mug rdo rje ma* (Mohavajrā) and the others. The sons also kill the father and proceed toward others; calling this very one an enemy, they kill him. The daughters also desire [this] because of the sublime bliss of the habitual tendencies of endless births.
U08: The sword is wisdom, and the noose is skillful means; or alternatively, the noose is wisdom and the sword is skillful means. The two becoming of one taste is the threatening gesture (*tarjanī-mudrā*). Looking downward to the left signifies protecting the seven subterranean realms. Looking upward to the right signifies protecting the seven Brahma-egg realms. Pressing the left knee to the ground signifies protecting the earth. Stamping firmly with the right foot signifies terrifying all the māras. Brahmā is the māra of the aggregates; Īśvara is the māra of the afflictions; Viṣṇu is the māra of the Lord of Death; and Indra is the māra of the son of the gods.
U09: The earth is the enjoyment of all young maidens upon the earth. Because of abiding for a long time with a young maiden, there is the lotus seat. Being born from the place of birth is the sun and moon seat. The male born from semen and sweat is existence; the female is non-existence. Black is consciousness; white is form; yellow is feeling; red is perception; green is Amoghasiddhi.
U10: Alternatively, dark blue is space; white is water; yellow is earth; red is fire; green is wind. Just as it is with the Bhagavan, so it is with the Bhagavati.
U11: Alternatively, dark blue is the wisdom of the completely pure expanse of phenomena; white is the mirror-like wisdom; yellow is the wisdom of equality; red is the wisdom of individual discernment; green is the wisdom of accomplishing activities.
U12: The Teacher, the Victor, is unique, and abides perfectly in the manner of five. The Perfection of Wisdom also abides perfectly in the manner of five aspects.
