# tantra.draft.md — draft after Pass 2 (faithful draft) and Pass 3 (style, seasoned practitioner)

### U01
HEADER: U01 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four English lines, order 2-1-3-4 (padas 1–2 recast as one sentence) · instruction · seasoned practitioner · not a citation (root text, Toh 431, ch. 15)
TEXT:
The yogin will gain siddhi quickly
through that union, without a doubt.
Or else, without a consort,
he should meditate in deep equipoise.
NOTES:
Q: de yi sbyor bas — "that union" may be union with her (the consort of the preceding verses) or "that practice"; "that union" keeps both open.
Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i — grammatically it qualifies the equipoise ("an equipoise without a consort"); the English reads it with the yogin, which is the same sense.
Issue: shes rab (wisdom, prajna) here is the female partner, rendered "consort".
Q: the verb of padas 1–2 is elided after the agentive "yogin"; supplied as "will gain".

### U03
HEADER: U03 · mixed: prose frame + verse, citation mode, rising, 2–3 beats; padas 7 × 4, four English lines, order 1-2-4-3 · framing and request (the Blessed Lady to the Blessed One) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Then the Blessed Lady asked:
What is the purity of the deities?
Guide, I wish to hear it.
Lord, explain to me the purity
of the mandalas you taught before.
Then the Blessed One said:
NOTES:
Q: lha — "the deities" (of the mandala) or "the deity" (Acala); plural taken.
Q: lha yi rnam par dag pa gang is an embedded question ("I wish to hear what the purity … is"); set as a direct question followed by "I wish to hear it".
Issue: gsol pa (humble "said") rendered "asked"; bka' stsal pa (honorific) rendered plainly "said".

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose · exposition (purity list) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
Here, the square is the four immeasurables. The four gates are the four truths. The four archways are the four dhyanas. The eight pillars are the noble eightfold path. The single tier is one-pointedness of mind. The lotus is the womb. The various colors are the various emanations. The nine deities are the nine branches of scripture. The red in the cardinal directions is great passion. The white, yellow, green and black in the intermediate directions are the brahmins, vaishyas, kshatriyas and shudras. The moon and sun are semen and sweat. The sword in the center is the emblem of Black Acala. The curved knife is Dveshavajri. The cardinal directions, beginning with the east, are White Acala and the others. The intermediate directions, beginning with the southeast, belong to Mohavajri and the others. This is the purity of the mandala.
NOTES:
Q: 'phar ma gcig pa — the architectural member is uncertain (a single tier, course or ledge of the palace); rendered "the single tier".
Q: phyogs dmar po — read as the red of the cardinal directions, in contrast with the colors "in the intermediate directions" that follow; "the red quarter" (one direction) is possible.
Q: rngul is "sweat"; one expects the female fluid as the sun's counterpart, but the Tibetan has sweat (again in U09).
Q: me la sogs pa'i mtshams rnams su … kyi'o — "belong to Mohavajri and the others" or "are the places of Mohavajri and the others".
Issue: me ("fire", Agni's quarter) rendered "the southeast".
Issue: the deity names zhe sdang rdo rje ma and gti mug rdo rje ma are given in Sanskrit (Dveshavajri, "Vajri of Hatred"; Mohavajri, "Vajri of Delusion").

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose · exposition (purity of the meditation) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
I shall state the purity of the meditation. First, the offering is the act of gathering the exceptional accumulation of merit. Emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom; it is the exceptional death and the stainless body, that is, the body of the gandharva. The culmination of the celestial palace is the level of buddhahood. The lotus is the womb. The moon and sun are the white and the red.
NOTES:
Q: the emptiness sentence may be two statements ("emptiness is the accumulation of wisdom and death; the exceptional stainless body is the body of the gandharva"); the single subject follows the Tibetan punctuation.
Q: gzhal yas khang gi mthar thug pa — "the culmination of the celestial palace" or "the celestial palace is the culmination, the level of buddhahood".
Issue: las ("action") of the accumulation of merit rendered "the act of gathering"; "gathering" is the verb English uses with an accumulation.
Issue: the text's gzhal yang khang read as gzhal yas khang (celestial palace).

### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose · exposition (purity of conception and birth) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The syllable HUM is the mind of the gandharva between the mother and the father. Akshobhya is the father, and Mamaki is the mother. Seeing their passion for each other, the gandharva feels hatred for the father and, attached to the mother, enters the womb through delusion, just as sentient beings do. The son who has entered the lotus kills the father and then attains his position. Having taken the mother, he in turn begets a son through the exceptional bliss of the imprints of endless births. The daughters, moreover, are White Acala and the others, and also Mohavajri and the others. The sons too kill the father and go on to another; thinking, "This one is the enemy," they kill him. Through the exceptional bliss of the imprints of endless births, the daughters too feel desire.
NOTES:
Q: gti mug pas — "through delusion" (the third poison after hatred and attachment) or "the deluded one" as the agent.
Q: the place entered is unstated; "the womb" supplied from the next sentence ("entered the lotus").
Q: de yang bur skye bar byed — read as causative, "he in turn begets a son"; the intransitive reading would be "he in turn is born as a son".
Q: bu mo ("daughters") is equated with White Acala and the others as well as Mohavajri and the others; the sense is probably the children (sons = White Acala and the others, daughters = Mohavajri and the others), but the Tibetan says daughters.
Q: gzhan la bgrod par byed — "go on to another" (whom they then kill) or, in the sexual sense of going, "approach another woman".
Q: 'dod par byed — the object of the daughters' desire is unstated (presumably the father); left unstated.
Issue: the text's pa'o read as pha'o ("is the father").

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose · exposition (purity of Acala's attributes and posture) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The sword is wisdom and the noose is means. Or else, the noose is wisdom and the sword is means. The two becoming one taste is the threatening forefinger. The left eye looking downward protects the seven underworlds. The right eye looking upward protects the seven eggs of Brahma. The left knee planted on the ground protects the earth. Stamping down with the right foot terrifies all the maras. The mara of the aggregates is Brahma, the mara of the afflictions is Ishvara, the mara of the Lord of Death is Vishnu, and the mara of the sons of the gods is Indra.
NOTES:
Q: g.yon pa / g.yas pa ("the left one", "the right one") have no noun; "eye" is supplied from "looking" (the gaze, one eye up and one down); "hand" is possible.
Alt: yang dag par bsnun pa — "stamping down"; literally "striking properly".
Issue: lha'i dbang po ("lord of the gods") rendered by the name Indra; the text's ses rab and bus mo read as shes rab (wisdom) and pus mo (knee).

### U09
HEADER: U09 · prose · exposition (purity of seats and colors) · seasoned practitioner · not a citation
TEXT:
The earth is the enjoyment of all the young women on earth. The seat is a lotus because he dwells a long time with the young women. Being born in the womb is the seat of moon and sun. The man born from semen and sweat is being. The woman is non-being. Black is consciousness. White is form. Yellow is feeling. Red is perception. Green is Amoghasiddhi.
NOTES:
Q: phyir in the lotus-seat sentence — cause ("because he dwells") or purpose ("so that he may dwell"); cause taken. The subject "he" is supplied.
Q: gzhon nu ma la — "with the young women" or "in the young woman".
Q: green is Amoghasiddhi where the series of aggregates leads one to expect formations; kept as the Tibetan gives it.

### U10

TEXT:
Or else, blue is space, white is water, yellow is earth, red is fire, and green is wind. As it is for the Blessed One, so it is for the Blessed Lady.
NOTES:
