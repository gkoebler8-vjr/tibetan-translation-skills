UNIT        U01
FORM        verse, 7×4; not a citation (root tantra's own verse)
GENRE       instruction (tantric practice), REGISTER: the Lord instructing, plain, third person "the yogin"
SOURCE      1 de yi sbyor bas rnal 'byor pas / 2 myur du dngos grub the tshom med / 3 yang na shes rab dang bral ba'i / 4 shin tu mnyam gzhag bsgom par bya
WORDS       sbyor ba = union, joining (prayoga/yoga; here the sexual-yogic union of the preceding practice); shes rab = prajna, here the female partner ("wisdom", the consort, as throughout this tantra); mnyam gzhag = meditative equipoise (samahita); shin tu = intensifier (deep/perfect)
CLAUSES     2. (a) padas 1–2: verb elided; rnal 'byor pas ERG agent implies a transitive verb of obtaining; dngos grub ABS object; myur du adverbial; the tshom med = without doubt (sentence adverbial). (b) padas 3–4: bsgom par bya, byed fut. = gerundive "should be cultivated / one should meditate"; agent elided = the yogin; object = shin tu mnyam gzhag (ABS).
RELATIONS   de yi (GEN) = of that → union with that/her; sbyor bas (-s ERG/INSTR) = means: through the union; rnal 'byor pas = agent; myur du = manner; yang na = alternative "or else"; shes rab dang bral ba'i = GEN modification: the equipoise that is without a consort (or: of one who lacks a consort)
NEG/QUANT   med: "the tshom med" — no doubt (assertion of certainty). bral = without (privative). No quantifiers.
VERSE-NOTES 'yi' short gen for count. Contrast structure: union → siddhi / or else, without a consort → equipoise.
SPINE       Two sentences: (1) through that union the yogin [gains] siddhi quickly, without doubt; (2) or else, [he] should cultivate deep equipoise without a consort.
CONSTRUAL   Through union with that [partner / that practice], the yogin quickly [attains] siddhi; of that there is no doubt. Or else, he should cultivate a deep meditative equipoise that is without a consort.
TERMS       rnal 'byor pa = yogin; dngos grub = siddhi; shes rab (partner sense) = consort (wisdom consort); mnyam gzhag = equipoise
DOUBTS      Q: de yi = "with her" (the consort of the preceding verses) or "of that" (the preceding practice); rendered "that union", which covers both.
            Q: shes rab dang bral ba'i: the equipoise "without a consort" (modifies the meditation) vs "[the yogin] who lacks a consort" (modifies the agent); sense the same, rendered as a condition on the agent would be an added relation, so kept on the act.
            Q: elided verb in pada 2 supplied as "gains"; could be "will gain" (future sense of the promise).

FORM        prose; announcement + 5 equations
GENRE       exposition (purity of the meditation, i.e. of the generation-stage sequence); REGISTER plain

UNIT        U07
FORM        prose; 10 sentences
GENRE       exposition (purity of the conception: the gandharva entering the womb, mapped on the deities); REGISTER plain, narrative-definitional

            Q: S8 gzhan la bgrod: "go on to another [man/father]" (then kill him, S9) vs "approach another [woman]" (gamana, sexual sense); the first reading fits S9.
            Q: S10 'dod par byed: object elided — presumably the father (parallel to the sons with the mother); rendered "feel desire" without an object.

UNIT        U08
FORM        prose; 7 equations + list of four maras
GENRE       exposition (purity of Acala's attributes and posture); REGISTER plain
SOURCE      S1 ral gri shes rab la zhags pa ni thabs so / S2 yang na zhags pa shes rab (text: ses rab) la ral gri thabs so / S3 gnyis ka ro gcig tu gyur pa ni sdigs mdzub bo / S4 g.yon pa 'og tu bltas pa ni sa 'og bdun skyong bar byed pa'o / S5 g.yas pa steng du bltas pa ni tshangs pa'i sgo nga bdun skyong bar byed pa'o / S6 pus mo (text: bus mo) g.yon pa sa la btsugs pa ni sa skyong bar byed pa'o / S7 g.yas pa'i rkang pas yang dag par bsnun pa ni bdud thams cad skrag par mdzad pa'o / S8 phung po'i bdud tshangs pa / nyon mongs pa'i bdud dbang phyug / 'chi bdag gi bdud khyab 'jug / lha'i bu'i bdud lha'i dbang po'o
WORDS       ral gri = sword; zhags pa = noose; shes rab = wisdom (prajna; paired with means); thabs = means; ro gcig = one taste; sdigs mdzub = threatening forefinger (tarjani); bltas pa = looking (gaze — so g.yon pa / g.yas pa = left/right eye, elided noun); sa 'og bdun = the seven underworlds; tshangs pa'i sgo nga bdun = the seven eggs of Brahma (brahmanda); pus mo = knee; btsugs pa = planted; sa skyong bar byed = protects the earth (tool slip: botok read sa skyong "king"); bsnun pa = strike, stamp; bdud = mara; skrag par mdzad = terrifies (honorific); 'chi bdag = Lord of Death; dbang phyug = Ishvara; khyab 'jug = Vishnu; lha'i dbang po = Indra (lord of the gods; tool slip: dbang po "faculty")
