## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U07 S10 "the daughters too feel desire": the unmarked bu mo rnams taken as agent of transitive 'dod par byed instead of its object (he desires the daughters). Grammar misread.
2. U07 S9 "they kill him": the sons made the killers of an unnamed second man; the text has the father killing the sons as enemies. Construal fork (agent of gsod par byed), unrecorded.
3. U06 emptiness equated with the stainless body and the gandharva's body; the stainless body is a separate equation (the bardo stage). Construal fork, recorded, but punctuation preferred over the death/bardo/birth sequence.
4. U07 S8 "go on to another": bgrod pa taken as plain motion rather than sexual approach, which in turn produced the invented victim of S9. Dictionary sense.
5. U06/U07 "the exceptional action", "the exceptional death", "the exceptional bliss of the imprints of endless births": khyad par 'phags pa rendered mechanically, dense nominal chains. Register / term choice.
