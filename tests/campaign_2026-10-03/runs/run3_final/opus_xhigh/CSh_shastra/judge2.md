## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U08 sentence 6: the definition of the fourth consecration (the focusing on the moment is what "is to be called the fourth consecration") is demoted to a relative clause on "the instruction". Construal fork: the genitive 'i is read literally rather than as the end of a clause, though the alternative is recorded.
2. U11 line 1: "One's own concepts of this and that proliferate." is cut off from the condition it opens, and de de is forced into a distributive. Grammar misread: asyndeton taken as the end of a sentence; verse constraint (one line, one sentence) in free line-for-line mode.
3. U06: mos pas nges par bzung nas is rendered "ascertained through their inclination" (the teacher diagnoses) instead of "made firm in conviction". Dictionary sense.
4. U05 sentence 4: the scope of de lta bas na (commentator's "therefore" or part of the rejected statement) is decided silently. Construal fork left unrecorded.
5. U08 and U05: dense, bracket-heavy sentences ("Moreover, as for that [fourth]: with …", "[the master consecration is so called]") that need an editor's pass. Register: academic scaffolding taken too literally.
