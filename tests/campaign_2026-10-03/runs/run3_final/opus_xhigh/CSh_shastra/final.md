### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose, 6 periods → 6 sentences · exposition → debate (rejection) → prescription · academic · not a citation
TEXT:
Here, in this Yoginī tantra, the master (*ācārya*) is [so called] because he conducts himself far removed from evil, unwholesome things; and [the master consecration is so called] because it is the *saṃvara* of that very [master]. The essence of the master consecration is what is termed actualizing the intrinsic nature of the four joys from the *karmamudrā* by means of the four moments. It is not, however, by having obtained the irreversible consecration shared with the Kriyā tantras and the rest that one is to be consecrated with the master consecration. Therefore the statement that one "also becomes fit to explain, to hear, and so on, all the Yoga and Yoginī tantras and the rest" amounts to nothing. Hence, to qualify [disciples] for hearing the Yoginī tantras such as the *Hevajra*, for meditating on them, and so on, one should first of all consecrate [them] with the master consecration itself. Moreover, it should be understood that, in accordance with realization (*abhisamaya*), this [consecration] is marked by the characteristics of the master, the secret, the wisdom, and the fourth [consecrations].
NOTES:
Q: sentence 1 is an etymology of *ācārya* (ring du gyur pa ≈ *ārāt* "far", kun du spyod pa ≈ *ā-√car* "conduct oneself"); the second reason, de nyid kyi de'i sdom pa yin pas, is read "because it is the *saṃvara* of that very [master]"; alt. "because that very [conduct] is his *saṃvara*" (MITRA: "the vow of that very state").
Q: slob dpon gyis dbang bskur ba (instrumental) read as the term "master consecration" (as slob dpon gyi in sentence 5); alt. "consecration by the master".
Q: sentence 3 — read: obtaining the irreversible consecration shared with the Kriyā tantras does not count as consecration with this master consecration; alt.: one who has obtained the irreversible consecration is not to be [re]consecrated with the master consecration (the reading MITRA takes). Sentences 4–5 favour the first.
Q: thun mong du gyur pa'i phyir mi ldog pa'i dbang bskur ba — read as "the irreversible consecration shared with …" (phyir mi ldog pa compound); alt. causal phyir: "because it is shared with the Kriyā tantras …, [having obtained] the irreversible (mi ldog pa'i) consecration …" (MITRA). Both parse; the sense converges.
Q: las kyi phyag rgya las — ablative "from the *karmamudrā*"; MITRA "by means of"; ablative kept.
Q: sentence 4 — the subject of the rejected statement is elided ("one also becomes fit …"): the holder of the shared consecration.
Q: mngon par rtogs pa ji lta ba bzhin du — "in accordance with realization (*abhisamaya*)"; alt. "as [set out] in the sādhana (*abhisamaya*) manual".
Alt: dbang bar bya ba'i phyir "to qualify" — lit. "to give [them] authority over", a play on dbang (power) and dbang bskur (consecration) that English cannot keep.
Issue: the two "therefore" (de lta bas na) rendered "Therefore" and "Hence".
Check: in-context; no findings (one CONSTRUAL? on sentence 3, the recorded Q).

### U06
HEADER: U06 · prose, 3 periods → 4 sentences · exposition, prescriptive · academic · not a citation
TEXT:
Those of dull faculties who have obtained such a master consecration should be ascertained through their inclination and then taught the meditation on the *karmamudrā* and the mudrā of elaboration. ### U07
HEADER: U07 · prose, 4 periods → 4 sentences · exposition, prescriptive · academic · not a citation
TEXT:
Likewise, the distinguished gnosis is wisdom (*prajñā*): the knowledge that all phenomena are mind only. The consecration [given] to bring it about is the consecration of the gnosis of wisdom (*prajñājñāna*). Further, it should be understood that the gnosis of wisdom is this: through the guru's instruction itself, the external mudrā and the moments are correctly indicated in the jewel at the heart, which embodies the condensed nature of the three consciousnesses—the dependent, the imagined, and the transformed. To those of superior faculties who are consecrated with that consecration, the *dharmamudrā* should be presented and explained by means of the illusion-like *samādhi*.
NOTES:
Q: khyad par can gyi ye shes ni shes rab — Tibetan topic order kept ("the distinguished gnosis is wisdom"); prob. an etymology of *prajñā* (*pra-* = distinguished, *jñā* = knowing), i.e. "wisdom is the distinguished gnosis".
Q: yongs su gyur pa — "transformed" as the text has it; the standard triad has yongs grub (*pariniṣpanna*, "perfected"), which MITRA substitutes.
Q: mtshon pa — "indicated"; alt. "represented", "symbolized".
Q: sgyu ma lta bu'i ting nge 'dzin gyis — means of presenting the *dharmamudrā*; alt. "the *dharmamudrā* [practised] in the illusion-like *samādhi*".
Issue: shes rab ye shes rendered "the gnosis of wisdom" (*prajñājñāna*), not the calque "wisdom-gnosis".
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the relative "which embodies …" had become a separate assertion) — restored inside one sentence.

### U08
HEADER: U08 · prose, 6 periods → 8 sentences; glosses U04 pada 2 and quotes U02 pada 3 · commentary gloss → debate · academic · lemma: Hevajra Tantra II.3 (as U04); quoted pada: as U02
TEXT:
Likewise, there is also the fourth, as in the words "in that same way … as well." "That" (*de*) means the gnosis of wisdom. "The same" (*de bzhin*) is suchness (*de bzhin nyid*, *tathatā*), and there is no difference in meaning between it and what are called "the limit of reality" and "the *dharmadhātu*." The consecration by which its very nature is seen, or actualized, is what the word "the same" denotes. The word "as well" (*yang*) means that [the fourth] is also to be conferred immediately after the consecration of the gnosis of wisdom. Moreover, as for that [fourth]: with the external mudrā and through the guru's instruction itself, one focuses on the perfection of the moment—a moment characterized by not delimiting objects as distinct—through the yoga of focusing without a focus. Merely presenting the instruction on this, which is to be called the fourth consecration, does not make it the fourth consecration. Otherwise—given that, in the words "by it one is washed and consecrated" and so on, the Blessed One holds consecration here to be the washing away of stains—how could it come about merely by teaching an instruction?
NOTES:
Q: the lemma repeats U04 line 2; Wylie is given so that the commentator's play on de bzhin ("the same") and de bzhin nyid (suchness) is visible.
Q: rnams la — "between it and" the limit of reality and the *dharmadhātu*; alt. "with respect to those".
Q: phyi rol gyi phyag rgya dang — comitative "with the external mudrā"; alt. coordination "the external mudrā and that which …".
Q: bla ma'i man ngag nyid kyis is taken as the means of the main verb of the gang-clause (dmigs par byed pa), as in U07; MITRA attaches it, with the external mudrā, to the negated delimitation ("not limited to specific objects by external seals or by the guru's instructions").
Q: … brjod par bya ba'i man ngag — genitive kept ("the instruction on this, which is to be called the fourth consecration"); alt., if 'i stands for an adversative: "that is to be called the fourth consecration; but it does not become the fourth consecration merely by …".
Q: skad cig ma'i phun sum tshogs pa — "the perfection of the moment" (prob. the fourth moment, free of characteristics); alt. "the excellence of the moments".
Q: gzhan du na … bzhed pa yin na — "otherwise — given that …" (grounds); alt. condition, "if the Blessed One holds …".
Check: in-context; 1 minor (the attribute of the moment had become a separate sentence) — restored as an apposition.
