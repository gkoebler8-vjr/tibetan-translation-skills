## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U10 line 2, "is heard and seen directly from speech", is stated without its concessive hedge, so on the page it contradicts U09's claim that reality cannot be expressed. Cause: construal fork (recorded, but the hedge stayed in the note).
2. U11, "how could reality be understood?": the note says rtogs/"realize", but the text uses the weaker "understood". Cause: term choice.
3. U05 sentence 2, "actualizing the intrinsic nature of the four joys from the karmamudrā by means of the four moments", is calqued and opaque, and "in accordance with realization (abhisamaya)" is vague. Cause: register.
4. U08's sentence on the fourth consecration ("one focuses on the perfection of the moment … through the yoga of focusing without a focus") is dense and stacks qualifiers. Cause: register / omission under compression of the syntax.
5. U07, "the distinguished gnosis is wisdom", keeps the Tibetan topic order, which hides the pra-jñā etymology the sentence exists to make. Cause: skill rule misapplied (literal order kept over sense).
