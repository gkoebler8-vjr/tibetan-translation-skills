# rays.construal.md — Rays of Sunlight (nyi ma'i 'od zer), opening page (U01–U12)

BRIEF (given, held): audience seasoned practitioner; purpose publication (reader edition, Edition Garchen Stiftung manner).
House style: Sanskrit technical terms anglicized, no diacritics (dharmakaya, sambhogakaya, nirmanakaya, sutra, tantra, mara, samsara, nirvana, bodhisattva); Tibetan names phonetic; no brackets/Wylie/locators in body; verse citations by tibetan-verse, citation mode; no contractions; em dashes allowed; doubts as Q: lines.
Speaker: the commentator, Ayang Thubten Rinpoche. Register: homage (U01–U05), framing + citation (U06–U09), exposition (U10–U12).

## PERIOD A — the homage, U01–U05 (one Tibetan sentence)

SPINE (whole period): one finite verb only, U05 'dud do ("I bow"). Everything in U01–U05 before "shAkya seng ge la" is a chain of relative/attributive clauses on the head bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shAkya seng ge. Chain: U01 ...bzhugs shing (and) → U02 ...ldan pa dang (and) → U03 ...snang ba dang (and) → U04 ...khyab cing (and) → U05 ...bstan te ... brlan te grol bar mdzad pa'i [head] la ... 'dud do.
Structure of content: rang don (own benefit) = dharmakaya + its qualities (U01–U02); gzhan don (others' benefit) = sambhogakaya (U03) + supreme nirmanakaya (U04–U05). The elided subject of every clause = the head, Shakyasimha. English plan: split into sentences with "he" (the head named in U05); the coordination "and" carried at each seam; "for his own benefit" / "for the benefit of others" kept as the structuring pair.

UNIT        U01
FORM        prose; homage period, clauses 1–2 of the chain
GENRE       homage (mchod brjod)
REGISTER    commentator to the Buddha, third person; elevated (homage), unhurried
SOURCE      rang don du chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa / khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa la / glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing / rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa / spros pa dang bral ba nam mkha' ltar srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing
WORDS       'pho 'gyur med pa = without transition or change (unchanging); khams = the element (dhatu, buddha-nature), not "realm" here; rnam par dag pa = pure (rnam par = preverb, not "completely"); glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains; rab tu zhi = completely stilled/pacified (rab tu here carries "completely": the twofold purity is "naturally pure" + "stains fully pacified"); 'dus ma byas = unconditioned; spros pa dang bral ba = free of elaboration; srid zhi mnyam pa nyid = the equality of samsara and nirvana (srid = existence, zhi = peace); ngang = state.
CLAUSES     1 [elided subj = head, Shakyasimha] rang don du chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa — attributive: "as the dharmakaya without transition or change" (chos sku in absolutive, apposed to the subject / or itself subject of bzhugs; see Q)
            2 khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa — verb dag (intr., state) frame ABS khams + INSTR rang bzhin gyis (manner: "by nature"); nominalized + la
            3 glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi — verb zhi (intr., invol.) ABS subj dri ma; perfective/stative
            4 rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa — 'dus byas (tr.) negated, passive-attributive: INSTR rgyu rkyen gyis = agent/means "by causes and conditions"
            5 spros pa dang bral ba — bral (intr.) with dang = from: "free of elaboration"
            6 nam mkha' ltar ... ngang du bzhugs — verb bzhugs (hon. "abide/dwell", intr., vol.) ABS subj = elided head; LA-DON ngang du = locus "in the state"; hon.
RELATIONS   du (rang don du) = purpose/role "for his own benefit" (parallel to gzhan don du in U03)
            gyis (rang bzhin gyis) = manner "by nature"
            la (dag pa la) = locus "in/on (the naturally pure element)" — the stains are pacified in it; alt. connective "and" (Q)
            zhing = coordination ("and")
            gyis (rgyu rkyen gyis) = agent/means of the negated "conditioned"
            dang (spros pa dang bral) = from (with bral)
            ltar = simile "like space"
            kyi (mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang) = modification
            du (ngang du) = locus
            shing = coordination to U02 ("and")
NEG/QUANT   med (the transition/change: none at all); ma ('dus ma byas: not conditioned by causes and conditions); bral (free of elaboration) — three privatives, all kept.
SPINE       English: 2 sentences. (a) for his own benefit he abides as the unchanging dharmakaya, the element pure by nature in which the adventitious stains are fully stilled; (b) unconditioned, free of elaboration, like space, in the equality of samsara and nirvana.
CONSTRUAL   For his own benefit, [he is] the dharmakaya, which has no transition or change. In the element, which is pure by its very nature, the adventitious stains have been completely pacified. [This dharmakaya] is not made up by causes and conditions and is free of elaboration. Like space, he abides in the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana — and ...
TERMS       chos sku = dharmakaya; rang don = his own benefit; khams = the element; glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains; 'dus ma byas = unconditioned; spros pa dang bral ba = free of elaboration; srid zhi = samsara and nirvana; mnyam pa nyid = equality
DOUBTS      Q: "chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa" — subject of bzhugs ("his dharmakaya, unchanging, abides...") or apposition to the elided head ("he, as the dharmakaya, abides"); taken as "he abides as the dharmakaya" (same sense; head stays subject throughout the chain).
            Q: "dag pa la" — taken as locus (in the naturally pure element the stains are pacified: the twofold purity); alt. connective "the element is pure by nature, and the stains are pacified".
            Q: "'dus ma byas pa spros pa dang bral ba" — taken as predicates of the dharmakaya; alt. as modifiers of nam mkha' ("like space, unconditioned and free of elaboration").

UNIT        U05
FORM        prose; homage chain close; the main verb
REGISTER    homage, elevated; the speaker's own act of bowing in first person
SOURCE      mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan te / zhi bde dam pa'i chos kyi char gyis rigs yang dag par sad pa'i gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud brlan te grol bar mdzad pa'i / bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shAkya seng ge la / lus ngag yid gsum gus pas 'dud do
WORDS       bstan (past of ston) = displays, shows; zhi bde = peace and happiness (nirvanic peace + well-being; one compound); dam pa'i chos = the sacred Dharma; char = rain (botok split cha + r — fused); rigs = potential/family (spiritual disposition), yang dag par sad pa = fully awakened (adverbial "truly/fully"); gdul bya = those to be trained (disciples); rgyud = mindstream (MITRA "tantra" is the wrong sense); brlan (fut/caus. of rlan) = moisten, soak; grol bar mdzad = free, liberate (hon. causative); gus pas = with respect (instr. manner); 'dud = bow (Erg.Obl.: obj in la).
CLAUSES     1 bstan: ERG agent = elided head (via the nirmanakaya of U04), obj implicit (himself / his deeds), terminative bcu gnyis su = "as the twelve deeds"; past
            2 brlan: agent elided head; INSTR char gyis = means; ABS obj rgyud
            3 grol bar mdzad: hon. causative "liberates"; obj = the same disciples
            4 'dud: agent = elided first person (the commentator); obj LA shAkya seng ge la; manner gus pas; means lus ngag yid gsum; final particle do = declarative
RELATIONS   su = terminative "as/in the twelve deeds"
            te (bstan te) = sequence/manner: displaying the twelve deeds, [he] ...
            'i (zhi bde dam pa'i chos) = modification
            kyi (chos kyi char) = metaphor genitive "the rain of the Dharma"
            gyis (char gyis) = MEANS of brlan
            par (yang dag par) = adverbial on sad
            pa'i (sad pa'i gdul bya) = relative: disciples whose potential is fully awakened
            kyi (gdul bya rnams kyi rgyud) = genitive
            te (brlan te) = sequence/means: moistening their mindstreams, he frees them
            pa'i (mdzad pa'i bde bar gshegs pa) = relative clause closing the whole chain U01–U05 onto the head
            kyi (gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo) = genitive "chief of the sugatas"
            la (seng ge la) = object of homage
            pas (gus pas) = manner "with respect"
NEG/QUANT   rnams (gdul bya rnams, gshegs pa rnams) = plurals; bcu gnyis = twelve; gsum = the three (body, speech, mind: summary count, carried by listing all three)
SPINE       English: (a) He displays the twelve deeds; (b) with the rain of the sacred Dharma of peace and happiness he moistens the mindstreams of disciples whose potential has fully awakened, and frees them; (c) To him, Shakyasimha, chief of the sugatas, I bow with respect in body, speech and mind.
CONSTRUAL   ... he shows himself in the twelve deeds; then, with the rain of the sacred Dharma that brings peace and happiness, he moistens the mindstreams of those to be trained whose potential has truly awakened, and so liberates them. To that one — Shakyasimha, chief of all the sugatas — I bow with respect of body, speech and mind.
TERMS       mdzad pa bcu gnyis = the twelve deeds; zhi bde = peace and happiness; dam pa'i chos = the sacred Dharma; rigs = potential; gdul bya = disciples (those to be trained); rgyud = mindstream; grol ba = liberate/free; bde bar gshegs pa = sugata; shAkya seng ge = Shakyasimha; lus ngag yid gsum = body, speech and mind
DOUBTS      Q: "zhi bde dam pa'i chos" — "the sacred Dharma of peace and happiness" (zhi bde modifies chos) vs "peace-and-happiness, the sacred Dharma" (apposition); taken as modification.
            Q: "rigs yang dag par sad pa'i" — "whose potential has fully awakened" (yang dag par = fully/truly); alt. "whose potential has been properly awakened" (by him).
            Q: "bstan te" — "displays the twelve deeds" (object = deeds) vs "teaches through the twelve deeds"; taken as display (ston = show), the standard collocation mdzad pa bcu gnyis ston.

## PERIOD B — the citation, U06–U09 (one Tibetan sentence across four stanzas)

UNIT        U06–U09 (one block; per-unit lines below)
FORM        verse, citation; 7-syllable padas: 4 + 4 + 4 + 3 = 15 as given (canonical 16: the citing text lacks D 64b-22 "bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang"); frame "de yang rgyud bla ma las" (U06) … "ces gsungs so" (U09)
GENRE       verse citation (scripture as authority for the twelve deeds just named in U05)
REGISTER    framing (one clause), then the quoted treatise's own doctrinal-narrative voice; plain, measured
CITATION    Uttaratantra (rgyud bla ma), Toh 4024, D 64b-15 to 64b-30 (dm.py identify: VERBATIM yes, canonical, run 42; segmentnr BO_T06_D4024:64b-12ff.). Also in the Vyakhya Toh 4025 and quoted widely; the index lists this commentary itself (BO_EGS_0002:75).
VARIANTS    Citing text omits P "bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang" (skill in the arts) between U07 P3 and P4; reads "dgyes rol ba" for D "dgyes rol pa" (orthographic). Decision: translate the citing text as given (15 padas), note the canonical pada.
SOURCE      P1 thugs rje chen pos 'jig rten mkhyen / P2 'jig rten kun la gzigs nas ni / P3 chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par / P4 sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis // P5 skye ba mngon par skye ba dang / P6 dga' ldan gnas nas 'pho ba dang / P7 lhums su 'jug dang bltams pa dang / P8 btsun mo'i 'khor dgyes rol ba dang // P9 nges 'byung dka' ba spyod pa dang / P10 byang chub snying por gshegs pa dang / P11 bdud sde 'joms dang rdzogs par ni / P12 byang chub chos kyi 'khor lo dang // P13 mya ngan 'das par gshegs mdzad rnams / P14 yongs su ma dag zhing rnams su / P15 srid pa ji srid gnas par ston // ces gsungs so
WORDS       'jig rten mkhyen = the Knower of the World (lokavid, epithet; agent), not a finite verb; gzigs (hon.) = look upon; g.yos = stir, move; sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs = emanations of many kinds (rang bzhin here "form/nature", Skt. citrarupin); skye ba mngon par skye ba = taking birth (cognate object: "is born into births"); 'pho ba = depart (cyuti); lhums su 'jug = enter the womb; bltams pa (hon.) = be born; btsun mo'i 'khor = the circle of queens; dgyes rol = delight and play; nges 'byung = renunciation (going forth); dka' ba spyod pa = austerities; byang chub snying po = the heart of enlightenment (bodhimanda); bdud sde = the hosts of Mara; rdzogs par … byang chub = perfect enlightenment (split across P11/P12 by filler ni); chos kyi 'khor lo = wheel of Dharma; mya ngan 'das par gshegs = pass into nirvana; yongs su ma dag zhing = impure realms (yongs su = preverb pari-, not "completely"); srid pa ji srid gnas par = as long as existence lasts (srid pa = subject of gnas; Skt. a bhavasthiteh).
CLAUSES     1 gzigs (hon. see, tr.; Erg.Obl.): agent 'jig rten mkhyen, means/manner thugs rje chen pos, obj LA 'jig rten kun la; nominal-sequence nas
            2 ma g.yos (intr.): subj same agent; source las; adverbial -par "without stirring"
            3 ston (tr., vol.; Erg.Abs.): agent same (elided after P1), means sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis (P4), obj = the list P5–P13 resumed by "gshegs mdzad rnams" (the deeds), locus zhing rnams su (P14), duration srid pa ji srid gnas par (P15); present, general
            (list items are nominalized deeds; in English each takes a finite verb under "shows how he …")
RELATIONS   P1 -s (chen pos) = means/manner of gzigs ("with great compassion")
            P2 la = object of gzigs; nas = SEQUENCE (having looked, then); ni = filler/topic
            P3 las = source ("from the dharmakaya"); ma = NEG; par = adverbial manner ("without stirring")
            P4 'i = modification; kyis = MEANS of ston (P15)
            P5–P12 dang (×9) = coordination, the list; P6 nas = source ("from Tushita"); P7 su = goal ("into the womb"); P8 'i = genitive; P10 r = goal ("to the heart of enlightenment")
            P11 ni = metrical filler (splits rdzogs par / byang chub)
            P13 par = goal ("into nirvana"); rnams = plural resumptive over the whole list ("these deeds")
            P14 su = locus ("in impure realms")
            P15 ji srid = duration ("as long as"); par = adverbial/terminative; ston = finite verb of the whole citation
            U09 ces = quotation close; gsungs so = "it is said" (hon. speech, the treatise as authority)
NEG/QUANT   ma g.yos (never leaves the dharmakaya while showing the deeds — keep the "without"); ma dag (impure); kun = the whole world; sna tshogs = many kinds; rnams (P13, P14) plurals; no numerals in the verse.
VERSE-NOTES filler: ni (P2, P11); the agent ergative on 'jig rten mkhyen is elided metri causa (it is the agent of gzigs and ston); enumeration P5–P13 is positional content (narrative sequence of the deeds) — order kept; climactic close P14–P15 (where and how long) kept last.
SPINE       one sentence; finite verbs gzigs nas (P2, non-final), ston (P15, final). English: two finite clauses — "the Knower of the World looks upon the whole world, and then … he shows how he …" — with ston brought forward to the head of the list (order: 1-2-3-4-[15 verb]-5…13-14-15), the place and duration closing the block as in the Tibetan.
CONSTRUAL   With great compassion the Knower of the World looks upon the entire world. Then, without moving from the dharmakaya, by means of emanations of many kinds he shows: taking birth, departing from the Tushita abode, entering the womb, being born, delighting in sport amid the circle of queens, renunciation, the practice of austerities, going to the heart of enlightenment, crushing the hosts of Mara, perfect enlightenment, the wheel of Dharma, and passing into nirvana — these deeds he shows in impure realms for as long as existence lasts. So the treatise says.
TERMS       'jig rten mkhyen = the Knower of the World; chos kyi sku = dharmakaya; sprul pa = emanation; dga' ldan = Tushita; byang chub = enlightenment; byang chub snying po = the heart of enlightenment; bdud = Mara (the person, capitalized here; "maras" lowercase as the class, U10); chos kyi 'khor lo = the wheel of Dharma; mya ngan 'das pa = nirvana; ma dag zhing = impure realms; srid pa = existence; rgyud bla ma = *Sublime Continuum* (TITLE, coined/conventional short title)
DOUBTS      Q: "skye ba mngon par skye ba" — taken as one item, "takes birth after birth" (birth as object of "is born": the series of births); alt. per the Sanskrit as two items (former births, jataka; and rebirth, upapatti, in Tushita).
            Q: "'jig rten mkhyen" — taken as the epithet Knower of the World (agent); alt. finite "with great compassion he knows the world" (a clause break the grammar does not mark).
            Q: missing canonical pada "bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang" (skill in the arts): not translated, since the citing text lacks it; the editor may restore it ("is skilled in the arts") to complete the twelve.
            Q: "srid pa ji srid gnas par" — taken as "as long as existence lasts" (srid pa subject of gnas); alt. "he shows himself remaining as long as existence lasts".

Per-unit split of the verse (for the draft and the check): U06 = frame + P1–P4; U07 = P5–P8 (with the verb "shows" brought forward from P15); U08 = P9–P12; U09 = P13–P15 + closing frame (folded into the opening formula).

## PERIOD C — the commitment to explain, U10–U12

UNIT        U10

SOURCE      gnyis pa ni / phung po dang 'chi bdag dang lha'i bu dang nyon mongs pa ste bdud bzhi las rgyal ba'i thub pa mchog gi gsung rab / rtag chad kyi mtha' dang bral ba / rgya mtsho'i klong ltar dpag tu med pa las / mdo sde dang rgyud sde rnams dang / klu sgrub dang thogs med la sogs pa'i bstan bcos dang / chos rje 'bri gung pa chen po'i man ngag zab mo'i don / klu rgyal gyi gtsug gi nor bu ltar bkur 'os pa'i nang nas / ma rtogs par dang log par rtog pa dang the tshom gyi dri ma med par bsdus pa 'di ni / rin chen rnams kyi mchog baidurya la gsol ba btab na dgos 'dod thams cad 'byung ba bzhin / gnas skabs dang mthar thug gi don thams cad 'grub par byed pa'i bstan bcos / gzhi dang lam dang 'bras bu gsum du bstan pa'i snying po / bshad par bya'o
