# rays.style.md — Pass 3 (seasoned practitioner) — the text under check (U01–U12)

### U01
For his own benefit he is the dharmakaya, which neither moves nor changes. In the element, pure by nature, the adventitious stains are completely pacified. Unconditioned by causes and conditions and free of elaboration, he abides like space in the equality of samsara and nirvana,

### U02
and he is endowed with qualities such as the ten powers and the four fearlessnesses.

### U03
For the benefit of others, he appears as the sambhogakaya endowed with the five certainties, the foremost guide of his retinues of bodhisattvas, the Victor's regents,

### U04
and from the cloud of his great, loving compassion, his supreme nirmanakaya pervades the realms of beings without partiality.

### U05
He shows the twelve deeds, and with the rain of the sacred Dharma of peace and happiness he moistens the mindstreams of disciples whose potential has fully awakened, and so sets them free. To Shakyasimha, chief of the sugatas, I bow with respect in body, speech and mind.

### U06
Accordingly, in the *Sublime Continuum* it is said:

With great compassion the Knower of the World
looks upon the whole world,
and then, not stirring from the dharmakaya,
through emanations of many kinds

### U07
he shows how he takes birth,
departs from the Tushita heaven,
enters the womb and is born,
delights in the circle of his queens,

### U08
renounces the world, practices austerities,
goes to the heart of enlightenment,
defeats the hosts of Mara,
attains perfect enlightenment,
turns the wheel of Dharma,

### U09
and passes into nirvana —
all this in impure realms
for as long as existence lasts.

### U10
Second: The supreme Sage has conquered the four maras — the aggregates, the lord of death, the son of the gods and the afflictions. His scriptures are free of the extremes of permanence and annihilation, and as immeasurable as the ocean's expanse. This text draws on the sutras and tantras among them, on the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga and others, and on the meaning of the profound pith instructions of the great Drikungpa, Lord of Dharma, which deserve veneration like the jewel on a naga king's crown. It condenses them without the stains of not understanding, misunderstanding and doubt. Just as everything one needs and wishes for comes forth when one prays to the beryl, supreme among jewels, so it is a treatise that accomplishes every aim, temporary and ultimate. It is the Essence, set out in three parts — ground, path and fruition — and I shall explain it.

### U11
Now, of the four points — the purpose and the rest — the subject matter is threefold: ground, path and fruition.

### U12
The purpose is to understand it through study and reflection. The ultimate purpose is that, through meditation on its meaning, the fruition becomes manifest. As for the connection, each relies on the one before it, and in this way they are connected step by step, as a means and what arises from that means.
