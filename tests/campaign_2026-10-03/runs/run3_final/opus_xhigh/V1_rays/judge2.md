## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U07 "takes birth" for skye ba mngon par skye ba — the first deed (former births / higher birth) is lost and the line duplicates "is born"; dictionary sense (the Sanskrit pairing was known to the construal but the simpler reading was taken).
2. U10 "which deserve veneration like the jewel on a naga king's crown" narrowed to Drikungpa's instructions — bkur 'os pa'i nang nas covers all listed sources; construal fork (nearest-noun attachment chosen).
3. U01 the sentence split detaches "the element, pure by nature" from the dharmakaya — the identification at the heart of the homage is weakened; omission under compression / sentence splitting.
4. U09 "all this in impure realms" left verbless after ston was moved to U07 — the citation's close loses its predicate; verse constraint (line reordering).
5. U10 closing sentence "It is the Essence … and I shall explain it" — the commitment, the period's only finite verb, lands weakly as an afterthought; register (long-period splitting).
