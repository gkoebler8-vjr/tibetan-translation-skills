## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U09: the citation's finite verb ston was hoisted to U07, which leaves "all this in impure realms / for as long as existence lasts" as a verbless tail; "yongs su" (utterly) is also dropped. Cause: verse constraint.
2. U07: "he shows how he takes birth" collapses skye ba mngon par skye ba (former births plus birth in Tushita) into a single item that duplicates "is born". Cause: construal fork (recorded, but resolved toward the weaker reading).
3. U10: "It condenses them" makes the text the agent of a present action, where the Tibetan has a past participle (bsdus pa 'di, condensed by the author). Cause: omission under compression (from splitting the period).
4. U10: the crown-jewel simile is hung on Drikungpa's instructions alone, though bkur 'os pa'i nang nas most naturally qualifies all the listed sources. Cause: construal fork (nearest-noun default).
5. U01/U08: stiff or calqued spots: "he is the dharmakaya, which neither moves nor changes" (with ngang absorbed) and "the heart of enlightenment". Cause: register / term choice.
