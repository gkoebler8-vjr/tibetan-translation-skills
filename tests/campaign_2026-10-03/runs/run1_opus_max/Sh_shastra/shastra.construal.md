# shastra — construal file (Pass 1). Bṛhaṭṭīkā (Toh 3808), introduction 1.197–1.206
Brief: academic · publication · IAST · [ ] interpolations · Q/R/Qualm marked as the Tibetan marks it · neutral-scholarly · notes in apparatus.
Dialectic markers (this page): `... zhe na` closes an opponent's question/qualm; `'dir smras pa` opens the reply; `yang na` = alternative reply.
Label policy (decided U01, revised at U05): `zhe na` closing an opponent's why/how request = **Question:**; `zhe na` closing an opponent's fault-claim = **Qualm:**; `'dir smras pa` = **Response:**; no label is added where the Tibetan has none — replies without `smras pa` keep their own openers (`yang na` Alternatively · `de ni 'di ltar gzung bar bya ste` This should be understood as follows · `yang` Again/But); a self-posed `ci'i phyir zhe na` inside a reply = "If one asks why".

FORM        prose; lemma (zhes bya ba la) + opponent's question closed by zhe na
GENRE       commentary lemma + debate
REGISTER    impersonal scholastic objection; neutral
SOURCE      (1) de'i tshe bcom ldan 'das kyis tshe dang ldan pa sha ra dwa ti'i bu la bka' stsal pa zhes bya ba la/
            (2) der mang po thams cad lhags na gcig pu la ci'i phyir bka' stsal/
            (3) de bzhin gshegs pa ni bdag gi mdun na bzhugs te chos 'chad do snyam du sems so zhes gong du smos pa gang yin pa de dang yang 'gal lo//

            · du zin na = "if [it be the case that] V" (counterfactual/granted condition) · 'khor = assembly (parṣad)
CLAUSES     (1) lemma: verb=bka' stsal (hon. speak) ERG bcom ldan 'das kyis, LA-DON recipient sha ra dwa ti'i bu; past; frame zhes bya ba la = "regarding the words ..."
            (2) verb=lhags intr., subj mang po thams cad, na = temporal/concessive "when/given that"; verb=bka' stsal (hon.), agent elided = Bhagavat, LA gcig pu = recipient; interrogative ci'i phyir

RELATIONS   zhes bya ba la = lemma frame · lhags na = given/when · nang nas = source "from among" · nas (gzigs nas) = sequence/manner · na (sems na) = condition · gal te ... na = condition · de'i tshe = "then, in that case" (apodosis marker, not "at that time")
NEG/QUANT   mang po thams cad = many, all of them · gcig pu = one alone (restrictive) · thams cad (×2) · ji ltar mi 'gyur = rhetorical negation → "how would it not" (= it would)

CONSTRUAL   Regarding the [sūtra's] words "At that time the Blessed One addressed the venerable Śāradvatīputra": [the opponent asks:] When so many — all of them — had come there, why did he address only one person? And [addressing one] also contradicts what was stated above: "[Each] thinks, 'The Tathāgata is seated in front of me and is teaching the Dharma.'" If, moreover, all who were gathered there think, "The Tathāgata looked at me alone and taught the Dharma," then, if the Blessed One singled out one among all of them and addressed him, how would that not contradict the thoughts of the assembly? — so it is asked.
TERMS       bcom ldan 'das = the Blessed One · tshe dang ldan pa = venerable · sha ra dwa ti'i bu = Śāradvatīputra (kept distinct from shA ri'i bu = Śāriputra) · de bzhin gshegs pa = the Tathāgata · chos = the Dharma · bka' stsal = address, speak (hon.) · 'khor = assembly · 'gal = contradict
CITATION    lemma: stock sūtra formula; dm identify VERBATIM yes; 19-syl run only in Toh 3808 itself (BO_T03_D3808:33a-22 = this passage); Toh 9 / Toh 8 / Toh 90 / Toh 181 have the formula with de nas ("then") (run 17). Quoted earlier statement: VERBATIM yes, 18-syl run only in Toh 3808 (3 hits) → refers back to an earlier passage of this commentary; not in the sūtras as worded (cf. motif in Toh 119, run 7 only).
DOUBTS      Q: gong du smos pa — "stated above" in this commentary (verbatim only in Toh 3808) vs. in the sūtra's opening; rendered neutrally "stated above".
            Q: bdag gcig pu'i ngor gzigs nas — "looked at me alone" vs "with me alone in view / for my sake alone".
            Q: mang po thams cad — "so many, all of them" vs "the whole multitude".
            Q: snyam du yang sems na — yang "also" (adds a second fact) vs "even".

UNIT        U02 (1.198)
FORM        prose; reply opened by 'dir smras pa
GENRE       debate (response)
REGISTER    impersonal exposition; neutral
SOURCE      (1) 'dir smras pa/
            (2) de bzhin gshegs pas ni thams cad las brtsams nas thams cad mngon du mdzad de bshad mod kyi/
            (3) 'on kyang shA ri'i bu la bka' stsal pa'i skad cig la thams cad la yang bka' stsal par 'gyur te/
            (4) bshad pa'i skad cig de la thams cad la yang bshad pa tha dad pa rnam pa sna tshogs mdzad na/ yang dag par sdud par byed pa rnams kyis rim pa de yang dag par sdud mi nus pa'i phyir/
            (5) bshad pa'i ye shes kyi mdzes brjid bzhag ste/ skye bo thams cad la 'os pa'i rim pa gcig bzung nas yang dag par bsdus so//
WORDS       las brtsams (nas/te) = with reference to (ārabhya) · mngon du mdzad = make directly present / perceive directly (sākṣāt-kṛ) · mod kyi = concessive "it is true that ..., but"
            · yang dag par sdud par byed pa = the compilers (saṃgītikāra) · rim pa = sequence, order · ye shes = gnosis (jñāna) · mdzes brjid = splendor
            · bzhag (past of 'jog) = set aside, leave aside · 'os pa = suited to, fit for · bzung (past of 'dzin) = adopt, take up
            · botok slip: "rim pa de yang dag par sdud" mis-split as rim pa / de yang / dag pa r — read rim pa de / yang dag par / sdud
CLAUSES     (1) smras pa: response formula, speaker = the commentator ("we")
            (2) bshad (past, teach) ERG de bzhin gshegs pas; manner: thams cad las brtsams nas; de: thams cad mngon du mdzad (hon. do); mod kyi = concession
            (3) 'gyur (comes to be): content = thams cad la yang bka' stsal pa (addressing all too); time = shA ri'i bu la bka' stsal pa'i skad cig la; te → continues
            (4) mdzad (hon. make) agent elided = Tathāgata; obj bshad pa tha dad pa rnam pa sna tshogs; LA thams cad la yang; time bshad pa'i skad cig de la; na = when/if
                sdud mi nus (unable to compile) ERG yang dag par sdud par byed pa rnams kyis, obj rim pa de; phyir = because
            (5) bzhag (set aside) agent = compilers (elided, from 4), obj bshad pa'i ye shes kyi mdzes brjid; ste → bzung (adopt) obj rim pa gcig (modifier: skye bo thams cad la 'os pa'i); nas → bsdus so (compiled; past, final)
RELATIONS   mod kyi = concession · 'on kyang = adversative "nevertheless" · skad cig la = temporal locus · te = sequence/elaboration · na = condition (factual "when") · phyir = cause → "for that reason" · ste = sequence · nas = sequence "having adopted ..., compiled"
NEG/QUANT   thams cad ×5 (all of them / everyone / all people) · yang "too, as well" ×2 · sdud mi nus = could not compile · rim pa gcig = a single sequence
SPINE       response label; concession sentence; "however" sentence; causal sentence (na + phyir) → result sentence (set aside, adopted, compiled)
CONSTRUAL   Response: It is true that the Tathāgata taught with reference to all of them, making all of them directly present [to himself]. Nevertheless, in the instant he addressed Śāriputra, he came to be addressing everyone as well. When, in that instant of teaching, he also gave everyone different explanations of many kinds, the compilers were not able to compile that sequence. Because of this, they set aside the splendor of the gnosis in [his] teaching, adopted one sequence suited to all people, and compiled [the teaching accordingly].
TERMS       las brtsams = with reference to · yang dag par sdud par byed pa = the compilers · yang dag par sdud = compile · rim pa = sequence · ye shes = gnosis · mdzes brjid = splendor · bshad pa = teaching / explanation (verbal noun of 'chad)
DOUBTS      Q: thams cad mngon du mdzad de — "making all of them directly present [to himself]" vs "perceiving all of them directly" vs "manifesting everything".
            Q: mdzad na — factual "when he gave" (taken) vs hypothetical "if he were to give".
            Q: bzhag — "set aside, left out" (taken) vs "laid down, established" (would make the compilers record the splendor; contradicts "could not compile").
            Q: bshad pa'i ye shes kyi mdzes brjid — "the splendor of [his] gnosis in teaching" vs "the splendor of the teaching and of [his] gnosis".

UNIT        U03 (1.199)
FORM        prose; alternative reply (yang na)
GENRE       debate (second response)
REGISTER    impersonal exposition; neutral
SOURCE      (1) yang na de bzhin gshegs pas ni shA ri'i bu nyid las brtsams nas bstan pas/ tshul 'di nyid dam pa'i chos yun ring du gnas pa'i rgyu yin pa'i phyir de bzhin gshegs pas kyang rjes su gnang ngo//
            (2) de bas na yang dag par sdud par byed pa rnams kyis kyang 'di nyid la brten nas yang dag par bsdus so//
WORDS       yang na = alternatively · nyid (after a name) = emphatic "specifically / himself" · tshul = approach, way, method · dam pa'i chos = the true Dharma (saddharma)
            · yun ring du gnas pa = remain for a long time · rjes su gnang = sanction, authorize (anujñā) · brten nas = relying on
CLAUSES     (1a) bstan (past, teach) ERG de bzhin gshegs pas ni; manner shA ri'i bu nyid las brtsams nas; pas → links to (1c)
            (1b) rgyu yin (is the cause) subj tshul 'di nyid; of: dam pa'i chos yun ring du gnas pa; phyir = because
            (1c) rjes su gnang (hon. permit/sanction) ERG de bzhin gshegs pas kyang; obj elided = [this approach]; final ngo
            (2) bsdus (past, compiled) ERG yang dag par sdud par byed pa rnams kyis kyang; nas ← brten (rely) obj LA 'di nyid la; obj of bsdus elided = [the teaching]
RELATIONS   pas (bstan pas) = means/cause: "by teaching ... / since he taught ..." → taken as means of the sanction · phyir = cause of the sanction · kyang (×2) = also / too (Tathāgata also; compilers too) · de bas na = therefore · nas = sequence/manner
NEG/QUANT   none; restrictive-emphatic nyid ×3 (Śāriputra specifically; this very approach; just this)
SPINE       2 sentences → English 2–3: alternative + sanction with its reason; therefore-sentence
CONSTRUAL   Alternatively: by teaching with reference to Śāriputra specifically, the Tathāgata also sanctioned [this approach], because this very approach is a cause for the true Dharma to remain for a long time. Therefore the compilers too relied on just this and compiled [the teaching].
TERMS       yang na = Alternatively · dam pa'i chos = the true Dharma · rjes su gnang = sanction · tshul = approach · las brtsams = with reference to
DOUBTS      Q: bstan pas — means "by teaching ... he sanctioned" (taken) vs cause "since he taught ..., [and because] ..., he also sanctioned it".
            Q: rjes su gnang — "sanctioned, authorized" vs "permitted".
            Q: first kyang — "also" (besides teaching so, he sanctioned it) vs "himself"; parallels the compilers' kyang ("too").

UNIT        U05 (1.201)
FORM        prose; reply (self-marked) with internal self-question (ci'i phyir zhe na) and a closing scriptural reference ('og nas ... zhes 'byung ngo)
GENRE       debate (response) / exposition
REGISTER    impersonal exposition; neutral
SOURCE      (1) de ni 'di ltar gzung bar bya ste/
            (2) shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa bshad pa ni spyir bshad pa yin gyi/ byang chub sems dpa' rnams nyid kyi don du bshad pa ni ma yin no//
            (3) ci'i phyir zhe na/ shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa ni thams cad shes pa nyid dang/ lam gyi rnam pa shes pa nyid dang/ rnam pa thams cad mkhyen pa nyid dang/ sgo gsum du bzhugs pa'i phyir ro//
            (4) de la gal te byang chub sems dpa' gang yang rung ba cig la bcom ldan 'das kyis bka' stsal te skabs phyer zin na/ 'khor gzhan dag zab mo 'di ni bdag cag gi spyod yul ma yin no snyam nas 'bad par mi byed kyang srid kyi/
            (5) shA ri'i bu las brtsams te skabs phye na ni bshad pa 'di skye bo thams cad dang thun mong yin te/ shin tu zab pa ma yin no snyam du gzhan dag sems par 'gyur ro//
            (6) de bas na shA ri'i bu nyid las brtsams te skabs phye'o//
            (7) 'di ltar 'og nas kyang dge slong rnams dang/ 'khor gzhan dag gis kyang chos mngon par rtogs nas so sor thob po zhes 'byung ngo//
WORDS       gzung bar bya = is to be understood/taken · spyir = in general · yin gyi ... ma yin = is X, not Y (gyi adversative) · don du = for the sake of
            · thams cad shes pa nyid = all-knowledge (sarvajñatā) · lam gyi rnam pa shes pa nyid = knowledge of the aspects of the path (mārgākārajñatā) · rnam pa thams cad mkhyen pa nyid = knowledge of all aspects (sarvākārajñatā; hon. mkhyen); nyid here = abstract -tā, not emphatic
            · sgo = gateway (MITRA "sgo gsum = body, speech, mind" is the wrong sense here) · bzhugs = hon. abide · gang yang rung ba cig = any ... whatever · spyod yul = domain (gocara)
            · 'bad pa = effort · srid = is possible, might · thun mong = common, shared · mngon par rtogs = clearly realize (abhisamaya) · so sor thob = attain individually (or so sor = prati- in pratilabh "obtain") · 'byung = it occurs, it says
CLAUSES     (1) bya (fut./oblig.) gzung bar: "is to be understood thus"; subj de = the matter just asked
            (2) yin (copula) subj shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa bshad pa, pred spyir bshad pa; gyi → ma yin (neg. copula) pred byang chub sems dpa' rnams nyid kyi don du bshad pa
            (3) self-question ci'i phyir zhe na; answer: bzhugs (hon., abide) subj shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa, LA-DON sgo gsum du; apposition: the three knowledges listed with dang ×3; phyir ro = because
            (4) de la = in that light; protasis gal te ... zin na: ERG bcom ldan 'das kyis, bka' stsal (LA byang chub sems dpa' gang yang rung ba cig la) te skabs phyer (opened); apodosis: 'khor gzhan dag (subj) snyam (think, quote: zab mo 'di ni bdag cag gi spyod yul ma yin no) nas 'bad par mi byed (not strive) kyang srid (might well); kyi = but
            (5) protasis shA ri'i bu las brtsams te skabs phye na ni (agent elided = Bhagavat); apodosis: gzhan dag sems par 'gyur (will think), quote: bshad pa 'di skye bo thams cad dang thun mong yin te/ shin tu zab pa ma yin no
            (6) skabs phye (past, opened), agent elided = Bhagavat, manner shA ri'i bu nyid las brtsams te; de bas na = therefore
            (7) 'byung (occurs, it says) loc 'og nas kyang; quoted: ERG dge slong rnams dang 'khor gzhan dag gis kyang → rtogs (realize) obj chos, adv mngon par; nas → thob po (attained), adv so sor, obj elided
RELATIONS   ste (1) = introduces the explanation · gyi (2) = contrast "not" · zhe na (3) = self-posed question → "if one asks why" · phyir (3) = cause · de la (4) = application "in that light" · gal te ... na (4) = counterfactual condition · nas (4) = sequence-cause (thinking that, make no effort) · kyang srid = "might well" · kyi (4) = adversative "but" · na ni (5) = condition, contrastive topic · te (5) = coordination inside the quote · de bas na (6) = therefore · 'di ltar (7) = evidential "thus, accordingly" · kyang ×2 (7) = also/too · nas (7) = sequence
NEG/QUANT   ma yin (2) scopes "for the bodhisattvas in particular" · ma yin (4, quote) "not our domain" · mi byed (4) "make no effort" · ma yin (5, quote) "not extremely profound" (shin tu scoped by the negation) · gang yang rung ba cig = any one at all · thams cad (skye bo thams cad) · rnams = monks (plural)
SPINE       7 sentences → English ~8; keep conditional pair (4)/(5) contrastive; scriptural report last
CONSTRUAL   This is to be understood as follows. The explanation of the prajñāpāramitā is a general explanation; it is not an explanation for the sake of the bodhisattvas in particular. If one asks why: because the prajñāpāramitā abides in three gateways — all-knowledge, knowledge of the aspects of the path, and knowledge of all aspects. In that light, had the Blessed One addressed any bodhisattva at all and opened the discourse [with him], the rest of the assembly might well have thought, "This profound [teaching] is not our domain," and made no effort. But if he opens the discourse with reference to Śāriputra, the others will think, "This explanation is common to all people; it is not extremely profound." Therefore he opened the discourse with reference to Śāriputra specifically. Accordingly, below too it is said: "The monks and the rest of the assembly too clearly realized the Dharma and attained [the fruits] individually."
TERMS       shes rab kyi pha rol tu phyin pa = the prajñāpāramitā · spyir bshad pa = general explanation · thams cad shes pa nyid = all-knowledge · lam gyi rnam pa shes pa nyid = knowledge of the aspects of the path · rnam pa thams cad mkhyen pa nyid = knowledge of all aspects · sgo = gateway · 'khor gzhan dag = the rest of the assembly · spyod yul = domain · zab mo = profound · thun mong = common · dge slong = monk · mngon par rtogs = clearly realize
CITATION    (7) dm identify: VERBATIM yes but 18-syl run only in Toh 3808 itself (BO_T03_D3808:34a-1) → wording is the commentator's report of "below"; source not located in the sūtras as worded.
            Same argument with Subhūti in Toh 3805 (BO_T03_D3805:7b-14–20) and Toh 3811 (D 13a-9); Toh 3805 7b-20: "... dge slong rnams dang 'khor gzhan gyi chos mngon par rtogs pa thob pas so" ("attained clear realization of the Dharma").
DOUBTS      Q: quote boundary in (5) — thought = "This explanation is common to all people; it is not extremely profound" (taken; parallel to the deictic zab mo 'di in (4)) vs the commentator asserting "this explanation is common to all people" and the others thinking only "it is not extremely profound" (the Toh 3805 parallel admits both).
            Q: so sor thob po — "attained [the fruits] individually" vs so sor = prati- (pratilabh): "obtained [clear realization]" (cf. Toh 3805 chos mngon par rtogs pa thob).
            Q: sgo gsum du bzhugs — "abides in three gateways" vs "abides as three gateways / through three avenues".
            Q: de la (4) — "in that light" (application) vs "in that case".
