### U02

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose (self-marked reply; self-posed question ci'i phyir zhe na; report of a later sūtra passage) · exposition/debate response, neutral-scholarly · academic · reported passage: source not located as worded (verbatim only in Toh 3808); same argument, with Subhūti, in Toh 3805 and Toh 3811
TEXT:
This should be understood as follows. The explanation of the *prajñāpāramitā* is a general explanation; it is not an explanation for the sake of the bodhisattvas in particular. If one asks why: because the prajñāpāramitā abides in three gateways—all-knowledge, knowledge of the aspects of the path, and knowledge of all aspects. In this context, had the Blessed One addressed any bodhisattva at all and opened the discourse [with him], the rest of the assembly might well have thought, “This profound [teaching] is not our domain,” and so made no effort. But if he opens the discourse with reference to Śāriputra, the others will think, “This explanation is common to all people; it is not extremely profound.” Therefore he opened the discourse with reference to Śāriputra specifically. Accordingly, it is also said further on: “The monks and the rest of the assembly, too, clearly realized the Dharma and then individually attained [the fruits].”
NOTES:
Q: quote boundary: the others' thought is taken as "This explanation is common to all people; it is not extremely profound" (parallel to "This profound [teaching]" before it); alt. the commentator asserts "this explanation is common to all people" and the others think only "it is not extremely profound".
Q: so sor thob po: "individually attained [the fruits]"; alt. so sor rendering Skt. prati- (pratilabh): "obtained [clear realization]", cf. Toh 3805 chos mngon par rtogs pa thob.
Q: sgo gsum du bzhugs: "abides in three gateways"; alt. "abides as three gateways / through three avenues".
Q: de la: "In this context"; alt. "in that case".
Alt: the three gateways are the three knowledges: all-knowledge (thams cad shes pa nyid, sarvajñatā), knowledge of the aspects of the path (lam gyi rnam pa shes pa nyid, mārgākārajñatā), knowledge of all aspects (rnam pa thams cad mkhyen pa nyid, sarvākārajñatā); nyid here is the abstract suffix (-tā).
Alt: spyod yul = gocara, "domain"; chos mngon par rtogs = dharmābhisamaya, "clearly realized the Dharma".
Issue: no response label added; the reply is self-marked (de ni 'di ltar gzung bar bya ste), and the internal ci'i phyir zhe na is rendered "If one asks why".
Source: dm.py identify on the reported passage: 18-syllable verbatim run only in Toh 3808 (D 34a), not in the sūtras as worded. Parallel argument with Subhūti in Toh 3805 (D 7b14–20, ending "... dge slong rnams dang 'khor gzhan gyi chos mngon par rtogs pa thob pas so") and Toh 3811 (D 13a).
Check: in-context: 2 minor; nas restored as cause ("and so made no effort") and as sequence ("and then individually attained").
