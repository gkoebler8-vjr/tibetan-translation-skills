### U01
HEADER: U01 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4 after a prose speaker line, four lines, order 1-2-3-4 · dialogue, respectful question (Vajragarbha to the Blessed One) · seasoned practitioner · Toh 381, Emergence from Samputa ch. 1 (84000 1.14); root text, not a citation
TEXT:
Vajragarbha asked:
Within the eye, whose nature is empty,
how does consciousness exist?
Within the ear, whose nature is empty,
how does consciousness exist?
NOTES:
Q: mig gi rang bzhin stong pa is read as "the eye, whose nature is empty"; it can also be "the empty nature of the eye", which puts consciousness within the nature rather than the eye. The same frame runs through U02–U03.
Q: ji ltar lags may be skeptical (how could consciousness be there at all?), which the "therefore … no" of U04–U05 supports; rendered as a plain "how" question.
Issue: lags is the polite copula (Vajragarbha addressing the Blessed One); carried by register, not by an added word.

### U04

TEXT:
There is therefore no form and no one who sees,
no sound and no one who hears,
no smell and no one who smells,
no taste and no one who tastes;
NOTES:
Q: lta ba is taken as "one who sees" (short for lta ba po, by parallel with the agent nouns that follow); it can mean "seeing".
Q: no speaker marker here; read as Vajragarbha drawing the consequence of his questions, since the Blessed One's reply is introduced only at U06.
Q: myong ba po, literally "experiencer", is rendered "one who tastes" in the taste slot.
Issue: agent nouns rendered "no one who sees / hears / smells / tastes", since "smeller" and "toucher" do not work as English; yang ("also, either") is carried by "and no".
Check: in-context; 1 minor (yang not visible as a word): noted here, body unchanged.

### U06
HEADER: U06 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4 after the speaker line (itself seven syllables, set as prose), four lines, order 2-1-3-4 with padas 1 and 2 interleaved · answer: instruction (listen) then statement, the Blessed One to Vajragarbha · seasoned practitioner · Toh 381, Emergence from Samputa ch. 1 (84000 1.19); root text, not a citation
TEXT:
The Blessed One said:
Listen to the path of nondual reality
as it is, wholly rid of the two.
It is free of reasoning, free of consciousness;
likewise, logicians do not realize it.
NOTES:
Issue: the speaker introduction is seven syllables and closed like a pada; set as a prose line per the brief.
Q: de nyid lam is read as "the path of reality" (genitive elided), not "reality, the path". Gnyis med is taken with reality ("the path of nondual reality") but may qualify the path; "wholly rid of the two" can go with either.
Q: ji bzhin nyon: "listen to it as it is" (taken) or "listen properly, accurately".
Q: line 3 is read as describing the path (free of reasoning and of consciousness); it can also be a general statement ("there is no reasoning and no consciousness").
Issue: the echo rtog ge / rtog ge pa / rtogs (reasoning, logicians, realize) is lost.
Alt: de bzhin, "likewise"; "accordingly" also fits.
Check: in-context; 2 minor: the doubt was reworded to match the body's attachment of "nondual", and glossary rows were added (rnam spangs, ji bzhin, de bzhin).

### U07

### U08
HEADER: U08 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 1-2-4-3 · doctrinal statement · seasoned practitioner · Toh 381, Emergence from Samputa ch. 1 (84000 1.21); root text, not a citation
TEXT:
Whatever wisdom comes from awakened tendencies
is an emanation of pratyekabuddhas.
None of them know the secret,
the very domain of the buddhas.
NOTES:
Q: bag chags sad pa'i ye shes: "wisdom that comes from awakened tendencies" (taken) or "wisdom that is the awakening of tendencies".
Q: sprul pa is kept as "emanation"; the sense may be "a mere projection" of the pratyekabuddhas, set against the uncreated secret.
Q: de rnams kun ("none of them") is taken as the shravakas and the pratyekabuddhas; it may mean the pratyekabuddhas alone.
Q: nyid in line 3 is taken as emphatic ("the very domain"); it may be restrictive ("the domain of the buddhas alone").
Check: in-context; no finding.

### U09
HEADER: U09 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 2-1-4-3; the sentence runs on into U10 · exposition (inner yoga: the crossing) · seasoned practitioner · Toh 381, Emergence from Samputa ch. 1 (84000 1.22); root text, not a citation
TEXT:
The mind that sets out to cross
from the terrors of samsara's ocean
is said to indicate the crossing
that circles at the body's center;
NOTES:
Q: the mind is taken as the agent of "indicate", by parallel with U10. Two other readings are possible: the mind circles the crossing in the body's center, or the crossing is indicated through the mind.
Q: "the crossing that circles at the body's center" (relative clause, taken) or "the crossing made by circling there"; what circles (wind, consciousness) is not stated.
Q: rgal bar 'gro ba'i sems: "the mind that sets out to cross" (taken) or "the mind of beings who would cross".
Check: in-context; 1 minor: glossary row added for rgal bar 'gro ba.

### U10
HEADER: U10 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 1-2-3-4 · exposition turning to instruction at line 3 · seasoned practitioner · Toh 381, Emergence from Samputa ch. 1 (84000 1.23); root text, not a citation
TEXT:
the mind that is consciousness and wisdom
indicates the mark and the marked.
With wisdom, look at the knowable;
through the knowables, examine the destinies.
NOTES:
Q: rnam shes ye shes sems kyis: one agent, "the mind that is consciousness and wisdom" (taken), or three, "consciousness, wisdom and mind".
Q: blta and brtag are future stems, read as instructions and rendered as imperatives. A descriptive reading, with the mind as continuing subject, is possible.
Q: 'gro ba is taken as the destinies of rebirth; "beings" is possible.
Issue: the register shifts to instruction at line 3. Mtshan nyid / mtshan gzhi are rendered "the mark and the marked" (defining characteristic and its basis), keeping their common root.
Check: in-context; no finding.

### U12
HEADER: U12 · verse: citation mode, rising, 3–4 beats; padas 7 × 4, four lines, order 1-2-4-3 · instruction, third person (the yogi) · seasoned practitioner · Toh 381, Emergence from Samputa ch. 1 (84000 1.25); root text, not a citation
TEXT:
Once the yogi has contemplated as empty
the mark of the mark taught before,
the wise one should sow the seed
in the body's empty field and the rest.
NOTES:
Q: lus kyi stong pa'i zhing: "the body's empty field" (taken) or "the field of the body's emptiness".
Q: zhing sogs ("the field and the rest") may name the sacred sites (kshetra and the others) located in the body.
Q: sa bon gdab: "sow the seed" (field image kept) or "set the seed syllable".
Q: sngar gsungs mtshan nyid kyi mtshan nyid: "the mark of the mark taught before" (taken; it points back to U10) or plural "marks".
Issue: mtshan nyid = "mark" as in U10. The two agent expressions (yo gis, blo dang ldan pas) are kept as "the yogi … the wise one".
Check: in-context; no finding.
