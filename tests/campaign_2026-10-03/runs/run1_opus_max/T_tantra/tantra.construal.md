NEG/QUANT   none

SPINE       intro line (prose) + 2 questions, each across 2 padas; finite verb lags at end of p2 and p4
CONSTRUAL   Vajragarbha asked: Within the eye, whose nature is empty, how is consciousness there (how does it exist)? Within the ear, whose nature is empty, how is consciousness there?
TERMS       rdo rje snying po = Vajragarbha · gsol ba = asked (speech to a superior) · mig = eye · rna ba = ear · rang bzhin = nature ·
            stong pa = empty · rnam shes = consciousness (CON-scious-ness, /..) · nang du = within · ji ltar lags = how does ... exist / how is there
CITATION    n/a (root text)
DOUBTS      Q: mig gi rang bzhin stong pa: "the eye, whose nature is empty" (nominalized clause, head = eye) or "the empty nature of the eye" (stong pa attributive to rang bzhin; consciousness then sits within the nature); taken as the first.
