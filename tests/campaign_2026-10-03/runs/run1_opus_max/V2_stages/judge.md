## Top 5 problems in C, ranked by damage, each with a one-line diagnosis of the likely cause
1. U12 "Then comes liberation, and in the great ocean of liberation …": splits a single locative frame into a sequential event and loosens the topic–predicate (ni) structure. Cause: construal fork (resolved toward the U04–U06 mirror).
2. U08 "it ripens into its result": shifts the subject of smin pa from the persons to the cause. Cause: construal fork / grammar misread of the nominalized smin pa + rang nyid.
3. U07 "practices that are not the same but differ from one another": redundant pair carried over from mi gcig pa tha dad pa. Cause: register (translationese).
4. U02 "the innermost room": imposes a fixed house image on phug before the text sets it up. Cause: term choice.
5. U11 "Wisdom enhances method's roots of virtue": clumsy possessive; the couplet is only roughly metred. Cause: verse constraint.
