# stages — Pass 2 faithful draft (from stages.construal.md)

### U01
The Blessed One taught all three precious trainings in various ways, with no fixed order for which of them comes first.

### U02
You may wonder, "If that is so, what is the use of this, when it has become a ground for disputes between all sorts of conflicting positions?" As was said above, the Blessed One taught eighty-four thousand doors of Dharma. Is it not so that you enter by one of these doors and go all the way to the innermost room?

### U03
Is the door not the mind's way in? By entering whatever is the mind's way in,

### U04
you completely give up afflictions and conceptual thoughts: that is the training in higher discipline.

### U05
By entering that door, you reach the house of liberation inside it: that is the training in higher samadhi.

### U06
In the innermost room behind that door, all phenomena of samsara and nirvana are of one taste from the standpoint of liberation: is that not the training in higher wisdom?

### U07
If that is so, those who have entered the door of the Tathagata's precious teaching and taken refuge in the Three Jewels take up particular practices that are not one and the same but different. All of them are alike in being Dharma. Yet they say, "Only this of mine is Dharma; that of his is not Dharma at all," "The Nyingma Secret Mantra is not Dharma," "Vajrapani is not Dharma," and "Amanasi, mental non-engagement, is not Dharma." By saying these and other such things, they have disparaged the word of the Tathagata.

### U08
They have created the cause — attachment, aversion and delusion — and it ripens into its result, samsara and the lower realms. They themselves are the ones to be pitied.

### U09
So, if with the eye of the wisdom of hearing, reflection and meditation you see and hear a great many of the Sugata's scriptures, you will disparage none of them. You will gain sight, and once you have become learned and gained understanding, you will become a great spiritual friend for all, one who takes them into care.

### U10
So too the Blessed One said in the *Hundred Avadanas*:

Those who abide in shamatha will be liberated through vipashyana. Those who abide in vipashyana will be liberated through shamatha. One who abides in both in equal measure will be liberated through the elements.

### U11
And the Protector of the Three Worlds, the precious guru, also said:

Wisdom enhances method's roots of virtue;
Method enhances wisdom's roots of virtue.

### U12
Therefore, for people whose minds have different ways in, practicing different approaches to Dharma becomes the antidote to afflictions and conceptual thoughts. Then comes liberation, and in the great ocean of liberation all phenomena become one taste. This is the crux of all the scriptures of the Buddha, the Blessed One, the subject matter of the three pitakas, the stages of practice; it delights the learned and makes the heads of the deluded spin; it is the domain of those whose realization is profound and vast. This alone is what you should understand thoroughly.
