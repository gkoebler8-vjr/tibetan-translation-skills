# stages — construal (working file, not shipped)

Work: Jigten Sumgön (1143–1217), lam rim teaching recorded by Ön Sherab Jungne ("On the Dharma of the Stages of the Path"). Page = U01–U12, one argument: the three higher trainings have no fixed order; every Dharma door leads inside; sectarian dismissal is rebuked; two proof texts (U10 Avadanashataka, U11 Phagmodrupa); closing instruction (U12).
Brief (given, not asked): audience seasoned practitioner · purpose publication (reader edition, Edition Garchen Stiftung manner) · Sanskrit anglicized without diacritics where the tradition uses it · Tibetan names phonetic · no brackets/Wylie/locators in body · verse citations by tibetan-verse, citation mode · no contractions · em dashes allowed · doubts as Q: lines.
Register plan: exposition/debate with polite rhetorical questions (lags sam) U01–U09; framing U10–U11; instruction at U12 close (khong du chud par bya'o).
Cross-unit spans (one Tibetan period runs over several units): U03→U04 (zhugs pas → spong ba ni …); U08→U09 (yin pas → …); U09→U10→U11→U12 ('gyur ba yin te / … gsungs pa dang / … gsungs pas / … bya'o). Seams are carried by explicit English connectives.

UNIT        U01
FORM        prose; one period, one finite verb (gsungs so)
GENRE       exposition (thesis)
REGISTER    exposition; the master to disciples; plain, assertive
SOURCE      bslab pa rin po che gsum char sngon du gang 'gro ba ma nges pa sna tshogs su bcom ldan 'das kyis gsungs so //
WORDS       gsum char = all three (char "the whole set"; botok split cha + r); sngon du 'gro ba = go first, precede (not 'gro ba "beings"); ma nges pa = not fixed (aniyata); sna tshogs su = in various ways
CLAUSES     2  (a) embedded: verb='gro (intr) subj=gang "which [of the three]" + sngon du "in front/first"; nominalized 'gro ba, predicated by ma nges pa "is not fixed"
               (b) verb=gsung (tr, hon) frame=ERG bcom ldan 'das kyis / ABS = the trainings (elided, from bslab pa … gsum char) tense=past hon=hon
RELATIONS   kyis = agent · su = manner (adverbial) · sngon du = adverbial "first"
NEG/QUANT   ma (nges pa): scope = which one comes first (the order), not the trainings · gsum char = all three
SPINE       one sentence
CONSTRUAL   The Blessed One taught all three precious trainings in various ways; which of the three comes first is not fixed.
TERMS       bslab pa = training · bslab pa rin po che gsum = the three precious trainings · bcom ldan 'das = the Blessed One · gsungs (the Buddha) = taught
DOUBTS      Q: ma nges pa … sna tshogs su gsungs: (a, taken) manner, "taught them in various ways, with no fixed order" vs (b) content, "taught in various ways that the order is not fixed"; same sense, (a) reads naturally.

UNIT        U02
FORM        prose; one period: objection (snyam na) + reply ending in a polite rhetorical question (ma lags sam)
GENRE       debate (objection and reply)
REGISTER    debate/exposition; the master answers an imagined objector, courteous (lags = honorific copula); rhetorical question expects "yes"
SOURCE      (1) de ltar gyur na / (2) mi mthun pa sna tshogs rtsod pa'i gzhir gyur pa 'dis ci zhig bya snyam na / (3) gong du ji skad smos pa bzhin du / (4) chos kyi sgo brgyad khri bzhi stong gsungs pa / (5) sgo de la zhugs nas phug tu phyin pa ma lags sam /
WORDS       mi mthun pa = conflicting, discordant (positions); rtsod pa'i gzhi = ground/basis of dispute; ci zhig bya = what is to be done / what is it for; phug = innermost part, inner recess (cave/house image, not "cave" here); phyin pa = go, reach; lags = hon. copula
CLAUSES     5  (1) verb='gyur (intr) "become so" + na = condition (inside the objector's thought)
               (2a) relative: gzhir gyur pa — 'gyur (intr) subj='di, LA-DON gzhir = "become the ground for disputes over various conflicting [positions]"
               (2b) verb=byed (fut bya, tr) frame='dis = instrumental "by/with this", ABS ci zhig "what" → "what is to be done by this / what does this serve"
               (2c) verb=snyam (thought frame) + na = condition: "if you think …"
               (3) smos pa (past) + bzhin du = "just as was mentioned above"
               (4) verb=gsung (tr, hon, past) agent elided = the Blessed One (from U01) ABS = chos kyi sgo 84,000; gsungs pa = relative/topic "the 84,000 doors that were taught"
               (5) verb='jug (zhugs, intr) LA-DON sgo de la "into that door" + nas = sequence; verb=phyin (intr) LA-DON phug tu = goal; ma lags sam = negative honorific copula + interrogative (rhetorical, "is it not so?")
RELATIONS   na (1) = condition · 'i (rtsod pa'i gzhi) = modification · r (gzhir) = result/goal of becoming · 'dis = instrumental/means · snyam na = condition (if one thinks X → reply) · bzhin du = manner/comparison "as" · gsungs pa = relative (the doors that were taught) · sgo de = resumptive "that door [of those]" · nas = sequence (enter, then reach) · tu = goal
NEG/QUANT   ma lags sam: negation inside a rhetorical question = affirmation expected ("surely one reaches the inside") · sna tshogs = all sorts · brgyad khri bzhi stong = eighty-four thousand
SPINE       Eng: objection as one sentence (thought quoted); reply in two: the 84,000 doors; the rhetorical question
CONSTRUAL   If you think: "If that is so, what is to be done with this — what purpose does it serve — since it has become the ground for disputes over all kinds of conflicting positions?", then: just as was said above, the Blessed One taught eighty-four thousand doors of Dharma; is it not the case that one enters by such a door and then goes all the way to the innermost part?
TERMS       chos kyi sgo = door(s) of Dharma · sgo = door (the house image is held through U06) · phug = the innermost room (U02, U06) · zhugs = enter · lags sam = "Is it not so …?" (no contraction)
DOUBTS      Q: 'dis ci zhig bya: (taken) "what purpose does this serve" vs "what are we to do about this" (practical); reply fits both.
            Q: de ltar gyur na: inside the objector's thought (taken) or the master's frame ("That being so, if you think …"); no change of sense.
            Q: mi mthun pa sna tshogs rtsod pa'i gzhi: "disputes over all kinds of conflicting positions" (taken) vs "a ground on which all kinds of dissenters dispute".

UNIT        U03
FORM        prose; rhetorical question + a means clause (zhugs pas) whose main clause is U04 (one Tibetan sentence U03→U04)
GENRE       debate/exposition
REGISTER    as U02 (courteous rhetorical question, lags)
SOURCE      (1) sgo bsam pa'i 'jug sgo ma lags sam / (2) bsam pa'i 'jug sgo gang yin pa de la zhugs pas /
WORDS       bsam pa = thought, mind-set, disposition (āśaya, "what the mind is inclined to"); 'jug sgo = way in, entrance; gang yin pa de = whatever it is, that
            TOOL SLIP: botok joined sgo + ma into "sgo ma" (gatekeeper) — wrong; read 'jug sgo / ma lags sam (negated honorific copula + interrogative). Polarity checked.
CLAUSES     2  (1) copula lags (hon, neg) + sam (interrogative): subj sgo "the door", pred bsam pa'i 'jug sgo "the mind's way in" — rhetorical, expects "yes"
               (2) verb='jug (zhugs, intr) LA-DON de la "into that [entrance]"; subj elided = the practitioner (generic "you"); gang yin pa de = correlative "whichever it is … that"; pas → means/cause, main verb in U04 (spong ba … ni … bslab pa)
RELATIONS   'i (bsam pa'i 'jug sgo) = modification: the entrance proper to one's mind/disposition · gang … de = correlative (whichever … that one) · la = goal (enter into) · pas = means ("by entering") → U04
NEG/QUANT   ma lags sam = rhetorical negative question (affirmation) · gang = whichever (each person's own entrance)
SPINE       Eng: one question; then the means phrase that opens the sentence completed in U04 ("By entering …, you …")
CONSTRUAL   Is the door not the way in for one's mind? By entering whichever entrance is the way in for your mind, [U04: you thoroughly give up afflictions and conceptual thoughts, and that is the training in higher discipline].
TERMS       bsam pa'i 'jug sgo = the mind's way in (same notion as blo'i 'jug sgo, U12) · 'jug sgo = way in · zhugs = enter
DOUBTS      Q: bsam pa'i 'jug sgo: (taken) the way in that suits each mind/disposition (gang yin pa "whichever" implies one per person; cf. U12 blo'i 'jug sgo tha dad pa) vs "the entrance of intention/motivation" (bsam pa = intention; discipline as the intention to abstain) — the latter would read "Is the door not the entrance of intention?"
            Alt: bsam pa = "disposition"; "mind" chosen so that the entrance image reads naturally.

UNIT        U04
FORM        prose; main clause of the sentence opened in U03 (zhugs pas →); nominal predication, copula elided
GENRE       exposition (definition of the first training)
REGISTER    exposition, plain
SOURCE      nyon mongs pa dang rnam par rtog pa rnam par spong ba ni lhag pa tshul khrims kyi bslab pa /
WORDS       nyon mongs pa = afflictions (kleśa); rnam par rtog pa = conceptual thoughts (vikalpa); rnam par spong ba = complete abandoning (rnam par = completely); lhag pa = higher (adhi-); tshul khrims = discipline (śīla)
CLAUSES     2  (a) verb=spong (tr, vol) ABS nyon mongs pa dang rnam par rtog pa; agent elided = the one who enters (from U03); means = U03 zhugs pas; nominalized spong ba + ni = topic
               (b) nominal predicate lhag pa tshul khrims kyi bslab pa, copula elided (the shad continues the series U04–U05–U06; the series closes with ma lags sam in U06)
RELATIONS   (U03) pas = means → spong ba · dang = coordination (afflictions and thoughts) · ni = topic · kyi = modification (training in higher discipline)
NEG/QUANT   none; rnam par = completely (degree)
SPINE       Eng: completes the U03 sentence: "By entering …, you completely give up afflictions and conceptual thoughts: that is the training in higher discipline."
CONSTRUAL   By entering whatever is one's mind's way in, one completely gives up afflictions and conceptual thoughts; that complete giving up is the training in higher discipline.
TERMS       nyon mongs pa = afflictions · rnam par rtog pa = conceptual thoughts · rnam par spong ba = give up completely · tshul khrims = discipline · lhag pa tshul khrims kyi bslab pa = the training in higher discipline
DOUBTS      Q: scope of U06's ma lags sam: it may govern all three predications (U04–U06 as one rhetorical question); English asserts U04 and U05 and asks at U06 — same force, since the question expects "yes".

UNIT        U05
FORM        prose; one sentence (means clause + nominalized subject + nominal predicate, copula elided; series continues to U06)
GENRE       exposition (second training)
REGISTER    exposition, plain; extended image (door → house of liberation)
SOURCE      sgo de la zhugs pas sgo de'i nang rnam par grol pa'i khyim du phyin pa ni lhag pa ting nge 'dzin gyi bslab pa /
WORDS       de'i = genitive demonstrative "of that [door]" (RY's "even though" connective sense does not apply); nang = inside; rnam par grol pa = liberation (vimukti; spelled grol pa here, grol ba in U06/U12 — same word); khyim = house, home; phyin pa = go, arrive; ting nge 'dzin = samadhi
CLAUSES     2  (a) verb='jug (zhugs, intr) LA-DON sgo de la; subj elided = the practitioner; pas = means
               (b) verb=phyin (intr) LA-DON khyim du = goal; sgo de'i nang = location "inside that door" (modifies the goal); nominalized phyin pa + ni = topic → predicate lhag pa ting nge 'dzin gyi bslab pa (copula elided)
RELATIONS   la = goal · pas = means ("by entering") · 'i (sgo de'i nang) = location/modification · pa'i (rnam par grol pa'i khyim) = modification (the house that is liberation) · du = goal · ni = topic · gyi = modification
NEG/QUANT   none
SPINE       Eng: one sentence parallel to U04: "By entering that door, you reach the house of liberation inside it: that is the training in higher samadhi."
CONSTRUAL   By entering that door one goes inside it into the house of liberation; that arriving is the training in higher samadhi.
TERMS       rnam par grol ba/pa = liberation · khyim = house · ting nge 'dzin = samadhi · lhag pa ting nge 'dzin gyi bslab pa = the training in higher samadhi
DOUBTS      none beyond the U04 scope note

UNIT        U06
FORM        prose; one sentence; nominal predication closed by the honorific rhetorical question (ma lags sam) that ends the series U04–U06
GENRE       exposition/debate (third training)
REGISTER    exposition; courteous rhetorical question (lags)
SOURCE      sgo de'i phug 'khor ba dang mya ngan las 'das pa'i chos thams cad rnam par grol ba'i ngor ro gcig pa ni lhag pa shes rab kyi bslab pa ma lags sam /
WORDS       phug = innermost part, inner recess (JH "deep; inner"); mya ngan las 'das pa = nirvana (botok split it; one term); ngor = from the standpoint of, in the face of; ro gcig pa = of one taste; shes rab = wisdom (prajñā)
CLAUSES     2  (a) stative: subj 'khor ba dang mya ngan las 'das pa'i chos thams cad, pred ro gcig pa "are of one taste"; location sgo de'i phug (case marker elided: "in the innermost part of that door"); viewpoint rnam par grol ba'i ngor; nominalized + ni = topic
               (b) copula lags (hon) neg ma + sam: "is [that] not the training in higher wisdom?" (rhetorical; may scope over U04–U06)
RELATIONS   'i (sgo de'i phug) = location/possession (the door's inner part) · dang = coordination (samsara and nirvana) · pa'i = modification (phenomena of samsara and nirvana) · ba'i ngor = viewpoint ("from the standpoint of liberation") · ni = topic · kyi = modification
NEG/QUANT   ma lags sam = rhetorical (affirmation) · thams cad = all
SPINE       Eng: one sentence parallel to U04/U05, the question at the end
CONSTRUAL   In the innermost part of that door, all phenomena of samsara and nirvana are of one taste from the standpoint of liberation; is that not the training in higher wisdom?
TERMS       phug = the innermost room ("behind that door": the door's inner part) · 'khor ba = samsara · mya ngan las 'das pa = nirvana · ro gcig = one taste · ngor = from the standpoint of · shes rab = wisdom · lhag pa shes rab kyi bslab pa = the training in higher wisdom
DOUBTS      Q: sgo de'i phug: read as location with the case marker elided ("in the innermost room behind that door", taken) vs apposition ("the innermost room, namely the one taste …"); same sense.
            Q: rnam par grol ba'i ngor: "from the standpoint of liberation" (taken) vs "within liberation / before liberation".

UNIT        U07
FORM        prose; one long period: condition → (entered, took refuge, nas) → practise different practices, all equally Dharma (la = adversative) → quoted sectarian slogans (bya ba la sogs pas = by saying such things) → finite verb skur ba btab
GENRE       debate / rebuke
REGISTER    exposition turning to rebuke; quoted speech of sectarians is blunt and colloquial
SOURCE      (1) de ltar lags na / (2) de bzhin gshegs pa'i bstan pa rin po che'i sgor zhugs pa gsum la skyabs su song nas / (3) nyams len gyi bye brag mi gcig pa tha dad pa nyams su len pa kun yang chos yin par 'dra ba la / (4) nga'i 'di kho na chos yin / (5) kho'i de kho na chos min / (6) gsang sngags rnying ma chos min / (7) phyag na rdo rje chos min / (8) a ma na se yid la mi byed pa chos min / (9) bya ba la sogs pas / (10) de bzhin gshegs pa'i bka' la skur ba btab /
WORDS       TOOL SLIPS: "a ma"(mother)+na+"se"(Sera) — wrong: a ma na se = transliterated Skt. amanasi(kāra), glossed by yid la mi byed pa "mental non-engagement"; phyag + na + rdo rje = the name Vajrapani; "len pa" grasping — wrong: nyams su len pa = practise; "kho" only — wrong here: kho'i = "his"; btab first sense "make an aspiration" — wrong: skur ba 'debs (past btab) = disparage, deprecate (apavāda)
            de bzhin gshegs pa = Tathagata; bstan pa rin po che = precious teaching; gsum = the Three [Jewels] (dkon mchog elided); bye brag = particular kind; mi gcig pa tha dad pa = not one and the same, different (doubling for emphasis); bka' = the word (of the Buddha); gsang sngags rnying ma = the Nyingma Secret Mantra
CLAUSES     (1) copula lags (hon) + na = condition "if that is so"
            (2) verb='jug (zhugs, intr) LA-DON sgor; verb='gro (song, intr) LA-DON gsum la, skyabs su = "go for refuge to the Three"; zhugs pa … song nas = relative/sequence: [those] who entered … and, having gone for refuge, …; subj elided = practitioners
            (3) verb=len (nyams su len, tr) ABS nyams len gyi bye brag mi gcig pa tha dad pa; nominalized len pa kun yang = "all [this] practising"; pred chos yin par 'dra ba "alike in being Dharma"; la = adversative/concessive connective ("whereas, yet")
            (4)–(8) quoted slogans: copulas yin / min (plain, colloquial); (4) 'di kho na = restrictive "only this"; (5) de kho na with min = emphatic "that very thing of his is not" (not restrictive: "only his is not" makes no sense)
            (9) bya ba = quotative; la sogs pa = "and so on"; s = instrumental/means "by [saying] such things"
            (10) verb='debs (btab past, tr) frame=agent elided (the same people, "they") ABS skur ba, LA-DON bka' la = target: "disparaged the word of the Tathagata"
RELATIONS   na = condition · 'i = modification · r (sgor) = goal · la (gsum la) = goal of refuge · nas = sequence · gyi = modification · par 'dra ba = respect ("alike in being") · la (after 'dra ba) = adversative/concession → "Yet" · bya ba = quotative · la sogs pas = "by these and other such statements" (means) · la (bka' la) = target
NEG/QUANT   min ×4 (each slogan denies "is Dharma"); mi gcig pa = not the same; kho na ×2 (restrictive in 4, emphatic in 5); kun yang = all, every one; la sogs pa = and so on
SPINE       Eng: four sentences: condition + who they are and what they practise; "All of them are equally Dharma."; "Yet they say: '…' '…' '…' '…'"; "By saying these and other such things, they have disparaged the word of the Tathagata."
CONSTRUAL   If that is so: those who have entered the door of the Tathagata's precious teaching and gone for refuge to the Three Jewels then practise particular practices that are not one and the same but different; all of that practising is alike in being Dharma. Yet with statements such as "Only this of mine is Dharma; that of his is not Dharma", "The Nyingma Secret Mantra is not Dharma", "Vajrapani is not Dharma", "Amanasi — mental non-engagement — is not Dharma", and so on, they have disparaged the word of the Tathagata.
TERMS       de bzhin gshegs pa = the Tathagata · bstan pa = teaching · skyabs su 'gro = take refuge in · dkon mchog gsum = the Three Jewels · nyams su len = practise / take up (a practice) · nyams len = practice · bka' = word (of the Buddha) · skur ba 'debs = disparage · gsang sngags = Secret Mantra · rnying ma = Nyingma · phyag na rdo rje = Vajrapani · a ma na se = amanasi · yid la mi byed pa = mental non-engagement · chos (teaching) = Dharma
DOUBTS      Q: phyag na rdo rje: the deity's cycle, or (likelier with amanasi beside it) the Indian master Vajrapani (b. 1017), Maitripa's disciple, who taught Mahamudra in Tibet; English keeps the bare name — footnote for the editor.
            Q: a ma na se: transliteration of Skt. amanasi(kāra) (Maitripa's non-mentation cycle); normalized to "amanasi"; the Tibetan spelling is a ma na se.
            Q: kho'i de kho na chos min: kho na taken as emphatic ("not Dharma at all"); a restrictive reading ("only his is not Dharma") is excluded by sense.
            Q: subject of skur ba btab elided: taken as the same practitioners ("they"), i.e. those among them who speak so; could be "people".
            Issue: gsum = the Three Jewels (head noun dkon mchog elided in the Tibetan, supplied).

UNIT        U08
FORM        prose; continues the subject of U07 (the disparagers); ends in yin pas, whose consequence opens U09
GENRE       exposition (karmic consequence)
REGISTER    exposition; sober, not heated
SOURCE      (1) chags sdang rmongs gsum gyi rgyu byas / (2) 'khor ba dang ngan song gi 'bras bur smin pa rang nyid snying rje ba yin pas /
WORDS       chags (pa) = attachment; sdang (ba) = aversion; rmongs (pa) = delusion; rgyu = cause; 'bras bu = result; smin pa = ripen; ngan song = the lower realms; rang nyid = they themselves; snying rje ba = pitiable, an object of compassion (not "compassionate person" here)
CLAUSES     2  (1) verb=byed (byas past, tr) agent elided = the disparagers (U07) ABS chags sdang rmongs gsum gyi rgyu "the cause [that is] the three: attachment, aversion, delusion"
               (2) verb=smin (intr) LA-DON 'bras bur "into the result [that is] samsara and the lower realms"; smin pa = relative/nominal linked to rang nyid; copula yin: rang nyid snying rje ba "they themselves are pitiable"; pas = cause/consequence → U09
RELATIONS   gyi (gsum gyi rgyu) = apposition (the cause = the three poisons) · gi (ngan song gi 'bras bu) = apposition (the result = samsara and lower realms) · r = result of ripening · smin pa → rang nyid = the ones who ripen thus (implicit reason for pity) · pas = cause → "So" at the head of U09
NEG/QUANT   gsum = the three (attachment, aversion, delusion)
SPINE       Eng: two sentences; the pas lands as "So" opening U09
CONSTRUAL   They have created the cause, which is attachment, aversion and delusion; it ripens into its result, samsara and the lower realms; they themselves are the ones to be pitied. So [U09] …
TERMS       chags pa = attachment · sdang ba = aversion · rmongs pa = delusion · rgyu = cause · 'bras bu = result · smin pa = ripen · ngan song = the lower realms · snying rje ba = to be pitied
DOUBTS      Q: chags sdang rmongs gsum gyi rgyu: appositive "the cause, namely attachment, aversion and delusion" (taken) vs objective "causes of attachment, aversion and delusion" (the disparagement breeds more of the three).
            Q: smin pa … rang nyid: the cause ripens (taken, English subject "it") vs the people "ripen into" samsara; and rang nyid snying rje ba = "they are pitiable" (taken) vs "one should feel compassion oneself".
            Issue: the final pas is realized as "So" at the head of U09 (one Tibetan period runs on).

UNIT        U09
FORM        prose; one period: (So, from U08 pas) condition (mthong ba dang thos na) → result clauses (mi 'debs shing / mig rnyed pa dang / … rnyed nas / … 'gyur ba yin) → te (semi-final; the support follows in U10–U11)
GENRE       exposition turning to the positive counterpart (what the non-sectarian practitioner becomes)
REGISTER    exposition with generic "you"; warm, unhurried
SOURCE      (1) thos bsam sgom gsum gyi shes rab kyi mig gis bde bar gshegs pa'i gsung rab mang po zhig mthong ba dang / (2) thos na gang la yang skur ba mi 'debs shing / (3) mig rnyed pa dang / (4) mkhas pa dang go ba rnyed nas / (5) yongs su 'dzin pa yongs kyi dge ba'i bshes gnyen chen por 'gyur ba yin te /
WORDS       TOOL SLIPS: rnyed pa "material gain" — wrong: mig rnyed pa = gain the eye, gain sight; 'dzin pa "grasping" — wrong: yongs su 'dzin pa = take into one's care (parigraha); 'debs "make an aspiration" — wrong: skur ba 'debs = disparage; bde ba + r + gshegs pa split — one term, Sugata
            thos bsam sgom = hearing, reflection, meditation; shes rab kyi mig = the eye of wisdom; gsung rab = scriptures; mang po zhig = a great many; mkhas pa = learned(ness); go ba = understanding; yongs kyi = of/for all; dge ba'i bshes gnyen = spiritual friend (kalyāṇamitra)
CLAUSES     (1)+(2a) verbs mthong (tr, invol) and thos (tr, invol) coordinated by dang under one condition na; instrument thos bsam sgom gsum gyi shes rab kyi mig gis; ABS bde bar gshegs pa'i gsung rab mang po zhig; subj elided = generic "you"
            (2b) verb='debs (pres/fut, tr) neg mi; ABS skur ba; LA-DON gang la yang "[at] any of them" → "disparage none"; shing = coordination
            (3) verb=rnyed (tr) ABS mig "gain the eye/sight"; dang = coordination
            (4) verb=rnyed ABS mkhas pa dang go ba "learning and understanding"; nas = sequence ("having gained …, then")
            (5) verb='gyur (intr) LA-DON dge ba'i bshes gnyen chen por = result "become a great spiritual friend"; modifiers: yongs su 'dzin pa (participle, "who takes [others] into care") and yongs kyi "for all"; yin te = semi-final, support follows (U10–U11)
RELATIONS   (U08 pas →) "So" · gsum gyi / kyi = modification (the eye of the wisdom of hearing, reflection and meditation) · gis = instrument · pa'i = modification · dang + na = coordinated condition (see and hear → if) · shing = coordination · dang = coordination · nas = sequence · r = result of becoming · te = continuation into the supporting citations
NEG/QUANT   mi 'debs with gang la yang = disparage none (universal negation) · mang po zhig = a great many · yongs kyi = for all
SPINE       Eng: "So, if … you see and hear a great many of the Sugata's scriptures, you will disparage none of them." + "Your eyes will be opened; you will become learned and gain understanding, and then you will become a great spiritual friend to all, taking them into your care."
CONSTRUAL   So: if, with the eye of the wisdom of hearing, reflection and meditation, you see and hear a great many of the Sugata's scriptures, you will disparage none of them; you will gain the eye; having gained learning and understanding, you will become a great spiritual friend for all, one who takes them into care — [as the following shows].
TERMS       thos bsam sgom = hearing, reflection and meditation · shes rab kyi mig = the eye of wisdom · bde bar gshegs pa = the Sugata · gsung rab = scriptures · skur ba 'debs = disparage · mkhas pa = learned · go ba = understanding · dge ba'i bshes gnyen = spiritual friend · yongs su 'dzin pa = take into one's care
