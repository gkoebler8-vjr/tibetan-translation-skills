### U01
HEADER: U01 · prose: enumeration, 13 items of one continuing period, set as seven "It is" sentences · exposition (definition) · new practitioner · not a citation (root text, Toh 127, ch. 4)
TEXT:
It is freedom from birth and from rebirth, and knowledge of how rebirth takes place. It is having laid down the burden. It is the wisdom of the tathagatas. It is becoming a leader among the buddhas. It is the cure for desire, the calming of anger, and the dispelling of delusion. It is applying oneself to what is proper and completely rejecting what is improper. It is the aspiration for virtuous qualities and the wish to be free of samsara.
NOTES:
Q: skye ba med pa taken as "freedom from birth", paired with the two rebirth items that follow; alt. "non-arising" (of phenomena).
Q: sangs rgyas kyi khyu mchog tu gyur pa: "a leader among the buddhas"; alt. "becoming a buddha, a leader of beings". The image of the lead bull of the herd is let go.
Q: 'dod chags gso ba: gso ba read as "cure" (desire treated as an illness); the sense "nourish" is excluded by the context.
Q: rigs par rnal 'byor du byed pa: "applying oneself to what is proper", read as the pair of the next item ("rejecting what is improper"); alt. "practicing yoga in the proper way".
Issue: mtshams sbyor ba (rebirth-linking, the connection to a new life) rendered "rebirth"; the third item is unpacked as "knowledge of how rebirth takes place".
Issue: the "It is ..." frame (per brief) supplies the copula; "it" is the samadhi defined by the whole list, U01–U05.
Check: in-context (no Agent tool in this session); no findings.

### U04

### U05
HEADER: U05 · prose: last 3 items and the closing definition, four sentences · exposition (definition), vocative address · new practitioner · not a citation (root text, Toh 127, ch. 4)
TEXT:
It is dwelling in none of the three realms. It is an acceptance of all phenomena that accords with emptiness. It is an intense aspiration for the wisdom of omniscience. This, young man, is what is called samadhi; young man, practicing such qualities as these, rather than practicing wrongly, is what is called samadhi.
NOTES:
Q: gang in the closing sentence may be "whoever" (the practitioner) rather than the act; the sense is the same.
Issue: khams gsum thams cad la mi gnas pa rendered "dwelling in none of" (the negation scopes over "all").
Issue: bzod pa here is "acceptance" (of the truth about phenomena), not "patience"; ste between the two definitions is carried by the semicolon.
Check: in-context; 1 minor: kyi after the verb is adversative and had become "and not"; restored as "rather than practicing wrongly".

### U06

TEXT:

NOTES:
Q: rgyas par may qualify the verses ("in extensive verses") rather than the teaching.
