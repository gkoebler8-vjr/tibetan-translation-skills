# sutra.construal.md — King of Samadhis Sutra (Toh 127), ch. 4, units U01–U12

Page brief: audience new practitioner; purpose publication (reader edition, 84000 Reading Room manner);
verse = free verse, one English line per pada, unmetred; no contractions; doubts as Q: lines.
Speaker: the Buddha (bcom ldan 'das) to the youth Candraprabha (zla 'od gzhon nur gyur pa).
U01–U05 continue one enumeration begun before this page: a string of nominalized items joined by dang,
closed in U05 by "... ni gzhon nu ting nge 'dzin ces bya ste" (these, young man, are called samadhi).
English frame (brief): "It is ..." sentences, 1–4 items each, Tibetan item order kept, no numbering;
"it" = the samadhi being defined. Not a citation: this is the root text itself (no dm.py identify).
Tool slips seen in annotate (corrected by eye): U01 gso ba glossed "individual/soul" (here: cure);
U02 "dang las" split as "dang la"+"s" (las = actions lost) and "mngon par 'du byed" split ('du = gather);
U05 "la stong pa" merged, "gang de" merged, "byed kyi" kyi labelled GEN (after a verb it is adversative);
U06 tshe = "life", rgya = "China", rnam par spros pa = "proliferation", 'di nyid = "here", rab tu = "best";
U10 "bde bar gshegs pa" split, gshegs pa = tathagata; U12 skyes pa = "man" (here: arisen).
Brief says 7-syllable padas: the stanzas U07–U12 count 9 syllables per pada (9 x 4 each).

UNIT        U01
FORM        prose; 13 list items, one period continued from before and into U02; shads after items 6 and 10
GENRE       exposition (definition by enumeration)
REGISTER    the Buddha defining samadhi to Candraprabha; neutral, plain
SOURCE      (1) skye ba med pa dang (2) mtshams sbyor ba med pa dang (3) mtshams sbyor ba shes pa dang
            (4) khur bor ba dang (5) de bzhin gshegs pa'i ye shes dang (6) sangs rgyas kyi khyu mchog tu gyur pa dang /
            (7) 'dod chags gso ba dang (8) zhe sdang nye bar zhi ba dang (9) gti mug bsal ba dang
            (10) rigs par rnal 'byor du byed pa dang / (11) mi rigs pa rnam par spong ba dang
            (12) dge ba'i chos la 'dun pa dang (13) 'khor ba las thar bar 'dod pa dang
WORDS       skye ba = birth; mtshams sbyor ba = rebirth-linking (pratisandhi: connecting to a new life);
            khur = burden, bor = past of 'bor (put down, cast off); khyu mchog = lead bull of the herd = leader;
            gso ba = cure, heal (NOT "nourish", NOT MITRA's "individual"); nye bar zhi ba = calming (upa-sam);
            bsal ba = dispelled, cleared away; rigs pa = what is fitting/proper; rnal 'byor du byed pa = apply oneself
            (yoga); rnam par spong ba = reject completely; 'dun pa = aspiration (chanda); thar ba = be freed
CLAUSES     13 nominalized predications, no finite verb (the copula is the list's frame, supplied as "It is").
            (4) bor ba: past stem, perfect ("having laid down"); (6) gyur pa: past, "having become/becoming";
            (10) byed pa present; (13) 'dod pa: wish, complement thar bar (terminative: to be free)
RELATIONS   dang x13 = coordination (list); (6) tu = terminative (become INTO a leader); kyi (6) = genitive,
            partitive "among the buddhas"; pa'i (5) = genitive "of the tathagatas"; (10) r on rigs pa = la-don:
            domain/goal ("to what is proper") or manner ("properly") — see Q; (12) la = goal of aspiration;
            (13) las = source ("from samsara"), r = terminative complement of 'dod pa
NEG/QUANT   (1) med pa: privative, no birth; (2) med pa: no rebirth-linking; (11) mi rigs pa = negated
            adjective "improper" (the negation is inside the object, not on the verb); rnam par = completely
SPINE       English: 6–7 "It is" sentences grouping items 1-3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7-9 | 10-11 | 12-13
CONSTRUAL   [The samadhi] is: being free of birth; being free of rebirth-linking; knowing how rebirth-linking
            takes place; having put down the burden; the wisdom of the tathagatas; having become a leader
            among the buddhas; the cure of desire; the calming of anger; the dispelling of delusion; applying
            oneself to what is proper; completely rejecting what is improper; aspiration for virtuous qualities;
            the wish to be freed from samsara; (and ... list continues)
TERMS       skye ba = birth; mtshams sbyor ba = rebirth; ye shes = wisdom; de bzhin gshegs pa = tathagata;
            'dod chags / zhe sdang / gti mug = desire / anger / delusion; dge ba'i chos = virtuous qualities;
            'dun pa = aspiration; 'dod pa = wish; 'khor ba = samsara
CITATION    none (root text)
DOUBTS      Q: (1) skye ba med pa = "freedom from birth" (paired with the two rebirth items); alt. "non-arising"
               (anutpada of phenomena).
            Q: (6) khyu mchog: "a leader among the buddhas"; alt. "becoming a buddha, a leader [of beings]";
               the bull image is let go.
            Q: (7) gso ba read as "cure" (desire as a disease); "nourish" is excluded by the context.
            Q: (10) rigs par rnal 'byor du byed pa = "applying oneself to what is proper" (paired with (11));
               alt. "practicing yoga in the proper way".

UNIT        U02
FORM        prose; 8 list items (continuing the U01 period)
GENRE       exposition (definition by enumeration)   REGISTER as U01
SOURCE      (1) lhag pa'i bsam pas sgrub pa dang / (2) mi nyal ba la sten pa dang (3) spong ba mi gtong ba dang
            (4) chos dkar po rnams yongs su srung ba dang (5) skye bo rnams la yid mi rton pa dang
            (6) las rnams la mngon par 'du mi byed pa dang (7) nang gi skye mched rnams yid la mi byed pa dang /
            (8) phyi'i skye mched rnams la kun tu mi spyod pa dang
WORDS       lhag pa'i bsam pa = high resolve (adhyasaya, "superior intention"); sgrub pa = practice;
            mi nyal ba = not sleeping = wakefulness (jagarika); sten pa = devote oneself to, rely on (seva);
            spong ba (noun, as a practice) = renunciation; gtong ba (verb) = give up; chos dkar po = "white"
            = wholesome qualities; yongs su srung ba = safeguard; skye bo = people; yid rton pa = trust;
            las = actions/karma; mngon par 'du byed pa = form, fabricate (abhisamskara) -> "create";
            skye mched = sense fields (ayatana); yid la byed pa = attend to; kun tu spyod pa = engage in/with
CLAUSES     8 nominalized predications; (1) instrumental means s (pas): "with/by means of high resolve"
RELATIONS   dang = coordination; (1) s = instrumental (manner/means); (2) la = goal of sten pa;
            (5) la = object of trust; (6) la = domain ("with regard to actions"); (8) la = object of engagement;
            gi / 'i = genitive (inner / outer)
NEG/QUANT   (3) mi gtong ba: not giving up; (5) yid mi rton pa: no trust; (6) 'du mi byed pa: not forming
            (negation inserted inside the compound mngon par 'du byed); (7) yid la mi byed pa: no attention;
            (8) kun tu mi spyod pa: not engaging. (2) mi nyal ba: lexicalized negative = wakefulness.
            rnams = plural (qualities, people, actions, sense fields)
SPINE       English: "It is" sentences: 1 | 2-4 | 5-6 | 7-8
CONSTRUAL   [It is:] practicing with high resolve; devoting oneself to wakefulness; not giving up renunciation;
            safeguarding the wholesome qualities; placing no trust in people; not forming (karmic) actions;
            not attending to the inner sense fields; not engaging with the outer sense fields; (and ...)
TERMS       sgrub pa = practice; sten pa = devote oneself to; spong ba = renunciation (noun) / give up (verb);
            chos dkar po = wholesome qualities; skye bo = people; las = karma; skye mched = sense fields
DOUBTS      Q: (3) spong ba taken as the practice of renunciation ("not abandoning renunciation"); alt. "not
               giving up the effort to abandon [faults]" (spong ba = prahana, which also means exertion).
            Q: (6) "creating no karma": mngon par 'du byed = forming/fabricating; alt. "not setting actions in
               motion".

UNIT        U03
FORM        prose; 8 list items (continuing)
GENRE       exposition (definition by enumeration)   REGISTER as U01
SOURCE      (1) bdag la mi stod pa dang (2) gzhan la mi smod pa dang (3) khyim la chags pa med pa dang
            (4) so so'i skye bo la yid mi rton pa dang / (5) tshul khrims kyi rgyu 'thun pa dang
            (6) bsnyen par dka' ba dang (7) gzi chen po dang (8) bdag shes pa dang
WORDS       stod pa = praise; smod pa = disparage; khyim = household/home (kula); chags pa = attachment;
            so so'i skye bo = ordinary people (prthagjana); tshul khrims = discipline (sila);
            rgyu 'thun pa = natural outcome, outflow (nisyanda; lookup: "natural consequence, outcome");
            bsnyen par dka' ba = hard to approach (durasada: unassailable); gzi = splendor (tejas);
            bdag shes pa = knowing oneself (one's own capacity)
CLAUSES     8 nominalized predications
RELATIONS   dang = coordination; (1)(2) la = object of praise/blame; (3)(4) la = object of attachment/trust;
            (5) kyi = genitive (the outcome OF discipline); (6) r = terminative complement of dka' ba
            ("difficult to approach")
NEG/QUANT   (1) mi stod pa: not praising; (2) mi smod pa: not disparaging; (3) med pa: no attachment;
            (4) yid mi rton pa: no trust. A bare "It is not praising ..." would read as negating the copula,
            so (1)-(2) take "refraining from", (3)-(4) take "having no"/"placing no".
SPINE       English: 1-2 | 3-4 | 5 | 6-8
CONSTRUAL   [It is:] not praising oneself; not disparaging others; having no attachment to households;
            placing no trust in ordinary people; the natural outcome of discipline; being hard to approach;
            great splendor; knowing oneself; (and ...)
TERMS       tshul khrims = discipline; rgyu 'thun pa = natural outcome; so so'i skye bo = ordinary people
DOUBTS      Q: (3) khyim "households" (lay families as well as one's own home); alt. "home".
            Q: (6) "hard to approach" in the sense of unassailable/imposing (beside "great splendor");
               alt. "unassailable".

UNIT        U04
FORM        prose; 9 list items (continuing)
GENRE       exposition (definition by enumeration)   REGISTER as U01
SOURCE      (1) mi rgod pa dang (2) spyod lam phun sum tshogs par gnas pa dang (3) gnod sems med pa dang
            (4) mi rtsub pa dang (5) gzhan la mi gnod pa dang / (6) bshes gnyen rnams la rjes su srung ba dang
            (7) gsang ba'i sngags kun srung ba dang (8) tshul khrims dang ldan pa la rnam par mi 'tshe zhing
            gnod pa med pa dang (9) 'jam por smra ba dang
WORDS       mi rgod pa = not agitated/excited (anuddhata; also "not frivolous"); spyod lam = deportment
            (iryapatha, conduct in the postures); phun sum tshogs pa = perfect; gnas pa = abide, maintain;
            gnod sems = malice; rtsub pa = harsh; gnod pa = harm; bshes gnyen = friends; rjes su srung ba =
            look after, protect; gsang ba'i sngags = secret mantra / secret counsel (see Q); kun = all;
            tshul khrims dang ldan pa = those who have discipline; rnam par 'tshe ba = injure, hurt;
            'jam por smra ba = speak gently
CLAUSES     9 nominalized predications; (8) two coordinated predicates (mi 'tshe zhing ... gnod pa med pa)
RELATIONS   dang = coordination; (2) r = terminative/manner ("abiding in perfect deportment");
            (5)(6)(8) la = object/recipient; (7) 'i = genitive; (8) dang ldan pa = "endowed with";
            zhing = coordination of the two predicates; (9) r = manner ("gently")
NEG/QUANT   (1) mi rgod pa; (3) med pa; (4) mi rtsub pa; (5) mi gnod pa; (8) mi 'tshe + gnod pa med pa.
            (7) kun = all. (6) rnams = plural.
SPINE       English: 1-2 | 3-5 | 6-7 | 8 | 9
CONSTRUAL   [It is:] not being agitated; abiding in perfect deportment; having no malice; not being harsh;
            not harming others; looking after friends; keeping all secret counsel [or: guarding all secret
            mantras]; not injuring and doing no harm to those who have discipline; speaking gently; (and ...)
TERMS       gnod sems = malice; gnod pa = harm; spyod lam = deportment; tshul khrims = discipline
DOUBTS      Q: (7) gsang ba'i sngags kun srung ba taken as "keeping all secret counsel": sngags renders Skt.
               mantra, which also means counsel, and the item sits among duties to friends and others;
               the usual Tibetan sense is "guarding all secret mantras" (MITRA/JH: secret mantra; JV also
               "secret religious instructions").
            Q: (1) mi rgod pa "freedom from agitation"; alt. "freedom from frivolity".

UNIT        U05
FORM        prose; last 3 list items + the closing definition (2 sentences)
GENRE       exposition (definition), closing formula   REGISTER as U01, with vocative address
SOURCE      (1) khams gsum thams cad la mi gnas pa dang (2) chos thams cad la stong pa nyid kyi rjes su 'thun pa'i
            bzod pa dang (3) thams cad mkhyen pa'i ye shes la shin tu 'dun pa ni gzhon nu ting nge 'dzin ces bya ste /
            gzhon nu gang de lta bu'i chos de dag la sgrub par byed kyi log par sgrub par mi byed pa 'di ni
            ting nge 'dzin ces bya'o //
WORDS       khams gsum = the three realms; gnas pa = dwell; stong pa nyid = emptiness; rjes su 'thun pa =
            in accord with (anulomika); bzod pa = acceptance (ksanti, of the truth; not "patience" here);
            thams cad mkhyen pa = omniscience; shin tu = intensely; gzhon nu = young man (vocative);
            ces bya = is called; de lta bu'i chos de dag = such qualities as these; log par = wrongly
CLAUSES     (a) [list] ni ... ting nge 'dzin ces bya: "[all these] are called samadhi" — topic ni marks the
            whole list as subject; verb bya (is called), ste = non-final.
            (b) gang ... sgrub par byed kyi log par sgrub par mi byed pa 'di ni ting nge 'dzin ces bya'o:
            "that one practices such qualities and does not practice wrongly — this is called samadhi";
            gang ... 'di = relative/correlative; final 'o.
RELATIONS   (1) la = locus ("in the three realms"); (2) la = domain ("with regard to all phenomena"),
            kyi = genitive (accord WITH emptiness), pa'i = modification (an acceptance that accords);
            (3) pa'i = genitive, la = goal of aspiration; ni = topic (whole list); ste = continuation
            between (a) and (b) (elaboration: "that is"); (b) la = object of practice; kyi after the verb
            byed = ADVERSATIVE ("practices, but does not practice wrongly"); r = complement.
NEG/QUANT   (1) thams cad la mi gnas pa: negation over the universal = dwelling in NONE of the three realms;
            (2) thams cad = all phenomena; (b) mi byed pa: does not practice wrongly. shin tu = intense.
SPINE       English: 3 "It is" sentences for (1)(2)(3); then (a) and (b) joined by a semicolon (for ste).
            CHECK (in-context): kyi (adversative) had become "and not"; restored as "rather than practicing wrongly".
CONSTRUAL   [It is:] dwelling in none of the three realms; an acceptance, regarding all phenomena, that accords
            with emptiness; an intense aspiration for the wisdom of omniscience. Young man, [all] this is
            called samadhi; young man, practicing such qualities as these, and not practicing wrongly — this
            is called samadhi.
TERMS       ting nge 'dzin = samadhi; stong pa nyid = emptiness; bzod pa = acceptance; thams cad mkhyen pa'i
            ye shes = the wisdom of omniscience; gzhon nu = young man
DOUBTS      Q: (b) gang may be "whoever" (the practitioner) rather than the act; the sense is the same.

UNIT        U06
FORM        prose; one narrative period, 2 clauses (ston cing / bstan to)
GENRE       narrative framing (introduces the verses)
REGISTER    narrator, plain; honorific subject (bcom ldan 'das)
SOURCE      de nas bcom ldan 'das kyis de'i tshe chos thams cad kyi rang bzhin mnyam pa nyid rnam par spros pa'i
            ting nge 'dzin 'di nyid ston cing / de'i tshe zla 'od gzhon nur gyur pa la rgyas par tshigs su bcad pa'i
            dbyangs kyis rgya cher yang dag par rab tu bstan to //
WORDS       de'i tshe = at that time (tshe NOT "life"); rang bzhin = nature; mnyam pa nyid = sameness;
            rnam par spros pa = fully elaborated / displayed (vipancita; NOT "proliferation"); this phrase is
            also the sutra's long title; 'di nyid = this very; ston = teach (present); zla 'od = Candraprabha;
            gzhon nur gyur pa = the youth (kumarabhuta); tshigs su bcad pa = verse; dbyangs = voice, melody;
            rgyas par / rgya cher = extensively, at length; yang dag par = correctly, fully; rab tu = thoroughly
CLAUSES     (a) bcom ldan 'das kyis (ERG agent) ... ting nge 'dzin 'di nyid (ABS) ston (pres., "teaching") cing;
            (b) [same agent] zla 'od ... la (recipient) ... dbyangs kyis (instrument) ... bstan (past) to (final);
            object of bstan elided = the same samadhi.
RELATIONS   de nas = then (sequence); kyis = agent; pa'i = modification (the samadhi of the displayed sameness);
            cing = coordination/simultaneity between teaching (a) and teaching in verse (b); la = recipient;
            kyis = instrument (by the melody of verse); r (rgyas par, rgya cher, yang dag par) = manner
NEG/QUANT   thams cad = all phenomena; 'di nyid = this very (emphatic)
SPINE       English: 2 sentences; seam at cing made explicit with "And"; both de'i tshe kept.
            CHECK (in-context): "which fully reveals" made the samadhi the agent of the passive participle
            rnam par spros pa; recast as "in which the sameness ... is fully revealed".
CONSTRUAL   Then, at that time, the Blessed One was teaching this very samadhi, the full display of the sameness
            of all phenomena in their nature; and at that time he taught it to the youth Candraprabha in the
            melody of verse, extensively, at length, correctly and thoroughly.
TERMS       bcom ldan 'das = the Blessed One; ting nge 'dzin = samadhi; rang bzhin = nature; mnyam pa nyid =
            sameness; tshigs su bcad pa = verse
DOUBTS      Q: rgyas par may qualify the verses ("in extensive verses") rather than the teaching.
            Note: the four manner adverbs are stacked Skt. preverb/adverb calques; rendered together.

UNIT        U07
FORM        verse, 4 padas x 9 syllables; not a citation (the Buddha's own verses in the root text)
GENRE       teaching declaration    REGISTER the Buddha, first person, plain and firm
SOURCE      (1) ngas ni bdud rtsi'i sgo yang rab tu phye / (2) chos kyi rang bzhin ci 'dra de bzhin bshad /
            (3) skye ba dag kyang ci 'dra ngas bstan te / (4) mya ngan 'das pa phan yon bcas pa bshad //
WORDS       bdud rtsi = nectar of immortality (amrta; JH "deathless"); sgo = door; rab tu phye = opened wide;
            ci 'dra de bzhin = just as it is; bshad = explained (past); skye ba dag = births; bstan = shown;
            mya ngan 'das pa = nirvana; phan yon = benefits; bcas pa = together with
CLAUSES     4 finite clauses, all past (perfect): (1) ngas (ERG) phye "I opened"; (2) [ngas] bshad "explained";
            (3) ngas bstan te "I showed, and"; (4) [ngas] bshad "explained"
RELATIONS   (1) 'i = genitive (door OF/TO the deathless); (2) kyi = genitive; ci 'dra ... de bzhin = correlative
            "as it is, so"; (3) kyang = additive "also"; te = non-final link (3)->(4) "and";
            (4) bcas pa = comitative "with its benefits"
NEG/QUANT   none
VERSE-NOTES ni (1) topic/filler; yang (1) filler (count); dag (3) plural, partly for the count; (3) ci 'dra
            elides the de bzhin of (2) (parallel); first person repeated in (3) (ngas) — keep "I have" x4.
SPINE       3 sentences: (1) | (2) | (3)+(4)
CONSTRUAL   I have opened wide the door to the deathless. I have explained the nature of phenomena just as it
            is. I have also shown what birth is like, and I have explained nirvana together with its benefits.
TERMS       bdud rtsi = the deathless (nectar image let go); chos = phenomena; mya ngan 'das pa = nirvana
DOUBTS      Q: skye ba dag = births (samsara, set against nirvana in (4)); alt. "how things arise".

UNIT        U08
FORM        verse, 4 x 9; not a citation
GENRE       instruction    REGISTER the Buddha instructing; second person implied, injunctive mood
SOURCE      (1) sdig pa'i grogs po rtag tu rnam par spang / (2) dge ba'i bshes gnyen la yang bsnyen par bya /
            (3) 'du ba spangs te nags khrod gnas bca' zhing / (4) byams pa'i sems ni rtag tu bsgom par bya //
WORDS       sdig pa'i grogs po = bad friends (papamitra); spang = future/injunctive of spong (avoid, give up);
            rnam par = completely; dge ba'i bshes gnyen = spiritual friends (kalyanamitra); bsnyen par bya =
            should rely on / keep company with; 'du ba = gatherings, crowds (socializing); spangs = past;
            nags khrod = forest; gnas bca' = set up one's dwelling; byams pa'i sems = a loving mind (maitri);
            bsgom par bya = should cultivate
CLAUSES     (1) [you] spang (injunctive) — object sdig pa'i grogs po; (2) [you] bsnyen par bya (gerundive);
            (3) spangs te (having given up) ... gnas bca' (injunctive) zhing; (4) bsgom par bya (gerundive)
RELATIONS   (1)(2) 'i = genitive (friends OF evil / OF virtue); (2) la = object of reliance; yang = "and";
            (3) te = sequence ("give up ... and"); zhing = "and" linking (3) to (4); (4) pa'i = genitive
NEG/QUANT   rtag tu = always (1) and (4); rnam par = completely (1)
VERSE-NOTES ni (4) filler; contrast sdig pa / dge ba (bad / spiritual friends) kept in lines 1-2.
SPINE       2 sentences: (1)+(2) | (3)+(4); all imperatives
CONSTRUAL   Always avoid bad friends completely, and rely on spiritual friends. Give up crowds and make your
            dwelling in the forest, and always cultivate a loving mind.
TERMS       dge ba'i bshes gnyen = spiritual friends; byams pa = love; bsgom pa = cultivate
DOUBTS      none beyond the mood: injunctive future/gerundive taken as imperative.

UNIT        U09
FORM        verse, 4 x 9; not a citation
GENRE       instruction + conditional promise    REGISTER as U08
SOURCE      (1) tshul khrims dag pa rtag tu bsrung byas te / (2) sbyangs la dga' ba'ang rtag tu thob par bya /
            (3) gtong ba dang ni shes rab bsnyen byas na / (4) ting 'dzin 'di ni rnyed par dka' mi 'gyur //
WORDS       tshul khrims dag pa = pure discipline; bsrung byas = guarded, kept; sbyangs = the ascetic practices
            (dhuta; JH, RY; sbyangs pa'i yon tan = dhutaguna); dga' ba = delight; thob par bya = should attain;
            gtong ba = generosity (tyaga); shes rab = insight (prajna); bsnyen byas = practiced, devoted
            oneself to; rnyed pa = find; dka' mi 'gyur = will not become difficult
CLAUSES     (1) [you] bsrung byas te; (2) thob par bya (gerundive); (3) bsnyen byas na (condition);
            (4) ting 'dzin (ABS subject) dka' mi 'gyur (future)
RELATIONS   (1) te = sequence/coordination ("keep ..., and"); (2) la = object of delight; 'ang = "also/and";
            (3) dang = coordination; na = CONDITION ("if"); (4) r = complement (hard TO find)
NEG/QUANT   (4) mi 'gyur: will not be [hard]; rtag tu = always (1) and (2)
VERSE-NOTES ni (3)(4) filler; 'ang (2) additive/filler.
SPINE       2 sentences: (1)+(2) imperatives | (3) if-clause + (4) result
CONSTRUAL   Always keep your discipline pure, and always take delight in the ascetic practices. If you devote
            yourself to generosity and insight, this samadhi will not be hard to find.
TERMS       tshul khrims = discipline; shes rab = insight (kept distinct from ye shes = wisdom); gtong ba =
            generosity
DOUBTS      Q: sbyangs = the ascetic practices (dhuta); alt. "training, purification".

UNIT        U10
FORM        verse, 4 x 9; not a citation
GENRE       promise (result of hearing)    REGISTER the Buddha, plain
SOURCE      (1) nyan thos mang po yi ni sa min pa / (2) zhi ba'i sa 'di dag kyang thos nas ni /
            (3) bde bar gshegs pa'i chos la mngon sum gyur / (4) sangs rgyas yon tan bsam yas thob par 'gyur //
WORDS       nyan thos = hearers (sravaka); mang po = many; sa = level/ground; min pa = is not; zhi ba'i sa =
            levels of peace; thos nas = having heard; bde bar gshegs pa = the Sugata; chos = Dharma;
            mngon sum gyur = become directly perceived/realized (pratyaksa); yon tan = qualities;
            bsam yas = inconceivable; thob par 'gyur = will attain
CLAUSES     (1) relative clause "which is not the level of many hearers" modifying sa 'di dag in (2);
            (2) [subject elided] thos nas (having heard); (3) mngon sum gyur (past stem: "come to perceive
