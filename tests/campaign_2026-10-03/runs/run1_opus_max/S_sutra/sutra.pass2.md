# sutra — Pass 2 faithful draft (from sutra.construal.md)

U01
It is freedom from birth, freedom from rebirth, and knowledge of how rebirth takes place. It is having laid down the burden. It is the wisdom of the tathagatas. It is becoming a leader among the buddhas. It is the cure for desire, the calming of anger, and the dispelling of delusion. It is applying oneself to what is proper and completely rejecting what is improper. It is aspiration for virtuous qualities and the wish to be free of samsara.

U02
It is practicing with high resolve. It is devoting oneself to wakefulness, not abandoning renunciation, and safeguarding wholesome qualities. It is placing no trust in people and creating no karma. It is paying no attention to the inner sense fields and not engaging with the outer sense fields.

U03
It is refraining from praising oneself and from disparaging others. It is having no attachment to households and placing no trust in ordinary people. It is the natural outcome of discipline. It is being hard to approach, having great splendor, and knowing oneself.

U04
It is freedom from agitation and maintaining perfect deportment. It is freedom from malice and from harshness, and doing no harm to others. It is looking after friends and keeping all secret counsel. It is causing no injury or harm to those who keep discipline. It is speaking gently.

U05
It is dwelling in none of the three realms. It is an acceptance of all phenomena that accords with emptiness. It is an intense aspiration for the wisdom of omniscience. This, young man, is what is called samadhi; young man, to practice such qualities as these, and not to practice wrongly, is what is called samadhi.

U06
Then, at that time, the Blessed One was teaching this very samadhi, which fully reveals that all phenomena are the same in their nature. And at that time he taught it to the youth Candraprabha at great length and in full, in melodious verse.

U07
I have opened wide the door to the deathless.
I have explained the nature of phenomena just as it is.
I have also shown what birth is like,
and I have explained nirvana and its benefits.

U08
Always avoid bad friends completely,
and rely on spiritual friends.
Give up crowds and make your home in the forest,
and always cultivate a loving mind.

U09
Always keep your discipline pure,
and always take delight in the ascetic practices.
If you devote yourself to generosity and insight,
this samadhi will not be hard to find.

U10
Once you have heard of these levels of peace,
which are not the domain of many hearers,
you will see the Sugata's Dharma directly
and attain the inconceivable qualities of a buddha.

U11
When you see intelligent people who are fit vessels
and who have truly entered the mind of awakening,
if you establish them firmly in unsurpassed wisdom,
this king of samadhis is not hard to find.

U12
Seeking food, consuming it, and what it turns into:
these are the things over which envy arises.
Understand them thoroughly,
and this samadhi will not be hard to come by.
