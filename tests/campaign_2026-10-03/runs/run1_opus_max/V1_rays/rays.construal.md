## Period A — U01–U05: ONE Tibetan sentence (homage)

Skeleton of the whole period (read with every unit below):
[U01 rang don du chos sku … bzhugs SHING] [U02 … yon tan ldan pa DANG] [U03 gzhan don du longs sku … snang ba DANG] [U04 … sprul skus … khyab CING] [U05 mdzad pa bcu gnyis su bstan TE … rgyud brlan TE grol bar mdzad PA'I] → head: bde bar gshegs pa rnams kyi gtso bo shAkya seng ge LA → [lus ngag yid gsum gus pas] 'dud DO.
= U01–U05a are one coordinated chain of predicates, all bound by the genitive -pa'i (U05) as a relative clause on the head noun Shakyasimha; the only finite verb is 'dud do (speaker = the commentator, 1st person elided, humble verb). Scheme: the two benefits (rang don = dharmakaya, U01–U02; gzhan don = form kayas, U03–U05) and the three kayas.
English plan (SPINE): keep units in order; the head arrives last as in the Tibetan, so the English uses "his/he" for the Buddha and closes "I bow … to him, Shakyasimha …" (the pa'i relation carried by the anaphoric "him"; normal order, no fronted "To him"). Each unit gets its own sentence(s); coordination at the seams carried by parallelism ("For his own benefit … / For the benefit of others …"), "also", and sequence.

### U01
UNIT        U01 (1st of 5 clauses of period A)
FORM        prose (homage period, opening member of the chain)
GENRE       homage (mchod brjod), doctrinal description of the dharmakaya (Uttaratantra vocabulary)
REGISTER    homage: commentator about the Buddha, 3rd person; elevated where the Tibetan is (homage), never archaic
SOURCE      1 rang don du chos sku 'pho 'gyur med pa khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa la/
            2 glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi zhing rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa/
            3 spros pa dang bral ba nam mkha' ltar srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du bzhugs shing/
WORDS       rang don = benefit for oneself (svartha), paired with gzhan don U03 · 'pho 'gyur med pa = without transition or change ('pho = shift/transmigrate, 'gyur = change) · khams = the element (dhatu, Uttaratantra sense), NOT "realm" here · rnam par dag pa (botok split rnam pa + r; read rnam par, intensive) = completely pure · glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains · rab tu zhi = completely pacified/ceased (intr., invol.) · 'dus ma byas pa = not compounded · spros pa = elaboration (prapanca) · srid zhi = samsara and nirvana · mnyam pa nyid = equality · ngang = state (light locus noun) · bzhugs = hon. abide
CLAUSES     main predicate of U01: bzhugs (intr., vol., HON) — ABS subject = chos sku (+ attributes a–f); LA-DON locus = srid zhi mnyam pa nyid kyi ngang du; manner = nam mkha' ltar; role/purpose = rang don du; tense: timeless descriptive present
            embedded attributes of chos sku: (a) 'pho 'gyur med pa; (b) khams rang bzhin gyis rnam par dag pa [khams in apposition to chos sku; rang bzhin gyis = manner]; (c) LA → (d) glo bur gyi dri ma rab tu zhi [ABS subj = dri ma]; ZHING → (e) rgyu rkyen gyis 'dus ma byas pa [gyis = agent of 'dus byas]; (f) spros pa dang bral ba [dang = from, with bral]
            elided: the possessor of chos sku = the head noun of U05 (Shakyasimha) — supplied as "his"
RELATIONS   du (rang don du) = role/purpose "for his own benefit" (scopes over the whole U01–U02 description)
            gyis (rang bzhin gyis) = manner "by nature" · la (after dag pa) = locus/connective linking natural purity and purity from stains (the two purities) · zhing = coordination · gyis (rgyu rkyen gyis) = agent · dang (bral ba) = separation "from" · ltar = simile, adverbial to bzhugs · kyi = gen. · du (ngang du) = locus · shing = coordination → U02
NEG/QUANT   med (no 'pho, no 'gyur — both negated) · ma (ma byas: not compounded by causes and conditions) · bral (free from elaboration) · intensifiers rnam par, rab tu = completely; no quantifier
SPINE       English: 2 sentences, head first (english.md failure 8): (1) For his own benefit, the dharmakaya [a] abides like space in the equality … ; (2) It is [b] in which [d]; it is [e] and [f]. Attributes keep their order; only the main verb is brought forward.
CONSTRUAL   For his own benefit [as the benefit for himself], the dharmakaya — which neither shifts nor changes; which is the element, completely pure by nature, in which [and in addition] the adventitious stains are completely pacified; which is not compounded by causes and conditions; and which is free from elaboration — abides, like space, in the state of the equality of samsara and nirvana; and [→ U02].
TERMS       rang don = his own benefit · chos sku = dharmakaya · khams = the element · glo bur gyi dri ma = adventitious stains · rab tu zhi = entirely pacified (Alt "have ceased") · rgyu rkyen = causes and conditions · 'dus ma byas pa = not compounded · spros pa dang bral ba = free from elaboration · srid zhi = samsara and nirvana · mnyam pa nyid = equality · ngang du bzhugs = abides in (ngang absorbed into the locus)
DOUBTS      Q: la after "rnam par dag pa": locus ("the element … in which the stains are pacified") or additive ("pure by nature and, besides, with its stains pacified"); same doctrine (the two purities); rendered as locus.
            Q: khams: apposition to chos sku ("the dharmakaya … is the element") or subject of an embedded clause ("whose element is pure by nature"); rendered as apposition.
            Q: nam mkha' ltar: simile on bzhugs ("abides like space", the grammatical reading) or on spros pa dang bral ba ("free from elaboration like space"); rendered on bzhugs.

### U05

FORM        prose

## Period B — U06–U09: frame + ONE verse sentence across four stanzas (citation)

CITATION    rgyud bla ma = Mahayanottaratantrashastra (theg pa chen po rgyud bla ma'i bstan bcos), Toh 4024 (Tengyur), verbatim run 28/28 syllables at BO_T06_D4024:64b-12 (dm.py identify); also inside Toh 4025 (the vyakhya, root verses embedded). VARIANTS    citing text OMITS canonical pada 64b-22 "bzo yi gnas la mkhas pa dang" (skill in the arts), which stands in D between "lhums su 'jug dang bltams pa dang" (U07 p3) and "btsun mo'i 'khor dgyes rol pa dang" (U07 p4). Rendered AS GIVEN (15 padas); omission flagged for the editor.
MODE        citation mode (verse quoted in prose) · kind rising · band 3/4 beats (7-syllable padas)
Skeleton    frame: de yang rgyud bla ma las [U06] … ces gsungs so [U09]
            [U06 p1 thugs rje chen pos (INSTR) 'jig rten mkhyen (SUBJECT, epithet)] [p2 'jig rten kun la gzigs nas ni (SEQ)] [p3 chos kyi sku las ma g.yos par (MANNER)] [p4 sprul pa'i rang bzhin sna tshogs kyis (MEANS)]

SPINE (Eng) U06: frame sentence (prose) + "With great compassion the Knower of the World / looks upon the entire world; / then, without moving from the dharmakaya, / through emanations of many kinds —" (gzigs nas rendered as a finite verb + "then", keeping the sequence; the dash opens the list)

            The closing frame "ces gsungs so" is folded into the opening formula ("it is said"); no English line for it (frame, not content).
