# rays — after Pass 4 revision (final text), all units

### U01
For his own benefit, the dharmakaya, never shifting or changing, abides like space in the equality of samsara and nirvana. It is the element, completely pure by nature, in which the adventitious stains have been entirely pacified; it is not compounded by causes and conditions, and it is free from elaboration.

### U02
It also possesses qualities such as the ten powers and the four fearlessnesses.

### U03
For the benefit of others, the sambhogakaya, endowed with the five certainties, appears as the foremost guide of a retinue of regent bodhisattvas.

### U04
From the clouds of his great loving compassion, the supreme nirmanakaya pervades the realm of sentient beings without partiality.

### U05
Displaying the twelve deeds, he moistens, with the rain of the sublime Dharma of peace and bliss, the mindstreams of disciples whose potential has truly awakened, and then liberates them. I bow in reverence with body, speech, and mind to him, Shakyasimha, foremost of the sugatas.

### U06
Accordingly, in the *Sublime Continuum* it is said:

With great compassion the Knower of the World
looks upon the entire world;
then, without moving from the dharmakaya,
through emanations of many kinds —

### U07
manifestly taking birth,
departing from the Tushita heaven,
entering the womb and being born,
delighting in his circle of consorts,

### U08
renunciation, the practice of austerities,
going to the heart of enlightenment,
vanquishing the hosts of Mara,
complete enlightenment, the wheel of Dharma,

### U09
and passing into nirvana —
in impure realms he displays these deeds
for as long as existence lasts.

### U10
Second:
The supreme Sage has conquered the four maras — the aggregates, the Lord of Death, the son of the gods, and the afflictions. His scriptures are free from the extremes of eternalism and nihilism and as unfathomable as the depths of the ocean. The sutras and tantras within them, the treatises of Nagarjuna, Asanga, and others, and the meaning of the profound pith instructions of the great Dharma Lord Drikungpa deserve veneration like a naga king's crown jewel. From within these, this text has been condensed with no stain of incomprehension, misunderstanding, or doubt. Just as everything one needs and wishes for comes forth when one prays to the beryl, supreme among jewels, this treatise accomplishes all aims, temporary and ultimate. I shall explain this essence, set out as the three: ground, path, and fruition.

### U11
Now, of the four points — the purpose and so on — the subject matter is the three: ground, path, and fruition.

### U12
The purpose is to understand it through study and reflection. The purpose of the purpose is that, by meditating on its meaning, the fruition becomes manifest. The connection is that the later relies on the earlier, so that step by step they are connected as means and what arises from the means.
